Send your finalized photos
straight to your Google Drive

Once you've reviewed and approved your wedding photos, Setnayan can upload everything to a folder in your Drive — full-resolution photos, 5-second clips, and a manifest of who's tagged in what.

1

Connect Drive

One-tap OAuth. Setnayan creates its own folder — never touches the rest of your Drive.

2

Review & approve

You have 7 days to approve photos before they unlock for guests & release.

3

Release to Drive

One button, one upload. We email you when it's done.

What we ask for: drive.file scope only Setnayan can write to and read files it creates inside the Setnayan folder. We can't see, list, or touch any other file in your Drive. Disconnect any time — your photos in the Setnayan folder stay yours.
