Guided saves ₱190,000 and 6 fewer vendors to coordinate. The Guided plan caught the corkage cost (₱46K), the 8km proximity rule (saving Tagaytay travel headaches), and booked the photographer 11 months out before slots closed. Net savings = 19× the ₱10K Guided fee.
The compare view doesn't always favor Guided. Some couples will run a DIY plan that's cheaper because they have a family caterer, a friend photographer, an inherited wedding gown. Setnayan shows the math honestly. The Guided plan wins when its intelligence (bundling, sequencing, pairings) actually compounds — which it usually does, but not always.
