## 🥇 THE ONE ASK THAT MATTERS — the Papic control room

**Why:** it is the only one of your six complaints with no drawing anywhere. The design
was made and accepted on 10 August; the bundle was never saved, and only its structural
summary survived — which is exactly why that page shipped **menus and no layout**.

### ⚠ Two things that decide whether this is worth doing twice

1. **Ask for it as a downloadable bundle** — a `.zip`, like `Foundation locked in.zip`.
   That is the whole difference between a design you own and a design that evaporates.
2. **Paste the corrections below with it.** The first version got four things wrong. If
   they are not stated, they come back.

### Paste this ↓

> I need a redesign of one screen: the couple's **Papic** page in a Philippines-first
> wedding platform — the control room for guest-and-crew photo capture. It ships and
> works; **nothing here is broken.** The problem is that ~20 cards live in a single
> vertical scroll, so nothing can be found and nothing feels related to anything else.
>
> **Who uses it:** an engaged couple, not technical, Filipino, English-reading, planning
> over 6–18 months. **They open it on a phone far more than a laptop.**
>
> **They arrive in one of three moods:** setting up (months before, long considered
> choices) · buying (shots, money decisions) · looking at photos (during and after the day).
>
> **What this page IS, in one line:** Papic is the couple's **collection of memories** and
> the **main source** that fills it. The Photos room is not a gallery bolted onto a
> settings page — it is the mouth of a collection the couple keeps for life. Their
> account-level memories shelf reads directly from what this page produces, including
> weddings they only attended. **Design the Photos room as the front of something
> permanent, and Set up / Cameras as what feeds it** — that hierarchy is the point, and the
> current page loses it by treating all twenty cards as peers.
>
> **The structure is already agreed and must be kept:** three rooms — *Set up*,
> *Cameras & shots*, *Photos* — with a tab strip; the room a couple lands on depends on
> where they are in the event; the money sits beside the cameras that spend it; a single
> attention row when the capture window is unset; one QR entry, not several.
>
> **What I need that I do not have:**
> 1. **Mobile screens** for each of the three rooms — this is the deliverable that
>    matters most.
> 2. **Desktop** for the same.
> 3. **A card-by-card mapping table** — every one of the ~20 cards, and which room it now
>    lives in, so nothing is silently lost.
> 4. The reasoning for any grouping that is not obvious.
>
> **Do not:** redraw the app shell, the navigation or the palette · invent features,
> prices or tiers · replace plain English with marketing copy · **solve it with tabs
> alone if the tabs just hide the same 20-item pile** (that is what happened last time) ·
> assume a big screen.
>
> **Four facts the previous version got wrong — these are binding:**
> - **Papic is now ONE product** — "Papic One" was retired as a separate purchase on
>   2026-08-11; the host buys shots into one pool and dedicates them to a camera's QR.
>   **The live ladder, read out of the catalogue today: 100 shots ₱50 · 3,000 ₱1,000 ·
>   10,000 ₱3,000 · 20,000 ₱5,000** (plus a free starter allowance). There is no ₱2,500
>   rung and **no ₱2,000 / 6,000 rung — that one was retired.** Draw the ladder as four
>   rungs, and never re-type prices from a design file: they are read from the catalogue
>   at build time so an admin price edit propagates without a redeploy.
> - **Preservation is a PAID product and nothing is "forever."** A purchase keeps the
>   full-resolution original until 3 months after the event ends (or 6 months from the
>   first photo, whichever is later). After that the original is replaced by a smaller
>   copy — **the photo is never deleted, only its resolution changes** — and the
>   compressed gallery is free for five years. Paid preservation exempts the couple's
>   *chosen* photos and videos from that shrink. **Never write "forever" anywhere.**
> - **Photo quality keeps all three choices** — Optimal · Full resolution · High
>   efficiency. Only the default moves: new events start on **Optimal**. So Set up has
>   **six** choices, not five.
> - **Preservation is opt-in and starts at nothing** — the couple picks what to keep. Do
>   not show a pre-filled selection or a standing bill.
>
> **Colours are locked:** page and cards cream `#FDFBF7` · ink `#2C2A29` · the ONLY action
> colour is terracotta `#C24E25` with cream labels · gold `#A9834B` is never a button.
> Money and dates in a monospace face. Minimum 44×44 tap targets.
>
> Deliver it as a downloadable bundle I can save.
