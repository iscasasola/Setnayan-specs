## 1. Strategy frame (reaffirmed)

- **Free cockpit = the magnet** (capped-not-crippled). Monetize the two emotionally non-negotiable moments: **"who do I hire?"** → the AI match (first paywall) · **"how do we remember this?"** → produced media + post-wedding keepsakes.
- **Two pricing logics, by moment:**
  - **Heroes = no-brainer pricing** (acquisition — get them in cheap, anchored against a scary alternative).
  - **Productions + keepsakes = value pricing** (ARPU — fired at high-willingness-to-pay moments, low price sensitivity).
  - Rule of thumb: *"Generous on the cockpit, firm at the moments."*
- **Ladder rule:** nothing à-la-carte except the big productions (SDE, Editorial, Pakanta Suite, the keepsakes) may exceed the **₱3,999 flagship.** This fixes the inverted RSVP (was ₱4,499 > flagship).
- **A no-brainer = perceived value ≈ 5–10× price, in the impulse zone, anchored against a costly alternative.** The lever is the anchor + packaging, not slashing the number.

## 2. Free-tier economics (established this session — "does maximizing free hurt us?")

- **Free couple lifetime cost ≈ ₱5–20** (R2 storage on a *capped* tier + a few emails; egress ₱0 on R2; renders/compute ≈ ₱0). Caps are the cost-control — hold them.
- **Net expected contribution ≈ ₱725 per free couple** (at 14% conversion, ₱6,000 ARPU). Every free signup ≈ ₱15 to hold, ≈ ₱725 expected value.
- **Worst case (100 users, 0 sales): bounded.** Cost = the fixed stack only (~₱3–8k/mo); the 100 users add ~₱20. **Never goes negative** — marginal cost ≈ ₱0. A 100/0 result is a *conversion/PMF signal* (check: paywall flag on? reached a buying moment? free too generous?), diagnosable at leisure on a tiny burn — not a cash emergency.
- **Two engines:** customer payments = profit · **vendor subscriptions = the recurring base that funds fixed ops** (~5–7 paying vendors covers all fixed cost). Free couples are also the ₱0-CAC viral loop (100–250 branded guest impressions each) and the marketplace liquidity that makes vendor subs worth buying.
- **The real risk isn't cost — it's over-generous free** (no reason to pay). Mitigation = caps hold + walls fire at the emotional moments.

- **Why ₱2,499 is defensible (not a gate):** a print magazine ≠ a printed webpage. It's a *different object* — fixed **A3** pages, cover, editorial spreads, **300 DPI** (print sharpness), **CMYK** (printer ink color), **bleed/crop marks** (clean trimmed edges), pulling **original high-res** photos. "Save-as-PDF the webpage" yields a broken screen-dump (cut-off images, printed URLs, ugly breaks). Market wedding magazines/albums run ₱10k–30k → ₱2,499 reads as a steal.
- **Build mandate (the price only holds if this ships):** the Editorial Magazine must be a *real A3 press-ready artifact*, NOT the website reflowed into a PDF. If it ships as a webpage export, the price collapses.
- **Ethical line:** don't cripple the free webpage to force the sale — *let them print it freely*. Win on the paid version being a **better object**, not on blocking the free path. (Same line as free-photo-wall-on-phone vs paid-venue-wall.)
- **Strategic note:** these two were deliberately moved OUT of the no-brainer tier (₱999) INTO the premium-keepsake tier (₱2,499). Correct, because they fire **post-wedding** — high emotion, low price-sensitivity. Different moment, different logic.

## 5. ✅ Web = compressed-only rule

> **Web/digital surfaces serve compressed (optimized) images. The high-resolution, print-grade originals are reserved for the paid print keepsakes** (Editorial Magazine + Photo Book).

- **Dual justification (keeps it honest):** (1) performance — every good site serves compressed images for fast loads; (2) it protects print value — "save-as-PDF the webpage" *can't* produce print quality because the web never shows it. You're following best practice that *happens* to reserve print quality for the paid product, not visibly crippling the free one.
- 🟡 **Recommended resolution — THREE tiers, so the "every guest gets their photos" guarantee isn't broken:**

| Tier | Serves | Free/Paid |
|---|---|---|
| **Display** (galleries, editorial site, photo wall) | Compressed, fast | **Free** |
| **Free download / Drive copy** | Standard "social" res — good enough to keep/share/post | **Free** (honors delivery guarantee) |
| **Print-grade high-res original** | 300 DPI, in the magazine / photo book | **Paid keepsake** |

- **Principle:** sell *print quality*, not *access*. Couples/guests still get shareable-quality photos free (promise kept); only the print-grade original is behind the wall.
- ⚠ Touches the **Papic "untagged-still-delivered" guarantee** + the **Drive-copy architecture** (R2 = system of record). Whatever "free download" res we set must keep "every guest gets their photos" true.
- ❓ **OPEN:** middle tier (free social-res download — my rec) vs **compressed-only-everywhere** (high-res *only* via paid keepsakes). Owner to settle.

## 8. 🟡 International payments architecture (V1.5 — NOT a V1 change)

- **Critical clarification:** **Apple Pay ≠ Apple IAP.** Apple Pay / Google Pay = a *card wallet* (0% store cut, physical goods + real-world services). **IAP / Play Billing** = the store's billing for *digital goods* (**15% < $1M/yr → 30%**), and it's *required* for digital content consumed in-app. Most Setnayan SKUs are digital → in-app would force IAP and take 15–30%, gutting the 95–99% margins.
- **The fix — web-first checkout** (the apps are Capacitor web-wrappers, so this is natural):

| Layer | Tool | Handles | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| International + wallets | **Stripe** | Global cards, **Apple Pay / Google Pay ON WEB** (0% store cut) | ~3.9% + ₱15 |
| PH-local | **PayMongo** | **GCash, Maya**, PH cards, bank | ~2.5–3.5% |
| Launch fallback (already live) | Manual BDO/GCash apply-then-pay | PH | ₱0 processor, 24-hr SLA |

- **Route app digital purchases to the mobile-web checkout** to keep full margin; native IAP only where Apple ultimately forces it. ⚠ Apple anti-steering rules (shifting via 2024–25 rulings) are the real gating risk — decide the IAP-vs-web-link split **before app-store submission**.
- **Key international buyer = the OFW funder** (parents/ninong abroad paying for the couple; relatives buying Panood access) → Stripe's wallet + global-card coverage matters.
- ❓ Verify PayMongo vs Stripe PH-local (GCash/Maya) coverage before committing; Stripe-alone is the simpler path if its PH coverage tests well.

**In-app vs website vs market** (in-app = IAP marked up ~15% to net the same; web = full margin):

|---|---|---|---|
