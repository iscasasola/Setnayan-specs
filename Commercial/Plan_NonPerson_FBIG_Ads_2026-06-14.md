> The same wedding questions swarm in from every direction → they (and the whole breadth of what you need) **converge into one app** → **"Set na 'yan."** — told entirely without a person on screen, in ≤29s, legible with the sound off.

## 4 · Channel + format spec (locked by owner 2026-06-14)

- **Platforms:** FB + IG paid (Reels, Stories, Feed).
- **Length:** **≤29s.** Hero cut ≈24s (leaves headroom); plus 15s and 6s cut-downs.
- **Aspect:** **9:16 hero** (1080×1920, Reels/Stories — primary spend) + **4:5 feed** (1080×1350). 1:1 optional.
- **Sound-off by default:** every line **burned in** as on-screen text. The spot must fully land muted.
- **Hook ≤1.5s:** tension first, brand later. Meta optimizes on 3-second views.
- **Safe zones:** keep text clear of top ~14% and bottom ~20% (Meta profile row + CTA button).
- **End-card CTA:** mirror the live site — *"Start planning · free"* + `setnayan.com` (confirm in §9).

## 5 · Creative direction — non-person (and the trade-off)

**Why non-person fits Setnayan:** zero likeness risk (no uncanny-valley AI faces that cheapen a premium brand), faster + cheaper, perfect brand control via the Clean Editorial palette, trivial reframing 9:16 → 4:5. **Trade-off:** loses the raw empathy of a real face — so emotion is carried by **motion, typography, the product UI, and sound** instead. For a premium founder-led brand on muted paid social, that trade is favorable.

**Strictness fork (see §9.4):** the hero needs **zero humans, zero hands** (pure UI + type + objects). If hands are acceptable, two warmer variants (device-in-hand "One Surface", artifact still-life "The Things") also open up for retargeting/organic.
