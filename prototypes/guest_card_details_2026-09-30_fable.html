5Private note and Access open

  • Private note is a text box. Closed, the row shows the first words of the note, or None.
  • Access is one dropdown: Guest only · Co-host · Limited helper. The line under it says what the system decided (live now, or starts when they sign in). Locked for the creator; a celebrant stays a co-host and the dropdown says so.
  • Account line: Not linked / Linked. Unlink lives in the ⋯ menu, not here — one place.
  • The three careful actions stay at the bottom of Access: Give this spot to someone else (only for a guest who has not replied — they type the new name), Take this seat back (new QR, unlinks their account), Remove guest (asks twice, as today).
