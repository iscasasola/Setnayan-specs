Setnayan · Admin console · designed pass · 2026-08-03

Nothing is removed. It is ordered — by how often you actually go there.

The admin has 108 pages — 96 with a fixed address plus 12 you open by clicking a record — behind a menu meant to show 79 links, and one bucket holds 27 of them. So this design stops sorting pages by topic and sorts them by how often you go there. Everything urgent collapses into one ranked worklist called Today — and you decide right in the list, without loading a page: open a row, confirm the payment, undo if you slipped. The four things you open every week get a menu row; the rest sit inside those rows as tabs. Every page stays reachable on both desktop and phone — the All surfaces screen is the full map, identical in each, and search is only a shortcut to it. On a phone the bottom bar leads with the must-do work, and the Menu tab opens that same complete map.

      What changed, in one line each. The 27-link “Overview” bucket became one ranked worklist you filter, so urgency lives in a single place instead of being spread across 27 menu rows. Money earns its own row because it is the only thing opened daily. Records, Studio and Platform keep one row each, with their contents as tabs inside — one door per destination, not four. Ugat Console and Setup drop to the quiet drawer — still one click, just not shouting. All surfaces is the full map, and it is the same screen on desktop and phone, which is the parity you locked on 4 July: same menu content, different orientation.

      Nothing is dropped — checked page by page. All 108 admin pages stay reachable: 79 sit on the All surfaces map directly (shown as 76 rows, because Payments, Payouts and the other queues appear once each rather than twice); 9 are old addresses that redirect onto it; 17 are pages you open by clicking something on a map page — a particular vendor, “new venue”, the compliance data sheet, the demo-vendor inquiries, the per-event-type screens inside Taxonomy; and 3 are the old menu-container pages this design replaces. The only thing gone is the sign-in hero video you retired yesterday.

      Deliberately left out: the duplicate tiles on the admin home page (the worklist replaces them), and any second or third door to a page that already has a tab. Those are removed doors, not removed pages.
