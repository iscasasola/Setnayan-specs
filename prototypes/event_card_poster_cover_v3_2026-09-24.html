What this is

  • Poster ratio. Every cover is a true 2:3 poster and the hero is composed for it: the paper’s hairline runs to the edge, the mark and names are sized from the poster’s own width, and the strip sits over the paper’s lower margin. Under 200px (the phone rail) the poster keeps mark · names · date, drops the minor lines, stacks the strip and keeps one chip.
  • Up next is the soonest event. Beside it sit only the facts the poster doesn’t print — days as the number, the one thing that needs you, the ring — with an (i) for how they are counted. On a phone the poster is full width and those facts fold back onto its strip.
  • Coming soon is everything else, soonest first, undated at the tail, and + New is the last poster in the row. On a phone it is a swipe rail with one poster peeking, not a two-up grid: the Up next poster already takes the first screen, a rail keeps the rest to one row beneath it, and a peeking edge is how a phone says “more this way”.
  • Unknown is not zero. A failed read prints “Couldn’t load what needs you” and an empty ring.
  • 8 events adds clearly labelled examples. The soonest of them is a wake, so it becomes Up next — the sort rule, applied honestly — and shows the quiet masthead in the featured slot.
