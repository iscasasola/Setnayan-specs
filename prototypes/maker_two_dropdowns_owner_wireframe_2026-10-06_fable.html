What this prototype settles (for review — nothing is approved):
  • Two controls, not a menu. The top bar's Stages | Studio toggle picks the side; the lower third's one item ▾ picks what you work on inside it. The pill shows a short name (March · Mood Board · Seat plan) — the full name is in the list. Each side remembers its last item.
  • The Maker edits exactly what guests see, page by page. Each stage carries its own guest tab bar, drawn at the bottom of the page preview just above the lower third — RSVP: RSVP form · When yes (the thank-you, the Digital Pass and the Celebration ◆ — Confetti · Fireworks · Petals · Sparklers · None) · When no (the sorry-to-miss-you note); Invitation: Welcome · Details · Our Love Story · Me; The Day: Live · Welcome · Camera · Gallery · Me; Save the Date is one page — with a caption You're editing · Invitation › Details. Two ways to pick a page: tap that tab bar as a guest would, or open the item ▾ (it reads "Invitation · Details") — the five stages; a one-page stage jumps straight there, a multi-page stage expands in place to its pages with their guest-bar icons, and the current page says Here. Swiping the lower third walks every part of the page and then continues into the next tab, so it is still one flow.
  • Stages: the row is [ item ▾ ] · [ Style | Text | Animate ] · 👁. Collapsed to that row while nothing is picked; a tap on the page (or on a tool) expands it upward in ~240 ms; × or an empty tap collapses it. The bar is still resizable when expanded (drag its top edge, up to half the screen). Swipe the row → the next part.
  • Tapping a part never leaves the stage. Every part gets Style · Text · Animate. Parts whose content comes from Studio (logo · dress code · entourage · love story · schedule · seat plan · E-Gifts · RSVP) or Info (event name · opening line · what to bring) show one quiet row at the top of Style — "Edit the Wedding March ›" / "Edit details ›" — which opens that editor; ‹ (or the Stages toggle) brings you back to the same stage and part. Text and Animate never show it. The canvas carries no badges — the part's Style says where its words come from: Names → "Edit in Studio › Info ›", Date → "Change the date in Suppliers ›" (locking a venue sets it), Place → "Change the venue in Suppliers ›". Names · Date · Place are three separately selectable parts, as is the line above them.
  • Studio › Look stays in the lower third with a small second choice (Background ▾ · Colours · Font · Music). Studio › Info is one full-screen form, no bottom nav: Your event (Event Name · A note from us · Opening line · What to bring), then Your Event Hub (Who can view · Event Hub address · QR code), with Reset (asks to confirm) and About as quiet rows at the very bottom. Every other Studio item is its own full-screen editor with its own bottom — the march's Not-walking tray, the schedule's add-a-moment, the mood board's palette.
  • ▶ Play runs the picked part's Build in → Action → Build out on the page; with nothing picked it plays the whole stage from the top as guests see it — the toolbars slide away, one tap stops it. There is no separate Preview. ✓ Apply carries the count of pending changes; ↺ Undo walks them back. Phone only — the owner asked to see the mobile version alone.
