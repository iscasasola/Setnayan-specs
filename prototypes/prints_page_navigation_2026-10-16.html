    Why a person loses their place

    • 7.5 screens, two signposts. "YOUR INVITATION SET" is on screen 1 and "FOR THE DAY" on screen 6 — five screens with no heading, while the top nav says only "Prints ▾".
    • Two pieces are taller than a screen (Finer Details 793 px, Event pass 994 px): mid-block you see switches with no piece name in view — the NFC switch sits right above "Menu" and reads as the Menu's.
    • The same row repeats — "Event Hub QR code · Always printed" three times, "Save · Guests see this right away" four times — so three screens look alike.
    • The heads that should anchor you are the tallest rows: the Event pass head lists all five sizes beside its picture and its dropdown — 11 wrapped lines, 245 px — exactly the screen the owner was on.
    • Global vs per piece is not drawn as such. Global: the QR (always), the NFC spot (prints on the pass, the details card and the QR poster), the one words form. Per piece: size, what it includes, its Saves, ticket style, poster photo, chart layout.

    The change — three things, nothing redrawn

    1. The piece bar, in the thumb zone. ‹ · the piece you are on ▾ · › under the form, 56 px. The ▾ is the one house dropdown (PickMenu → a sheet on a phone, group headings sticky, a tick on the current). A pick runs the shipped select(k), which already scrolls the editor column to that piece; the label follows the scroll (the piece whose head last passed the top). Phone only — on a computer the navigator column already lists the pieces.
    2. ON EVERY PRINT — one block at the head of the form. "Event Hub QR code · Always printed", the "Add an NFC sticker spot" switch (its ⓘ already says where it prints), one Save. The three per-piece "Always printed" rows and the NFC row under Finer Details' Kindly reply go. Same form, same field names — one place.
    3. Head lines name the chosen size, not every size. "Calling card · 90 × 54 mm · one per guest" (Event pass), "A5 · 148 × 210 mm" (Event card), "A2" (Entrance chart). Every size stays in the dropdown, where it is already picked. A head goes from 245 px to about 110.

    What stays exactly as shipped

    Every piece's picture in its head and its full-size sheet (approved 2026-10-14), the Saves, the include switches, the menu editor, Parents, Kindly reply, Ticket style, poster Background, the chart Layout, Download the set, the top nav. The four inline Saves stay (one form, saved where you changed it).

    Decided, not asked

    • The bar sits at the bottom, not under the top nav: tools live in the lower third, and the top nav stays as shipped. It is a bar, not a second Stages-style pill row.
    • The global block sits first: you meet it once and know it applies to everything below; the Invitation is the second thing on screen, as today.
    • "Prints ▾" in the top nav keeps naming the page; the bar names the piece.
