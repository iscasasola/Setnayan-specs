People & the places rail

Outside a place the side menu shows only your places. Inside one, the place announces itself the same way every time — mark · name · one line — then its own menu. People gets the Planning shelf pattern: title · ⓘ · count · (+), and a dashed + Add tile when a shelf is empty.

Rail classes are the shipped ones (fd-row · fd-rlabel · fd-rsub · fd-rctx); the delta is the header inside fd-rctx growing a mark and a sub-line, and the tool groups leaving the rail until a place is open. Names and numbers are fictional.
