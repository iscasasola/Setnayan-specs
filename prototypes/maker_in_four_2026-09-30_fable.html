The Maker in 4 — Page · Look · Details · the page itself

The Event Hub Maker with the same four-item feel the owner liked on the event dashboard (Home · Guests · Your Team · More Services). The preview is the editor: the hub exactly as guests see it fills the centre, you tap any text to type, tap any scene for its few settings. Around it only three buttons — Page ▾ · Look · Details — and the essentials (Exit · Undo · Phone/Desktop · Apply). Everything else under one ⋯. Nothing is lost — the table at the end says where every one of today's controls went. Sample: Ana & Miguel · March 13, 2027 · 2:30 PM.

1The page

The hub as guests see it, full centre. Tap text → type in place. Tap a scene → a slim sheet with its few settings. Every edit shows at once; nothing reloads.

2Page ▾

One dropdown, top-left: the stage (Save the Date · RSVP · Invitation · The Day · Post Event), then that stage's guest pages, in the guest bar's own words.

3Look

One panel. Pick a theme — one pick sets background, fonts and colours. Then Make it my own: colours · fonts · background · logo.

4Details

One panel of the event's facts: names & joiner, date & time, venues, love story, programme, entourage & Wedding March, seat plan. Saved once, shown on every page.

ADesktop, at rest — Invitation › Welcome

  • The centre is the hub itself, drawn the way a guest gets it on their phone (the toggle is on Phone; Desktop widens it). The guest's bar at the bottom — Welcome · Details · Our Love Story · Me — is the guest's real bar, not a Maker control.
  • Top bar, left to right: Exit · Page ▾ (stage › page) · Look · Details · Undo · Phone/Desktop · Apply (3) · ⋯. The count on Apply is the number of changes waiting; guests see nothing until Apply, as today.
  • No navigator column, no inspector column. The scenes are the page; you scroll it. Hovering a scene shows a soft outline; tapping one opens its sheet (frame B).
  • Every edit is instant — the preview is what you edit, so there is nothing to reload. Apply is the only round-trip.
  • ⋯ holds: View as ▾ (a guest who replied · not yet · new) · Play the page · Prints · Restore · Reveal · Add a scene. Nothing else lives there.
  • Cut on purpose: no Search, no Quick setup, no presets, no opening strips, no "apply to other hero pages", no fashion figures, no palette animation.

BTap the names — type in place

  • One tap on any text puts a caret in it. A wine outline shows what you're editing; you type, it changes right there. No form, no side field, no reload. (Owner: "I cannot edit texts?" — now you can, anywhere.)
  • A tiny floating bar above the text: Wording ▾ (how this fact is phrased — Ana & Miguel · Ana Reyes & Miguel Santos · Miguel & Ana) · Style ▾ (size · font · colour · align, one list) · Hide. Dates and times also get Format ▾ (frame C).
  • Tapping inside a scene also selects the scene — its sheet slides in on the right with its five settings: Background · Layout · Motion · Parts shown · Order, plus Play · Add a scene below · Hide scene. That is the whole of today's Format · Animate · Arrange · Content tabs, as five rows.
  • Names are a Detail. Typing Ana Reyes here is the same as typing it in Details — one source, shown everywhere (owner 2026-09-25). Apply counts it once.
  • No "Open Hero editor". The hero is this scene; you are already in it. Its logo lives in Look › Logo.
  • Click anywhere else, or press Esc, and the bar and sheet go away. Nothing asks "are you sure".

ELook — one button, one panel

  • Theme first: four thumbnails of the hub in that theme (Classic ✓ · Rustic · Modern · Luxe — today's shipped themes), then More themes ▾ for the other six. One pick sets background + fonts + colours across the whole hub, all stages.

  • Hover a theme and the preview wears it; click to keep it. Apply is still the only thing guests notice.
  • Nothing here is per stage — the Look is the hub's. Per-scene background stays in the scene's sheet (frame B).

FDetails — one panel of facts

  • Every fact the hub shows, in one list: Names + Joiner ▾ (& · and · at) · Date · Time · Venues · Love story · Programme · Entourage & Wedding March · Seat plan. Each row opens in place (▴), never on another page — no go-edit-elsewhere links.
