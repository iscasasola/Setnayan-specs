1 · The front door — live

Everything here works. Search really searches, the categories and chips really filter, cards really open, and you can walk the whole round trip from a stranger reading an article to a member inside the app and back out again.

Walk the seam in four presses. Signed out → open Studio Azul (bottom of the feed) → press Save this shop → sign in → watch the left menu swap while you stay on the shop, with it saved. Then Events in the menu takes you inside the app, and the SETNAYAN wordmark brings you back out still signed in. Signing out only ever happens under your picture, top right.
Try search too. Type photographer, caterer, emcee or photobooth — none of those is a category name, and the panel shows your word with our folder beside it as a place. That is the real problem this page had to solve.
The Marketplace is signed-in only. Switch You above and watch the whole group appear and disappear — a stranger gets stories and the tools; browsing suppliers starts once there is an event to browse against. "Find a supplier" goes with it, because it is the same destination under another word and leaving it would defeat the rule with a label.

Two things deliberately stay. Search still answers a signed-out person who types "photographer" — finding one supplier you already need is not browsing a directory, and cutting it would remove the one thing this page exists to solve. And "The first shops" stays on the feed, because it is recruitment, not a catalogue — and a shop's own page is public and in our sitemap either way, so hiding it here would not make it private.

⚠ One real cost, worth knowing: a search engine is always signed out. Those category rows were internal links to each category page, and removing them signed-out removes them for Google too. The category pages stay in the sitemap and keep working — the front page just stops pointing at them.

2 · The seam, as a diagram

The same round trip you just walked, in one picture. The rule that makes it one product: the rail never leaves. Same width, same side — the sign-in prompt is replaced in place by the account's own destinations.

1On the public site
Reading a shop page or an article. The rail's second group is the sign-in prompt. Nothing nags.
2They press Sign in
It opens over the page, not instead of it — what is behind stays visible, so it is obvious nobody is being taken anywhere.
3The prompt becomes the account
The same slot now holds Events · Alaala · People, and Spaces if they run something. Everything else in the rail is untouched.
4They land where they were
Back on the same page, with the thing they were doing done — the shop saved, the enquiry intact. Only a rail sign-in with nowhere to go lands on the board.
5Inside the app
Already drawn and approved — nothing redrawn. It wears terracotta, the front door wears gold, and that difference is deliberate.
6The wordmark is the way out
Back to the front door still signed in, account group open, first row reading "Back to your events." A visitor, not an ex-member.
7Signing out is its own act
Under the picture, top right, and nowhere else. Visiting the public site never signs anyone out.
8On a phone
The index sheet closes and the bottom pill rises. That is how a phone says you are inside.

3 · The decisions behind it

Every number was read from the live database or the shipped code on 2026-08-12.

The categories are real — and they need renaming

The labels a customer reads today are the internal words — the live marketplace says Look, Feast, Documentary, Booths. Below is a copy change only; addresses and anchors are untouched.
Why those five, when they are not the five biggest. The service count says how finely we chopped a folder up, not how much anyone wants it. Booths has 42 because there are 42 kinds of cart; catering has 7 because catering is one thing and everybody buys it. The visible five are ranked by what a couple books first and spends most on.

Three things found while drawing

1 · “Contact us” has no page. Drawn as Help, which exists.

2 · Pakanta has no front door. Sold, on the price list, reachable only from inside the app. Not drawn in the rail — a row that goes nowhere is the one thing this page forbids.

3 · The tools list was short, not long. The concept drew six; eight public doorways ship. Pawebsite, Palogo and Patiktok were missing.

Recommendations — not redraws

a · Match the furniture across the seam — same rail width and side, even carrying different content and a different button colour.

b · The sign-in panel wears the app's terracotta, not the front door's gold — it is the first room inside, not the last step outside. That is drawn here; press Sign in to see it.

c · The “usable without an account” dots are drawn but unpainted — a per-tool answer nobody has checked.

d · Reading time is real data, already computed.

e · The publishing schedule is now a front-page dependency.
