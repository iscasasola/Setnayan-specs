Final design pass — what this is

The Event Hub Controller is the one editing mechanism for the couple’s Event Hub: Logo · Reveal │ Save the Date · Invitation · On the Day · Post Event. Every scene comes from a template (25, in five families, shown as desktop or phone as you edit), reads scene → transition → scene, and is edited on the canvas itself. Changes are a draft until Apply; Restore goes back to what guests see; Reset returns a stage to the page we wrote. Free is real but limited to words and facts; everything that changes the look, motion or media is one Event Hub Pro unlock, tried in the draft and paid at Apply. Nothing on the page says “website”.

Walked end to end (phone and desktop), and fixed on the way

Save the Date → Reveal (No reveal → Sheer veil, Pro) → Logo (letters moved) → Invitation → ＋ template → transition marker (Scroll → Auto · speed) → ◐ Background (colour) → text typed on the canvas → eye hides a scene → Apply (needs Pro when the draft holds a Pro change). Friction fixed: a shared scrim opened Reset by itself; hover labels were clipped by the toolbar; the phone twin thumbnails bled out of their frames; the toolbar could outgrow the viewport; “Main” truncated; the first-run hint now also names ＋; on phone two sheets could stack (transition popover under the template picker) — opening any sheet now closes the popover; in Logo/Reveal mode the stages stay tappable (in the build a stage tap returns to scenes).

Five further improvements (not yet approved — recommendations)

  1. Undo / redo (S). A couple who drags the wrong scene or overwrites a line needs one tap back, before they learn Restore. Keep a short history inside the draft document — no migration.
  2. Autosave to the draft with a quiet “Saved” mark (S). Phones lose tabs; the draft should never be lost between Apply presses. Same event_site_drafts row, saved on pause — no migration beyond the draft table already planned.
  3. “Preview as a guest” walkthrough (M). One button that plays the four stages in order as a guest would meet them on the dates — reveal, Save the Date, Invitation, day, story — so the couple sees the handover, not four separate pages. Uses the existing ?phase= and ?as= views.
  4. Stage handover on the live dates, shown (M). A small line under each stage chip — “opens 18 Nov · closes 18 Dec” — from std_invitation_launch_date, event_date, event_end_date and the pin (launch_mode), so nobody is surprised by what guests get tomorrow. No migration.
  5. Low-end phone performance and accessibility (M–L). Thumbnails as small static renders, scenes lazy-mounted, one live preview at a time; every canvas action reachable by keyboard (select · move by arrows · ⌥↑/↓ to reorder), labels on every icon, reduced motion = fades. No migration.

Logo: letters drag and drop (pre-baked lifted “&”; the real build drags); “Upload your own logo” (SVG · PNG, monogram_uploaded_svg) replaces the letters, letter tools dim, frame · ink · animation still apply.
