What is pressable is framed; what is read is not. A neutral control is the surface colour with a 1.5 px --sn-edge outline (and the tile shadow where it floats); the main verb is filled terracotta; a destructive verb filled red; a dropdown the same outline with a terracotta chevron. A read-only stat, chart, gauge or group sits on the ground and ends with a --line divider. The one floating surface is a popup — something opened — and it darkens and blurs the page.

Dark works. Every value is the dormant html.dark pair from globals.css; the four marked NEW / CHANGED are the ones the file lacks or that measure under 3:1 (an edge, a divider) or 4.5:1 (a word). --sn-surface is new in both themes: in Light it equals --color-cream (white); in Dark the only raised value, --sn-glass-bg-raised, is blue-grey (30, 34, 41) against the kit's "warm, never grey", so a warm step #232019 is proposed. Appearance = Light · Dark is one dropdown in account settings (approved 2026-10-07).

Which already wear this frame

  • The dropdown (c) — the shipped PickMenu: sn-glass-bare rounded-2xl, rows min-h-11 rounded-xl hover:bg-ink/5, group dividers, terracotta chevron and tick, a sheet on a phone. The board only moves it onto --sn-surface and the float shadow.
  • The account menu (a), the ⋯ menus (b), the confirm box (d), the older toast (e) — each draws its own today. Here they borrow the dropdown's frame: radius 22, the float shadow, 44 px rows, a hairline between groups, one chip at a row's right.

Where the anchored panel and the sheet differ

The reference's panel hangs from the trigger at the top right — the rows sit in the top third, out of the thumb's reach, and the first row is nearest the trigger. Your ruling puts the same rows at the bottom: the first row is highest, Sign out is nearest the thumb, and the sheet is the same frame every other popup uses. Both darken and blur the page and close on a tap outside. The panel is drawn here only so the difference is visible; the ruling stands unless you change it.

What the sample values stand on

  • Replies — dated guest replies; so the bar strip, "+12 this week" and "This week ▾" are honest. The two bars after Today are the days ahead, drawn faded and empty.
  • Paid — dated payments; a ring of ₱150,000 of ₱200,000.
  • Guests seated — a ring: 70 of 120 have a seat. No change chip: seating has no history.
  • Shop views · Bookings — both stored with dates, so "+18%" and the sparkline are allowed.
  • Failed read — never ₱0: "—" and a plain line with Try again. Empty — "Nothing yet" and what will appear.

One meter per page: on a real page only one of these rings or strips is the page's meter; the rest are numbers.

Doors and not-doors

Doors (framed, pressable): the Guest list pill under Replies, the Budget pill under Paid, the This week ▾ dropdown, Try again. Not doors (no frame): every number, caption, bar strip, ring and sparkline; Guests seated, Shop views, Bookings and the empty state are read only. A stat that opens a page is not boxed whole — the pill beside it is the one framed thing, icon + word, following the button rule.

Three honest uses

  • (i) Budget — payments are dated, so the trend line (five payments, April → October) and "+₱35,000 this month" are true readings. Segments: Paid · Due soon · Remaining; the strip under it is Paid | Still to pay.
  • (ii) Guest replies — replies are dated; "+12 this week" is true. Segments: Coming · Not coming · No reply yet.
  • (iii) Setting up your Event Hub — 5 of 7 steps. No change chip and no trend line are drawn, on purpose: no history is stored for the steps, so "up from last week" would be an invented figure. The gauge shows only what is measured now.

Every number counts up to its value and every ring grows from zero on load (the 700 ms family), and shows at once under reduced motion.
