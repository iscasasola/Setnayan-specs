Wedding March — drag the names

The navigator holds one item, "Wedding March" — it never opens into names. The maker is the walks in order, by section — two columns, the left and the right of the aisle; every walk is one row with a left slot and a right slot, and an empty slot means that person walks alone. Every edit is a drag of a name — "no need to the toolbar. we can just drag the names." Walking together says nothing about being a couple.

Try it: drag a name onto another name → they trade places · into the line between walks → its own walk · into the line right above or below its own pair → the pair splits into two rows, one after the other (both walk alone) · onto an empty slot beside a alone walker → they walk together (names move within and across both columns) · down to Not walking → out of the march, and back up → into it. On a phone, hold a name ~¼ s to lift it; a plain swipe still scrolls.
