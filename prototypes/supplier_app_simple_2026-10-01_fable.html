✓What moved where

  • Today: the "Today at" tile, the KPI bento and the Needs-your-answer feed collapse into one Next card — the most urgent answer wins; the rest queue behind it, one at a time. Award banner, payout nudge, fee bills, lock/deposit notes → that same queue. Ongoing + Upcoming → Coming up (three rows).
  • Bar: five → four. Insights and Event Hub move into More; the shipped keys (overview · customers · shop · performance · on-the-day) do not change — only what the phone bar shows. The unread/inquiry badge stays on Customers.
  • Customers: the pipeline month-grid, the three dropdowns (type · service · agent) and the five folds (Messages · Clients · Availability · Proposals · Contracts) → one Filter ▾ (stage), one Show ▾ (column), and ⋯. Rows stay "waiting first" as shipped. The round + is the shipped Import an outside client.
  • A customer: the six tabs (Overview · Quote & Payments · Files · Schedule · Script · Activity) → key facts + one stage button + closed rows. Same words as the tabs. The lock handshake stays couple-asks, supplier-agrees.
  • Shop: services and packages become rows with a price and an on/off switch; profile, verification, payouts, reviews, team, the tool shelves (What couples see · Working with others · Protection) → ⋯. One round + adds.
  • More: Calendar, Earnings & payday, Messages, Insights (Performance + Demand), Event Hub (On the Day), Settings (Notifications · Plan · Team · sign out) — one line each. /more today redirects to Today; the sheet replaces it.
  • Desktop shows the same screens with more columns and the rows' panels open — never a different screen. The five-key side rail stays as is.
Admin next — same method. One Next card on Today (the oldest thing waiting on an admin), lists first with one search + one Filter ▾ + one Show ▾, one round + where adding exists, a bar of four with the rest in More. Not drawn here.
