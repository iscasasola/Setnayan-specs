Customers — opens on what's unanswered

Worth deciding

A fifth state is missing: Holding

You said yes; they haven't booked yet. A shop holding four couples for one date is exposed and nothing shows them that — and it's where a nudge earns money: "Camille hasn't booked yet, 9 days since you replied."

⛔ Not "whitelist"

In this product that word already means a calendar day you approve requests on one by one — and it means a second thing in the tier documents. Two meanings, one word. Keep it out of anything a shop owner reads.

⚠ Waitlist isn't finished

A shop can be waitlisted against, but picking someone does nothing today and still reports success. Honest to draw, must be fixed before a real shop sees the tab.

Nothing moves

The same five rooms

The rail carries the rooms the phone bar carries, in the same order. Somebody who learns the app on their phone already knows the desktop.

What width buys

You stop losing your place

On a phone, opening a customer replaces the list. On desktop the list stays, so answering three people is three clicks rather than three round trips.

Desktop only, honestly

The live preview

Seeing the card as a couple sees it, beside the form, needs width. On a phone it becomes a "Preview" button — the same thing, one tap away.

What works on a phone: everything

A shop owner is at a venue, in a car, on a shoot. Blocking work until they reach a desk means the work doesn't happen.

The rule

No feature is desktop-only

Every room, every action, on both. What changes is the layout, never what a shop is allowed to do.

Shopee is going the other way

Their own banner: from 31 August, all seller functions move exclusively to the phone app. The largest seller base in this market is being moved to mobile, not away from it.

Different from the admin, deliberately

Your console rule doesn't apply here

You ruled the admin answers on a phone and edits on a desktop, because an admin moves other people's money, prices and approvals. A shop owner edits their own shop. Different stakes, different rule.

Nudge, never block

Four things are simply easier big

Uploading a set of photos · writing a long description · reading a contract properly · going through a proposal's numbers. Each says "easier on a bigger screen" and then lets them do it anyway.

What changed from the last drawing

  • The four-tab bar is withdrawn. Your five rooms stay. Shopee's launcher solves a volume problem — 99+ chats a day — that a wedding supplier doesn't have, and it would have added a tap to every job to free two slots.
  • Answers moved into Customers rather than becoming a tab, with the count surfaced on Today.
  • "Grow" is gone. Positioning belongs where a shop sets its price — on the card, and in Performance.
  • On the Day is now the Event Hub, and works before the day as the prep list.
  • Kept from Shopee: the reach meter, the hidden-shop message, and opening on what's unanswered. Three controls, no architecture.
