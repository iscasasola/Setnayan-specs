About this prototype

Three builds, one journey: the sign-in popup, creating an account and your profile, and opening a shop. Approved on 2026-09-22 ("approved. account inside step 3, consent per event, small card for signup"). This file adds the states the first drawing skipped: every refusal, the check that is still running, the email that already has an account, and the submit that half-succeeds.

1 · What changes, one sentence per screen

  • Sign in — unchanged in words (44 words · 6 buttons as shipped); it is the card everything else now copies.
  • Create account — the /signup page becomes this same small card (39 words here; shipped 136 for a couple, 107 for a vendor). The brand panel, the four bullets, the first/last name boxes and the Stories consent leave this screen.
  • You — one new card right after the account exists: photo (optional), display name, @account name with a live availability check, full name folded away, phone. Done or Later; all of it also lives under Profile & settings.
  • Create event — the Stories consent is asked HERE, once per event, in words that name the event type (17 types; the old sentence said "my wedding").
  • Open a shop, step 3 — a password under the company email creates the account with the shop in one press; Google / Apple; "Have an account? Sign in" opens the popup in place and keeps what was typed. Reverses the 2026-07-10 "login first" bounce.
  • Open a shop, last step — the Terms box (unticked) above the one button that creates anything, when an account is being created.

1b · The controls contract — every live control, and where it is now

Measured on setnayan.com on 2026-09-22 before drawing. Nothing is removed to remove its text; each one is kept, moved, or named as removed with what replaces it.

  • Sign-in popup (6 buttons · 3 inputs): ✕ · Continue with Google · Continue with Apple · Forgot password? · Continue · Create one, free · Email · Password · Stay signed in — all kept, unchanged.
  • /signup (7 controls · 6 inputs): SETNAYAN wordmark → kept as the ✕ / page behind · Continue with Google · Continue with Apple · Create account · free · Terms · Privacy · Sign in — all kept · First name + Last name (optional) → moved to the You card · Email · Password · Stay signed in — kept · Include my wedding in Stories (couples only) → moved to Create event, per event, event-neutral words · the Terms box from PR #5867 — kept, unticked. Removed: the v2.1 brand panel, "Start free · 90 seconds", "Set your day in motion", the four benefit bullets, "One account, every celebration", "We never sell your data — RA 10173 compliant" → replaced by the same small card as sign-in; the RA 10173 line lives on /privacy.
  • Profile tab (Profile & settings): photo · Display name · Account name (@slug) · Prefix · First · Middle · Last · Suffix · Phone · Birthday · Save — all kept there; the You card is a first-run subset (photo, display name, @account name, full name, phone). Birthday stays on the profile page only.
  • /open-shop (4 steps): Shop logo · Shop name · web address · Primary service (drill) · Events you serve (17) · Your name · Your position · Contact number · Company email · address · map · Use my location · Yes, that's right · Continue · Back · Open my shop — free · the fee line — all kept. Added: Password (new account only) · Continue with Google / Apple (step 3) · Have an account? Sign in · the Terms box (last step, new account only).
  • Doors kept: "Create your shop" in the account menu · "Open your shop" on the front door and /vendors · /login?next=/open-shop&as=vendor survives as the no-JavaScript fallback of the step-3 Sign in link.

2 · What is deliberately NOT changing

  • The four shop steps, one question each, the map pin you must confirm, the permanent web address, the logo optional until verification (owner 2026-08-10 / 2026-07-21).
  • The consent field's name, value and the 8 RA 10173 guardrails (2026-05-19) — only WHEN it is asked and its wording move.
  • The Terms gate itself (CTRL-B3, PR #5867) — one implementation, imported, not re-written.
  • Google and Apple as the only sign-in providers; email-only inside the phone shell; the Turnstile bot check.
  • Signed-in vendors: name read from the account, no password, no Terms box on this path.

3 · What this cannot prove

  • The Google / Apple leg is simulated. Only a real tap through Google's consent page proves the typed steps come back (a one-hour, same-tab draft, never the password). A scripted walk cannot log into Google, so this is the one check that needs a person.
  • Email confirmation is the shipped V1 work-around (admin auto-confirm, no link waited for) on both doors; PR #5872's isEmailVerificationRequired gate is not on main yet, and this prototype does not draw the "check your email" branch that gate will re-introduce.
  • The "@name checking…" delay is faked at 700 ms; the real check is a network call to the same availability helper the shop address uses.
  • "Account made, shop failed" is simulated with a switch. In production the two writes are one server action; what is proven is only what the screen says afterwards, not how often it happens.
  • The bot check (Turnstile) does not render here. Measured earlier: it cannot render inside the browser pane at all, so a real phone is the only test.
  • A preview of the built code needs R2_PUBLIC_URL and ENCRYPTION_KEY in Vercel's Preview scope (measured 2026-09-22: Production-only today) — until then a preview can 500 in ways that look like this design's fault.
  • Nothing here touched production data. Real numbers used: 17 event types, the service tree's Photo & video and Attire branches, the live fee schedule (5% of the first ₱100,000, then 1%, minimum ₱50, first 5 free).

4 · Open questions

  1. Where does per-event consent live? Today users.public_summary_consent_at is one timestamp per ACCOUNT, written at sign-up, and eight surfaces read it (front door, Real Stories admin, the event's Story page and privacy page, the wedding onboarding). Per event needs a column on events and those readers repointed. Recommendation: events.public_summary_consent_at plus keep the account column as the legacy fallback for events created before the change. Cost if wrong: a couple's Debut is shown in Stories because their Wedding once said yes, or the reverse — a consent that legally attaches to the wrong thing.
  2. What does "Later" on the You card leave behind? A display name is required to finish; Later keeps the email's local part as the name and the @tag. Recommendation: allow Later; the profile page already exists. Cost if wrong: guest lists show "maria" instead of a name until they visit Profile.
  3. Build order. Build 1 (this card) edits apps/web/app/signup/actions.ts, which PR #5867 and PR #5872 both edit and conflict over. Recommendation: build 3 first (done, committed, waiting), then 2, then 1 once both PRs are on main. Cost if wrong: a third writer on a file already in a two-way conflict, rebased twice.
