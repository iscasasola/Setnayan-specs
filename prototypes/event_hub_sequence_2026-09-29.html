Why this order
  • Each step fills the next — names, date, theme and colours build the logo; the logo builds the hero; parents and RSVP write "Kindly reply".
  • Nothing is asked before it's needed — the wedding march comes with the invitation it appears on; seating waits for replies.
  • Every round ends with something to send — Save the Date, then invitations, then the day.
  • Skip is always there — skipped steps stay in "What's left".
