Ala ala · why we exist

A Filipino celebration was never one family’s. It belongs to the whole samahan — the ninong and ninang, the titos and titas, the barkada, everyone who showed up. So the memory shouldn’t belong to one camera either. Every one of them is holding a piece of your day. Setnayan is where those pieces come together, and everyone goes home with their own. Plan it, run it, remember it — and keep it, for life.
