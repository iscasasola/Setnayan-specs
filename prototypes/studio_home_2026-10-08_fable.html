Studio home — eleven cards: a mark, a topic, one plain line, and a small ⓘ beside it

Each card is the mark (a distinct icon, never two pages sharing one), the topic (the page's name) and one plain sentence saying what the couple does there. The ⓘ is its own 44-px target beside the card, never inside it (a button inside a button is invalid and the tap would open the page); it opens the house popover (INTERACTION_RULES § 1: "Details behind ⓘ"; § 8: one open at a time) with what the page controls, where a guest sees it, and what to do first. One column at 375 — two columns cannot hold a sentence and a 44-px ⓘ — two at 900. Done / unread is one small dot on the card; "Full screen" is a quiet tag. The home keeps its "Studio" title and the ready count.
