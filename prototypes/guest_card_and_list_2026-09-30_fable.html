Notes for the owner

  • Top: name, status pills, and the account line (Linked to … · Unlink, or Not linked).
  • Ticket first: the real Digital ticket thumbnail, then Invite (phone share sheet: message + link + QR) · Copy message · Copy QR.
  • Download ticket / Download QR / NFC / QR look move into one More ▾ — no duplicates.
  • Removed: "Email a sign-in link" (no email to guests); the second Download QR; the second "Customize guest QRs".

  • RSVP and Extra seats are dropdowns (None · +1 · +2 · +3 · +4). "Invited to" stays as on/off chips (several can be on).
  • Their guests: each named +1 with its own Invite.
  • Bride/groom rows: RSVP shows "Attending · always"; no Invite (they are the hosts).
