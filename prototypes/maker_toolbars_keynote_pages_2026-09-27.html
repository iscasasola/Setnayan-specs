The Maker’s toolbars, arranged like Keynote and Pages

Same controls we ship today, put where Apple puts them: the Maker’s own actions on the left, one place picker, a few big labelled buttons, Restore · Undo · Apply on the right, and three inspector switches — Format · Animate · Arrange. The look stays ours. Nothing here is built yet; it is for your yes or no.

Questions for youSeven yes-or-no calls, each with my recommendation

  1. Dawn. Keep it off the per-scene list? Recommend: yes. Diagonal and Glow cover it; Dawn stays for the whole Event Hub only.
  2. The Joiner part. Build it? Recommend: yes, small — and · & · + · your own word, styled like any part. It answers “why can’t I edit and”.
  3. The size number. Show the size as you see it on this view (48 on desktop, 32 on the phone, scaled together), or a scale like 100%? Recommend: the number you see, with the phone number printed beside it. Rails stay on either way.
  4. Where the Transition tab goes. Fold “Into the next scene” into Animate (Keynote does this) or keep a fourth tab? Recommend: fold it. Three switches in the toolbar, three tabs.
  5. Opacity on Opaque. Opaque means solid — should it have a slider at all, or only Frosted? Recommend: both, 20–100%. Then “Opaque” is only a name for “no blur”.
  6. Where “every scene” stops. This stage only (the 8 scenes of Invitation), or all four stages? Recommend: this stage. Save the Date is one screen and Post Event is a story; a background chosen for the Invitation rarely suits them, and a wrong guess there is four times the undo.
  7. The Colour panel on desktop. A popover from the well (drawn here) or a section that pushes the rows down like the phone? Recommend: popover on desktop, section on the phone — each is what the platform does.
