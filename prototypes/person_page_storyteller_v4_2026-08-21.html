Version 4 — answering the research. Two judges scored v3 6.5/10. The finding that changed it: cream paper + thin rule + serif name + gold + italic line is, in the Philippines, what a memorial page looks like — and v3's own bride said “she got married; nothing on that page is happy.”

So: warmth and clay instead of funeral cream · her people on the page, which no competitor does · condensed caps on display and a serif for reading (the pairing GRID Magazine and Exposure both use, and the letterform of Filipino signage) · the big slot built for two sentences and four hundred photos, not an essay · no drop cap, no “colophon”.

Kept from v3: scale carries meaning — which Zola (1,618 designs, two layouts) and Appy Couple (six identical polaroids) do not do — and a page that has a life after the day.
