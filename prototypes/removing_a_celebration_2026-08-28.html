The one thing that is yours, not mine

You asked whether the panel should say refund is not available. That sentence is a promise about money on a screen, next to services carrying an official receipt, so it is your call and not an engineering one. Three readings, and they are genuinely different products:

A.No refund, and they can press on anyway. Fastest for the couple. It also means somebody can destroy ₱2,899 of paid service at 1 a.m. with nobody in the loop, and we have to honour the sentence when they write in the next morning.
B.A person answers every one. They tell us why and what they want; we void it, move it, or refund it. Recommended — one order has ever been placed, so the queue is empty, and it keeps the money decision with a human until there is enough of it to write a rule from.
C.Both. A request by default, with “remove it anyway and lose what I paid for” underneath. More words on a small panel, and the second door makes the first one look optional.
