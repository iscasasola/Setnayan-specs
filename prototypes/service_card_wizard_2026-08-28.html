Why this shape — the rationale, kept current
01 · THE APPROACH LANDED ON

Two answers, because the shop already gave the rest

Three shapes were argued against each other. The winner: ask only what the shop's own record cannot answer. The kind of service comes from what the shop already covers — for a one-service shop it is never asked, just shown with a "change" link. A name is auto-written from the kind. So the required path is: one photo · one sentence (the Setnayan Exclusive). That is the whole gate, honestly — nothing was hidden to get there.

02 · WHAT WAS REJECTED, AND WHY

Two honest losers

The 4-question pass (rev 1 of this file): it asked the kind — a thing the shop's record already knows — and made the price feel required when it is not; two of its four questions were unearned. The one-screen tap-in-order card: it keeps every region on screen at once, which is exactly the bombardment the owner named; with no current question there is nothing telling a first-timer where to start.

03 · WHERE THE PRICE WENT

Invited, not demanded

A price is what couples look for first — but the save has never required one, and "price on request" is a real answer. So the finished card shows a tappable "Add a price — couples look for it first" line: one tap opens the three shapes (per event · per head · per hour), one number, done. Demanding it up front would have made the easiest path longer for a rule that does not exist.

04 · THE CATEGORY CORRECTION — MEASURED

Coverage first; the full list one tap away; nothing silently removed

Today's chooser offers ~34 kinds in 6 groups to every shop. Measured on the owner's own shop: it covers two things, its plan holds one family — so only about 6 of the ~34 pills could actually publish, and the caps are checked after the card is built: photo uploaded, Publish pressed, then bounced with the work gone. The fix drawn here: what the shop covers leads (or is pre-filled), "Something else I do" opens the full grouped list, and what the plan cannot hold is greyed with its reason — a shop grows, and a kind that quietly vanishes reads as "Setnayan does not do that".

05 · DELIBERATELY LEFT OUT

What this drawing does not do

No half-way saving — one save at the end, as today. No new required fields, and none removed. No score or percentage during the questions — the card filling in is the progress. The quality meter and every optional depth (gallery, clip, inclusions, choices, discounts, crew, lead time, bundles) live as quiet rows under the finished card, unchanged.

06 · OPEN — THE OWNER DECIDES

Two questions only he can answer

(a) Should a half-finished card survive as a draft? Two answers make abandonment rare, but a phone call still interrupts people. (b) When the plan blocks a kind, should the greyed pill lead to the upgrade page directly, or to a "tell us what you do" note first? Drawn here: the upgrade sentence names the plan and the price page.
