Checkpoint — what this shows

  • Pick a stage, pick a part, edit it here. Each part's panel shows its real fields from today's code: Hero (uploadHeroPhoto, monogram_*, display_name, event_date, invite_theme) · Your words (love_story) · Details & schedule (venue_*, public event_schedule_blocks) · Dress code (dress_code_config) · Reply form (rsvp widget, rsvp_backdrop) · Camera cues (photo_moments_config) · Special message · What to bring · Show, hide & reorder (invitation_widgets) · The film (std_reveal_template, mood, std_invitation_launch_date, song, stamp) · Chapters, Cover, Publish (event_editorial) · Website settings.
  • Shared parts say where they appear (Hero: Save the Date · Invitation · On the Day · Poster). “Try” makes a draft edit; every thumbnail and the preview move; the lit block is the part being edited.
  • Free vs Pro on every field, one quiet mark. Scroll · Scrub · Auto-scroll on each section (Pro). The draft bar appears after an edit: N changes · Restore · Apply — or “Apply needs Event Hub Pro · [PRICE] · one-time · all four stages” when the draft holds a Pro change.
  • The navigator (Keynote-style, owner 2026-09-24): a numbered column of live scene thumbnails down the left of the chosen stage, the selected one ringed, the transition (Scroll · Scrub · Auto + speed) as a marker between two thumbnails, “shared” on scenes that appear on other stages, ⋮⋮ to drag with ▲▼ as the fallback, “+ Add a scene” at the end. Choosing a thumbnail lights that scene in the middle preview and opens its editor on the right. Phone / tablet portrait: the navigator becomes a horizontal thumbnail strip above the preview; the editor rises as a sheet.
  • Marked “next” (not yet measured): the invite theme names, typeface and Magic Move option lists, the other opening names, which RSVP fields are optional, the camera-cue fields, the seat-plan fields shown on the day, the album count.
