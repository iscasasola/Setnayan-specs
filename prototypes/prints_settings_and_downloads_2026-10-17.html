    Decided, not asked

    • The "Sample (PDF)" button goes: the Event pass's own Download is one real guest's pass (the guest shown), which is what a sample was for.
    • The count line keeps the shipped rule: the number IS the file — "109 passes · 6 coming, 103 not replied yet"; place cards count seated guests only.
    • Everything ◆ is one PDF of every piece above in the theme being edited — the invitation set, every guest's pass and place card, the day's sheets — and replaces "Download the set"'s two buttons; the piece bar's ⬇ offers "This piece" and "Everything ◆".
    • A free couple's ◆ opens the centred Pro note (the shipped StudioProDoor), never a page jump; owned, the same button saves the file.
