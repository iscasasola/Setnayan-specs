Open questions for the owner (≤5)

Q1 · Where the menu lives on a desktop.Recommended: top bar One slim top bar (× · names · the five tabs with their words · account) from 1024 up — the approved 2026-09-29 desktop frames and the Maker's top bar already speak this language, and a top bar fits at 1024 where the shipped left rail only fits from 1280. Alternative: keep the shipped left rail at 1280 and add nothing at 1024.
Q2 · How the Reply opens.Recommended: side panel The same sheet slides in from the right (440 px) so the invitation stays in view while the guest answers — the phone's sheet, turned on its side. Alternative: a centred dialog over a dimmed page.
Q3 · The Camera tab on a laptop.Recommended: hand it to the phone The desktop Camera page shows the guest's QR — "Scan to open the Camera on your phone" — and their shots below; no webcam capture (a laptop webcam at a table makes poor Papic shots, and 99 % are on phones anyway). Alternative: allow the webcam with the same capture flow.
Q4 · The bar while reading.Recommended: stays After the cover opens, the top bar stays pinned at the top of every tab's page (a desktop has the room; a guest always sees the exit and the tabs). Alternative: hide on scroll down, return on scroll up.
Q5 · Which scenes may go two-column.Recommended: the seven drawn Countdown + calendar · Program | Venues · The entourage (its March and Roll call styles) · Photos (Glimpse) · Live | run of show · Seat map · Gallery. Everything else stays the centred reading column. Alternative: fewer — only Photos, Seat map and Gallery use the width; every text scene stays one column.
