All ten are now referenced by nothing at all — including the one at the top of this drawing, which is a guest holding up a phone to photograph the couple. C · Every change, and how far it reaches

Five changes. Three touch only this page.

01

The page finally shows the productchanges the shared template

A photograph at the top, and a picture inside two of the three steps. Two of those three already exist in our files. This is the one change that touches the template all eight product pages share — Live Studio, Patiktok, 3D Plan and the rest are equally picture-less today, so it is worth deciding once.

02

The price list drops from sixteen lines to threethis page only

Free first, then three amounts that show the shape of the ladder, then the honest sentence that thirteen more exist, then the link to the full list that is already there. No price is changed, hidden or invented — the numbers still come from the price table an admin owns, so a reprice still moves this page by itself.

03

The biggest block on the page gets a titlethis page only

Today it has none — a fifth of the page begins with no heading, which is why it reads as a wall rather than a section. It is called What it costs.

04

“Two ways to run it” moves above the pricethis page only

You are told what you would be buying before you are told what it costs. Today the price arrives first.

05

The six answers close until they are askedchanges the shared template

Six answers open at once is the second-tallest thing on the page. Closed, it is about a third of the height, and every word stays on the page — so search engines and answer engines read exactly what they read today.

Together these take the page from about seven phone-screens to about five and a half, while adding three pictures. Nothing was deleted that was the only statement of anything, and no sentence was rewritten.

D · Deliberately not changed

No new sentence under the headline. A short explaining line there is the exact thing the owner removed from every page on 2026-08-19 — “it just eats up space and we want it to be simpler to understand on each page without too much side comments.” The drawing respects that and reaches the free-price fact by making the page shorter instead.

No new promise. The page still never says photos are matched to faces automatically. That claim was removed on purpose in July, because the matching model is not running.

No price moved. Every figure still comes from the live price table.

The headline, the comparison rows, the answers and the closing invitation are untouched, word for word.

E · The one thing that is yours

Pictures on one page, or on all eight?

Papic, Live Studio, Patiktok, 3D Plan, Pakanta, Logo Maker, Event Hub and Setnayan AI all run on one shared page-template, and none of the eight shows a single picture today. Putting a photograph at the top of Papic means either giving the template the ability to hold one — which then invites the other seven to use it — or building Papic its own top, which is how eight pages drift apart again.

  • Give the template a picture slot and use it on Papic now. The other seven look exactly as they do today until each one is given a photograph. This is the recommendation.
  • Papic only, its own top. Faster to draw, and the eight pages stop matching.

Everything else in this drawing needs no decision and can be built as soon as you have looked at it. And the one photograph marked needed — several guests at a table with their phones up — is the only thing here we do not already own.
