The control templates — round 2

Twelve more kinds, numbered on from the twenty-two you approved on 2026-10-08. Same colours, same press, same 0.7 s. Each one is drawn in Light and Dark, for a phone and for a computer, with its pressed, loading, empty and failure states. Tap everything.

23 · Chat newChatThread

One chat page for every conversation: a supplier about an event, a person, a Samahan group, and Setnayan's own notices. Yours on the right in our colour, theirs on the left in grey; in a group each message names its writer. Not for a comment under a post or a form reply — those are rows.

What moves: a new bubble rises in with the same small spring as every press. Send is grey until there is something to send, then terracotta. A sent message says Sending… then Sent, then Seen; one that did not go turns red and says Not sent · tap to try again — never silent. The message box grows to four lines, then scrolls inside itself. On a phone, Enter is a new line and the round button sends; on a computer, Enter sends and Shift + Enter is a new line. The box stays at the bottom while the thread scrolls under it, and sits in the thumb zone above the keyboard. The Setnayan thread has no message box: it is notices you read, with one button on each.

24 · QR card newQrCard

A code people show, scan, share or download: your page's link, a shortlist code, a booked one-use code, a pay-to code. Not for the guest's pass — that keeps its approved round code (2026-09-29). The code is always dark on white, in Dark mode too, because a scanner needs it.

What moves: the card presses like a button and opens big on tap (so a cashier can scan from a phone held up). Share opens the phone's own share sheet; Download saves a PNG with the name under it. While the code is being made, the square shimmers — nothing fake to scan. A used or void code is greyed with the word on it, so nobody tries it twice. A problem says so with Try again, never an empty square. The small SETNAYAN mark in the middle is our only brand on it.

25 · Map pin field newMapPinField

An address and its pin: a venue, where a supplier is based, how far they travel. The words are a Form row; the pin is on a small map under it. Not for directions or a live map — that is the phone's own maps app, opened from a row.

Address

What moves: typing an address finds it and the pin drops in with a small bounce; dragging the pin lifts it (it rises under your finger) and sets it back down where you let go, and the address words update to match. "Use my location" spins while the phone finds you, then drops the pin. For a supplier's reach, a terracotta ring grows and shrinks with the slider (kind 17). An address that cannot be found says so under the map and asks you to drop the pin yourself; an offline map goes grey with the words "Map is offline — your address still saves" — the field never blocks the form. The pin can also be moved with the arrow keys.

26 · Price summary newPriceSummary

What a quote comes to: its lines, what comes off, the total, then how it is paid. Read-only rows with hairlines, no box. Not for editing the lines — that is the quote's own Form rows; never a switch that buys. Buying is always a confirm + pay step (owner, 2026-10-12).

What moves: a changed total counts up or down to its new figure instead of jumping. A line that comes off is green with a minus. The payment plan is one row per instalment with its date and a badge: Paid · Due Oct 3 · Not yet. The main button names the amount it will take; pressing it opens the confirm box (kind 12), and only Pay there pays. A supplier's copy carries one extra small line, the Setnayan booking fee, since the fee is between Setnayan and the supplier (owner, 2026-10-12). Loading shimmers the rows; a problem says "Couldn't load this quote · Try again" and the button is dimmed.

27 · Notification row newNotificationRow

One notice in the in-app list: who or what, one line, when. A dot means unread. Tapping opens the thing it is about and marks it read. Not for a result you just caused — that is a toast (kind 12).

The bell3

What moves: tap a row and its dot fades and its title goes light as it opens; the bell's number counts down. Rows are grouped Today · Earlier with a caption between, hairlines, no boxes, a terracotta arrow at the end. A ⋯ on the page holds "Mark all as read". Empty says "You're all caught up" with nothing to press; loading shimmers rows; a problem says so with Try again. The list is the same list the envelope and the bell open; the Setnayan thread (kind 23) carries the same notices in chat form.

28 · Photo grid newPhotoGrid

A set of pictures to look at, add to and arrange: a shop's portfolio, an album, a mood board. Pictures sit edge to edge with no frame; only Add is framed, because it is pressed. Not for choosing one look (that is the Style card) or a single cover (that is Upload, kind 21).

18 of 30 · the first one is your cover

What moves: tap a picture and it opens big, dark around it, swipe or arrow for the next; Remove lives there and asks once. Add opens the phone's picker; each new picture arrives as a tile with its own 0–100 pie, then the photo. Hold a picture to lift it — the same feel as moving a scene in the Maker: about a third of a second, a small buzz, it grows a little with a terracotta edge — then drag; the others slide aside and the first slot is always the cover. A picture that did not upload is a red tile that says so, with Try again, and never a blank. "Arrange" shows every ✕ for anyone who cannot hold.

29 · Star rating display onlyStarRating

What a shop's reviews come to: one gold star, the number, the count. Read, not pressed. Not for giving a rating (that stays StarRatingInput) and never "☆ 0.0 · 0": a shop with no reviews reads New (owner, 2026-09-11).

30 · Search box newSearchBox

Find something in the place you are in: this event's guests, your customers, a supplier's services. Its own bar, not a Form row; on a phone it lives in the thumb row and takes at least 60 % of it, with one Add beside it. Not for filtering by a value — that is a Dropdown.

What moves: the box lights terracotta when you tap in and the keyboard comes up. Results change as you type — the matching letters are terracotta in each row — under one small line that says how many. The ✕ appears as soon as there is a word and clears it with the same press as every button; tapping outside only puts the keyboard away, the word stays. No one found says so and offers Add; a problem says so with Try again. It searches the place you are in (owner's rule § 4): what is searched is said in the placeholder.

31 · Calendar days with several states extends kind 8CalendarGrid

The approved calendar, with meaning under the days: a supplier's dates (booked · holding · blocked · free), a couple's plan. The picked day stays the one terracotta circle; a dot says what is on the day; a blocked day is crossed out. Not for picking one date in a form — that is kind 8 as approved.

BookedHoldingBlockedYour event

What moves: the circle lands on the day you tap with the same press as everything else, and the day's rows appear under the month: one row per booking or hold with its badge. Two things on a day are two dots; a full day (its limit reached) is bold. A blocked day cannot be picked by a couple and shows why to the supplier. The colour is never the only sign — every dot has its word in the legend and in the day's list. Changing the month slides, as approved.

32 · Charts newBars · MixBar · Fill

Three shapes for Insights and Money: bars over time, a mix bar (what makes up a whole), a meter (how far along). Read-only, no frame, each ends in a divider. One grey for the series; terracotta only for "now" or "you". Never a second axis, a pie, or red/green for up/down.

What moves: bars grow up from the baseline when they come into view (once). Tap a bar and its number pops above it; on a computer, hover does the same. The current bar is terracotta and the days ahead are hollow. The mix bar's parts have a hairline gap between them and are named under it with their numbers — the colour alone never carries the name. A meter fills to its figure with the number beside it. Empty says what to do ("Your page went live Tuesday — views show from tomorrow"), loading shimmers the shape, a problem says so with Try again. The half-ring gauge on the look board is not drawn here — it is still awaiting your look there.

34 · Reorder — hold to lift extends kind 18Reorder · useHoldToLift

Put things in your own order: service cards, photos, the pages of a Hub. Hold a row to lift it (the Maker's feel: about a third of a second, a small buzz, it grows a little with a terracotta edge), drag, let go. Arrows and the keyboard do the same for anyone who cannot drag. Not for moving one thing between lists.

Hold a row to lift it, or use the arrows. With a keyboard: Space picks up, ↑ ↓ move, Space drops, Esc puts it back.

What moves: while you hold, the row tints to show the hold is counting; at the lift it rises with a shadow and a terracotta edge, and the rows it passes slide out of its way. On a computer the mouse lifts it at once. Let go and it settles with the family's small bounce; "Order saved" peeks from the top. A row that cannot move (the Reveal, the cover) does not lift and says why under the list. If the save fails the row slides back where it was, and a red line says "That did not save. Nothing changed — please try again." In the Maker only, the lower third slides away while a scene is held and slides back on release (owner, 2026-10-11).
