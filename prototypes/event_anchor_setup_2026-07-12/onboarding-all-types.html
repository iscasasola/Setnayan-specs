Each type replaces the wedding's couple + faith anchor with its own master signal, then asks only its own deltas.

Religion, civil status, age & gender already live on the profile. Onboarding reads them and never asks twice — it only asks when a field is still empty, then writes it back. Flip the switch to see both states.
