What runs by itself

Store an anchor once and the engine generates the future — every year, forever, with nothing typed again. Three things are automatic: the moment appears, the reminder fires at the right lead time, the draft is pre-filled. One thing never is: creating the event — that's always your tap.

Viewing from Jul 2026 — press fast-forward and watch moments keep arriving with zero effort.

The boundary: the engine proposes, you dispose. Reminders and drafts cost nothing and expire quietly if ignored — no event rows, no picker clutter, no charges. The go-signal is the only door from “tracked” to “planned.”
