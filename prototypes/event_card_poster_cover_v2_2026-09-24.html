What changed from the first draft

  • The cover is the hub’s own first screen, filling the whole shape. No paper-inside-a-frame-inside-a-card. The paper’s hairline runs to 5.5% from the edge, the way the hub draws it (border-gild/45), and the strip sits on top of its lower margin.
  • Nothing under the hero repeats the hero. Names and date are printed once, by the paper. The strip carries only what the paper cannot say: what needs you (number first) and how far · how long.
  • It adapts to whichever hero the couple built. Three shapes exist in the hub today and all three are here: the invitation card in their theme (House · Capiz · Velvet · Galeriya), their own hero photo with the masthead type over it, and the solemn masthead with no card at all. One host reads “You are invited / invites you to celebrate”; no date prints no date line.
  • Small covers crop, they don’t shrink. Under 200px the paper keeps its three load-bearing parts — mark · names · date — and drops the eyebrow, the invitation line and the time; the strip keeps only the most urgent item and stacks it above the progress so no word is ever cut; the kicker keeps the type chip and drops the stance. Everything is sized from the cover’s own width, so the phone two-up and the desktop four-up are the same design at two crops.
  • Unknown is not zero. A failed read prints “Couldn’t load what needs you” and a blank ring — never a confident 0.
  • Sorted soonest first, undated at the tail. The dashed + New tile is part of the grid. The whole card is one link; only ⋮ is nested.

Two decisions for the owner

  • Strip over the hero (glass) or below it (paper)? Compare with the bar. Recommendation: over — the card stays one object and the paper shows through; “below” re-introduces a second surface the design brief bans.
  • Phone: two-up or one-up? Recommendation: two-up — the mark, names and date still read at 163px, and a phone sees three events above the fold instead of one. One-up is a poster feed; it looks lovely and hides the other events.

Not verified from a screenshot

  • The Velvet and Galeriya covers are drawn from the theme tokens ([data-hub-theme='velvet'] · [data-hub-theme='galeriya'] in globals.css) and the door skins, not from a rendered hub — Velvet’s card on the hub reads as velvet-dark paper with cream Bodoni because the theme moves --color-cream and --color-ink together.
  • A wake’s quiet masthead defaults its eyebrow to “You are invited” (PahinaMasthead, eyebrow = 'You are invited') and site-body.tsx passes none; the card above omits it. Worth an owner look on the hub itself.
