BInvite, on the phone

  • Tapping Invite opens the phone's own share sheet. It sends the message, their link and their Digital ticket together, in one go.
  • Pick the app you already use with that guest — Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp, Messages, or More.
  • Nothing to copy. When the app confirms it went out, the status line turns ✓ Sent Sep 30.
  • What's attached is the whole ticket as one image — their QR is on it, so it scans on the day.

CInvite, on a computer

  • A computer has no share sheet, so Invite opens a small box with three steps in order: 1 Copy message · 2 Copy ticket · 3 Mark as sent. Hint under them: "Paste the message, then paste the ticket in the chat." Copy ticket copies the Digital ticket as one image — never a bare QR.
  • Each step ticks green as you do it; step 3 sets the status to ✓ Sent Sep 30 by hand, since the computer can't know the chat went out.
  • The table's Invite column is the same pair — Invite · ⋯ — with the status under it. The Account column stays (Linked / Not linked).
  • Clicking a row opens the same card on the right, no page change; below the ticket it is unchanged.
