The control templates

Twenty-eight kinds so far. Every screen uses these and nothing else. Tap each one to see how it moves. Everything you can tap answers the same way: a quick dip, a spring back and one soft ring.

1 · Pill selector

Use it to switch between two or three sections of a screen, or between two named choices. Not for picking one of three or more values.

Sections
Mode
Tools (icons)

What moves: one terracotta highlight slides to the choice you tap, lands with a small bounce, and pulses once to show the press. Choices that are off stay grey. Every pill selector uses this one colour.

2 · Dropdown

Use it to pick one of three or more values. It always opens its choices; it never jumps to the next value by itself.

Blur
How much

What moves: on a phone the choices rise from the bottom of the screen as a sheet; on a computer they open as a list under the button. The terracotta arrow turns over. The current choice is ticked. Dropdowns in the same list share one width, so their edges and arrows line up and the button does not change size when the answer changes. Tapping a choice applies it at once and the sheet goes back down.

3 · Switch

Use it for on or off. Not for a choice between two named things.

Show this page to guests
Music starts on the first tap

What moves: the knob travels across, stretches a little under your finger and lands with a small bounce. Terracotta when on, grey when off — the same two colours as every other control.

4 · Page card new

Use it as the door to a page. It looks and presses like an app button: logo, topic, one plain line, and a small ⓘ for the longer explanation.

What moves: the whole button presses in the moment you touch it and springs back. The ⓘ is its own button: it opens the explanation and does not open the page.

5 · Style card

Use it to choose a look by its picture: a background, a scene, an effect. Always this one size, phone-shaped. Every picture is fitted to it, with no frame.

Applying to your Hub…

What moves: the terracotta highlight lands on the card the moment you tap. A colour applies at once. A picture or film shows a pie filling from 0 to 100 on the card while its file loads, then a tick. The card you pick slides to the middle of the row, as far as the row allows. While it loads, the other cards wait and cannot be pressed. Tapping the loading card cancels it and nothing changes. One line underneath says the step in words.

6 · Form row new

Use it for one fact to type or pick. The name on the left, the answer on the right, one thin line between rows.

Event nameSaved
Opening line
Reply by
Show the date

What moves: every answer is the same white pill, and the small terracotta mark on its right says what a tap does: a pencil to type, an arrow to choose, a calendar to pick a date. Tapping a typed answer opens the field across the whole row. The row's name steps aside so the row stays the same height; if you clear the field, the name shows inside it. Tapping anywhere else, or pressing Enter, keeps the change; the one ✕ leaves it as it was. Only one row is open at a time: opening another keeps the first. Closing is animated too: the field folds back to the right, the row's name slides back in from the left, and the pencil becomes a tick for a moment to say it was saved. An answer too long for its pill scrolls slowly to its end so the whole of it can be read, rests one second, then starts from the beginning again. It stops while you are editing that row.

7 · ⓘ explanation

Use it for the longer "what is this". Never for something the couple must read to continue.

Blur None
Focus Centre

What moves: on a phone the explanation pops up in the centre of the screen with a “Got it” button; on a computer it appears as a small note beside the ⓘ. Only one is open at a time. Tap outside to close it.

8 · Calendar new

Three uses, one look. Pick one day. Pick a range of days. See what is on each day. The picked day is terracotta; a dot means something is on that day.

What moves: the terracotta circle lands on the day you tap with the same press as everything else. In a range, the days between fill softly. Changing the month slides to the next one. On a phone this calendar rises from the bottom when it belongs to a form row.

9 · Action button new

Use it to do something: save, send, add, delete. One main button per screen; the rest are quieter.

The five

What moves: the same press as everything else. Tap Save: it shows it is working, then says Saved. Tap Delete: it asks once, because that cannot be undone. A button that cannot be used yet is grey. An icon with no word is only for marks everyone knows (⋯ for more, ✕ for close); anything else carries its word, like Share.

What moves: the same as the Form row. Tap a pill and its field opens across the row with one ✕; tapping anywhere else or pressing Enter keeps the change; an amount keeps its ₱; a problem is said in plain words under the row and the pill turns red until it is fixed. A long message opens a taller box under its name. When someone is filling in something new, the fields are already open so they can type straight down the list. 11 · Ticks, chips and badges new

Tick box for yes on one line. Chips to choose several from a few. A badge only shows a state; it is not pressed.

Chips (choose any)
Badges (states)
ComingNo reply yetCan't comeDraft

What moves: the tick draws itself in. A chip fills terracotta when chosen and goes grey when not, the same two colours as the pill selector and the switch. Chips are all the same height and never narrower than a set width; a longer word makes its chip wider. A chip does not change size when you choose it.

12 · Messages new

Toast for a quick result, like a notification from the top. Status line when the result must stay on the page. Confirm box only for what cannot be undone.

Your changes are on your page.
Couldn't save. You may be offline.

What moves: whenever something pops up over the page (a sheet, an explanation, a confirm box), the rest of the screen goes dark and blurred, nothing behind it can be pressed or scrolled, and tapping the dark part closes it. A toast is a notification: it peeks down from the top of the screen, stays a moment and leaves by itself; it does not darken the page because you can keep working. A failure never looks like success: a good result is terracotta with a tick; a failure is dark red with a warning mark, says why in plain words, and offers Try again. The confirm box pops up in the centre.

13 · Timeline row your idea

One row per moment: when, and its name. The same row makes the Schedule (start, end, name) and the Love Story (month and year, chapter, photo).

What moves: tap a time and a small ticker opens: hour, minute and AM/PM, with the chosen time in the centre band. Roll each one with your finger; the row behind changes as you roll. Moving the start moves the end with it, so the length stays. The line above the ticker says the start, the end and how long that makes it. Rows put themselves in time order. "Add a moment" starts where the last one ended, one hour long, with the name ready to type. The Love Story uses the same row: its "when" can be just a year, a month and year, or a full date (you choose how exact at the top of the ticker), then the chapter's name and one small picture square. A chapter holds up to 3 photos or videos: the square shows how many, and tapping it opens the three slots, each with its own upload pie.

14 · Top bar and ⋯ menu new

Top bar: the strip at the top of a page once you have gone deeper than the bottom bar. Back on the left, the page's name in the middle, more on the right. ⋯ holds the things done rarely, so they do not crowd the page.

Guest list

What moves: ⋯ opens the same way as a dropdown: a sheet from the bottom on a phone, a list under the button on a computer. Each line has its mark and its word. Anything that cannot be undone is red and still asks once.

15 · List row and card new

List row for a person or a thing in a list. Card when there is a picture to choose by.

The Garden at TagaytayVenue · Tagaytay · up to 200 guestsFrom ₱185,000

What moves: the whole row or card presses like a button. The heart fills terracotta with the same press when you save. A state is a badge, never the only colour clue: it always has its word.

16 · Empty, loading, problem new

Three states that must never look alike. Nothing here yet · still loading · something went wrong.

What moves: loading shows soft grey shapes of what is coming, shimmering, so the page does not jump when it arrives. Empty says what to do first and gives the button to do it. A problem says so in plain words with Try again, and never pretends the list is empty.

17 · Counter and slider new

Counter for a small number. Slider for more-or-less, where the exact number matters less than the feel.

Seats at this table8
Music volume 60%

What moves: the counter's number ticks; the minus goes grey at the lowest it can be. The slider's line fills terracotta up to the knob, and the number beside it changes as you drag. The knob answers your finger like every other control: it dips and fills terracotta with a soft ring while you hold it, and springs back when you let go.

18 · Reorder new

Use it to put things in your own order. Drag by the dots, or use the arrows.

What moves: the row lifts when you take it and the others slide out of the way. The arrows do the same job one step at a time, for anyone who finds dragging hard. The order is saved when you let go.

19 · Fold new

Use it to keep the less-used things out of the way. One line says what is inside.

What to bring
Thank-you note

What moves: the fold opens downward and the arrow turns over. Opening one fold closes another on the same screen.

20 · Pro mark new

◆ marks something that is part of Pro. It is shown, not hidden, so people know it exists.

What moves: a Pro choice can still be tapped and tried on the preview. It is not applied; a small note says it is part of Pro, what else Pro gives, and offers See Pro or Not now. Nothing a guest sees ever changes by surprise.

21 · Upload and colours new

Upload: one clear place to add a file. Colours: the couple's five circles, each with its job.

What moves: the upload shows a real 0 to 100 pie, then the picture with a ✕ to remove it. A chosen colour gets a terracotta ring. These five circles are the couple's own colours; the ring around them is ours.

22 · Progress and "show more" new

Progress says how far along. Show more loads the next part of a long list only when asked.

Studio 3 of 11 ready
Guests Showing 20 of 180

What moves: the bar grows as things get done. "Show more" says how many are showing out of how many, and asks the server only when you press it, which keeps the page light.

24 · Photos new

A grid of photos, and one photo opened large. The same viewer everywhere a photo can be opened.

What moves: a tapped photo grows to fill the screen over a dark page. Arrows or a swipe go to the next one; ✕, the dark part or a swipe down closes it. It says which photo this is out of how many.

27 · Notification row new

What moves: the row presses like any button and its dot fades once opened. The newest is on top. Nothing here asks the server again by itself; a new one arrives when the server says so.
