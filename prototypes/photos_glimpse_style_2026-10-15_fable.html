How the couple picks it

Event Hub Maker › Stages › Save the Date › Photos › Style

3:4 cards are the Style tab of the new Maker (house rule: style cards are phone-shaped, never wide strips). Where the Photos row still uses the older website editor's scene-style-row.tsx, the same style is simply the 4th option of its PickMenu: Style · Glimpse ▾ with the hint line “One photo at a time, centred; the next peeks in.” Picking is FREE (no ◆). The pick is one canvas.style value on the our_photos row — exactly as Mosaic/Grid/Film strip are stored today.

Decisions shown above

  • Mechanics: native horizontal scroll with scroll-snap-type: x mandatory and scroll-snap-align: center — the same snap family Film strip already uses (snap-x/snap-start), so swipe, momentum and the back-gesture all behave like the shipped strip. Rail padding of half the leftover width lets the first and last photo centre too.
  • Centre card: scale 1 · opacity 1; neighbours scale .92 · opacity .72, eased 250 ms as they move. Measured per frame from each card's distance to the rail centre — no index state, so a half-swipe looks right.
  • Where am I: dots up to 8 photos; from 9 up, a tabular “3 / 12” counter instead (12 dots on a 375 screen read as noise). Shown here: switch Photos 5 ↔ 12.
  • Tap: the centre card opens the full-screen viewer — the shipped GalleryLightbox (app/_components/gallery/gallery-lightbox.tsx, kind: 'photo', prev/next, close); a neighbour tap just scrolls it to the centre (it is 72 % opaque — a tap says “bring it forward”, not “open”).
  • Desktop: 380 px centre card, two neighbours visible each side, ‹ › arrows on hover / keyboard focus, ← → keys move one photo. No arrows on phones (the thumb is the arrow).
  • Autoplay: no. The guest is reading an invitation at their pace; a timer fights the finger on a snap rail (it jumps mid-swipe), fights the page's Reveal/motion budget, and the shipped Film strip has none either. If the owner wants motion later, it belongs in the Maker's Animate row as a choice, not baked into the style.
  • Reduced motion: no scale, no fade, no smooth-scroll — cards simply snap. Toggle it above.
  • 1 photo: one centred card, no dots, no hint, no peeking. 2 photos: same rail — first centred, second peeks right; one swipe swaps them. Nothing special-cased beyond hiding the dots at 1.
  • Aspect: every card is cropped to 3:4 portrait (object-fit: cover) — the shape the phone is, the shape Mosaic's tiles already use, and the only way neighbours line up as a rail. A landscape photo loses its sides; the couple orders photos today, so the one they put first is the one that opens centred.
  • Light + dark: ivory paper / deep-plum paper via the theme's tokens; card shadow is the only thing that changes weight.
