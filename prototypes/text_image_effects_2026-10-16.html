The six, as the dropdown would list them

Each card’s Show ▶ selects the part and the option in the phone above and plays it. Reduced motion is drawn as the finished still (the ambient-effects rule: never hidden, never a start frame).

Where they sit in the four rows

Build in keeps its four chips (Fade · Blur · Move · Size, they combine). The new Build-ins are how the part arrives, so they take the slot that “Rows ▾” already has in row 4 (owner 2026-10-07: rows live in Build in): a text part gets Words ▾ — All at once · Word by word · Spread; a picture gets Reveal ▾ — Plain · Develop · Curtain. One dropdown each, and they combine with the chips (Fade + Word by word = each word fades up).
Action is Still | Drift today, a two-way pill. Shimmer (in flight, rd/cover-scene-tools) makes it three, and the panel’s own rule says one of several is a dropdown — so Action ▾: text gets Still · Drift · Shimmer · Glow; a picture gets Still · Drift · Ken Burns · Parallax · Light leak. Action has no Movement (shipped rule): Glow breathes every 4.4 s, Light leak drifts across every 16 s, fixed.

None of the six is too heavy for a low-end phone as drawn here. The two to guard: Spread must never animate letter-spacing, and Glow must never animate text-shadow — both re-paint every frame. Every animation above is pure CSS; the only JavaScript a builder needs is the word/letter split, which the hero can do at render time.

Calls made here, for the owner to overrule

  • Spread’s true per-letter drawing only on parts of 24 letters or fewer; longer lines open out as one piece.
  • Glow’s halo takes the part’s own colour (here the scene accent); no colour picker of its own.
  • Light leak is the shipped background Light leak ◆ redrawn inside the photo, so it stays Pro ◆ like its parent. Develop · Curtain · Glow · Word by word · Spread are offered free, like Fade and Move.
  • Develop at Quick (0.6 s) barely reads as film; Calm or Cinematic is the look. The feel is the couple’s to pick, as everywhere.
