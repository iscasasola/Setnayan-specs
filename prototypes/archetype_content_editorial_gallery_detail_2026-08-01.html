The Setnayan manifesto

Setnayan is where the memories of every event in your life are kept — the ones you hold and the ones you attend. A Filipino celebration was never one family's; it belongs to the whole samahan — the ninong and ninang, the titos and titas, the barkada, everyone who showed up. So the memory shouldn't belong to one camera either. Every one of them is holding a piece of your day. Setnayan is where those pieces come together, and everyone goes home with their own.

Designer's note · Editorial

  • One terracotta per view. The hero CTA is the only filled terracotta on the page — nav uses a slate "Sign in", every band closes with a link, and the footer carries none. When everything shouts, nothing sells.
  • The cultural line holds top-to-bottom. The hero speaks samahan, ninong/ninang, barkada — words every Filipino celebration shares. Kasal, binyag and kumpil appear only in the event-types band, deep in the scroll.
  • Line-reveal is the whole show. Headings rise in ≤240ms with 60ms stagger; the hero image settles from 1.02. Nothing the user must read moves under them — no parallax, ever.
  • No peso figure exists on this surface. "Free", "Free to start" and "See pricing" are the only price words allowed; live numbers come from the catalog at runtime.
  • Locked shows the goods; Denied shows the reason. The paid gate lists exactly what's behind it (11 chapters, templates, scripts) with one unlock step. The denial is slate, shielded, and explains whose surface it is — it must never wear Empty's warm face.

Designer's note · Gallery

  • Dark is a surface, not a mode. Obsidian #17160F exists only here, because photographs carry the colour and the chrome must recede. Every other screen in the app stays cream — no toggle exists.
  • Empty vs Denied is a shipped-bug fix, not taste. Both come back as count:0 with no error. Empty is warm gold with a QR and the one filling action; Denied is cool slate with a shield, a reason, and deliberately no blurred teaser — a permission wall that previews private photos would itself be the leak. A surface once printed "no requests yet" over three real pending rows; these two faces existing separately is the cure.
  • Locked teases, because the viewer owns what's behind it. The blurred mosaic and the mono tally (1,284 photos · 214 contributors) are the couple's own day — showing its weight is the sell. One unlock step, no price digits.
  • Lightbox honours the scroll. It rises 26px over the grid in 240ms and swipe-down dismisses it; the grid never re-renders, so you land exactly where you left. Clips scrub with a gold thumb — captured moments, not produced deliverables.
  • Credit is a feature. Every tile names its camera — "Ninang Cora · 4:12 PM" — because in a samahan album, who shot it is part of the memory.

Designer's note · Detail

  • Identity first, numbers second. The hero settles who this is (mark, name, verification, place, canonical S89V id in mono); the facts strip answers the five questions a couple actually asks, in Space Mono so figures read as figures.
  • The sticky bar and the hero CTA are the same button, never twice. "Send inquiry" lives in the hero; the bar rises in 240ms only once the hero's copy has scrolled out of view — so exactly one terracotta is ever on screen.
  • ₱— is a feature, not a placeholder. Package rows carry the peso glyph and an em dash; real amounts stream from the live catalog. A literal figure typed on this surface is a defect.
  • Locked earns the upgrade with the ghost of the answer. The blurred price-position chart shows the shape of what Pro knows — your gold bar among 38 slate ones — without leaking a single number.
  • Tabs, not accordion. Gold underline on the active tab (gold rules the highlight, ink carries the label), mono uppercase labels, sticky under scroll so the body never loses its map.
