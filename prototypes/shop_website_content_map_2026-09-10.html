Two things worth knowing.
“About” needs a paid plan. The one place a shop writes about itself in its own words is behind the page-personalising upgrade. On the free plan a couple reads prices and a song list, and never a sentence from the shop.

Reviews shows even at zero. It prints “This vendor still has no review” with an empty five-star breakdown — on a brand-new shop that is the third thing a couple reads. Whether that is honest or discouraging is your call, not an engineering one.
