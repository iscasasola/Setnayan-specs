Making a card — mid-way

The mechanism

Why this beats a notice afterwards

A warning after the fact reads as an accusation. A bar the shop is filling reads as progress — and it is the same rule, enforced earlier and more kindly.

Publish is disabled, not just asterisked

Both of your existing cards have no price because the field is optional. Shopee greys out Publish until the required fields are in; that single choice is why their listings carry prices and yours don't.

The three rungs are honest

Limited → Findable → Full reach describes what actually happens to the card, not a score we invented. If it doesn't change reach, it doesn't belong on the bar.

⛔ What it must never become

A shop declaring a higher price to climb the bar. The rungs measure completeness, never the size of the number — otherwise we've sold placement.

Answers — opens on the work

Already being built

One list, every kind

A date question, a service question and a money question are all the same thing to a supplier: somebody is waiting. They belong in one queue, sorted by who has waited longest.

The money row is the one to watch

"Downpayment received?" is where a shop can say no — and today saying no erases the couple's proof of paying. That has to change before this ships to real people.

What this changes in the plan

  • Publish disabled until priced replaces "make the price required" — same fix, better shape, and it's the smallest change here.
  • The reach meter replaces the gap notice as the first move. Telling a shop before they publish beats telling them after they book.
  • "Hidden because…" is new, wasn't in the plan, and is live-relevant to both your shops today.
  • The four-tab bar is a fresh decision on a layout approved three days ago — the only item here that undoes something.

Drawn against the palette and type of The Shop Dashboard, Rearranged (26 Aug), which this extends rather than replaces. Nothing here is built.
