The three data archetypes.

Roster · Ledger · Comparison — one pattern each for people, money, and choices. Together they retire the 45 raw <table> elements currently in the app: the literal "Google-sheet experience" being replaced. Every archetype ships with all six states — Ideal, Loading, Empty, Locked, Denied, Error — because a surface once printed "no requests yet" over three real pending rows.

Archetype 01 · People

Roster

A dense list of people — avatar, name, one meta line, a status pill. Selection lives on the avatar itself: press it and the face becomes the checkmark. No checkbox column, no horizontal budget spent. Swipe a row on mobile; hover a row on desktop.

/dashboard/[event]/guests /vendor-dashboard/clients /vendor-dashboard/team /admin/accounts
Designer's note
  1. The avatar is the checkbox. Tapping the identity token toggles selection — the roster never grows a selection column, so density survives select mode.
  2. Density reads as trustworthy. 44px desktop rows, one 11.5px meta line, tabular counts in Space Mono. Shopee-dense, not SaaS-airy.
  3. Actions are peripheral. Swipe-left (mobile) and hover-reveal (desktop) keep the resting state pure reading. Every control is labelled — never icon-only.

Archetype 02 · Money

Ledger

Money rows, grouped by category, every numeral right-aligned in Space Mono like a bank book. The running total is furniture — pinned so it never scrolls away, and it rolls when a row changes. Payment history hides inside the row until asked for.

/dashboard/[event]/budget /vendor-dashboard/earnings /admin/reconciliation /dashboard/[event]/payments
Designer's note
  1. Magnitude scans down one edge. All numerals right-aligned, tabular Space Mono — the eye reads the column of amounts the way a cashier reads a tape.
  2. The total is pinned, and it rolls. Log a payment and PAID/REMAINING count to their new values in ≤240ms — the ledger visibly absorbs the change instead of silently rewriting itself.
  3. Summary first, history on demand. Each row expands to its dated payments and receipts; collapsed, the ledger stays one screen of truth.

Archetype 03 · Choices

Comparison

Two to four things side by side. Working memory holds 3–4 items, so a tier list that repeats every feature for every tier is unreadable. This archetype shows the delta, never the repetition: the higher tier says "Everything in Solo, plus:" and lists only what it adds — under 3–4 named category groups. One card is visually the answer; the reference stays neutral. Nobody is greyed out.

/pricing /vendor-dashboard/subscription /explore/compare (vendor vs vendor) tier matrix (admin catalog)
Designer's note
  1. Delta, not repetition. Pro restates nothing from Solo; Enterprise restates nothing from Pro. The eye compares additions, so the cheapest and the dearest are legible at a glance.
  2. One answer, one axis, one CTA. The recommended card is ink-filled and carries the view's single terracotta CTA. The billing toggle sits above both cards — the axis that applies to everything lives above everything.
  3. Checkmarks are mute. Every tick is identical (gold, 1.75px). Presence of a row is the differentiator — a tick that carries meaning is a tick nobody reads.
