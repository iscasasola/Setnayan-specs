The phone toolbar, per element — Style · Text · Animate

Tap any part of the page and the lower third becomes that element's toolbar: one shared header Style | Text | Animate, the panel filling the rest. Swipe right on the toolbar → the next element; the page scrolls to it and the ring moves. Words are typed on the page (tap the text of the picked element). Big tools get one Edit that opens full screen; facts (Event name · Reply by · Gifts) are full-screen forms that save as you type; the Venue comes from Suppliers. "We want to fill up the bottom toolbar to fit these functions."

Try: tap the countdown → Style shows its three looks (Big number · Boxes · Line) and a background; Text = Font ▾ · colour · size; Animate = Build in ▾ · Action ▾ · Build out ▾ and ▶ plays it on the page. Swipe right/left on the toolbar (or ‹ ›) to walk the 14 elements. Tap Our Entourage → "Edit the Wedding March"; Gifts → "Edit details"; the Venue → book one in Suppliers. Drag the bar on the toolbar's top edge to make it taller (up to half the screen); tap it to toggle. Undo works; Preview is drawn only.

What the prototype settles (for review — nothing is approved):
  • One header for every element — Style | Text | Animate. Big tools (Logo · Mood Board & Dress Code · Wedding March · Love Story · Schedule · Seat plan) keep the header but the panel is one Edit; facts (Event name · RSVP's Reply by · Gifts) keep it and the panel is one Edit details that opens a full-screen form, saving as you type. The dimmed header says "this one is edited inside".
  • Swipe right = next, left = previous, anywhere on the toolbar except the size slider and the grab bar. ‹ › do the same. "4 / 14 · Countdown" is where you are.
  • Every set of choices is a dropdown that opens inside the lower third — Background · Font · Build in · Action · Build out. Presets are the one exception: three thumbnails, because they are pictures.
  • Colour swatches are the Mood Board's five main colours (Dominant · Supporting · Accent · Neutral · Accent 2) + ink, with "+" for a custom one.
  • The Venue is not edited here: the panel shows the booked venue, or "Book your venue in Suppliers — or add one manually there".
  • Resize: drag the bar on the top edge between 268 px and half the screen (406 px); a tap toggles; the size is remembered on this device.
  • Desktop is unchanged this round (right panel holds the same three tools).
