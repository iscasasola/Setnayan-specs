Event onboarding — one engine, steps by event type

Owner, 2026-09-30: the setup lives at the end of creating the event, the theme is asked too, and every event style gets what it needs. This is one engine: seven shared essentials every type answers (name · when · where · cover photo · look · who can reply · guests), 0–2 type steps only where the type needs them, and one Yes/No list at the end. Every card needs an answer, none is work (Not decided yet · Use a theme picture for now · I'll add them later), every answer changes later, no Skip, no Later, no confirm dialogs. Drawn end-to-end for three contrasting types — Wedding · Birthday · Wake — plus one frame each for Corporate and Hangout. Each type's onboarding looks like the event — wedding: serif, gold and wine on paper · birthday: coral and sunflower, rounder · wake: grey, no gold, no emoji · corporate: navy, sans, square · hangout: teal, sans, casual — one skin per profile, never one generic skin. Each frame is marked exists (the screen or its data is in the tree today) or new.

AWhat exists today, and what the engine keeps

Today's three doors (read from origin/main)

  • Wedding wizard (/onboarding/wedding, 17 screens): welcome · role · kind · faith · name · date · region · pax · budget · picker · prefs · account · find · congrats · plan · services · services_summary.
  • Generic wizard (/onboarding/[type], flag-gated): welcome · name · honoree (debut · christening · birthday · graduation · reveal · corporate · gala) · anchor (anniversary) · date · recurs (tournament · reunion · gala) · pax · region · type questions · specialty · 5 quiz axes · persona reveal · services (Papic + AI) · congrats.
  • Simple Event (/onboarding/simple): one form — name + date, Papic card under it.
  • Already per type in the tree: event_type_profiles terminology (couple / celebrant / graduate / organizer / family; wedding / debut / trip / wake), the solemn register (wake: no countdown, no "celebrate", solemn quiz copy in code), specialty-catalog.ts signature fields for 14 types, the honoree link, forced yearly repeat (birthday · anniversary), multi-day (travel · wake), MINOR_HEAVY face opt-in (christening · debut).
  • Already in the Maker, not in onboarding: Theme (10 looks: House · Galeriya · Velvet · Vintage · Regency · Abaca · Cinderella · Gatsby · Whimsical · Cyber), First screen (the cover), Logo Maker, RSVP questions, Who can reply (rsvp-ask.ts: Only my Guest List / Anyone, I approve), Guests, Papic, Gifts.

The engine — five rules

  • One sequence, built from the type's profile. shared essentials + type steps (from the profile: honoree · couple · anchor · multi-day · solemn) + the Yes/No list. The same screen component draws every card; only the words and the control change.
  • Every card is answered, nothing is work. A quick answer is a real answer: Not decided yet · Use a theme picture for now · I'll add them later. Each card says You can change this anytime and each answer lives in its real home (Details · Look · RSVP · Guests).
  • Pre-answered where the type is light. Date · Hangout · Get-together open with the House look, a theme picture and "Only my list" already set; those cards don't show, the chips do (frame H1). FREE stays very easy.
  • The words follow the type (EventWords / profile): couple · celebrant · graduate · organizer · family; guests · attendees · travellers; gifts · abuloy. A wake never sees a logo card, a confetti toast or a questions card.
  • What leaves onboarding — an owner call: pax · region-only · budget · the 5 quiz axes · the persona reveal · the vendor picker · congrats/plan. Proposal: they become the first thing Setnayan AI asks on the dashboard ("Plan with me"), so the hub is ready in ~8 answers and the plan is a second, optional sitting. Old events keep the slim Overview card (finish_your_event_hub_2026-09-30_fable.html).

KWake notes — the tone is automatic

  • Everything solemn here is already decided by the profile's register: no gold, no emoji, no confetti, no countdown, gathering not celebration. The engine reads the register and swaps the words and the accent; nothing is a wake-only screen except the words on it.
  • K1 is the catalog's departed_name · born_on · passed_on as one card. The event name is written from it (Lamay for Lola Remedios) — the family never types a "title". The rite, the resting place, the pasiyam, those carrying and speaking stay in Details, unrequired, as the catalog says: in the first days most answers are honestly "not yet".
  • K2 is first night · last night (multi-day is already true for a wake); Not settled yet everywhere instead of "Not decided". K3 adds At home.
  • K4 asks for a photograph of them, with A plain notice for now as the quiet answer. K5 shows only the muted looks — Capiz light (Vintage) · White lilies · Plain & clean (Galeriya) — and no "More looks".
  • K6 is the same two answers with a solemn line: Who may leave a message of sympathy. No RSVP pressure — the guest side never asks "are you coming?" on a wake. K7's quick answer is simply Later.
  • K8 has two rows only — no logo, no questions. Papic is worded as Photographs for the family and defaults to family-only review; E-Gifts is renamed Abuloy. K9: the toast says Your notice is ready (the solemn rounds already say "Share the news"), and the tiles speak in People · Messages · The service · Abuloy.
