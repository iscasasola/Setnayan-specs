What lives where — and how you reach it

The Maker's menu has five groups — Stages · Look · Studio · Event Info · Settings. Studio holds the own editors (Logo · Mood Board & Dress Code · Wedding March · Love Story · Schedule · Seat plan · E-Gifts · RSVP) · Event Info is one form (Event Name · A note from us · Opening line · What to bring) · venue and date come from Suppliers. Every destination screen has a How to get here card with its paths. Try: Home card → the next missing item · Maker › Menu ▾ (phone) / sidebar (desktop) · tap any part of the page · Suppliers › Lock ▾ sets the date.

Paths this prototype shows.
  • Home › Event Hub card ("7 of 10 ready ›") opens the Maker on the next missing item; the missing items are listed under the card. Desktop: the sidebar row Event Hub › Event Details does the same.
  • Phone — the lower third keeps ONE Menu ▾ button that says where you are ("Invitation ▾"). Tap it: a sheet slides up inside the lower third with one row of five icon tabs — Stages · Look · Studio · Info · Settings — and only the active group's items as tiles. Stages is the default tab.
  • Desktop — the left sidebar has the same five groups as an accordion (one open at a time, Stages open by default; headers carry a count like "Studio · 6 of 8 ready"); the page in the middle; the picked item's tools on the right. Event Info and Settings open as a form in the main area.
  • Tap the part on the page → the lower third (desktop: the right panel) shows that item with one Edit (Studio), Edit details (Event Info) or Change in Suppliers (venue · date).
  • Suppliers: compare cards show each supplier free/busy on the candidate dates; Lock ▾ on a venue picks the date and sets the event date — it appears in the Maker and on Home at once. Add a venue manually creates a manual Venue entry here, which the Maker then shows.
