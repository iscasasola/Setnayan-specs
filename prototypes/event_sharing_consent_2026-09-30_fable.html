Face tagging — the guest's choice, on the RSVP

Tagging needs no account. The guest chooses on the RSVP — Yes, tag me means one quick selfie on the day, and their face data stays with that one event. Their tagged photos are theirs to see and download from the personal link, no sign-in. Only a guest who also saves the ticket to a Setnayan account is offered one optional extra: reuse the face already on the account (skip the selfie) — off until they turn it on, per event, changeable in Profile → Privacy. Sample: Ana & Miguel · March 13, 2027 · guest Mr. Manuel Cortez Casasola · account manuel@email.com. No email is sent from any of these screens — the address is only the account the ticket is saved to.
