One shell — the whole product beside one rail

Walk it. Press Your events → open Cale & Ice → the rail pushes an "In this event" group and your own rows stay. Then Guests, Marketplace, Studio, Budget — the rail never moves and the active row follows you. Press Home to land back on the public front door, still signed in.
Four places have their own menu, and all four behave the same way — the rail names where you are, then pushes that place's menu in under it, and your own rows stay above. An event pushes in three named sections: Plan · Go live · Also in this event. People pushes in Connections · Samahan · Alaga. Studio Azul pushes in its five. Setnayan HQ pushes in its six, flat, with All surfaces as a link. Every one of these was read out of the live code — none of it was invented for the drawing.
Two corrections in this pass. The event's menu is three named sections, not one list — that is how it is built today and the drawing had flattened it. And People is not "coming soon": the page exists, and Samahan works on it right now. What is genuinely still to come is the Connections half — which the code already calls by that name. Family, godparent and friend are one list with the relationship written on each person, not three menus; lola, pinsan and kumpare are worked out from those and nobody types them. And Alaga is not only a child — a pet, a business and something you own are all real kinds, and the single question it asks changes its own name for each.
Two things this proves that the shipped code cannot do yet. ① The active row follows you — today "Home" is nailed on, so all 296 pages would light Home. ② The chrome never changes: one rail, one top bar, one gold + Create — while every action inside the content stays terracotta. That boundary is the content column, not a route.
