The RSVP scene — an editable head, a standard form, one switch

Ana Reyes replying to Indalecio & Claire, 18 December 2026. The RSVP is one scene the couple edits like any other: the heading, the intro line and the background are tappable elements (font · colour · size · animation, with “↺ use the Event Hub font”), and they inherit the theme by default. Everything under them — the answer buttons, the plus-one name boxes, meal, dietary, song, note, mobile, the Terms tick and the one Send — is the standard form: themed, not editable, so it always works on a phone.

One switch in the Maker: Ask one question at a time. OFF = everything on one scrolling page (the three theme frames). ON = one question per screen with progress dots and Back. Static pictures — nothing is clickable. Phones are 390 × 844; the OFF scene is drawn at full scroll length.

Legibility rule applied: body and muted text ≥ 4.5:1 against the composited background (theme scrim/panel over the poster’s brightest and darkest pixels, not the average). Classic and Modern pass on the theme’s own tokens; Luxe needed a deeper scrim behind the form (its gold labels measured 3.5 at the theme’s 62 %) — noted on its frame and in the numbers at the foot.

Contrast measured (composited, worst pixel in the text zone)

  • Classic — ink on paper 13.6 · muted 5.0 · gold eyebrow 4.5 (labels only, 13 px small caps — same as the shipped theme). No change.
  • Luxe — at the theme’s 62 % scrim, over the floor spotlight (#e0d8d0) muted text composites to 4.8 and over a chandelier hotspot (#f8cc95) to 4.9 — at the line — and the gold accents (seat count, “Required”, eyebrow) fall to 3.5. Fix: the scrim deepens to 86 % from the first question down (the top 150 px keep 62 % so the chandeliers still read behind the heading, which is 36 px foil and needs only 3:1). At 86 % over the same pixels: ink 12.7 · muted 10.1 · gold 7.4. Gold pills/buttons: black ink on #e3a86f 9.7.
  • Modern — 86 % acrylic over the darkest pixel (fern #242c0b): ink 9.8 · muted 4.9 · green 6.6; over the white window: ink 12.9 · muted 6.4. No change; green button white-on-#3a4a1c 9.7.
  • Placeholders are set in the theme’s muted ink (not faded) so “or later” and “Song · artist” pass too.

What is new against what ships

  • Three editable elements on the RSVP scene (heading · intro · background), stored like the other made-once scenes (config_json, the same shape Hero/Love Story use) — font · colour · size · animation, each defaulting to the theme.
  • One stored boolean per event: ask one question at a time (default OFF). Read by the guest RSVP page (app/[slug]/_components/rsvp-widget.tsx) which already carries the shipped labels; the questions and their switches are unchanged.
  • Theme tokens come from lib/invite-themes.ts (palette · fonts · radius · scrim) — nothing new per theme except the Luxe form-zone scrim step (62 % → 86 %), which belongs with the theme’s scrim, not with the scene.
  • Maker → RSVP page: MakerPage pattern from #5996; the switch is the only new control on the settings side.
