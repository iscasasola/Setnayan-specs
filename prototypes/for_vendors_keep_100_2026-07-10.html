The model

We monetize the doorway — never the deal.

A marketplace that takes a percentage is quietly rooting for your invoice to be bigger.

We're not. Because your transactions never pass through Setnayan, you keep full control of your pricing — and you never have to expose your true costs or margins to your clients or to us. That privacy is yours to keep.

So how does Setnayan get paid? You pay a small fee for access — a plan and a lead token when you choose to answer a matched couple. That fee covers the tools you actually use: your profile, your calendar, your client threads, the storage that holds your portfolio and bookings. Nothing more.
