The final admin — every job has one place

Owner, 2026-10-02: “admin page should become simpler so when we build the final design of the admin, it can serve all its functions and still keep everything simple to navigate. today, i am lost on the admin page. i only use search bar and rely on the overview page because it does so many things and has so many buttons.”
This extends the approved phone admin (Work · Money · People · More, one Next card) to the whole admin and organises it around the 19 jobs the owner named. Four words in the rail; under More, one heading per job. Every one of today's 138 destinations has exactly one place; 12 new pages are tagged new. Search sits at the top of every screen. The Overview becomes ONE Needs action list — every pending item across admin, oldest and most urgent first, with its action in the row. Words: supplier, never vendor · Event Hub, never website · one dropdown for any set of choices · no “Edit in X ↗” · live counts read —.

What the desktop does

  • ② The heart: ONE Needs action list (owner 2026-10-02: “an easier way to show all that has needs action”). It extends the shipped /admin/work feed (ADMIN_QUEUE_META · BASE_ROWS · work/actions.ts settle-from-the-list) from 20 queue rows to every pending ITEM across admin, oldest and most urgent first (the shipped compareQueuePriority: overdue → due soon → oldest). Each row is one plain line with its age, a due mark when urgent, and its action IN the row — Confirm · Match… · Approve · Map to ▾ · Decline ▾ · Clear · Send export · Open. A cleared row slides out with Undo; a judgement queue (disputes, fraud) only ever offers Open. One Show ▾: All · Money · Suppliers · Safety · Privacy · Problems, each with its count. The total is the badge on Work in the rail and the phone bar. Empty = Nothing needs you · last checked 9:41 — only after every source read.
  • The rail is the phone bar, opened out. Work · Money · People · More, and under each its pages — 25 rows replace the shipped six-group menu (83 links). Work has two pages (Work = the list, Problems) because every queue is a row ON the list, never a menu item.
  • ① Help a person — the main support tool. People › Everyone is one search; a person opens ONE page: events (each with Fix ▾ — reopen guest list · move the date · resend access · unlink a seat · force-delete), people on their events, memories, shop, orders & payments, the problems they hit (linked to the trace), access, and their slice of the Admin log. new View as this person is read-only — every use is written to the log and nothing can be changed while viewing.
  • ① Ban · ⑧ Force-delete. Never loose buttons: one Account actions ▾ on the person, the shop and the event. The dangerous ones open a panel in the page: a required reason, a typed confirmation (no browser dialog), the plain consequence, an Admin-log entry, and a 24-h undo where the data allows.
  • Confirm in the row only when Setnayan AI read the screenshot and amount + reference match (owner 2026-10-01); otherwise the row offers Match… or Open. Verify Lumina Studio offers Open because an ID must be seen (ACT-FROM-THE-LIST 2026-08-04: a fact gets one click, a judgement gets the case file, details get a small form).
  • ③ Search on every screen, ⌘K: pages by name AND by what you are trying to do (the shipped alias list), then actions, then people and orders, then Ask Setnayan AI. A result opens the page with its panel already open.
  • Today's events new are rows in the same list — every event happening today with its live state and its Problems — so a wedding in progress is never on a second screen. A source that failed to read shows — and couldn't load in its row, never 0.
  • ④ Content › Categories is the approved one-page design unchanged; ⑤ Problems replaces Connection logs: issues grouped, most-hit first, one row → the trace in place (where · action · what failed · what it looked like · build · five steps); closes itself when the fix ships and it stops.
  • ⑥ Templates › Event Hub themes new: the ten shipped themes and made-in-admin ones in one list (thumbnail · Free/Pro · recommended for · status). Duplicate → the maker: Free or Pro ▾ · Recommended for ▾ (checkmarks) · Status ▾, then the SAME controls the Maker has (Background ▾ · Font ▾ · Palette ▾ · Ornament ▾ · Motion ▾ · one Look ▾ per scene), a live preview on a sample event at phone and desktop width with Event type ▾, the AA check, and Publish → it appears in couples' theme pick and the onboarding look step.
