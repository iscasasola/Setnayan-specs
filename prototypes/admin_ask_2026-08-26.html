No menu here, on purpose — this phone only answers what needs a decision. Prices, categories, the website and settings are editing; they wait for the computer, and asking for one here says exactly that.

Why a menuless admin still keeps its menus

Asking is faster when you know what you want and useless when you do not. The six menus stay so you can still wander and discover; the ask box is for the 90% of days when you already know. The moment something is reachable only by typing the right sentence, that is a hole in the menu, not a feature of the assistant.

What it costs to run

Pennies. One short question to the AI per sentence you type — and only when the plain word-match cannot answer it on its own. If the AI is ever unreachable, the box quietly goes back to being today’s type-and-go, so the admin never stops working.

What it can never do

Confirm money. Move a price. Approve a shop. Publish anything. Delete anything. It can prepare every one of those and hand it to you finished — the press stays yours.
