The hardest calls in v4, then v3’s, then v2’s

00 · The one leg this drawing changes in the resolver, said out loud

On the shipped bench a ★ Chosen card gets no actions at all — the resolver’s rule 2 withholds Add-to-build, Lock and the inquiry leg once a supplier is locked. So for Hiraya and Casa Verde, “Open conversation” is not a relabel; it is the conversation leg extended to locked cards. It is the smallest change that makes the feature work (the booked supplier is exactly the one with two things waiting), and it is the only line in this drawing that is not a port or a relabel. It should be built as narrowing rule 2 to withhold build and lock only.

000 · A card drawn from a description is a card drawn from scratch

v3’s brief described the page and never the card, so v3 drew a card. It looked plausible and it was missing eleven things a couple sees today. The rule for anything that ships: open the component, list what it renders, draw that list, then add. A summary of a card is a list of the things the summariser thought of.

0 · A duplicate is safe only when it is a projection

Hiraya appears on the bench row and in the Picks column. That is fine exactly as long as both are drawn from ONE derivation of the conversation — the same function that builds the Decisions standing line — and only the bench row carries the button. The moment the Picks card grows its own “Open chat” with a different target, or its own count computed a second way, the two will disagree within a week and the couple will believe whichever they read last.

00 · Write the preview short — do not widen the box

The desktop list column is 272px and the phone is 375px; a preview gets one line of roughly 36 and 54 characters. Widening the column steals from the conversation; a second line doubles every row. The fix is upstream: every card we generate gets a preview sentence written to fit, fact first. Only a typed message has no short form, so only a typed message is ever cut.

1 · One vocabulary, not two

The tempting design gives every card its own status word — Sent / Accepted / Confirmed / Moved / Pending — and within a week the summary line disagrees with the badge above it. v2 refuses that: the pill on a card is the stage it produced, the sentence under it is a date and a fact. A meeting never “books” anything, so it never wears Booked; it says “moved to Sun 27 Sep · confirmed”.

2 · The couple keeps a list column but loses the inbox

Removing the column entirely would send a couple back to the plan to switch from Hiraya to Kusina. Keeping it as a destination recreates the inbox. So it stays as the left side of the conversation screen, headed “‹ Plan”, and nothing in the couple’s navigation links to it on its own. On the phone the equivalent screen IS the plan’s category list.

3 · Counts about other couples

“2 other couples asking about 18 Dec” is useful to the supplier and dangerous the moment it carries a name or reaches the couple. It is a number on the supplier’s own rows, sourced from their own inquiries, drawn nowhere on the couple’s side. If it ever becomes a link to those couples, it should open the supplier’s own list filtered by date — never a card naming who they are.
