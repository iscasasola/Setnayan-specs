The rule that shapes every state above

It suggests. It never decides, and it never refuses.

  • Miscellaneous stays on the screen, always. The card is universal (owner, 2026-08-28: “the card is universal fit for any service”). A supplier who disagrees with every suggestion must be able to walk past all of them without a fight.
  • A low-confidence match shows nothing. A confidently wrong suggestion is worse than none — a supplier who trusts it files their card under somebody else's trade, and then couples find the wrong shop.
  • The model may not invent a trade. At step 4 it chooses nothing at all; at step 5 it writes a proposal, and a person presses. Removing a trade later strands the shops that listed under it, so every one we mint is close to permanent.
  • Every step fails silent. No key, no network, a refusal → the box behaves exactly as step 2. A supplier must never meet an error about an assistant they did not ask for.
