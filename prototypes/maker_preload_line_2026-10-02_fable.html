The tool-preload line — a thin gold line under the bar

DECISION_LOG 2026-10-02: the Maker downloads all its tools right after it opens (“can you download the whole maker once it loads?”), and the download shows as a thin line under the top bar (“can we show the download progress?” — “yes”). The line fills 0 → 100% while the tools land in the background, then fades out. No text, no percent, nothing to tap — the Maker is usable the whole time. Nothing else in the Maker changes; the bar below is today's bar (stages │ Details · Prints; one picker on a phone). Sample: Ana & Miguel · March 13, 2027.

1Where

Directly under the Maker's top bar, full width, 2 px, the gold accent. No track or background behind it — only the filled part is drawn.

2When

Appears as soon as the Maker is on screen and starts preloading. Each tool that lands moves it; it is never a guess.

3Then

At 100% it holds a beat and fades. Gone means every tool opens with no download. Hidden entirely when Save-Data skips the preload.

4Never

No words, no number, no spinner, no tap target, no dimming. Reduced motion: it appears and disappears without animating.
