  • The cover's background today is the Hero — lib/event-hero.ts resolveHero: the couple's photo (landing_page_hero_image_url) or the written card, from Details › Hero, ONE hero for every stage, the poster, the home card and Post Event's starting cover (every-hero-reads-the-one-resolver.test.ts).   • The cover is not full-screen today — the hero is a section of the page (pahina-masthead.tsx), as tall as its content, with the next scene under it.

What's new — the answers to the five questions

1 · How the group is selected — three doors, one of them the fix. (a) The grey line becomes a door. A cover word's Background tool keeps its sentence and gains one tap: "This sits on the Cover Scene." → Select the Cover Scene ›. The owner's exact moment of confusion was a word picked and Background tapped; that moment now answers. (b) A tap on the cover's empty space — the one place a couple taps when they mean "the whole thing" — selects the group, with the dotted outline round every word. (c) Cover Scene is the first row of the parts ▾, its words indented beneath it. No fourth: a long-press, a "Group" button or a Shift-tap would all need telling.

2 · What the group is — every word on the cover (logo · title · names · invite line · date · place · link), named Cover Scene by the owner. Three reasons, and now a fourth: a scene that fills the screen holds everything on it. The hero's four designs (lib/hero-design.ts: The Card · The Marquee · The Crest · The Letter) lay these words out as ONE composition — a plate behind only the logo and title cuts that composition in two, with the names sitting half on the Hero and half off it, and the couple then wants a second plate for the names (two groups, two backgrounds, two bars). A couple says "the words" and "the picture"; nobody says "the logo-and-title group". And it is one thing to select, one outline, one Background.
3 · The Cover Scene is the whole first screen, and its Background is that screen's background — the Hero folded in, so there is ONE. Owner, verbatim: "Cover scene will take up the whole screen initially whatever screen type it is." So on load a guest sees the Cover Scene filling the viewport — phone, tablet or desktop — the words centred inside it, the page's first scroll beginning under it. Framed now means the screen inset with a rounded margin, the page colour (Global Look) showing round it — a card on the page, still full height; Full width means edge to edge. Both fill the height; only the edges differ. Which of the two "cover backgrounds" survives: the Cover Scene's. Details › Hero does not die — it becomes a second door to the same value (the rule every Info field already follows: two doors, one value). Its Upload pick IS landing_page_hero_image_url; its first source, The theme's own, IS today's written card; a Scene ◆ or a Colour are what the Hero could never be. resolveHero keeps answering the poster, the home card and Post Event — from the Cover Scene's still (a loop's poster frame, the photo, or the card). Why this way round and not Hero-replaces-Cover-Scene: the Hero is a photo-or-card and knows nothing of a loop, a colour, a glass or a shape; the Cover Scene's Background is the shipped four rows and knows all of them, and it is where the couple is standing when they want it.

4 · Legibility. Row 3 on a Scene or a photo is the one Darker ━●━ Lighter bar, as shipped; and under it the free rule already in the tree: the words' tone is chosen over the screen's ground (the loop's measured lightest and darkest clusters, the colour, the photo's sample under the words), and the scrim is strengthened until AA passes. In the prototype: slide the bar past the middle and watch the words flip from cream to ink on their own; the caption under the phone says what was measured. A glass (Opaque · Frosted) sits over the theme's own ground, so the written card shows through it.

5 · Free and Pro, and the couple's media. Exactly the shipped rule: The theme's own (the written card) and Colour (Plain · Diagonal · Glow · Opaque · Frosted) free; Scene ◆ and Upload ◆ Pro — tried free, named at Apply as Unlock Pro and Apply, never a padlock. The Scene ◆ loops are the "animated video" the owner asked for, and they play full screen. Upload ◆ opens the couple's media: one line Your media · 120 MB of 500 MB (owner, same day: "so they can upload photos, media files to enhance the look of their website" — 500 MB for Pro is PROPOSED, not confirmed) and a picker of their files; a photo is picked, a video is greyed and says won't play for guests yet — it is kept, it is counted, it is not worn.
