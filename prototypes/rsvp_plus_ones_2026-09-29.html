What’s new vs 27 Sep

  • One dropdown, not a stack of name boxes. The 27 Sep RSVP (frame 3) drew “Seat 2 — their name / Seat 3 — their name” as a list. Now “Filling in for: ▾” picks the person and only that person’s boxes show — PickMenu, the shipped dropdown, never a pill row.
  • Meal + dietary per plus-one. 27 Sep asked one meal for the bringer only. Each seat now carries its own first name, last name, meal preference and dietary notes — and nothing more (no song, no note to the couple, no selfie).
  • TBA, add later — everywhere. A blank seat reads “Guest 3 · TBA” on the Thank-you, on Me and on the host’s list, with “Add name” opening the four boxes in place. 27 Sep had the “Add their name” verb but no in-place form.
  • The host count adapts live. The +0…+4 dropdown on the Guest List row; changing it re-shapes Maria’s RSVP, Thank-you and Me without a new link or QR. Too many names for the seats shows as a quiet warning with per-name Remove — the host resolves it, the guest is never blocked.
  • Prefilled on the plus-one’s own link. Ben sees what Maria answered for him, marked “from Maria”, and is asked only the gaps (frame F). 27 Sep’s frame 9 showed the plus-one arriving with nothing filled.

Owner rules honoured (2026-09-26 → 29): host sets the count · guest names only given seats · same link/QR adapts live · minimum four questions per plus-one · blank = TBA allowed · every named plus-one gets their own QR/link and “Save to my account” · any set of choices is ONE dropdown · phone first, plain English, “Event Hub” never “website”.
