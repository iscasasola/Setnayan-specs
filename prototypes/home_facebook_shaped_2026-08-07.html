The home a Facebook-and-TikTok Filipino already knows how to use.

Same four surfaces, same doors, same locked palette. What changes: the page re-ranks itself by life stage, the composer asks "What's your event?", and storytellers get a TikTok-shaped lane whose chapters carry the real, bookable vendors behind each video.

Chassis

Four surfaces, unchanged: Events · Alaala · Yours to run · People. This file re-skins and re-ranks; it never re-conceives.

Familiarity

Facebook shapes the home (composer · feed · pages · bottom bar). TikTok shapes the chapter (vertical poster · byline · shop links under the video).

Honesty

Setnayan hosts no video. A chapter mounts a third-party player on tap — it never autoplays. The vendor links are the thing that is instant.

No lurking

Private people connect only by mutual handshake. The ONE public follow is a storyteller — a person who publishes deliberately, to strangers. No private event ever appears in any feed.

Archetype delta · the chapter page · obsidian zone

02 · The Chapter — TikTok’s rhythm, without TikTok’s player

A chapter IS a video with the vendors underneath — TikTok Shop’s shape exactly. But Setnayan hosts no video: the edit lives on the storyteller’s own YouTube / TikTok / Instagram and renders as a sandboxed embed. So the design is poster-first: the poster fills the screen instantly, ONE tap mounts the real player, and the vendor links are the thing that is always instant. Tap the poster below to feel it.

Where this differs from real TikTok — say it plainly
  1. No autoplay, no sound-on scroll. The video starts only when the person taps the poster. That is the honest cost of not hosting video — and of not shipping a heavy stack of live iframes to a mid-range phone on mobile data.
  2. Swiping shows the next POSTER instantly, not the next playing video. Posters are light (YouTube chapters use the channel’s own thumbnail; TikTok/Instagram chapters get the branded obsidian poster, because no thumbnail can be derived without hosting).
  3. What IS instant: the vendors. The links under the video need no player at all. Video → vendor’s shop = one tap. Video → inquiry started = two taps. TikTok Shop’s habit, kept.
Every element above is real or explicitly new
  • Ships today: the chapter page, the embed allowlist, the vendor cards with the real-relationship gate (a vendor with no accepted collab and no booking renders as plain text — the "Chef Andoy" line), the viewer-promo line, follower + view counts, the Follow button, the Storyteller badge.
  • New in this design: the poster-first player (today the iframe mounts immediately), the vertical next-chapter rhythm, the 9:16 poster grammar.
  • The vendor count chip on rail cards counts only vendors that pass the relationship gate — never inflated by plain-text mentions.
Archetype delta · private event ↔ public story

03 · The Seam — two doors, and everything that never crosses

A wedding is private; a story is public; the same person owns both. They meet in exactly two places — both visible only to the owner. The public chapter never shows the event’s name, date, venue, guest list or gallery. The video itself is whatever the storyteller already published on their own channel — Setnayan adds nothing to it.

Door 1 · inside the private event

After the event date, in the event’s own dashboard. Owner-only — a coordinator or guest never sees it.

Tell this storyPublish a Chapter — your video, and the vendors who made this day.

Opens a chapter draft already linked to this event, with its booked vendors pre-suggested as the shoppable list. Nothing publishes until the person presses Publish on their own public page — the same gates that ship today.

Door 2 · on the home’s event card

Once a chapter is published, the private card gains one owner-only chip.

The chip jumps to the public chapter. This is the only place the two worlds are visible together — and only the owner ever sees them side by side.

What NEVER crosses the seam

The paranoid list — a wedding has guests who never agreed to be public.

The guest list — no name, count, or RSVP state reaches the chapter.

The Papic gallery — the public page may say a gallery exists behind the chapter; it shows zero photos from it.

The event’s name, date and venue — the chapter shows only the title the storyteller typed.

The event’s visibility — publishing a chapter never makes the event public, listed, or findable.

Faces & consent — Setnayan adds no faces. The video is what the storyteller already published on their own channel, under their own responsibility, on their own platform.

Also specified (see the .md): day-of posture

On the event day itself the home collapses to one card — "Today is the day." — that jumps into the live event. No board, no ribbon, no rail, no prompts. It is not mocked here because it is one rule, not a layout: everything except the live event steps back for 24 hours.
