Suppliers — one screen

2026-10-07 · Fable · prototype only, nothing built.

Owner's brief: "we want a uni screen interface that adapts to both desktop and mobile view… navigate properly to all their categories, find suppliers, compare combinations, add supplier manually, chat with suppliers, and still make it look legible to the eyes. with animation"

Below is the same page twice: a phone at 375 and a computer at 1280. Both are a working model — every button does its job (find, save, inquire, accept, build, compare, book, pay, set a price, remove with undo, change the date). What you do is remembered in this browser; "Reset" is at the bottom.

The Maker's shape — one control, one body, tools at the thumb

  1. Find · Build · Booked — one segmented control on top, like Stages | Studio; it and the date · place line stay pinned under the shell bar as you scroll. Find is steps 1–3, Build is 4–5 (your cart), Booked is 6–9.
  2. Find opens on the shipped "Cover your event" ring as rows (shortlist-categories.tsx); "＋ Add to your event" is one dropdown of the admin taxonomy. Tap a category and it unfolds in place (the Your Team FINAL accordion, one open at a time): Yours (one row shape; the verbs follow the step: Ask for a quote → Nudge → Read their reply → Add to build · Book), then the marketplace under it, full width and every candidate shown so they compare at a glance — price first, then free on your date, rating, events, area (the approved frame 7: one Filter ▾; pressing the row opens the supplier's page adapted to the category — their badges (the shipped four-badge system: New · Verified · Couple Trusted · Most Booked, plus Top Pick and "Vouched for by …" from the trusted circle), their service card (the shipped ServiceCardFace) first, then proof — why they fit this category, their rating with its count, couples' reviews in this category (the shipped reviews, words and date only), their work as photo grids per event labelled type · venue · month, their own shots only, no names and no guest details — then the rest of their portfolio; the one verb on a new supplier is Ask for a quote; Add to build appears once a price is in). At the thumb: CATEGORY ▾ opens a row and scrolls to it, and the build pill.
  3. "Add to build" peeks the cart — the line you added, the new total, "View build ›" — then tucks away.
  4. Build is the cart (BuildLocked + BuildCompare): one line per category with Change ›, the money, the date-fit line, saved builds side by side. At the thumb: Save this build · Book N ›.
  5. Booked (TeamRows, MerkadoBudgetLens): your team with what they sent and what they feed across the event, and the original's money rows.
  6. Date · place is the line under the title, nowhere else. Tap the date: month → day → time, the Date Finder (FindYourDate). Tap the place: region → city → venues → room size (VenuesEditor). They sharpen as you book.

Sheets only for input: chat (the One Chat Box), pay, set price, book, date, venue, add your own, save build. One face, Hanken Grotesk, as the Maker uses it; the Find · Build · Booked control is the shipped ISegmented in its wine tone (the Maker's Stages | Studio). Chat hangs off the row; the shell's icon is the one inbox. The shipped fit badge (Merkado scoring) is not redrawn — nothing here guesses whether a price fits.

One scroller in the order above, no navigator, sheets only for browsing and input, motion with meaning (height transitions, sliding sheets, counting money, a one-time "Booked" pop). Phone first; two columns at 1024.

Kept as shipped: the shell bar, the five-tab bar, TeamRows content and one action per row, the bench data, BuildLocked picks, the budget lens ("Not knowable"), BuildCompare, FindYourDate, the manual-add modal, the One Chat Box, MiniTour.
