Suppliers — one screen

2026-10-07 · Fable · prototype only, nothing built.

Owner's brief: "we want a uni screen interface that adapts to both desktop and mobile view… navigate properly to all their categories, find suppliers, compare combinations, add supplier manually, chat with suppliers, and still make it look legible to the eyes. with animation"

Below is the same page twice: a phone at 375 and a computer at 1280. Both frames are live — tap the rows, the categories, the date, the venue, "Find more", "Add your own", a chat. Nothing leaves the page.

What changes (the delta)

  1. One scroller, no menu. "Your planning" and the hidden find-area go. Six sections: Your suppliers · Cover your event · Picks · Plans · Payments · Chats. A sticky Jump to dropdown navigates and follows the scroll.
  2. Date · venue are the second line of the page, editable in place. Date Finder opens there. Venue shows the room size the venue sent, or "Ask them".
  3. Categories open in place. Only what this event type needs; the rest behind one "More categories" dropdown. A row opens to a swipe strip (Add to picks · Inquire · Book · Remove), then "Find more" and "Add your own" as sheets.
  4. Picks + money always visible, with the date-fit line. "Compare plans" opens side by side, in place.
  5. Chats on the page: three latest threads; a conversation opens as a sheet (phone) or panel (computer).
  6. Motion with meaning: height transitions, sheets slide, the Jump label crossfades, money counts to its new value, "Booked" pops once. Respects reduced motion.

Kept as shipped: the shell bar, the five-tab bar, TeamRows content and one action per row, the bench data, BuildLocked picks, the budget lens ("Not knowable"), BuildCompare, FindYourDate, the manual-add modal, the One Chat Box, MiniTour.
