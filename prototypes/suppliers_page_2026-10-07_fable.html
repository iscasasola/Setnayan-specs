Suppliers — one screen

2026-10-07 · Fable · prototype only, nothing built.

Owner's brief: "we want a uni screen interface that adapts to both desktop and mobile view… navigate properly to all their categories, find suppliers, compare combinations, add supplier manually, chat with suppliers, and still make it look legible to the eyes. with animation"

Below is the same page twice: a phone at 375 and a computer at 1280. Both are a working model — every button does its job (find, save, inquire, accept, build, compare, book, pay, set a price, remove with undo, change the date). What you do is remembered in this browser; "Reset" is at the bottom.

The page follows the couple's journey

  1. Cover your event — the shipped "Cover your event" ring (Reception · Ceremony · Catering · Cake · Bridal Car on a wedding) as rows; the rest of the admin taxonomy behind one "More categories" dropdown, grouped as the Find page groups it. Open a row to find suppliers, save them, ask for a quote, or add your own.
  2. Chats — inquire, read the quote, accept the price. The supplier lands in your build.
  3. Build — one supplier per category; the money and the date-fit update as you pick. Save it.
  4. Compare builds — side by side, with the dates each one is free on. Use one.
  5. Booked — book from a build or a chat. Each booked supplier shows what it feeds: Event Hub, seat plan, schedule, guests.
  6. Budget — booked · in your build · paid · buffer, and a meter. Buffer refuses to guess while anyone is unpriced.
  7. Date & place — two ladders that fill in as you book: month → day → time, region → city → venue → room size. The line under the title sharpens with them.

One scroller in the order above, no navigator, sheets that never leave the page, motion with meaning (height transitions, sliding sheets, counting money, a one-time "Booked" pop). Phone first; two columns at 1024.

Kept as shipped: the shell bar, the five-tab bar, TeamRows content and one action per row, the bench data, BuildLocked picks, the budget lens ("Not knowable"), BuildCompare, FindYourDate, the manual-add modal, the One Chat Box, MiniTour.
