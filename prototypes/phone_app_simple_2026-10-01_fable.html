✓What moved where

  • Home: the nudges (set the date, Papic ready, comeback offer, day-of grid, details guide) collapse into one Next card — the most urgent wins; the rest queue behind it, one at a time.
  • Guest list: Guests · Hosts · Check-in tabs → columns (decided 2026-09-30); the part picker, view switcher, invite panel, add bar, select bar and card carousel → ⋯, the row's own Invite, and the round +. Select stays on long-press (approved rows, frame C).
  • Filters: RSVP · Side · Role · Group → one Filter ▾. Sort ▾ and the phone's one-column Show ▾ move into ⋯.
  • Guest card: as approved — ticket + Send on top; Name · Reply · Their guests · Seat · Access · Check-in as closed rows.
  • Your Team: shortlist categories, budget lens, compare, reuse-a-booking, quotes waiting → behind Find a supplier and ⋯. The booked rows and their next step stay on top.
  • More: the five cards → six rows in a sheet. The bar drops Maker and Services; Maker is reached from Home.
  • Desktop shows the same screens with more columns and both panels open — never a different screen.
