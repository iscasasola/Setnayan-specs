0The four patterns · one sentence each

AList + detail

Search + ONE Show ▾ on top, two-line rows, tap a row → one detail panel in a fixed order (Summary · What happened · Related · History) with one primary action; everything else under More ▾; the dangerous ones under Account actions ▾ with a reason and a typed confirm.

BEditor

A list on the left, the editor in the middle, a live preview on the right; one Publish; Status ▾ and Duplicate instead of separate forms.

articles · /features entries · Event Hub themes · invitation & print · email & notification templates · categories
CSettings

Grouped rows, one setting per row: plain name, who changed it and when, the control in place (switch · field · dropdown); saves as you leave the field; no “Edit in X ↗”.

switches · prices · payment accounts · integrations · secrets · team & permissions · business details
DNumbers

At most 4 numbers with one date-range dropdown, one chart, then the list behind the numbers; a count that could not be read shows “—”, never 0.
