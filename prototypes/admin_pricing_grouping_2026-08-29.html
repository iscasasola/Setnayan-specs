What changed vs the approved 26 Aug screen

Three moves, each answering a measured problem. Everything the shipped screen can do, this one can still do.

One product, one row

The 17 Papic sizes stop interleaving the list. One summary row carries the whole ladder — expand it to read the rungs, edit them where they already edit: on the Papic credit prices tab. Setnayan AI keeps its row and gains the same pointer to its bands tab. Search still reaches every rung — typing “10,000” opens the ladder to the matching size.

Grouped by what a customer buys

Customer rows cluster into five small families (Papic · Planning · The day on film · Design & music · The Event Hub); vendor rows cluster by offering (Subscriptions · Add-ons · Seats & branches · Custom tier). Nine products read as nine products. Nothing sorts by price any more.

Dead means dead — and shows its distance from gone

Every switched-off row was filing under Drafts, because the retirement stamp is NULL on all of them — a dead price read as work in progress. The third tab is now Switched off: greyed title, struck price, the date it went off, and a per-row meter of what still holds it in place. Zero holds and never sold → Remove for good offers itself, exactly as the 26 Aug design ruled.
