Walk it

  1. Edit The cover's lines, top to bottom, two to a row, each with the house switch. Switch Title off — it leaves the cover at once. Names has no switch ("always"); a tap says why. Tap a line's name → that word is picked, with its own tools.
  2. Style Not grey any more: it is the Opening — the Reveal's own cards (None free · Sheer veil ◆ · the rest after the envelope proof), folded in from the tile that used to float above the cover. The tile is gone; the cover's corner names the opening.
  3. Animate Entrance ▾ — one dropdown of whole-cover entrances, each setting every word's Build in in order (three beats = the shipped Delay steps None · Short · Long). Movement ◆ = the feel (Quick · Calm · Cinematic). Row 3 draws the beats. Row 4: who is on their own, and Reset to the cover's entrance.
  4. ▶ Tap: the closed veil with no words → the unveil → every line entering in its beat. Hold ▶: the whole page as a guest — the bars slide away, the veil waits for your tap, "Exit preview" brings everything back.
  5. A word Tap Logo on the cover → its own Animate shows the cover's values with "Follows the cover's entrance". Change a chip → on its own, and row 3 offers "Reset to the cover's entrance ›". The group's row 4 now counts it.
  6. Background As shipped — no Framed | Full width any more (the cover is always edge to edge); Upload ◆ now takes your video (≤ 15 s): it plays muted for guests, a still for strangers.
  7. Shimmer Cover Scene › Animate › Shimmers ▾ — Nothing · The names · The title · both: a lighter band of the word's own colour passes across it every 5 s (the competitor's is always gold; ours follows the couple's colour, Gold is a choice). On a word: Animate › Action › While on screen ▾ = Still · Drift · Shimmer (three choices = one dropdown). Still under reduced motion.
  8. Confirm attendance The cover's Link (a shipped word, the couple's own text) now has Goes to ▾: The details (today) · Reply. With Reply it sits at the foot of the cover, small spaced caps + ⌄, enters last with the cover's entrance, after the unveil. Hold ▶, unveil, tap it: the shipped reply sheet opens — no new screen. Switch The guest above: "You're coming ✓ · Change", "You can't make it · Change", "Replies are closed" (not pressable).

Decisions made here (the owner may overrule any)

  1. Names cannot be hidden. The names are what the cover IS: the loading screen's cover band draws them (ruling 2026-10-16), the home card and the poster read them, and "names wait for their real entrance" was ruled for them this morning. A cover with no names is a photo, not an invitation. So Names has no switch; a tap on its place says "The names are the cover — they are always shown." Builder note: today's model allows hidden on names (TEXT_FIELDS) — the sanitiser should drop it for names so the two doors agree. Every other line hides; the Logo switch is the same value as Studio's "Show the logo on Cover and QR" (owner 2026-10-15 (4)) — one fact, never two switches.
  2. A hidden line leaves the cover at once (the owner's words), and it comes back from this list — so the old "ghosted on the canvas" drawing of a hidden part (the inspector's frame B) is no longer needed on the cover: the list is where you see what is hidden. Guests never see it (hubElementHidden, the one rule).
  3. Style stays — and gets a job: the Opening. The four tabs stay on every part (owner 2026-10-10, "make Edit | Style | Background | Animate consistent"), so removing Style for the group was never on. With the veil folded into the cover, the Reveal's own tool — a strip of phone-shaped cards (None · Sheer veil ◆ …, the 2026-10-08 fault), Extras ▾ — is exactly a Style tab, already built (MakerLookPages.reveal). So Cover Scene › Style = that picker, unchanged, with "Opening" as its name. Alternative, if the owner prefers: an Opening ▾ dropdown as Animate's first row (then Style goes back to grey). The cards are why Style won: an opening is something you look at.
  4. The tile goes. No "Opens once, over the cover" picture above the cover: the opening is picked in Cover Scene › Style and named in the cover's corner ("Sheer veil ◆ opens over it"). Per-stage Shown | Hidden becomes picking None on that stage's Cover Scene — each stage that leads with the cover (Save the Date · Invitation › Welcome · The Day › Live, exactly revealStageOf) has its own. ▶ on the Cover Scene plays opening → unveil → entrance.
  5. One entrance, three beats. An Entrance sets each word's Build in (the shipped four effects + way) and its Delay. A word's Delay has three steps (None · Short 0.3 s · Long 0.8 s), so a cover entrance has at most three beats — nothing new is invented; if the owner wants more beats, Delay needs a fourth step (not this delta). Movement ◆ sets every word's speed (Quick 0.6 s · Calm 1.1 s · Cinematic 1.8 s). "Plays" is not offered on the cover: it is the first screen, it plays on arrival (after the unveil, when there is a veil).
  6. A word's own Animate wins, and says so. Picking a word shows the cover's values in its own rows with "Follows the cover's entrance". The first change makes it its own (the shipped per-element save); the group's row 4 counts the words on their own and offers one door, "Reset to the cover's entrance ›", which clears those words' own Build in (the entrance applies again). Stored how: the entrance is ONE value on the hero row's canvas (canvas.entrance), resolved at read time into each word's Build in unless the word stores its own — so nothing is written into seven elements, and a later entrance pick still reaches every word that follows.
  7. Behind the veil, nothing. With an opening on, the closed veil shows none of the words (owner, today); the unveil starts the entrance — every line, lines with no Build in taking the cover's default (the builder shipping this uses the shipped default Build in; this Animate is where the couple picks that default). ▶ tap plays that whole sequence; hold ▶ = the page as a guest, where the veil waits for a real tap or swipe.
  8. Shimmer is an Action, not a Build in — it plays while the word is on screen, looping, which is exactly what Action is for (Still · Drift today; HUB_EL_DURING). Measured on the competitor: not a glow but a metallic sheen — a gradient clipped to the text with a bright band sliding across every 5 s, always gold. Ours: the word's own ink with a lighter band of the same hue (color-mix), so a navy title shimmers navy; Gold (#835C2F → #F0D9A8) is a Band choice, offered because every theme has a gold in its palette (--gold). Reduced motion or Save-Data: no band, the plain colour — the word never becomes transparent text with no paint. Three Action choices on a text part means the shipped Still | Drift pill becomes one dropdown (the owner's rule). The cover's Shimmers ▾ is the same resolver shape as the entrance: a word's own Action wins.
  9. "Confirm attendance" is the cover's Link, pointed at Reply — not a new part. The Link already exists (link: the couple's own words, today "the day, the place, the story ↓", a scroll to #site-details). The delta is one Goes to ▾ (The details · Reply). Pointed at Reply it (a) is drawn on every cover shape, at the foot of the full-screen cover (today link is card-only), (b) enters last, after the unveil, with the cover's entrance — never over the closed veil, (c) opens the shipped reply: the RSVP sheet on a phone, the 440-px side panel on a computer (approved 2026-10-15, "Reply as a side panel"), the passcode first for a visitor without a key (2026-10-06); no new screen, and no bar appears over the cover — the line is on the cover, the bars still wait for the swipe up. It reads the guest's own state, the Reply card's three (guest-me-parts.ts): before → "Confirm attendance ⌄" · coming → "You're coming ✓ · Change" · can't → "You can't make it · Change"; replies closed → "Replies are closed", not pressable (no frame, dimmed). Default words "Confirm attendance" (the couple retypes them on the page as any Link). The one-page Invitation's Home jump mark still exists; this line is the cover's own shortcut.
