The rules

Who gets what, and when — as decided on 29 September (DECISION_LOG rows “Tickets on the Thank-you screen”, “A requester gets their QR at once”, “It is their Digital ticket, in a Request pending state”, “No email to guests”).

1A guest on their own link is already accepted

After RSVP the Thank-you shows their Digital ticket with Table and Arrive, one ticket per named plus-one, “Save all tickets”, and “Copy my link”. Blank seats read “+2 · TBA · Add name” — no ticket until there is a name.

Frame A
2A requester gets their ticket at once — marked Request pending

Same card, same file name, the guest’s name large, their own QR — but a clear “Request pending” band where the facts would be, and “Not valid at the door yet” at the foot. It never looks like a ticket that admits.

Frame B
3The same QR and link change meaning when the couple decides

Pending → “Waiting for the couple to confirm you”. Accepted → their own invitation, the full ticket, “Save to my account”. Declined → “Sorry, your request was not approved.” Nothing new is ever sent; the key they already hold does it.

Frames C · D · E
4A saved picture can’t update itself

So on acceptance the page says “You’re in! Save your Digital ticket” — the earlier picture still reads “pending” in their Photos, but its QR works, because the door checks live. The page always shows the current table and time.

Frame D
5The door scanner checks live, every time

Green: valid ticket, name, table, mark arrived. Amber: “Not confirmed yet” — jump to the Pending row. Red: “Not approved”. A screenshot of a pending or declined ticket is refused the same way. A typed name alone never admits.

Frame H
6No email to guests

The RSVP no longer asks for an email; nothing is mailed on acceptance and the guest reminder emails are off. Delivery is on screen: the ticket, “Save all tickets”, “Copy my link”. The couple can nudge with Send invite / Copy message from the Accept row.

Frames A · G2
7Inside Messenger, “Save to my account” becomes “Open in your browser”

Messenger / Facebook / Instagram block Apple and Google sign-in in their built-in browser, and there is no email link any more. One tap copies the link; the sheet says: paste it in Safari or Chrome, then tap Continue with Apple or Google.

Frame F
8Accept is one tap, and the row becomes the send

Right after Accept the same Pending row shows Send invite · Copy message (the shared invite message with their link and QR) and an Undo. Afterwards their guest card has the same buttons as every guest. Decline also gets Undo.

Frames G1 · G2
9Plain words, one word per thing

It is a Digital ticket (never “pass image” / “QR card”), the couple’s Event Hub — the only name for it, and any set of choices is one dropdown. Known loss, accepted: a guest who loses their phone and their saved ticket gets back in only through the couple re-sending their link.
