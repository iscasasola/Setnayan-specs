Nothing is dragged on a phone: tap → one dropdown → done; drag stays on desktop.

5Walking order

One list of walks: number · who · role. ↑/↓ reorder (drag on desktop). Tap a walk to open it: Walks with… picks anyone from the guest list; Trade places with… swaps. A sponsor may walk alone or with anyone; a +1 does not walk with their bringer unless you say so. Every line shows each person's own full name.

Walking together says nothing about who is a couple. Being a couple is a separate fact on the guest's partner link in Guests — never set or shown here.

7The printed seat plan — A3 landscape, the same layout

What it shows
The room to scale 20 × 30 m · same positions as 2D/3D
Every placed element stage · sign · buffet · dance floor · entrance · service
Every table with its chairs round: a ring · long: along the sides
Table number + count a filled chair = seated
Who sits where names per table, in the legend
Names go in the legend, not on the chairs: at A3 a chair is ~3 mm, too small to read. Black-on-white friendly — the gold is only the rule lines and the eyebrows. Prints from ⋯ › Share & print, next to the table signs and place cards.
