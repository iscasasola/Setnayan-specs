The admin app, simple

Owner, 2026-10-01: "how can we do this for the vendors and admin?" The admin is the owner's own console — he runs the business from his phone. Same method as the approved host app and supplier app: one Next card, lists first, four words in the bar, nothing floats over the bar. Every one of the 113 shipped admin pages keeps a home — what is not needed right now moves one tap away. One new rule for admin, from the 2026-09-30 audit: a read that failed shows "—" and "couldn't load", never "all clear". Words: supplier, never vendor.

✓What moved where

  • Work ← Today (/admin) + All work (/admin/work) + the 21 queue links of the shipped "Today" menu group (Disputes · Pax changes · Force majeure · Completions · Reviews · AI abuse · Account deletions · Celebration removals · User reports · Chat flags · Repost watch · Corrections · Integrity watch · Fraud · Approvals · Music Maker · Editorial review · Help …). Each is a row; each row opens the shipped page. Data Privacy & NPC Filing is not a queue — it goes to More › Privacy.
  • Money ← the shipped "Money" group's desk: Payments · Fees owed · Payouts · Subscriptions · Payment options · Receipts · Payment methods (our BDO/GCash). The list IS /admin/payments; the rest sit in ⋯. Pricing · Custom plans · Price bands · Discount codes · Referrals · Supplier recommendations → More › Pricing.
  • People ← the shipped "People & shops" group and /admin/accounts' five tabs (Users · Suppliers · Demo suppliers · Events · Venues) as ONE search with Filter ▾. Verify · ID documents · Partnerships · Founder seats · Gifts → ⋯. The user card, the new event page and the shop pages are unchanged underneath.
  • More ← Studio (14) · Set up/Ugat (9) · Settings (8) · Numbers (10) · Pricing (7) · Privacy (6, pulled out of the queue group and Settings because the owner is the DPO) · My account. Counts are from the shipped admin-nav-groups.tsx est.; re-measure with grep -c "label:" _components/admin-nav-groups.tsx.
  • Words: "Vendors" → Suppliers everywhere the owner can see it (audit row 43 · ~220 strings). "Vendors to verify" → Suppliers to verify; "Demo vendors" → Demo suppliers; "Vendor recommendations" → Supplier recommendations. The shipped nav keys do not change.
  • Honesty: every count on Work and Money, and every cell on an event, is one of three things — a number the read returned, — with couldn't load, or all clear only after a read that went through. The pattern to copy is in the repo (reads-are-honest.test.ts, guests-read-is-honest.test.ts, and the new event page itself).
  • Desktop shows the same four screens with more columns and the rows' panels open — never a different screen. The six-group side rail becomes the same four words with the groups under More.
What is drawn but not shipped (needs a build, in this order): the Work rows reading "—" instead of 0 when a queue fails (audit rows 5 · 33) · the Money list's "couldn't read" state (rows 1 · 2 · 14) · Record a payment received behind Money's + (§3.5) · one People search across the five Accounts tabs · the event page's face switch as a switch (it is two confirm buttons today) · the More sheet. Everything else on these screens exists on origin/main at #6213 and is only re-laid for a phone.

Owner questions

Keep the one-tap Confirm (fast), or make the Next card's button Open so the screenshot is always seen first? (Recommendation: one-tap only when Setnayan AI read the receipt and both amount and reference match; otherwise the button says Open.)
  3. Reopen a guest list stays an admin-only door, asked once (as shipped 2026-09-30). Fine on the phone, or desktop-only like the Maker's editing?
  4. Privacy as its own heading in More (Data Privacy & NPC filing · deletions · reports · Papic storage) — because you are the DPO — or keep it inside Settings as shipped?
  5. Money's + = "Record a payment received". This does not exist yet (audit §3.5). Build it with this phone work, or leave the + off until it does?
