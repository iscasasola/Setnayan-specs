Design prototype · Live Studio Control

One screen runs the whole broadcast.

Channel 1 is the controlled screen — the broadcast itself. Every camera is its own channel (CH 2, CH 3…), and tapping one puts it on Channel 1. Free users get this exact screen with one camera; the rest sit in view, locked, until ₱2,999 unlocks the event.

Tally discipline

Red means on air. Nothing else is red. The live camera carries a red edge on the monitor and in the strip — glance down, know what's broadcasting.

The one gesture

Tap a camera channel → it takes over Channel 1. No menus, no drag, no second confirm mid-show. The Ⓜ monogram overlay ships with the unlock; Split/PiP arrive in phase 2 (tagged P2).

Why dark

Broadcast surfaces are dark on purpose — the operator stands in a dim venue next to a bright monitor. The app stays cream; the control room earns the night.

Add a camera

Any phone scans this — no app, no login — and it appears in your strip.

Pay-to-unlock, in place

Free users see the whole controller — locked cameras stay visible with real previews dimmed. The upgrade sells itself in context; no separate sales page.

Cameras join by QR

Tap + Add camera → a helper scans → their phone becomes a channel. No install, no account, no login. You name every channel — the text under each tile is yours (tap ✎).

Broadcast extras

Ⓜ Monogram — tap it on the screen to move it corner to corner. Lower third — a news-style info bar. Event QR — show the scan-to-join code right on the broadcast. All ride the ₱2,999 unlock. ⚡ while live marks a highlight moment for the after-event reel.

Free streams carry the brand

On Free, the lower third is a permanent "Powered by SETNAYAN" bar — every free wedding stream advertises us. Unlocking replaces it with the couple's own lower third.

Guest-pick

One switch. On = guests may leave Channel 1 for any camera channel and come back. Off = everyone watches your cut.

Vs Switcher Studio

Their $65/month buys the switching. It doesn't buy guest-pick, QR joins, or a one-time ₱2,999 that ends when the event does.
