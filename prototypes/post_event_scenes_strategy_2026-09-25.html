What this is

A prototype of the Post Event stage as many small scenes — the owner's ruling of 2026-09-25 — on top of what shipped in Maker Phase 8 (lib/post-event-scenes.ts). Nothing here is the product. Full strategy: POST_EVENT_SCENES_STRATEGY_2026-09-25.md.

The default scenes, in order

  1. The cover — your hero, names, date, the edition.
  2. Before the day — the Love Story timeline (true before the day).
  3. By the numbers — invited · attending · captures · wishes.
  4. As the day unfolded — one scene per chapter, from the schedule, filled by the camera.
  5. Open-ups — Gallery · Watch the Film · Were you there? · What they whispered. A preview in the flow; a tap opens full screen; Back returns.
  6. Voices — What we asked · Letters to the editor.
  7. The team — From your vendors · Powered by Setnayan · Vendors we loved (Live Photo Wall and What they said when their source has something).
  8. Your own — up to six, from the Post Event presets (Pro).
  9. The close — From the couple · Their song · What comes next.

Before the day

Every scene says what is already true (the Love Story, the schedule's chapters, invited/said-yes, the booked team, the questions, the letter) and names what arrives — never an empty box. Only the couple sees this state; guests are on the Invitation until the day.

The Event Bar — recommendation (c)

RecapFilmVendorsGalleryMe

Not (a) no bar: ~20 scenes is ~25 screens on a phone; a guest returning in March for the photo of me scrolls it all. It would also delete shipped behaviour — the bar already says Recap after the day — and drop the stranger's Join.

Not (b) Recap · Gallery · Vendors alone: it drops Me, the guest's own door (Were you there?), and Film.

(c) keeps the one five-slot bar every guest already learned — slots empty when there is nothing honest to put in them (Film only with a replay; Vendors only with a team scene). Reading moves; the bar stays still.

RecapFilmVendorsGalleryMe

A typical event draws four. Slot 5 reads Me for a guest, Join for a stranger, Manage for the couple.

The twelve presets ("+" on Post Event only)

Thank You, From Us · The Toast · Best Of · Before & After · Behind the Scenes · What Almost Happened · By Our Count · From Near and Far · Our Playlist · The Guestbook · Wish You Were Here · Since Then.

Each is a named start on one of the 25 templates with its fields named for the day — not a 26th template. Pro (padlock); try in the draft, pay at Apply. Switch the Tier above to Pro to see the diamond.

Free · Pro

Free: the whole auto story, editing every scene's words, show/hide/order, a background colour, Classic. Pro: the other nine themes, template swap, own photos and clips in a scene, own scenes, motion, music.

Try it

  • Maker: tap + to open and close the preset sheet.
  • Guest page: Gallery, Film and Me open layers; switch Reader to see the gallery's tabs and slot 5 change; Stranger removes Were you there?; Couple shows Edit on every scene.
  • Everything is CSS state — no script runs.
