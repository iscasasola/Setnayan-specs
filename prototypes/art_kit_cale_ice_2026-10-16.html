The pieces, alone (as cut)

The pieces, and how they were cut

The owner generated the kit in Higgsfield from prompts A1–A8, each on white with one contact shadow. All eight arrived today; the originals are untouched in controller-2026-10-12/art-kit-hf/. They meet the bar the references set: photoreal, studio-lit, ivory and brass and narra, and each overlaps a card edge here.

  • The cut (src/matte.py, ImageMagick): a small-fuzz flood from the edges keeps the object and the faint shadow core opaque; a difference-from-ground soft alpha, with a noise floor, carries the shadow halo; the two are joined and the piece trimmed, resized to 2× its slot and encoded WebP q78 / AVIF q50. Per piece: A2's grey ground takes a higher floor; A3's white card is seeded from inside and the mask opened so only the flowers stay; A5 is keyed on warmth (ivory against neutral grey) and its centre opened for the photo; A6 is recoloured by a per-channel multiply inside the mask, so the gloss scales with the wax.
  • Where the cut falls short: white petal highlights that touch the ground go slightly translucent (they read as cream on the cream page, which is why it passes); the shadow halo keeps the generator's neutral grey rather than a warm one; the seal's highlights went tan instead of staying white. A dedicated matte (or the generator's own transparent export, if it offers one) would clear all three.
  • Blender (blender/kit_scenes.py) is now a fallback, kept for a rosewood satin drape if the owner wants the curtain in the palette rather than ivory.

What the owner is deciding

  1. The drape colour: the generated curtain is ivory with a gold tassel (shown); the palette has it in rosewood. Ivory reads as the church; rosewood reads as the couple.
  2. The araña hangs into the cover (shown) — or above "A note from us", in the gap the page already has there. The abaniko (A8) lies over the e-gifts card's lower-right corner (shown).
  3. The shimmer's default band on Vintage: the title's own ink (shown) or gold — a Band choice either way (sibling prototype).
  4. The closing photo: whose picture, and may it go black and white by default?

Where it hooks in — nothing new where something exists

  • The registry: lib/invite-themes.ts already carries ornament: 'lace-postmark' on Vintage (read today only by find-seat/page.tsx for the postmark date). The kit is that key's full meaning: ART_KITS[ornament] → eight pieces, each with its slot id, 2× size, motion and bytes. The theme is the only switch; the couple arranges nothing (FREE very easy). Whether a kit is Pro rides the theme's tier as everything else does.
  • Drawing: one component, <KitPiece slot="…">, mounted at eight fixed places — the veil and the masthead (pahina-masthead.tsx: drape pair, seal), the countdown card (countdown.tsx, sceneCardClass), the section eyebrow rule, the E-gifts card, the reply card (rsvp-sheet.tsx's opener), the sign-off (invitation-shell.tsx, data-shell-sign-off: today "See you soon." + "Powered by Setnayan · setnayan.com", hideWatermark kept as is). Decorative: aria-hidden, pointer-events:none, except the seal, which is the veil's existing tap.
  • Motion: Sway and Float are two keyframes next to the shipped effect layer (lib/ambient-effects.ts already has sn-fx-sway for petals and a prefers-reduced-motion rule that names every animation). Shimmer is the sibling's: a new HUB_EL_DURING value in lib/element-style.ts beside HUB_EL_DURING_LABEL (Drift · Still · Ken Burns · Parallax), set from Cover Scene › Animate › Shimmers ▾ or a word's Action; Vintage's kit asks for it on names by default the way Luxe and Gatsby ask for foil (foilNames).
  • Look › Elements · Animate › Action: untouched. A kit piece is not an element the couple styles; the words it sits beside keep their own Edit · Style · Animate.
  • Closing: the band is the sign-off restyled, not a new section: photo (the couple's chosen picture, hero's or a new closing_photo — owner's call), oval piece, "See you soon.", names in the Event title face, the date, the Powered-by line. Contrast measured: cream on Narra Brown 7.6:1.
  • Files: public/art-kits/vintage/*.avif|webp (or the R2 theme prefix next to the loops), each ≤ 80 KB; a guard that sums a kit and fails above 600 KB, the way loopMb is held by theme-order.test.ts.

What a builder touches

  • Small add: ART_KITS in lib/invite-themes.ts (typed by slot) · KitPiece + eight mounts · two keyframes + the reduced-motion line · the sign-off's band · the seal on the veil's tap.
  • New code: none beyond those; Shimmer belongs to the sibling's build.
  • Not now: no per-couple arranging, no uploads of own art, no new emails, no per-piece settings. One theme first (Vintage, on cale-ice); the other six Pro themes each get a kit only after this one is seen live.
