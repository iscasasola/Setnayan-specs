One · the controller

Three states, one page — only the stage and the next step change

The order never moves: stage, four facts, one next step, the parts, set-once, money, offers last. Before the day the stage is the page they are still building; on the day it is the page their guests are holding; after, it is the story.

Two · the laptop

The same controller at 1080 — the stage spans, the work splits under it

Nothing is added for the wider screen and nothing is hidden on the narrow one. The stage grows to the full column and its facts go four across instead of two-by-two; underneath, the single phone column becomes two — what to do now on the left, what is set once on the right. And the rail is where the menu ruling becomes visible: one row, Event Hub, in the section that already exists for it.

Two rules this switch must obey
Privacy

“As Ana Reyes” is a real person’s page

The named read is dashed on purpose — it renders one guest’s personal view to the host. Almost certainly fine, since the host issued her QR. It is still a privacy surface, and it is the owner’s call as DPO, not engineering’s.

Safety

Preview must go through the host check

A previous resolver read any membership row as “host”, and a guest could open a private site and jump to unlaunched stages. This switch ships that override to five roles instead of one. Every path goes through isHostMemberType, tested with a guest row.

Four · upgrades

One unlock, offered seven times — never a shop tab

The cinematic reveal, the save-the-date video, the photo gallery, background music, editorial editing, background colour, button colour: seven Pro items, one ₱3,500 unlock — COUPLE_WEBSITE_PRO, titled Event Hub Pro in the live catalog. So the controller does not grow seven upgrade slots. It offers the one unlock on whichever channel the couple is standing on when they meet the wall.

What moves, what is added, what is deleted
