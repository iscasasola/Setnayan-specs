3Date — the ceremony time, typed in place

  • One new line under the date: Ceremony time. It is the Schedule’s Ceremony start, the very value the invitation prints as Ceremony at 3:00 PM. One source, two doors — changing it here changes the Schedule.
  • The phone’s own time picker (the ◷ field), no custom control.
  • Waits for Apply like the date above it; the preview above shows the new time at once.
  • Everything else on this item is unchanged: the Your date ▾ dropdown (I have a date · Help me choose), the booked-supplier check on the day.

Owner questions — recommended answer first

1City or area: one for the event, or one per venue card?

Recommend: one, the reception’s, as today — it is what the Save-the-Date prints and what supplier matching uses. When the couple drops the reception pin, the dropdown pre-fills with the nearest city on the list (by km); they can change it.

The other way: a city under Ceremony and another under Reception. Two more taps for every couple, and the Save-the-Date and supplier search still have to pick one.

2No ceremony in the Schedule yet — does typing a time here create it?

Recommend: yes. On Apply, a “Ceremony” block is made on the event date at that time if none exists; if one exists, its time moves. If the date later moves, the ceremony moves with it (it already does in the Schedule).

The other way: show the line only when the Schedule already has a ceremony. Then a brand-new couple sees nothing here and the invitation prints no time until they find the Schedule.

3Reception time too?

Recommend: not in this build — the invitation prints only the ceremony time on its face; the reception time is on the Finer Details card and in the Schedule. Add it later as one more line if couples ask.

The other way: a second line, Reception time, right under the ceremony’s, same door. Cheap, but the Date item grows past what the invitation shows.
