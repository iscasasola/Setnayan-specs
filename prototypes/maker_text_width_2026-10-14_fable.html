What's new — the answers to the four questions

1 · Does Colour · Size · Width fit one row at 375? Yes, as drawn — with two decisions. The row has 355 px inside the toolbar's inset. Three written words + a circle + a slider + its value + a labelled pill leaves the slider about 30 px (press "All three words" above and read the measurement under the phone). So: the word "Colour" is dropped — the circle is the approved colour-circle kind and needs no caption, exactly as the Background rows draw it — and Width is the house two-line dropdown at its short size (the word WIDTH sits inside the pill, above the value). That leaves the Size bar about 125 px: ten steps, a 24 px thumb, usable. The owner's row stays one row; nothing moves to a second one.

2 · What Width means. The widest a part's words may run, as a share of the stage inside its padding: Narrow 60 % · Normal the theme's own (stored as nothing, like Size 100) · Wide 90 % · Full 100 %, to the padding, never the edge. Each has a cap in characters so a desktop stays readable: Narrow 32ch · Wide 60ch · Full 76ch (max-width: min(90%, 60ch)). Framed and Full-width stages behave the same — the share is measured inside whichever box the stage is. The box sits where the part's alignment puts it (centre → centred; left → to the left); Size and Width are independent — bigger words in a Narrow box wrap sooner, which is the point.

3 · Which parts take Width. Every part whose words can run to more than one line. A part that does not take it draws no Width control — the same rule as the mark drawing no Colour today.

Control map (INTERACTION_RULES § 9 — every control is one of the kinds)

  • Colour circle → Colour circle (the approved kind 21), opens the one ColourSheet — unchanged.
  • Size → the app Slider with its value beside it — unchanged.
  • Width → Dropdown (PickMenu, short size); a ▾ opens its choices as a sheet from the bottom on a phone, a list on desktop. Never cycles on tap.
  • A part on the stage → a tap only selects it (the Maker rule); the row then shows that part's colour, size and width.
  • Light | Dark · Framed | Full width · Row words → prototype-only harness pills (2-way Pill selector), not in the build.
