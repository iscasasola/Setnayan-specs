Who can see your story

Every celebration gets a story. The people whose celebration it is decide who may read it — and the setting has to mean the same thing everywhere: on the page, on the stories shelf, and in Google.

Ships already built — drawn for context, never rebuilt New the delta

1 · The choice

This screen already exists with the first three options. The fourth is the new one. Wording is what a person reads — not the words the database uses.

setnayan.com/dashboard/…/website/privacy
The host

Who can see this celebration?

You can change this at any time. Changing it takes effect straight away.

Four options, not three. The new one sits between “anyone with the link” and “only me”, because that is exactly where it falls: narrower than a link, wider than yourself. Its own description states the honest consequence rather than leaving the host to discover it — a setting that quietly shows the story to nobody would read as broken.

Today this admits nobody — and that is the rule working, not failing.

Across every celebration in Setnayan right now, no guest has an email written down. So this setting currently means “only me”, exactly as you put it: no tagged account means it is private for them. It fills in on its own as hosts add emails and guests sign up — there is no switch to remember to flip.

One thing worth deciding later.

Guests are matched by email. If a host writes down the wrong email, the story opens for whoever owns that address. Names and phone numbers have the same weakness in reverse. It is the same trust the invitation itself already relies on — but it is the seam to watch.
