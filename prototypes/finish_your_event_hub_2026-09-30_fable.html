Finish your Event Hub — five quick answers

Opens by itself once, the first time the host enters a newly created event. One question per card, one control, and an answer that is never work — "Not decided yet", "Use a theme picture for now" and "I'll add them later" all count as answers. No Skip, no Later, no confirm dialogs; every card says You can change this anytime. When the five essentials have real answers: a toast, Your Event Hub is ready ✓. Sample: a birthday — Birthday Salubong ni Ate · Saturday, November 14, 2026 · 6:00 PM. 3Who can reply?

  • Two answers, one line each: Only people on my list — Names you add. Nobody else. · Anyone, I approve — Anyone with the link can ask. You say yes. Tapping one answers and moves on.
  • Drawn with ⓘ open (the filled gold circle): a small gold note under the line, nothing more. Tap ⓘ again to close it.
  • This is the same setting as the Guest list's first-visit pop-up — answering here answers there, and the other way round.
  • No default is pre-picked here: the rule changes who gets in, so the host must choose one.

6Five more, as one list

  • Rises right after the toast, once, as one sheet — not five cards. Each row: title, one line, its control. Every row needs an answer; none is work.
  • Logo — Yes, make one opens the Logo Maker and comes back; No, use the name writes the event name where the logo would sit. (A wedding reads No, use our names — EventWords.)
  • Pick a look — one dropdown, the default look pre-selected is the answer; frame 6b shows it open.
  • What to ask guests — the defaults are shown in the line; Change opens the questions right here (no "edit elsewhere" link).
  • Papic and Gifts — Yes / No. A Yes on Papic turns tagging on with it; a Yes on Gifts asks for GCash or bank details on the next line, in place.
  • Done closes the list. Nothing here blocks the toast or the hub.
