Three things I left for you to rule on

All three are about the shape of a placeholder, not the retired title. Each is a design call rather than a defect, so I stopped and drew them instead of deciding.

The invitation doors

A guest opening an invitation link gets a wide full-width form shape, then lands on a small centred paper card. The placeholder is honest about the title and wrong about the shape.

Redraw the door placeholders?

The Studio buy pages

These open with a large display heading. The placeholder reserves a bar about a fifth of its height, so the page still shifts — the opposite mistake, and a smaller one.

Match the hero, or leave it?

The supplier’s shared shape

Eighteen supplier screens borrow the shop home placeholder, which paints a briefing strip and a tile grid they don’t have. Their titles are right; their bodies aren’t.

Worth its own pass?

How honest this page is. The placeholder shapes are rebuilt here from the real code, and the jump numbers are measured by your own browser as the page runs — not copied from my notes. What it is not is a photograph of the running app: these screens sit behind a login, so nobody has yet opened one on a real phone. If anything here looks wrong to you, trust the phone over the prototype.
