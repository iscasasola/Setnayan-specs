How to read this. Each scene is one section. A is the shipped look, redrawn from its component (named under the heading); B and C are the gap the owner asked for. Every style shows the SAME data the scene already holds — a style never adds a word or asks for a new field. A countdown still counts down; the RSVP still replies; the seat map still walks you there. Picking a style is free (no ◆); it is one dropdown in the inspector (see Maker), stored per scene like canvas.template. Words follow the event type: “Until we say ‘I do’” is the wedding line; every other event reads “Until the day” (EventWords).
