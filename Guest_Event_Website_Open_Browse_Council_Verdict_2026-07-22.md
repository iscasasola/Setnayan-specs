# Guest Event Website — Open-Browse Redesign · Council Verdict (2026-07-22)

> **Scribe note:** Four architects (browse-first · manageability · engineering-safety · moments-preserved), three critiques each, three judges. Panel split 1–1–1 on winners; combined scores: **The Open House 22.5 · Overture & Program 22.5 · The Program Board 19 · The Anchored Monolith 16.5**. The tie resolves cleanly because the two leaders are complementary, not competing: Open House supplies the *structure*, Overture supplies the *phase machinery*. Every judge's graft list converged on the same ~10 fixes; they are absorbed below as requirements, not suggestions.

## 0 · Verdict

**Adopt The Open House as the base architecture — a five-tab menu, identical for every visitor, where identity fills personalization slots but never unlocks navigation — with Overture & Program's phase-emphasis engine (degraded terminal states, STD seen-flag, moment front-doors) absorbed wholesale, the Anchored Monolith's engineering discipline (per-event `website_open_browse DEFAULT false`, the exported+tested public allow-list firewall, CI editorial/PII regression checks) as hard gates, and the Program Board's couple-side inventions (three-state `mode` column with an explicit `is_visible` backfill rule, auto-populate status chips) as the manager's spine.** Phases flip from gates to spotlights: everything the couple has published exists from day one behind a persistent menu; the lifecycle phase decides only what Home leads with. The Save-the-Date film and the date-gated editorial ending survive as *moments*; the personal QR becomes reachable in every phase (fixing the live `WIDGET_PHASES` defect that strips `qr_card` in `save_the_date`/`editorial`); the no-QR public site fully works; and the couple gets one mirror-of-the-menu manager replacing today's nine-surface scatter. **Nothing flips until the privacy-hardening PR and the couple manager ship — both sequencing locks are load-bearing.**

### 1.2 Identity model — three tiers, one structure; QR personalizes, never unlocks

1. **No-QR public — the site fully works.** Full browse of Home/Details/Story/Photos + find-mode Me. ### 1.3 Phase-as-emphasis — gates become spotlights, with degraded terminal states (Overture graft)

`getLifecyclePhase` untouched (null-date and inactive-disambiguation edge cases owner-locked). `WIDGET_PHASES` is superseded by **`WIDGET_SPOTLIGHT`**: same four `LifecyclePhase` values, but the matrix yields an ordering weight + Home spotlight pick + **per-widget degraded-state renderer** — never a render boolean. Time-bound widgets get explicit terminal states instead of blanket-open (the finding that sank the Monolith):

- **RSVP** → status-aware: open form pre-event (stays open through the live window — day-of confirmations are real PH behavior); after close, responders get "You were marked attending", non-responders get a quiet closed card. Never "you responded" shown to a non-responder.
- **Countdown** → "Married [date]" stamp post-event; hidden on null date.
- **`what_to_bring` / `photo_moments`** → retired to archive-tense post-event.
- **Home spotlight is identity-aware** (graft): anonymous rsvp-phase spotlight reads "Invited? Find your invite" → find-mode, never an RSVP imperative into a tab with no form.

Per phase: **save_the_date** — the film keeps its full-bleed first-visit moment (`InvitationShell` fullBleed, `RevealOverlayServer`, `StdViewBeacon`, music suppression preserved verbatim; `ownsStdReveal` ₱999 keeps gating only premium film beats), ends on "Explore the site"; thereafter a **seen-flag** (localStorage + cookie mirror) makes it Home's "Watch the reveal" spotlight, and **any `#fragment`/deep-link arrival suppresses the film** (graft — shared section links must not be hijacked); filmless/null-date events bypass the overture to plain Home. **rsvp** — Home spotlights the RSVP card deep-linking Me. **event** — Home spotlights Watch Live; hub chip appears; pinned schedule in Details; no-live-content fallback leads with Venue/directions + a photos promise. **editorial** — see §2. `?phase=` preview keeps working for demo events + hosts. A shared **`hasContent()` predicate per widget** (graft — `widgetShouldRender` checks only `is_visible`/`is_always_on`, never emptiness; the critiques proved auto-hide doesn't exist) is consumed by both the menu and the renderer so they can never disagree.

### 1.4 Couple management — the mirror (with Program Board grafts)

`/dashboard/[eventId]/website` becomes a **mirror of the guest menu**: five section rows in the guest's exact order, replacing the nine scattered surfaces. Per row: live-preview thumbnail; filled/empty status with **auto-populate chips** ("Schedule · 6 public blocks · edited in Schedule", "Photos · fills from Papic on the day" — teaches couples the site fills itself from work they already did); a **three-state control** — Auto / Shown / Hidden — stored as `invitation_widgets.mode` (`'auto'|'shown'|'hidden'`), **with the explicit backfill rule `is_visible=false → 'hidden'`** so the flip never un-hides a couple's deliberate choice, and **Shown disabled while the source is empty** (with the "Add from →" deep-link) so force-on can never manufacture a blank guest-facing section; deep-links to ALL existing editors (widgets, dress-code, photo-moments, special-message, what-to-bring, our-photos, editorial, STD studio, schedule, mood-board, monogram). Home and Me are never holdable. The 16-widget list demotes to "Advanced". The board header consolidates Launch (single `publishSaveTheDate` path), Privacy/visibility, Appearance, and the per-event **"Open browsing" toggle**. Consolidate wayfinding first, editors later.

**Hygiene folded in:** one `requireHostMembership` helper in `lib/host-gate.ts` (`requireCouple` stays only on launch + STD studio — the inconsistency is *surfaced*, not silently resolved, §5); one `revalidateGuestSite(slug)` helper; single-writer rule per `events` column — hero image keeps `hero-photo/actions.ts` as sole writer, site-chrome's unconditional `r2RefOrNull` null-clobber of `landing_page_hero_video_r2_key` (site-chrome/actions.ts:73) fixed by omit-when-untouched, site-chrome declared sole site-music owner; `/site-editor/[eventId]`'s duplicate `saveHeroPhoto`/`clearHeroPhoto` deleted in favor of the canonical actions.

## 4 · REJECTED alternatives

**The Program Board (manageability lens) — REJECTED as base; two grafts taken.** The 9-section registry driving both surfaces is the correct *end-state idea* (one judge scored it 8), but three structural findings survived critique unresolved: (1) the anchors-menu vs verbatim-body-swap contradiction — after T+8h the widget sections are not in the DOM, so every menu item except The Story is a dead anchor on the terminal artifact, and the design never picked a resolution; (2) `mode` vs `is_visible` interaction unspecified — the flip would silently un-hide sections couples deliberately hid; (3) it promoted the verified no-OTP fuzzy-claim flow to the PRIMARY public RSVP behavior with only "watch the queue" as mitigation — the closest-to-fatal finding in the dossier. Its identity-blind `hasContent` also collapses sparse events to a 2-item menu, and its duplicate no-QR doorways ("Find your invite" + "Get your invite") confuse the exact visitor they exist to convert. **Grafts absorbed:** the three-state `mode` column (with the backfill rule its own critique demanded) and the auto-populate status chips.

**The Anchored Monolith (engineering-safety lens) — REJECTED as base; three grafts taken.** Its central thesis — "open everything is a one-predicate change" — was disproven in code: the body is a hard 3-way ternary at four sites (page.tsx:1993-2031, 2713-2730), so `widgetInPhase` returning true changes *nothing* in the two phases that matter, and the fullBleed-drop-after-film mechanism is undesigned server/client machinery. Blanket-open with no degraded states permanently serves a live RSVP form, registry instructions, and camera prompts under the editorial. Weakest on the owner's #1 criterion: management stays a link index over the existing scatter. **Grafts absorbed:** the per-event `DEFAULT false` column honoring the auto-apply-migrations trap (replacing a global env flip as the real rollback), the allow-list-as-exported-tested-firewall + CI anonymous-bytes check, and the `?phase=editorial` CI regression gate.

**Overture & Program (moments-preserved lens) — NOT adopted as base; its machinery absorbed wholesale.** Highest single judge score (8, best migration sequencing; only "minor" fact-check verdict in the dossier) and the council's best missing piece — `WIDGET_EMPHASIS` degraded terminal states. Rejected as the *structure* because: ~13 suppression-ruled menu entries churn as the couple works (vs 5 stable tabs — orientation is the guest's first need); the dual-chrome problem (GuestHubBar + Program menu with overlapping destinations) was left unresolved; its safety premise ("widgetShouldRender auto-hides empties") is false as verified — `widgetShouldRender` (invitation-widgets.ts:298-302) never checks content, leaving unbudgeted work; the null-date sparse event is trapped forever in a fullscreen reveal of nothing; and its two env flags were secretly coupled (menu without emphasis = dead anchors in 2 of 4 phases). Its motivating bug was also overstated — GuestHubBar already serves the QR modal in all phases; the `WIDGET_PHASES` strip is real but the guest never fully loses the QR today. **Absorbed:** degraded terminal states, the STD seen-flag + deep-link suppression + replay entry, status-aware RSVP, the manager-before-flip sequencing rule, and the flags-collapse-to-one-switch discipline.

**Also rejected in passing:** route-per-section and `?view=` modes (re-pay the resolution block / fork the body a third time); a global env-only flip (reshapes in-flight weddings without consent); rewriting `/hub` into the page (locked precedent); deleting `WIDGET_PHASES` before the flip (breaks flag-off prod); treating "type system guarantees no PII" as a control (types erase at runtime — the fetch-time split + CI assertion is the control).

## 6 · Privacy / DPO notes (RA 10173)

**Mandatory DPO sign-off gates, attached to PR5 and pre-flip:**

- **Guest-roster enumeration oracle:** `/api/seat-lookup/[slug]` returns `{display_name, table_label}` to any anonymous caller once a plan is published; its only throttle is a per-instance in-memory Map that resets on cold start — a prefix sweep dumps the roster. The menu converts this from unlinked to one-tap. Durable rate limiting + no other-guest name echo + couple toggle + date-window gate before promotion. Guests consented to attend a wedding, not to a public directory.
- **Identity takeover via name-claim:** `/[slug]/invite` auto-resolves the event's universal join token server-side for any visitor; a confident fuzzy match (0.86) hands out `setGuestSession` with no OTP (verification was owner-retired for the *shared-link* context). Promotion to a public menu button requires re-adding a possession/verification factor. Also: success/failure is a membership oracle — non-confirming responses required.
- **Bearer tokens in URLs:** `?invite=<qr_token>` and `/seat/claim?t=` put permanent, non-expiring credentials into history, logs, and link previews; the redesign extends the QR's display surface to every phase forever. Rotation/revocation (§5.11) + log-scrubbing + no tokens in any NEW URLs the menu mints.
- **SPI-adjacent fields:** `meal_preference`/`dietary_restrictions` (health/religion signals) and couple-authored `notes` are selected for the guest render (page.tsx:997) — strip from anything guest-facing; they are host-ops data.
- **Live broadcast consent:** anonymous Watch/Live-Wall means strangers watch identifiable guests (including minors; multi-event-type platform) in real time; verify the face-blur opt-out pipeline runs on live surfaces; consent text review.
