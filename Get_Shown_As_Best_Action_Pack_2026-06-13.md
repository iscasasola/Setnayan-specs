## PART 2 — STEP-BY-STEP (written for a first-timer)

### ✅ Task 1 — Google Business Profile (≈15 min) — DO THIS FIRST

This is the single highest-leverage "now" move: it anchors your brand on Google and becomes where couples leave the reviews that AI reads.

1. On your computer, go to **google.com/business**.
2. Click the blue **"Manage now"** button (top right).
3. Sign in with the Google account you want to own the business (use a business-y one, e.g. your Setnayan/Gmail account — *not* a throwaway).
4. In **"Business name,"** type `Setnayan` → Next.
5. **"What kind of business?"** → choose **"Service business"** (you serve customers online, no storefront).
6. **"Business category"** → type and pick **`Wedding Service`** → Next.
7. **"Where do you serve customers?"** → add **`Philippines`** (and key cities: Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, Tagaytay) → Next.
8. **Contact info** → website `https://www.setnayan.com`, your business phone, email → Next.
9. **Verification:** Google will offer a method — phone call/text, email, or video. Pick **phone or email** if offered (fastest). Follow the code it sends you. *(If it only offers postcard and asks for a registered business address, pause and tell me — we may need your DTI registration first; flag it and we'll plan around it.)*
10. Once verified: open **"Edit profile" → "About,"** paste the **Description** from Part 1.A, add the additional categories (`Software Company`, `Wedding Planner`), and upload your logo + a couple of brand images.
11. Done. Later, ask happy pilot couples to leave a Google review here — those stars are the #1 "best" signal AI surfaces.
