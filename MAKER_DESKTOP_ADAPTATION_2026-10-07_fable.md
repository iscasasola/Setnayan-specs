## What changes at ≥ 1280 — per screen

**Top nav** — the same four controls, every word shown (✕ Exit · Stages | Studio · ↺ Undo · ✓ Apply with its count), the event's name in the middle, and **Preview ▾ Phone · Desktop** (the shipped desktop's 👁 Preview, as the one dropdown) while the page is on screen. Hidden inside a full-screen Studio tool, exactly as on the phone.

**Stages — three columns.**
- **LEFT (264 px) — the parts of this page.** Stage ▾ on top (the same five-stage list; it opens as a pop-over under the button), then this stage's pages as rows (Welcome · Details · Our Love Story · Me, with the passcode lock where it applies), then every part of the page in order, with its source as a small caption (Studio · Info · Suppliers). Click a row = the same as tapping the part on the page: it is picked and centred. The picked row carries **＋ Add above · grip (mouse drag to reorder) · 🗑 · ＋ Add below** — the same four the page shows on the part. The **Reveal is locked first**: a lock, no grip, no ＋ above, no 🗑 (hide it in Arrange). A hidden part reads dimmed. Phone: this list is the strip in the lower third when a tool is tapped with nothing picked — same rows, laid out tall.
- **CENTRE — the page**, as a guest sees it, on a guest's phone (390 px) or a guest's desktop (Preview ▾), centred with its "You're editing · Invitation › Welcome" caption and the guests' own tab bar at its foot. The picked part is centred (`centrePicked`, as on the phone); ＋ above/below, grip and 🗑 sit on it as on the phone; the words type in place. A real keyboard: Enter or a click elsewhere ends typing — the tools stay (the phone slides them away for its on-screen keyboard).
- **RIGHT (384 px) — the dedicated tools column** = the phone's lower third with room, full height. Row 1: **Style · Text · Animate** as tabs with their words, ▶ Play. Then the picked part's caption (COUNTDOWN · 7 of 8) and **‹ Previous · Next › · ✕ Done** (the phone swipes the row; a mouse clicks). Then the tool:
  - **Style** — the **Look | Background | Arrange** bar stays, but the three sections are **stacked underneath** (Look, then BACKGROUND, then ARRANGE, each the phone's own rows at 44 px); the bar jumps to a section instead of switching. The layout carousel becomes a **2-column grid of real previews**. Nothing picked: one hint row ("Tap a part of the page — Style will act on it").
  - **Text** — Font · Colour · Size, unchanged.
  - **Animate** — **Build in | Action | Build out** bar, the three phases stacked under it (How it moves ▾ once at the top; Fade · Blur · Move · Size toggles with the direction buttons; Duration · Delay; Does ▾ · Timing ▾; Build out's toggles), "Into the next scene ▾" once at the bottom. The column scrolls when a phase set does not fit at 800 px tall; at 900 it fits.
- **Sheets** — the stage list, Preview, every dropdown: a **pop-over beside its button** (anchored, 400 px, opens downward or upward to fit); the tools column's own sheet (fonts, layouts, backgrounds) slides up inside that column; a confirm is a centred dialog. Nothing opens from the bottom of the screen.
- **Mouse** — hover on every row, pill, tile, swatch and page part (a soft gold ring); the grip drags with the mouse on the page and in the list; nothing needs a swipe.

**Studio.** The home: the same eleven tiles in **four columns** (max 1100 px, centred), same words and ✓ / Missing. A tool: the same full screen; the form **in one comfortable column (760 px)** for Info · Love Story · Schedule · E-Gifts · RSVP · Prints; **Mood Board · Logo · Wedding March · Seat plan use the width (1100 px)**; the tool's own bottom (the march's Not-walking tray) stays at the bottom. Wedding March and Seat plan still hide the top nav; ✓ Done returns. **Studio › Look** keeps the page beside its controls: two columns (no parts column — there are no parts), the four tabs Background · Colours · Fonts · Music in the column's first row, the background previews as a 2-column grid.

## Build plan for an Opus builder — PR-sized, no new features (read `apps/web/app/dashboard/[eventId]/launch/_components/` on origin/main first; RULE 0)
The shipped new Maker is phone-only by one gate: `maker-shell.tsx` `const ss = stagesStudio && phone` (and `MakerLowerThird` / `lowerThird: phone && !studioCovers`). A desktop today draws the OLD Maker. Each PR below keeps the phone pixel-identical (`one-panel-at-a-time-on-a-phone.test.ts` and the 2026-10-07 side-by-side fixes stay green).
- **PR 1 — the shell adapts.** `maker-shell.tsx`: `ss = stagesStudio` on every width; at `lg`+ lay the work area out as grid columns (parts · page · tools) via `use-responsive.ts` (`lg` 1024 = two columns, `xl` 1280 = three); `MAKER_TOOLBAR_STAGES_STUDIO` (`maker-bar.ts`) shows words at `lg`+ and keeps `previewTool` as Preview ▾ with `makerViewOptions` minus Both; `lib/maker-phone-room.ts` / `maker-lt-size.ts` heights apply only below `lg`. The old desktop Maker's navigator / inspector branches are no longer mounted when the flag is on.
- **PR 2 — the tools column.** `maker-lower-third.tsx` + `stage-tools.tsx`: at `lg`+ the lower third renders as a full-height column (no grab handle, no resting height), `StageTools` row 1 shows the tab words, adds the picked-part caption and Previous · Next · Done (the existing ‹ › step), and the stage ▾ only at `lg`–`xl`. `maker-sheet.tsx` (the one `PickSheet`) renders as an anchored pop-over at `lg`+ (same rows), the confirm as a centred dialog; `stage-item-menu.tsx` opens under the stage ▾.
- **PR 3 — stacked sections + grid of previews.** `website/editor/_components/part-inspector.tsx` and `element-sheet.tsx`: at `lg`+ render Look · Background · Arrange (and Build in · Action · Build out) stacked under their segmented bar, the bar scrolling to a section; `scene-style-row.tsx` `[data-style-carousel]` as a 2-column grid at `lg`+. Guarded by a test that the phone still renders one phase at a time.
- **PR 4 — the parts column.** New `stage-parts-column.tsx` built from the existing strip data (`lib/maker-stage-room.ts` parts, `lib/maker-navigator-tabs.ts` pages, `lib/maker-scene-list.ts` order): rows, source captions, pick → the existing `pickPart`, ＋ / grip / 🗑 → the existing `PartEdits` actions (`add-part-sheet.tsx`), drag via the existing reorder action; Reveal rows locked (`lib/maker-parts.ts`). Mounted only at `xl`+. Retire the old `maker-navigator-data.ts` desktop column when the flag is on (the three `*-navigator*.test.ts` in `lib/` move to this component).
- **PR 5 — Studio widths.** `studio-home.tsx` four columns at `lg`+; `studio-tools.tsx` / `details-workspace.tsx` one 760-px column with a per-tool 1100-px allowance for Mood Board · Logo · Wedding March · Seat plan; Look's four-tab bar in the column's first row with the preview grid (`details-look-pages.tsx`).
- **PR 6 — the check card + the flag.** Walk every screenshot state live at 1280 and 375 before the owner looks (memory: walk the whole flow first); then widen `makerStagesStudioEnabled`'s desktop gate.
