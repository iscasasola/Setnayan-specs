# Creator Economy — The Simplest Best Approach (Council Verdict)

## 1. The approach in one paragraph

Ship the six-rule loop in exactly **two in-flight PRs + one paper session + two new PRs**, and cut everything that isn't the loop. The loop is: a vendor spends one token to offer a storyteller a promo → the creator accepts and publishes a chapter crediting the vendor for free → a viewer books through that chapter and the vendor's promo rides the existing inquiry→quote pipeline → the owner features the best stories with one click on the one hub. Everything the four seats independently flagged — the Nano/Micro/Macro/Mega tier ladder, the dormant second rate field, the second dispute desk, the clawback lever, the "bookings" label, the ROI column Setnayan can't know, the benefits/coaching block, and four pre-volume analytics surfaces — is decoration on that loop, and each one either lies (rule 3/4/5), duplicates (simple-wins), or adds a queue (rule 6). We cut them all, keep only the data capture that is cheap now and unrecoverable later (the spend-time influencer tag, the attribution column), and gate every deferred surface on real usage numbers so nothing creeps back in by default.

## 3. What we COLLAPSE (merged pieces)

- **Six P2 connectors → ONE PR (PR-C).** Attribution column → `startServiceInquiry` threading → Book CTA → vendor-thread surfacing → Proposal Maker discount line → `/proposals` reflection. Each is useless alone and all touch the one converged inquiry pipeline; three review cycles bought no safety.
- **Hub phases S1+S2+S3 → ONE PR (PR-D).** Deny-by-default + the empty-shelf self-gate means nothing public changes until the owner clicks Feature — the 10–20-chapter threshold is a when-to-click decision, not a build gate. The `/v/[slug]` "Featured in these stories" backlink **rides this PR** (it joins over featured chapters, so it has nothing to show earlier).
- **Admin footprint → exactly TWO surfaces, total.** The existing user-reports queue (widened target types absorb chapter, collab, and dishonored-discount reports) + the Studio Storytellers tab (the one Feature click). "Collab desk" → read-only offers view; "analytics" → a query; "tier auditing" → two WHERE clauses.
- **Three influence renderings → ONE line on /u:** *"Featured by N vendors · X inquiries driven · Y followers."* The vendor Creators browse card shows the same two facts; any future badge is a rendering of this number, never a second metric.
- **Two money moments → one vendor mental model, one line of copy:** tokens = reach, stated at send time as *"1 token; refunded only if no reply in 14 days"* (surfacing the decline-consumes rule plainly instead of burying it).
- **B5 copy fix + the publish≠listed expectation gap → one publish-time sentence:** *"Your chapter is live on your page now — Setnayan may also feature it on Real Stories."* One line kills the reverse-fake-door and the where's-my-feature confusion.
- **One offer form, evolving:** one rate + one deliverable ("publish a chapter crediting us") at P1; the audience rate joins the **same form** in PR-C.
- **Folds into in-flight PRs:** influencer-spend tag → the token-fix migration (capture at spend time); creator opt-out toggle + P1 feature flag → the funnel PR (they gate the same exposure event).

## 4. What stays exactly as planned (and why it earns its place)

- **P1 collab loop core** (Creators browse → token-gated offer → creator inbox accept/decline → chapter credits vendor) — this IS rules 1–3 in code; all four seats: nothing to cut.

- **B4 doorway + B5 copy fixes** — the creator's journey literally starts at a door that exists and copy that tells the truth.
- **S0 safety floor** (report target `'chapter'` + ReportPageButton + ShareButtons) — owed to live pages regardless, and it's the exact pattern the collab reports reuse.
- **The P2 attribution spine's reuse-first shape** — `referring_chapter_id` on `chat_threads`, Book CTA with audience rate, "referred by [Creator] via [Chapter]" on the vendor thread, Proposal Maker pre-applied discount line, `/proposals` reflection. Rules 3/4/5 mechanized with zero recurring operator work; certified reuse-first by the readiness council.
- **P4 per the Storytellers verdict** — one hub at `/realstories`, deny-by-default, owner's Feature click IS the moderation review, report-hide atomically unfeatures, empty shelf never renders, `/storytellers` redirects. Rule 6 delivered verbatim; do not reopen.
- **The four RA-10173 must-plan items** — creator solicitation opt-out (essential, not decoration: an unsolicited offers inbox is the fastest way to make a Filipino user feel farmed), viewer disclosure line at the Book CTA, creator-rate never on the public read path (whitelist `audience_rate` only), `/privacy` update.
- **Req #3a notifications** ("your chapter drove an inquiry") — the creator's payoff loop; one row in the existing pipeline.
- **Canon rule 2 closing the editorial-credit Pro-gate** — retire the shipped `editorialTagged` gate in the funnel PR; credit is always free, any tier. Deleted from the open-decisions list, not re-litigated.
- **Feature flags on CreatorInfluence + OfferInbox**; **true-affiliate cash stays OUT**; **locked invariants** (no video hosting, no creator cash, 0% commission, canonical RLS, cron-free).

## 5. The delivery plan (the fewest-PRs sequence)

**2 in-flight PRs + 1 paper session + 2 new PRs. Everything else is usage-gated.**

1. **PR-A (in flight) — Token integrity.** B1+B2+B3 as ONE migration + B7 commit-pinning + the influencer-spend tag column written at spend time (req #4's unrecoverable half). *Gate:* migration green in prod; ledger provably charges. Nothing downstream is honest until this lands.
2. **PR-B (in flight) — Funnel honesty.** B4 launcher doorway to `/dashboard/creator` · B5 copy fix merged with the publish-time expectation sentence · **remove** the audience-rate field (closes B6) · rename "bookings" → "inquiries driven" everywhere · one-line token price copy at send · retire the editorial-credit Pro-gate (rule 2) · `/creators` marketing page + `/pricing` callout + home promo, honest-to-shipped only · creator "accept vendor offers" on/off toggle + decline-implies-cooldown · P1 feature flag. *Gate:* no public copy promises anything unshipped. (S0 safety floor rides in flight alongside.)
3. **Paper session (no PR) — the locks.** Attribution rule locked as **CTA-click attribution** (the chapter whose Book CTA started the thread gets the credit — the degenerate last-touch both the Solo Operator and Sequencer were reaching for; no windows, no multi-touch math) · B10 shape = fulfilled/unfulfilled state + discount↔chapter link, no clawback · `audience_rate`-only public whitelist · owner signs the two lock reversals (§7.1) and the badge-word + thumbnail decisions. *Gate for PR-C and PR-D.*
4. **PR-C — the P2 spine (one PR, six connectors).** One migration (`referring_chapter_id` on `chat_threads` + fulfilled state) · audience-rate field joins the offer form · Book CTA with audience rate + viewer disclosure line ("discount is offered and honored by the vendor") · `startServiceInquiry` threading · vendor-thread "referred by" surfacing · Proposal Maker pre-applied discount line · `/proposals` reflection · req #3 notifications · the raw "inquiries driven" line on /u + the Creators browse (renders nothing at 0; two WHERE-clause guards) · `user_reports.target_type` widened to `'vendor_offer'`/`'collab'` · `/privacy` update. *Gate:* PR-A/PR-B live + paper locks signed.
5. **PR-D — the hub (one PR, was three phases).** Feature columns + Studio Storytellers tab + feature/unfeature spine · tile variant + YouTube-thumb + OG renderer · `/realstories` shelf + metadata + `/storytellers` redirect + cross-rail chips · `/v/[slug]` "Featured in these stories" backlink. Deny-by-default; empty shelf never renders — self-gating dark until the owner's first Feature click. *Gate:* badge word + thumbnail settled; S0 merged.
6. **Usage-gated, no date:** P3 analytics (≥25 attributed unlocks) · trimmed benefits block (≥20 active collabs) · tier bands (nonzero counts + owner sign-off) · Overview cards · event-dashboard publish entry · everything the Storytellers verdict already parked.

## 6. The one-breath explanations (the actual sentences we ship as copy)

- **Vendor** (Creators browse / send-offer sheet): *"Spend one token to offer a storyteller your promo — they publish their story crediting you for free, and anyone who books you through it gets the deal you chose. You keep 100%."* (Beneath the send button: *"1 token; refunded only if no reply in 14 days."*)
- **Creator** (`/creators` page / offer inbox): *"Post the story of your event for free. Vendors who like your audience will offer you a discount in exchange for a chapter that credits them — and your viewers get their own promo when they book through your story."*
- **Viewer** (Book CTA disclosure line): *"This storyteller partnered with [Vendor]. Book through this chapter and [Vendor] honors the promo shown — the discount is offered and settled by the vendor directly."*
