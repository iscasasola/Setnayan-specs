Setnayan · the emcee script system

Two phases, three lenses

The same wedding seen six ways. Planning is the weeks before — building the night, writing the script, answering questions; nothing is live and nobody is “on”. The night is the event itself, when one pointer moves and every screen follows it. The roles have different powers in each, which is the whole model.
