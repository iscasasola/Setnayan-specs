# Entrance video backgrounds — the five prompts (rewritten 2026-10-09 from the recorded ruling)

Ruling: DECISION_LOG 2026-10-08 "VIDEO BACKGROUNDS ARE ENTRANCES". Owner: "i also want to create more videos where it
feels like you are entering effect. like a walking in something … create 5 different videos" · "all in this perspective so
it feels like they are entering into something".

## The grammar every one follows
1. It STARTS behind a close foreground frame (an arch, doors, drapes, branches, window panels) that fills the edges.
2. A slow, steady PUSH-IN at walking pace passes through that frame — centred, eye level, no turn, no shake.
3. It EASES TO REST on a calm, wide scene; after that only small ambient life moves (a bird, leaves, light on water).
4. PAINTED, not filmed: soft gouache / oil illustration, visible brushwork, gentle light.
5. The upper-middle of the picture stays PALE and plain (the names sit there); the bottom stays calm (the button).
6. No people, no faces, no text, no logos. Portrait 1080 × 1920 (9:16). About 8 seconds. Silent.
7. Our own scenes, Filipino-first. The technique is taken, never another product's artwork.

## Paste-ready prompts (one per video)
Add to each, word for word: "Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 8 seconds, silent. One continuous slow push-in at walking pace,
centred, eye level, no cuts, no camera shake, no turn. The camera eases to a complete stop in the final two seconds.
Hand-painted gouache and oil illustration, soft visible brushwork, warm natural light. The upper-middle third of the frame
stays pale and uncluttered; the bottom fifth stays calm. No people, no faces, no text, no lettering, no logos."

1. HACIENDA GARDEN — "We begin close behind a weathered stone archway hung with bougainvillea, clay jars at its foot,
   filling the edges of the frame. The camera moves forward through the arch into a sunlit hacienda garden: a gravel path,
   clipped hedges, old acacia trees, a white-washed house far at the end under a pale morning sky. It comes to rest on the
   open garden. Two small birds cross the sky; leaves move slightly."

2. OLD STONE CHURCH — "We begin just inside heavy carved wooden church doors standing half open, their edges dark at the
   sides of the frame. The camera moves forward between them into the nave of an old Philippine stone church: coral-stone
   walls, a long tiled aisle, tall windows letting in soft shafts of light, a simple retablo far ahead, pale plaster vault
   above. It comes to rest in the centre of the aisle. Dust drifts in the light; a candle flame wavers."

3. BEACH AT SUNSET — "We begin behind sheer white drapes and the leaning trunks of two coconut palms framing the edges.
   The camera moves forward between them onto a wide, empty beach at sunset: pale sand, a calm sea, a low sun behind thin
   cloud, a large soft peach-and-cream sky filling the upper half. It comes to rest facing the horizon. Small waves fold
   onto the shore; the drapes at the edge of the frame move in the breeze."

4. LAKE TERRACE — "We begin behind an open pair of capiz-and-wood sliding panels and a hanging fern, close at the edges.
   The camera moves forward through them onto a stone terrace above a wide crater lake ringed by soft green hills, morning
   mist over the water, a pale silver-blue sky above. It comes to rest at the balustrade. Mist drifts slowly; a single
   banca crosses the far water, very small."

5. CAPIZ HOUSE AT DUSK — "We begin outside in the blue of dusk, behind the dark leaves of a garden and a low wooden gate
   standing open. The camera moves forward through the gate toward an old bahay na bato whose capiz-shell windows glow
   warm from inside, lanterns along the path, a pale lavender-to-cream evening sky above the roof. It comes to rest before
   the house. The lanterns sway a little; fireflies drift near the hedges."
