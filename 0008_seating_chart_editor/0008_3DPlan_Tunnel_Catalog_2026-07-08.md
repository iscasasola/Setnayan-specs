# Evolved Entrance Tunnel Catalog — 3D Plan Reception Designer

Grounded against `apps/web/lib/reception-scene.ts` (worktree copy at `.claude/worktrees/agent-a318f5a67e00443b1/_code-wt/apps/web/lib/reception-scene.ts`): the live enum has **9 treatments + `none`** — `floral · draped · fairy_light · greenery · balloon · lantern · crystal · butterfly · cherry_blossom` (note: `lantern` exists in the options list and the `entrance()` dispatcher; the brief said 8 — it's 9).

**MeshReflectorMaterial check: CONFIRMED** — it is exported from `@react-three/drei` core in both 9.x and 10.x. Caveat: it renders the scene to an offscreen FBO (≈2× cost for that plane), so it is desktop/Play-tier only; every mobile fallback below substitutes a `roughness≈0.12` envMapped dark plane.

Design rules encoded from the research (apply to ALL treatments): repetition at regular intervals = forced perspective; bare point sources at high density = bloom/starring; darkness is a material (dim the room shell near the tunnel); content flowing against walk direction = vection; PH heritage materials get **warm gold only**, never palette-RGB.

## 2 · Ship-First Ranking (wow-per-effort, PH-weighted)

1. **`cold_spark` (NEW)** — PH's named money effect (cold pyro is a line-item at ₱2–5K/unit in every reveal stack; EventNest lists it as the dominant lighting trend). Built almost entirely from drei Sparkles = lowest build cost in the catalog, and it's the treatment that makes cinematic Play mode's bloom pay for itself. Also the only treatment that *performs* the walk (sequenced firing).
2. **`floral` (UPGRADE)** — the Gideonized vault is THE Philippine tunnel (Hermosa's five-reasons sales script is industry canon). It upgrades the current *default* enum value, so every existing saved design instantly looks evolved with zero migration.
3. **`fairy_light` (UPGRADE)** — the TikTok budget king and the best photograph-per-peso build in the PH market; mostly Sparkles, ~4 draw calls, trivially mobile-safe.
4. **`led_portal` (NEW)** — the clearest "tunnels evolved past the arch" statement (Tron portal / Vivid Sydney lineage), and absurdly cheap: 3 draw calls, chase = 9 color writes per frame. Highest modern-wow per line of code.
5. **`lantern` → Parol Lantern Walk (UPGRADE)** — the Filipiniana differentiator no competitor's 3D tool will have; rides the 2026 "modern love, Filipino soul" editorial direction, and it's an upgrade of an existing key so it's additive-safe.

Runners-up: `led_video` (synergizes with the LED Background Maker + Animated Monogram SKUs — the content pipeline already exists), `infinity_mirror` (highest selfie-magnet value but reflector cost makes it Play/desktop-first).

## 4 · Walk Integration Spec

**Tunnel volume (canonical, all treatments unless noted):**
- Length **9.0 m** = 6 bays × 1.5 m (exceptions: `infinity_mirror` 3.0 m vestibule; `cold_spark` 6.0 m; `wisteria_rain` may run 12 m since it has no walls).
- Interior clear width **2.4 m**; structural outer footprint **3.2 m** (legs at x = ±1.35 m from centerline).
- Clearance **2.6 m** at centerline apex, springing to 2.2 m at the walls; `wisteria_rain` strand tips hang to **1.9 m** (deliberately below clearance — the brush-through is the feature, and the chase cam at 1.15 m stays clear).

**Path threading:** the entrance→seat path spline threads the tunnel **centerline exactly**, with path nodes at each bay midpoint (every 1.5 m) plus lead-in/lead-out nodes 0.5 m beyond both mouths so the chase cam settles straight before entering and after exiting. The tunnel exit node aligns to the existing aisle start so tunnel → aisle → seat is one continuous spline.

**Obstacle discs (for guest/waiter pathing + collision):** radius **0.35 m** at every structural leg — one pair per bay at x = ±1.35 m → 12–14 discs per tunnel. Structureless treatments: `cold_spark` = discs only at the 8 machine boxes (r 0.3 m); `wisteria_rain` = discs only at the 4 corner lattice posts; `infinity_mirror` = two wall-length capsule obstacles instead of discs. Always preserve a **1.6 m clear channel** on the centerline.

**Chase-cam framing (the phone-walk money shot):**
- Cam height **1.15 m**, trailing **2.2 m** behind the walker, FOV **55°** (portrait 9:16 bias) — low enough that foreground elements (strand tips, bead columns, spark columns) brush past the lens for parallax.
- Through the tunnel: walker centered in the **lower third**, tunnel vault + converging repetition filling the **upper two-thirds** — the forced-perspective read is the shot.
- At path-t **0.85** of the tunnel segment, fire the treatment's climax beat: `cold_spark` final pair ignites · `draped` curtain parts · `led_portal` whiteout on the last frame · `led_video` monogram hero frame · all others ramp bloom intensity +30%.
- Exit: ease a **tilt-up** over the last bay so the monogram terminus crests into the top of frame exactly as the walker breaks the tunnel mouth into the brighter room — the reveal-contrast beat every PH source scripts (tunnel brighter/darker than room, exit reads as the burst).
