№ 02 Our story

Eight years,
one ridge line.

We met on a bus to Tagaytay in 2018 — Cale had the window seat, Ice had the itinerary. By the overlook, we had traded both. Every year since, we've gone back to the same ridge on the same weekend, through storms, through lockdowns, through one memorably burnt bulalo.

Last December, at the overlook where it began, Cale knelt. Ice said yes before the sentence finished.

"We're not starting something new — we're naming something that's been true for years."
