The flywheel

Earned tokens lower a vendor's net cost per lead — so the vendors who promote Setnayan services get the cheapest leads, which makes them promote more, which sells more of our services and books more weddings. The token is both the meter and the loyalty currency, so the loop feeds itself.

recommend → couple buys → earn tokens → cheaper leads → recommend more ↻
