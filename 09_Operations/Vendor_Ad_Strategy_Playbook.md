# Vendor Ad Strategy Playbook

**Locked 2026-05-20.** Vendor-facing companion to [Boosted_Ads_Activation_Playbook](./Boosted_Ads_Activation_Playbook.md) (owner-side outbound) and [0022 § 5b](../0022_vendor_dashboard/0022_vendor_dashboard.md) (Boosted Ads + Sponsored Boost spec). Teaches vendors how to maximize ad ROI by matching ad inventory to vendor maturity. **Bundled into Pro/Max onboarding kit as PDF + in-dashboard widget when [[task-17-hiring-predictive-guide]] surface lands in 0023.**

> **Why this exists.** Setnayan's revenue depends on vendors getting *value* from ads, not just buying ads. A vendor who blows ₱20k/wk and sees zero leads churns within a quarter and tells every vendor in their network. A vendor who spends ₱5k/wk strategically, sees 3 new bookings/month, and credits Setnayan for the growth renews forever and evangelizes. **This playbook is the difference between Setnayan being "an ad platform that takes our money" vs. "a partner that taught us how to grow."** That positioning is worth far more than any individual ad sale.

> **⏸️ ACTIVATION GATE.** Per [marketing-SKU traffic-gate row 2026-05-20](../CLAUDE.md), this playbook becomes broadly relevant only after Setnayan hits **1,000 verified vendors + 5,000 weekly unique visits** sustained 4 weeks. Before that gate clears, all marketing SKUs are disabled and vendors shouldn't be running ads at all. The playbook ships as part of Pro/Max onboarding so vendors arrive informed when the surface unlocks.

## Five strategic principles every vendor should follow

| # | Principle | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Build social proof before buying ads** | Spending ₱5k/wk on Boosted Ads with 0 reviews wastes 70% of click-throughs — couples land on a thin profile and bounce. Get to 10+ reviews first. |
| 2 | **Match radius to actual service area** | A Cebu photographer buying Nationwide Boosted Ads is paying for Manila clicks they can't service. Buy the smallest radius that covers your real catchment. |
| 3 | **Time spend to SEARCH seasonality, not WEDDING execution season** | Couples search 6–12 months ahead. Heavy ad spend during Jan-Mar / Aug-Oct (search peaks) returns more than spend during May (wedding peak but planning already done). See Seasonal Calendar below. |
| 4 | **Stack different funnel stages, don't double up** | Profile Spotlight (awareness) + Boosted Ads (active discovery) is complementary. Two Boosted Ads at the same radius is redundant. |
| 5 | **Test small before committing long** | Run 2-week Boosted Ads campaigns to measure ROI before buying Sponsored Boost Quarterly at ₱249,999. The quarter-long commit only makes sense if 2-week tests prove ROI. |

## Strategy by vendor archetype

### 🌱 New solo vendor (0–5 reviews, just signed up)

- **Goal:** Build reviews + first 10 bookings
- **Recommended spend:** **₱0/wk on ads**
- **Focus on instead:** completing profile · getting first reviews · responding fast to inquiries · self-tagging your work in showcases as photos publish
- **Why no ads:** every ₱ spent before social proof is wasted; couples won't book from a thin profile

### 📸 Established solo (10+ reviews · 4+ rating · 2–4 weddings/month)

- **Goal:** Increase volume to 6–8 weddings/month
- **Recommended:** Pro ₱4,999/wk + Boosted Ads 25km ₱4,999/wk during peak search months only
- **Total budget:** ~₱40k/month during peak (Jan-Mar, Aug-Oct); ~₱22k/month off-peak (just Pro)
- **Off-peak swap:** pause Boosted Ads in Jun-Jul, run Profile Spotlight off-peak slot at ₱1,499/wk to maintain visibility cheaply
- **Avoid:** Sponsored Boost (too premium-tier for your volume); Nationwide ads (out of catchment)

### 🏢 Small studio (4+ rating · 50+ reviews · 2–5 team · 5–15 weddings/month)

- **Goal:** Brand recognition + steady high volume
- **Recommended:** Pro ₱4,999/wk + Boosted Ads 50km ₱9,999/wk + Profile Spotlight during peak weeks
- **Total budget:** ~₱75k/month peak; ~₱35k/month off-peak
- **Test:** Sponsored Boost Quarterly mid-year (year 2+) once ROI on shorter campaigns is proven
- **Watch for:** cross-province reach opportunities (Tagaytay, Cavite, Laguna) — bump to 50km radius before going Nationwide

### 🏭 Production company (multi-category · 15+ events/month · ₱3M+/month revenue)

- **Goal:** Dominate top-of-search across categories + regions
- **Recommended:** Max ₱24,999/wk + Sponsored Boost Annual (₱799,999/yr ≈ ₱15,385/wk amortized) + alternating Profile Spotlight for brand reinforcement
- **Total budget:** ~₱180–200k/month
- **Logic:** Sponsored Boost = always-on premium placement; Spotlight = brand pulses during peak; Boosted Ads stacked in expansion-target regions (breaking into a new metro)
- **Region-expansion play:** when entering a new city, add Boosted Ads 100km for that city's catchment for 6–12 weeks to build initial booking volume → graduate to Sponsored Boost regional once recognized

## Seasonal Calendar — Filipino wedding SEARCH season

Filipino wedding-vendor SEARCH peaks track engagement-season planning surges, NOT wedding execution dates. Couples planning a May 2027 wedding start searching in summer 2026.

| Month | Search activity | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| **January** | **Peak search** (post-NY engagements + new-year planning surge) | Max ad spend · Profile Spotlight peak rate ₱3,499/wk · Boosted Ads on |
| **February** | **Peak search** (Valentine's engagements drive next-wave search) | Sustain max spend |
| **March** | **Peak search** (planning surge tail) | Sustain max spend through end of month |
| **April** | Shoulder | Maintain Boosted Ads at matched radius; Profile Spotlight shoulder rate ₱2,499/wk if budget allows |
| **May** | Shoulder | Continue moderate spend; couples already in shortlist mode |
| **June** | **Off-peak (rainy season lull)** | **Pause Boosted Ads if budget-constrained.** Use this time to: optimize profile · refresh portfolio · request reviews from past clients · plan Q3-Q4 spend |
| **July** | **Off-peak** | Same as June; this is the rainy-season planning lull |
| **August** | **Peak search** (next-year planning kicks off) | Resume max ad spend; this is the second-biggest opportunity window |
| **September** | **Peak search** | Sustain max spend |
| **October** | **Peak search** (final next-year planning push before holidays) | Continue full spend |
| **November** | Shoulder | Moderate spend; engagement season starts in Dec (less searching, more proposing) |
| **December** | Shoulder | Engagement-season activity but low active-search; reduce spend |

**The June-July pause rule is the most overlooked tactical insight.** Vendors who pause Boosted Ads during the rainy-season search-lull save 15–25% of annual ad budget with negligible booking impact, freeing capital for the Aug-Oct surge when it actually converts.

## Profile Readiness Score — earn the right to spend on ads

Vendors below 80% readiness get warned before buying ads; below 60% get **ads disabled entirely** until they fix the profile. Setnayan refuses to sell ads to vendors whose profiles will waste the spend.

| Readiness signal | Weight | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Logo + banner uploaded | 5% | Upload PNG transparent logo + on-brand banner |
| Bio ≥ 200 words | 5% | Tell your story — who you are, what you do, why couples should care |
| Portfolio: 30+ photos minimum | 15% | Upload a focused set — quality > quantity |
| 10+ published reviews | 20% | Request reviews from past couples; full reviews not just star |
| Avg rating ≥ 4.0 | 10% | Reply professionally to 1–2 star reviews; deserved low ratings hurt CTR |
| Response time < 2 hours (last 30 days) | 10% | Set up push + email + (Max-tier) SMS notifications; auto-response covers off-hours |
| At least 3 service packages with clear pricing | 10% | Couples filter by price band — undocumented packages get skipped |
| Verified Setnayan Pay status | 10% | Booked-through-Pay = verified editorial credit + premium placement (see [[0046]]) |
| Portfolio photos tagged with Filipino moments / style tags | 5% | Cord-and-veil, money dance, sagala, Filipiniana attire — get tagged for inspiration browse |
| Active in last 7 days | 10% | Stale profiles fail — log in weekly minimum, post one portfolio update/month |

| Score | What happens |
|---|---|
| Below 60% | **Ads disabled.** Dashboard banner: "Complete your profile to unlock ad placements" with link to weakest signals |
| 60–80% | **Warning.** "We recommend completing your profile first — your ad ROI will be much higher" |
| 80%+ | **Full ad access.** All marketing SKUs purchasable |

## Performance KPIs to track

Available in Pro analytics dashboard (basic) and Max (advanced cohort + attribution). Watch these weekly:

| Metric | Pro | Max | Healthy target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impressions (couples who saw the ad) | ✓ | ✓ | Baseline (no target — depends on radius) |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | ✓ | ✓ | **2–5% healthy · 7%+ excellent** |
| Profile visits from ad clicks | ✓ | ✓ | **80%+ of clicks land** |
| Chat threads opened | ✓ | ✓ | **10–20% of profile visitors message** |
| Bookings closed | ✓ | ✓ | **5–15% of chat threads convert** |
| Cost per booking (CPB) | — | ✓ | **<10% of avg package price** |
| ROI (booking revenue / ad spend) | — | ✓ | **3:1 minimum · 5:1+ great** |

**The Cost Per Booking is the most important number to watch.** If you charge ₱30k/wedding and your CPB is ₱4,000, that's a 7.5:1 ROI — excellent. If CPB is ₱15,000, you're at 2:1 — break-even territory, time to reassess radius or creative or profile readiness.

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Buying Nationwide when you serve NCR only.** Costs 4× more; you can't service the leads outside your catchment.
2. **Running ads with a 60% profile readiness score.** Couples land on a thin profile and bounce; you pay for clicks that never convert.
3. **Stacking two Boosted Ads tiers in the same radius.** Redundant — pick the largest radius you need, not two overlapping.
4. **Buying Sponsored Boost Annual without running 2-week Boosted Ads tests first.** ₱799,999 commit before knowing your CPB is reckless.
5. **Running ads year-round without seasonality.** Vendors who pause Jun-Jul save 15–25% of annual ad budget with zero booking impact.
6. **Ignoring response time.** Couples who wait >2hrs for a reply go to the next vendor. Notification setup is free — use it.
7. **Setting it and forgetting it.** Review your weekly dashboard every Monday. Pause campaigns that hit CPB > 10% of package price.

## Worked examples per archetype

### Mariposa Bloom Photography — established solo (Tagaytay-based, 6 yrs operating)

- **Pre-launch promo (now → Jan 30, 2027):** Free Pro + 50km radius. Spend ₱0 on marketing. Get to 30+ reviews + 200+ portfolio photos.
- **Jan 31, 2027 sunset:** Convert to founder-rate ₱3,999/wk Pro. Confirms 50km radius (covers Tagaytay + Cavite + Laguna + Manila).
- **Feb–Mar 2027 peak:** Add Boosted Ads 25km local (₱4,999/wk) for 8 weeks. Total ad spend ₱40k.
  - Result: 12 new bookings closed in Feb-Mar at avg ₱40k = ₱480k revenue. CPB = ₱3,333. ROI = 12:1.
- **Apr–May 2027 shoulder:** Drop to Pro-only ₱3,999/wk. Bookings continue from word-of-mouth + showcase appearances.
- **Jun–Jul 2027 off-peak:** Pro-only. Update portfolio. Request reviews from Q1 weddings. Plan Aug-Oct push.
- **Aug–Oct 2027 peak:** Repeat Feb-Mar playbook. Bump to Boosted Ads 50km if expanding to Pampanga.
- **Year-1 ad spend:** ~₱80k. Year-1 attributed revenue: ~₱950k. Year-1 net ROI: ~10:1 after Pro subscription cost.

### Habagat Productions — small studio (Manila-based, photo+video, 4-person team)

- **Pre-launch promo:** Free Pro + 50km. Build to 75+ reviews across both services.
- **Jan 31, 2027:** Convert to founder-rate ₱3,999/wk Pro.
- **Year-1 marketing strategy:** Boosted Ads 50km (Citywide ₱9,999/wk) during 6 peak months · Profile Spotlight Hero slot 4 peak weeks (₱3,499 × 4 = ₱13,996)
- **Year-1 total ad spend:** ~₱260k (~₱22k/mo). Year-1 attributed revenue: ~₱2.4M. ROI: ~9:1.

### Setnayan Showroom Productions — production company (multi-city, multi-category)

- **Pre-launch promo:** Free Pro + 50km. Don't burn ad spend — focus on completing 9 canonical categories + building 200+ reviews across services.
- **Jan 31, 2027:** Convert to Max ₱24,999/wk · ₱999,999/yr annual lock.
- **Year-1 marketing:** Sponsored Boost Annual (₱799,999/yr) for always-on premium placement + Profile Spotlight Hero 12 peak weeks (₱3,499 × 12 = ₱41,988) + Boosted Ads Nationwide ₱19,999/wk Feb-Mar + Aug-Oct (16 weeks total = ₱319,984)
- **Year-1 total ad spend:** ~₱1.16M (~₱97k/mo). Year-1 attributed revenue target: ~₱6M+. ROI: ~5:1+.

## When to upgrade tier vs. when to spend more on ads

| Symptom | Solution |
|---|---|
| Ads converting well at CPB <10% but capped by team capacity | **Upgrade tier** — hire more team, Pro→Max if 6+ categories |
| Plenty of capacity but bookings not arriving | **Spend more on ads** — more radius, more peak weeks, add Spotlight |
| Bookings arriving but CPB >15% | **Don't spend more.** Improve profile readiness or shrink radius |
| Premium clients aren't finding you | **Sponsored Boost** — premium placement signals premium tier |
| You're entering a new region | **Boosted Ads radius expansion for 6–12 weeks** — temporary push to build initial booking volume |
