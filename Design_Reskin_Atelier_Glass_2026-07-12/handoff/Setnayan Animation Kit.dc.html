07 · Usage

When to move, when to hold still

Do
Animate on entry once, then rest
Grow data into place on first paint / scroll-in
Loop motion only for genuinely live states
Prefer transform & opacity (GPU-cheap)
Always ship the reduced-motion guard
Don't
Loop attention-seeking motion on idle content
Animate width/top/left (layout thrash)
Durations over 700ms on interaction feedback
Bounce/overshoot easings — keep it calm
Pulse anything that isn't actually live
Alive, never restless. Motion earns its place by clarifying — an entrance, a change, a live state — then gets out of the way.
