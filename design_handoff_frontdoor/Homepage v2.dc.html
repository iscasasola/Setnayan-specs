The integration rule: one index over all four doors — the app's own features (Papic, Live Studio, 3D floor plan, Alaala, Setnayan AI, Pakanta) answer under 01, so "what does this app do?" is searchable too — results grouped under the doors' own numbers — the search IS the menu, answered. A vendor hit shows its craft + verification; a story hit shows its chapter + series; a journal hit shows its shelf. Groups with no hits don't render (never an empty "Vendors (0)" header). If the index read fails, the panel says "Search isn't answering right now — the menu above still works", never a false "no results".

Where it lives: hero on /, and collapsed into the header (magnifier at the wordmark's right) on /explore, /realstories, /blog — same panel, same grouping, so search behaves identically on every archetype page.

Empty catalogue: with today's two hidden shops, a vendor-name query returns only story/journal groups — real answers, not apologies. The vendors group appears the day shops go visible.
