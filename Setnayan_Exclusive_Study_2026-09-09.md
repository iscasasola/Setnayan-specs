## 2 · 🔑 THE FILTER THAT MATTERS: face value vs what it costs US

A gift's price is what the couple values it at. What we give up is something else
entirely, and the two are **not** proportional. This is the whole economics of the
idea and it should decide what goes on the shelf.

| product | face | what it costs us | fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Papic credits | ₱70–₱24,000 | **storage only** — a kept photo is ~₱0.06 for fifty years | ✅ best |
| Setnayan AI | ₱2,499 | **~₱0** — deterministic planner, not an LLM (spec 0016) | ✅ best |
| Event Hub Pro | ₱3,500 | **~₱0** — software only | ✅ best |
| Patiktok | ₱1,500 | **~₱0** — renders in the guest's own browser | ✅ |
| Animated Monogram | ₱500 | small — image generation + vectorise, once | ✅ |
| Thank You film | ₱2,500 | small — one server render (~₱2–5 range) | ◐ |
| Mood Board Render Pack | ₱1,000 | **50 renders** at the same per-render cost | ◐ the pack multiplies it |
| Live Studio | ₱2,500 | small but real per event; audience-independent | ◐ |
| Pakanta | ₱2,500 | **real per song** — outside generation, 1–3 songs | ⛔ worst ratio |

⚠ **EVIDENCE GRADE.** The Papic storage figure and the ~₱2–5 per render are from the
corpus's own measured notes. The rest is **reasoned from how each product is built**
(client-side render vs server render vs outside API), not separately measured. Before
this ships, price the render-backed ones properly — do not let a ₱2,500 gift that
costs real money onto the same shelf as one that costs nothing.

🔑 **Read the table and the answer falls out:** the best gifts are the ones made of
software and storage. **Pakanta should not be on the shelf** — it is the one product
here with a genuine per-unit cost, and it is the one a supplier would pick most often
because it sounds the most generous.

## 3 · How the choice should work

The owner has proposed both shapes. They are better together:

> **The booking decides HOW MUCH. The supplier decides WHICH.**

- The **value** is ours — set by the booking, identical for every supplier, so nobody
  games it by picking the biggest thing on the list.
- The **choice** is theirs — a photographer gives credits, a band gives Live Studio, a
  planner gives Setnayan AI. It fits their trade and it feels like their gift.
- The couple compares **rungs**, not sentences, which is exactly what the bench needs
  when three caterers sit side by side.

### The shelves, built on real catalogue prices

| shelf | worth | what the supplier may choose |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | ~₱350 | 500 Papic credits · Animated Monogram (₱500) |
| **B** | ~₱1,000 | 1,000 credits · Mood Board Render Pack · Patiktok (₱1,500) |
| **C** | ~₱2,500 | 4,000 credits · Setnayan AI · Live Studio · Thank You film |
| **D** | ~₱3,500 | 6,000 credits · Event Hub Pro · Live Studio hosted channel |

Nothing here is an invented price — every item sits at its live catalogue figure.

### Which booking earns which shelf

**1% of the booking, capped at ₱3,500**, rounded down to the nearest shelf:

| booking | 1% | shelf | our fee | gift as % of fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₱35,000 | ₱350 | A | ₱1,750 | 20% |
| ₱100,000 | ₱1,000 | B | ₱5,000 | 20% |
| ₱250,000 | ₱2,500 | C | ₱6,500 | 38% |
| ₱350,000+ | ₱3,500 (cap) | D | ₱7,500 | 47% |

🔴 **THE CAP IS LOAD-BEARING, AND HERE IS WHY.** Our booking fee **tapers** — 5% on the
first ₱100,000, then 1% above. A gift that scales with the *booking* grows faster than
a fee that tapers, so without a cap a large booking's gift overtakes the fee that funds
it. At the cap the gift is 47% of the fee **at face value** — and a fraction of that in
real cost, provided § 2's filter is applied. Take the filter away and this arithmetic
stops working.

## 4 · What must be true for it to be safe

1. **The perk is a purchase, not a fee component.** The booking fee applies to *sourced*
   clients only — BYO, vendor-invited and returning are free forever, and a verified
   supplier's first five sourced bookings are free. "Add it to the fee" therefore costs
   nothing on most bookings. The perk must be billed at the same moment without
   inheriting those exemptions.
2. **Grant when the money clears, not when the booking locks.** Spent credits cannot be
   clawed back; a couple who has already shot 200 photos cannot be un-gifted.
3. **Never store the peso value on the card.** Store the credits or the product; work
   the pesos out from the live catalogue when the card is drawn. The ladder is repriced,
   and a stored "worth ₱70" becomes a lie the marketplace repeats.
4. **"Starts at" becomes the bottom rung, but never a block.** Nothing today compares a
   quote to the card's own "from ₱X" — a supplier can advertise ₱35,000 and quote
   ₱18,000. Making it the floor gives the number meaning; **refusing** a lower quote
   would push that conversation off the platform, which is the exact thing this idea
   exists to prevent. Quote below it and there is simply no gift.

## 5 · Why any of this is worth building

Owner, verbatim: *"this way, integrating our services is something they cannot offer
outside the app."*

A supplier can offer a discount anywhere. They cannot offer Papic credits anywhere but
here. The gift is the one part of the deal that only exists if the booking is locked in
the app — and it puts the **couple**, not us, in the position of wanting that.

It also fixes the number we charge on. The fee is levied on the figure the supplier
declares, so today the only pull is downward. Tie the gift to that same figure and the
pull reverses: declare low, the couple gets a smaller gift and asks why.

## 6 · The mechanism already exists — do not build a new one

`comp_grants` with `source = 'vendor_self_comp'` already means *"this supplier granted
this Setnayan product to this user"*: scoped to one event, naming which products, with
an expiry, a per-quarter quota enforced by a database trigger, and an admin-raisable cap
table. Measured 2026-09-09: **zero rows of any source, ever.** What is missing is the
link from a service card's Exclusive to a grant minted when the money clears.

## 6c · DOES 20% OF THE FEE LOSE US MONEY? No — because 20% is FACE VALUE

The gift's price is what the couple values it at. What leaves us is its **cost**, and on
the approved shelf that is a fraction of face.

| booking | our fee | gift (face) | face as % of fee | what it actually costs us | real % of fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₱35,000 | ₱1,750 | ₱350 · 500 credits | 20% | ~₱30 storage | **1.7%** |
| ₱100,000 | ₱5,000 | ₱1,000 · 1,000 credits | 20% | ~₱60 storage | **1.2%** |
| ₱100,000 | ₱5,000 | ₱1,000 · Mood Board pack | 20% | ₱100–250 *(estimated)* | **2–5%** |
| ₱250,000 | ₱6,500 | ₱2,500 · Setnayan AI | 38% | ~₱0 — deterministic, not an LLM | **~0%** |
| ₱350,000+ | ₱7,500 | ₱3,500 · Event Hub Pro | 47% | ~₱0 — software only | **~0%** |

**We keep 95–99% of the fee.** The 20% never leaves the building.

⚠ **THREE THINGS THE ARITHMETIC EXPOSED, all worth a ruling:**

1. **A gift only starts at a ₱35,000 booking.** 1% of anything smaller falls below shelf
   A (₱350), so a ₱10,000 cake or a ₱20,000 host earns nothing at all. That is
   self-protecting, but it means the promise is not universal. The catalogue already has
   **100 / 200 / 300-credit rungs (₱70 / ₱140 / ₱210)**, so a shelf below A would reach
   bookings from ₱7,000 and keep the offer true for every supplier.
2. **On a fee-free booking we do not lose — we EARN.** BYO, vendor-invited, returning and
   a verified supplier's first five sourced bookings pay **no fee at all**. With the perk
   billed as a purchase (§ 4.1) the supplier pays the gift price with no fee beside it,
   so those bookings are pure margin rather than a hole.
3. **Storage is the one cost we can never stop paying.** The compressed gallery is kept
   **for life** (owner 2026-08-18), so a credit's cost is small and permanent rather than
   one-off. At this scale it is trivial — 6,000 credits is roughly ₱360 spread over fifty
   years — but it is the only line here with no end date, and it grows with every gift
   ever given.

## 6d · ⚖ OWNER RULING 2026-09-09 — NO BOOKING FEE, NO EXCLUSIVE

> *"this only applies if there is booking fee. if no booking fee is paid, then no
> exclusive setnayan."*

**This SUPERSEDES § 4.1's recommendation** that the perk be a purchase billed
independently of the fee's exemptions. It is simpler and it closes the same leak from
the other side: a BYO, vendor-invited or returning client pays no fee, so that booking
carries no gift at all — and a supplier can no longer hand out credits free on clients
they brought themselves.

⚠ **THE CONSEQUENCE HE MAY NOT WANT, FLAGGED NOT DECIDED:** a verified supplier's
**first five sourced bookings are free** (`lib/booking-fee.ts`). Under this ruling those
five carry no gift either — and they are precisely the bookings where a new supplier
most needs to look good to a couple. Either the free-five keeps its gift as an
onboarding cost, or a supplier's first months on the platform are their least
attractive. **Owner's call.**

## 6e · 🔴 ADDED TO THE BILL, NEVER DEDUCTED FROM THE FEE — the whole economics turns on this

> *"losing 3500 from a 7500 big from a booking fee. that is a lot considering they only
> technically paid 4000 for that booking."*

That arithmetic is right **for one of two models, and it is not the model the owner
originally described.**

| | supplier pays us | we hand over | we net (face) | we net (real cost) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ADDED** — *"that amount will be added to what they need to pay to us to lock"* (owner, earlier) | ₱7,500 fee **+ ₱3,500 gift = ₱11,000** | ₱3,500 of software | **₱11,000** | ~₱11,000 |
| **DEDUCTED** — the gift comes out of the fee | ₱7,500 | ₱3,500 of software | **₱4,000** | ~₱7,500 |

🔑 **Under ADDED nothing is lost at all — we collect ₱3,500 MORE, not ₱3,500 less.** The
supplier buys a gift from us at face; we hand over something that costs us close to
nothing; the couple receives ₱3,500 of real value they could not get anywhere else. The
₱4,000 the owner is rightly unhappy about only appears if the gift is DEDUCTED, and
nothing in his original design says it should be.

### And the 25% comparison argues FOR added, not against

> *"Other online charges 25% commission. and we only charge 5% and 1%."*

On a **₱350,000** booking:

| | what the supplier pays |
|---|---|
| a 25%-commission platform | **₱87,500** |
| Setnayan, fee only | **₱7,500** — 8.6% of theirs, **11.7× cheaper** |
| Setnayan, fee + a ₱3,500 gift for the couple | **₱11,000** — 12.6% of theirs, **8× cheaper** |

🔑 **A THIN FEE HAS NO ROOM TO GIVE ANYTHING AWAY, WHICH IS EXACTLY WHY THE GIFT MUST BE
ADDED.** A 25% platform earning ₱87,500 could hand back ₱3,500 and lose 4% of its take.
At 5%/1% the same gift is **47%** of ours. We cannot afford to be generous out of the
fee — but we can afford to be generous *alongside* it, because what we give costs us
almost nothing to give.

It is also the stronger sales sentence: **"You would pay ₱87,500 elsewhere. Here you pay
₱7,500 — and for ₱3,500 more, your couple gets ₱3,500 of something no other platform
can hand them."**

## 6f · ⚖ OWNER RULING 2026-09-09 — TWO GIFTS ARE UNLIMITED; THE REST CAN BE TAKEN

> *"vendors need to see their current setnayan services availed already. so they can pick
> what exclusive setnayan service would can they still offer the guest. incase that
> service is already taken. last fall back would always be papic credits. and moodboard
> render, since those 2 are unlimited."*

| gift | how many times per celebration |
|---|---|
| **Papic credits** | **unlimited** — a pot that is added to |
| **Mood Board Render Pack** | **unlimited** — 50 consumable renders per pack |
| Setnayan AI | **once** — an unlock |
| Event Hub Pro | **once** — an upgrade |
| Live Studio | ⚠ **per event-DAY** per the 0011 spec, so a multi-day celebration may take more than one. **CONFIRM before building — do not assume once.** |

### 🔒 Show what is GIFTABLE, never what the couple OWNS

The obvious build — show the supplier the couple's Setnayan purchases — hands a supplier
the couple's spending history for no reason. **The shelf simply does not offer what is
already taken.** Same outcome, nothing disclosed: the supplier sees *"available to
give"*, never *"they already bought Setnayan AI"*.

### 🪤 THE PICK IS A PREFERENCE, NOT A RESERVATION

The gift is granted when the money CLEARS (§ 4.2), so there is a real gap between
choosing and granting, and two suppliers can pick the same one-off in it. **Resolve at
grant time, automatically** — if the chosen gift is gone, fall back without asking
anyone. That is exactly what the two unlimited gifts are for.

⚠ **THE FALLBACK MATCHES THE VALUE, NOT THE ITEM.** A supplier who chose Setnayan AI
(₱2,499) and lost it must give ₱2,499 of credits — roughly 3,500 — not the smallest rung
on the shelf. The supplier paid for a rung; the couple receives that rung.
