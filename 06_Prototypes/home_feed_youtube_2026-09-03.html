Rev 2 — what changed, and why

  • Added the category anchor above the feed: one headline (the live HOME_TITLE claim), three micro-claims, one primary CTA. A pure YouTube feed assumes a returning viewer with watch history; nearly every visitor to / is a first-time stranger with none — they get the five-second answer first. This is not the retired narrative hero: no essay, no group-chat mockup, no “couldn’t fly home” section.
  • Shops promoted to co-equal: moved from the page’s tail to directly beneath the anchor, substantiating the 0%-commission claim it sits under. Marketplace research: supply liquidity is the survival predictor at this stage, and a content-only first screen reads as “empty site”. Still its own room — the twice-rejected feed-merge stays rejected; tiles stay compact, the weight comes from position and the full-bleed band.
  • The sparse Shops room recruits supply: the real shops render plus one dashed “Your shop belongs here” seat (list free · 0% of every booking) — honest about today’s two-shop reality while working the liquidity problem, instead of faking a full row.
  • Unchanged, deliberately: the card language, kind tags, mono meta, New uploads, the honest sparse-Trending band, all chrome (top bar, rail structure, compact toggle). Feed order below the anchor is now Shops → New uploads → Trending.
  • Rev 2.1 — the anchor question is settled: owner confirmed it renders for signed-out visitors only. The signed-in return visit (no anchor, feed straight away) is mocked as tab 6 of the “Setnayan Shell States” artifact, alongside the new Planner / Builder / Together rail groups.

Notes for engineering

  • Trending needs one boolean per piece, not a score UI. The design assumes each chapter is either earned or not (real-view threshold is engineering/owner’s to define); the section renders only the earned list, and the sparse band needs no data beyond “earned count = 0”. Editorials never enter — they carry no view counts by rule.
  • The chip row (All / Your people / Stories / Articles) is dropped per “drop everything” — the two named sections replace it. Confirm the top-bar search (?q=) still answers on this page, beneath the feed.
  • The manual rail-collapse control is mocked as the hamburger beside the wordmark (try it on tabs 1–3). Shipped CSS already auto-collapses at 1024–1279px; the manual control needs a which-wins rule — user choice should probably persist.
  • Marketplace folder counts in the rail are illustrative. The shipped rail reads FOLDER_SERVICE_COUNT; production today would show single digits.
  • Anchor CTA destination — mocked as “Start your celebration — free”; wire to the real signup/create flow. “How it works →” assumes the shipped explainer page. Answered (owner-confirmed): the anchor is signed-out only — a signed-in visitor landing back on / skips straight to Shops → New uploads → Trending, with no anchor strip at all. That state is mocked as tab 6 of the companion “Setnayan Shell States” artifact.
  • The recruit seat’s destination should be the existing vendor-grow page — an open seat that opens marketing copy is fine; one that 404s is a fake door.
