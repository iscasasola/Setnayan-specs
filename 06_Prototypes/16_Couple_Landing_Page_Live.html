Our Story · The theme

An Intramuros evening

It started with a friend's despedida in March of 2021. Maria was helping her cousin pack for Toronto. Juan had come because his college roommate insisted he'd regret missing it. They argued for forty minutes over which adobo recipe was correct — Pampanga's or Cavite's. Neither convinced the other.

Three weeks later, on a sunset walk through Intramuros, the argument turned into a friendship, and the friendship turned into the rest of our lives. On Christmas Eve last year, on the same wall, Juan asked Maria to spend forever with him.

Our wedding takes its theme from that walk: warm cream, capiz light, and the soft champagne of late afternoon in old Manila. We hope you'll feel it too.
