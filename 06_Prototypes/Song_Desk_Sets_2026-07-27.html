The question

What “(anytime)” means

You wrote: “the band can set 1/2/3/4/5/6 sets, and name the x number of songs per set. allow requests (anytime).” I read that third item two ways, and they build differently — so tap the three buttons and tell me which one is your app.

Is “anytime” a mode among several, or is it simply always-on?

  • A mode — “anytime” sits beside “only during the sets I choose”. That’s what the prototype shows, and it’s the richer read: a band can take requests all night, or only during Set 3.
  • Always-on — requests are permanently open and the band never switches them off. Simpler, but it retires the open/close control you asked for one message earlier, so I didn’t assume it.
Two things the sketch decides

Worth a look while you’re here

Accepting doesn’t file a song into a set. A request comes in mid-song; making the band choose “which set?” in that moment is a decision they don’t need. Accept means “we’ll play it”. If you’d rather it drop into a specific set, that’s a real change.

Repeats collapse. “Through the Years · 6 people asked” is one row, not six — that’s already built and enforced in the database, not just in this picture.

Set songs come from the band’s repertoire. Not free-typed — you pick from the list they already keep on “Your repertoire”. Which is also why the next panel says the song matching is already live rather than something to build.
