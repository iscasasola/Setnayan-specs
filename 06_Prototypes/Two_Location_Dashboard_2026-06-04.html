Messages

The model in one line: two locations = one event, one dashboard — location is a lens, not a fork.
· One thing stays whole: the couple, guest list, budget total, messages, the website — never split by place.
· Each place gets a role: Ceremony (QC) · Reception (Tagaytay). The two onboarding "candidate areas" resolve into roles here — toggle Still deciding ⇄ Roles set above to see both phases.
· Three surfaces gain a place filter (the lens): Service (vendors tagged + filtered), Schedule (two legs + the travel block), Seat plan (tied to the reception venue).
· Graceful collapse: pick just one place and the lens, roles & travel block disappear — single-venue couples never see two-location chrome.
· Vendor side: each vendor only sees the leg they serve; the couple sees the whole picture.
