What is not on the page

From your own 2 July list, and from what the rest of the product already stores.

  • ✗A "Verified by Setnayan" mark. A vendor passes a video call, references and paperwork to get on the site — and their page never says so anywhere. It is the single strongest thing they have and it is invisible.
  • ✗Awards and recognitions. Setnayan can already hand a vendor an award from the admin side. Their page shows it nowhere. It can be given and never seen.
  • ✗The areas and cities they cover. How far they travel is stored and used to match them — a couple simply cannot read it.
  • ✗How many people viewed them. You asked for viewers, events and favourites. Saved-count shows; a view count does not.
  • ✗How many events they have completed. The number exists — it appears only when a mouse hovers, on a desktop, on a paid plan.
  • ✗How fast they reply. It is measured, and it is printed on the search results card — but not on their own page.
  • ✗Their calendar. A couple can be told one date is unavailable, but cannot see which months are already filling up.
  • ✗An Inquire button that stays on screen. On a phone, once you scroll past the top there is no way to ask — you must scroll back up.
  • ✗A button on the service card. The cards have no button at all right now.
  • ✗The vendor cannot make their own page live. Only Setnayan can. Their publish switch was lost when an old page was retired.
  • ◐Their social links and their own website. Collected at sign-up under wording that says "your public links" — then shown to nobody but Setnayan staff.
  • ◐Their one-line tagline. The page has a place for it. There is no field anywhere for a vendor to type one.
  • ◐Their own domain name. The whole manager is built and tells them "It's free" — but every button answers "not enabled yet" until two settings are added to our hosting account.
  • ◐Extra branch locations. ₱999 each per 28 days. Paid for, and invisible on the page and in search.

The one that needs your ruling

On the free plan, the page hides the reviews and sells an upgrade instead. Where the reviews should be, a couple reads: “Reviews unlock when this vendor upgrades their Setnayan plan.” Written words only appear from the Pro plan up. Your own principle says a vendor's real reviews are never tiered. Both are written down as settled.
