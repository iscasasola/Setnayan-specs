Papic promotion wave · PR-G · owner decision

Where does Papic live on the couple’s home?

Every event already holds a free pool of shots and a free camera of its own — armed at creation, no purchase — and the couple is never told so from home. Three placements below, each drawn in its real insertion slot using the shipped tokens. Pick one, or the pair I recommend.

Scope correction

The build spec names three home surfaces: page.tsx, today/page.tsx and for-you/page.tsx. Two of them no longer exist. today was retired 2026-06-03 and is a 36-line redirect to event-home; for-you was retired 2026-06-04 and redirects to /vendors. Both are stubs whose only job is to keep old bookmarks alive.

So this is a decision about one surface, not three — which is why every mockup below is the same page. Same class of stale premise as PR-A and PR-B in this wave.

What the page looks like today

Event-home renders <EventDashboard>: an obsidian “Big Day” focal, the at-a-glance bento, then a slotAfterBento slot, then the six-stage journey rail and the decisions board. The slot already carries the cultural and set-date overlays, so it is a proven insertion point rather than a new one.

One law governs the bento, quoted from the component: “Real-data-or-nothing — each tile renders only when its own data exists.” Papic passes it. Every event holds a live shot balance, so a Papic tile is never an empty promise.

Real estate
A full-width band, but only until dismissed. Same footprint the set-date nudge already occupies for date-less events.
Lifetime
Dismissible, remembered per event in localStorage — the shipped pattern. Should also self-hide once the couple has opened Papic or a shot has been taken, so it never nags someone already using it.
Why it pairs with A
The nudge answers “I didn’t know I had this”, which a status tile cannot. The tile answers “where does it stand”, which a dismissed nudge cannot. Different jobs, one each.
The honest downside
Two nudges can collide: a date-less event would show set-date and this. Order them, or hold this one back until the date nudge is gone.

Inserts at
event-dashboard.tsx → the cockpit decisions list
Real estate
Cheap to add, expensive in trust — it competes directly with decisions that carry money and dates.
Why not
Papic is a capability the couple already owns, not a decision awaiting them. A board that cries wolf about a free camera teaches couples to skim the board — and that board is where the photographer deadline lives.
When it would be right
If Papic ever gains a genuine deadline — “your pool expires”, “shots run out at your current rate before the reception ends” — that is a decision, and it belongs here. Not today.

Recommendation

Ship A and B together; leave C alone. They answer two different questions and neither can do the other’s job. The nudge tells a couple something they do not know — you already have this — and then removes itself. The tile answers where it stands, forever, next to the other things they check.

Together they cost one bento cell permanently and one dismissible band once. That is the smallest footprint that actually fixes the gap, on a page where real estate is contested.

What I need from you

1

A, B, both, or C?

My recommendation is both. One word is enough and I’ll build it.

2

What should the tile count before anyone shoots?

“50 shots ready” is honest but abstract. “1 camera out” is concrete but small. I’d lead with shots and switch to photos-gathered the moment the first one lands — say if you’d rather it always read one way.

3

Does the nudge wait its turn behind the set-date nudge?

A date-less event would otherwise show two bands at once. My default: set-date goes first, Papic appears once that one is gone or the date is set.
