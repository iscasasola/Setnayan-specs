What this changes vs. shipped
  • Pool rungs come back on sale. All four Pool SKUs are is_active=false today, deliberately — the 2026-07-22 guardrail said don't GA them until purge + clip compression ship. This prototype supersedes that: needs your explicit "go" to reactivate.
  • New Pool prices. Catalog rows say ₱999 / ₱1,999 / ₱2,999 — this says ₱1,000 / ₱2,000 / ₱3,000. Round numbers replace charm pricing.
  • Papic One becomes a ladder. Today One is flat ₱100 = 250 pts. New: ONE free camera with 5 pts, then per-camera rungs 50 pts ₱50 · 100 pts ₱100 — a clean ₱1 = 1 shot rule.
  • Onboarding card un-blanks. The card maps to the two live models instead of the retired PAPIC_SEATS/PAPIC_GUEST codes.
