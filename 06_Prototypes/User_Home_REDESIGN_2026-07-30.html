⚠ Two corrections after checking this against the shipped code. (1) The Zoom ladder isn’t new — it’s relocated. /dashboard/life-flash already ships <nav aria-label="Flash scope"> with life · year · month · event pills, chapter cards and a people row (page.tsx:260, :155, :349). The delta is bringing them into the home tile and reusing scopeMomentGraph() — not inventing them. (2) It costs Alaala’s five shipped lenses. The tile today renders Recent · Owned · Attended · People · With me (alaala-lenses.tsx:18-24) in exactly the slot the Zoom ladder now occupies. That is a swap, not an addition, and it needs your call: lenses answer “whose photos?”, the ladder answers “how far back?” — different questions, and only one fits.

Also removed since the last pass: the “37 overdue” pill on event cards. The shipped card comment is explicit — “attention/overdue signals deliberately live ONLY in The Watch … owner 2026-07-15: one home for overdue counts — this card carries identity/type/date/progress, never a decision pill” ((launcher)/page.tsx:1164). The count now lives once, on the board.

Storyteller now lives inside Alaala, not beside it. The schema already allows it — creator_chapters.event_id references events(event_id), and the migration's own comment says “a chapter may wrap a real Setnayan event or stand alone”. So a Storyteller chapter is an Alaala chapter with status='published'. Published chapters carry a gold dot in the strip and a PUBLIC tag on the card; the rest offer “Tell this publicly”. Spaces stops saying “Become a Storyteller” and says something true: 2 of 12 chapters are public.
