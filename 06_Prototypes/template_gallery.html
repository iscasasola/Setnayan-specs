How this catalog gets consumed

Each template above becomes a Remotion JSX component, a .cube LUT file, a Lottie JSON manifest, and a music-track reference into the owned Suno catalog. A single template entry powers every compatible SKU from one definition — Capiz Garden's render of a Save-the-Date and Capiz Garden's render of a Same-Day Edit both pull from the same manifest, just with different durations and music-edit points.

Free templates are flagship-grade. Pro tier variations expand the catalog post-launch — they are not gated for cost reasons (per-render cost to Setnayan is ~₱2–5 across all templates) but for design-effort, niche, or upgrade-signal reasons.
