# Setnayan — one way to do each thing (interaction rules)

Owner, verbatim (2026-09-30): *"how we ask questions for them to fill up, how we navigate and search around, how we interact. it should always feel the same but despite of its simplicity, we have the complex part of deciphering what to do"*.

**The promise:** every screen feels the same and simple; the hard part (what applies to this event, this person, this moment) is worked out by us, behind the screen — never handed to the user ("we handle the chaos").

## 1 · Asking
- One question per card. Title ≤5 words, one line ≤12 words, the answer, nothing else. Details behind ⓘ.
- Every question gets an answer; quick answers are allowed ("Not decided yet", "I'll add them later", "Use the defaults"). Everything is changeable later — say so once, small.
- We pre-fill whatever we already know (profile, event type, earlier answers). Never ask twice.
- Only ask what this event type needs (the event-type profile decides).

## 2 · Choosing
- 2 options → two buttons or a toggle. 3+ options → ONE dropdown (PickMenu). Never a row of pills.
- Yes/No settings → a switch. Several-of-many → a dropdown with checkmarks.

## 3 · Navigating
- About four things at every level: event menu (Home · Guests · Your Team · More Services), the Maker (the page · Page ▾ · Look · Details), the guest bar (Welcome · Details · Our Love Story · Me).
- Where you are is one dropdown (Page ▾, Guests ▾), never a wall of tabs.
- No "go edit it elsewhere ↗": the control is where you are.

## 4 · Searching
- The top bar searches the place you're in (this event's guests on the Guest list, the Maker's scenes in the Maker, everything on Home). The page itself only has "Add".

## 5 · Doing
- Tap what you see to change it (type in place). Changes show instantly; saving happens quietly behind.
- Success = a small toast. Failure = the plain reason + Try again, never looking like success.
- No "Are you sure?" — except for what can't be undone (delete, sign out erasing data, finalize), and then once.
- Phone first: fits 375 px without sideways scroll; one step per screen where steps exist.

## 6 · Behind the screen (our job, not theirs)
- The event-type profile decides stages, pages, scenes, guest-list parts, suggested suppliers and which questions appear.
- Defaults are chosen for them (the best one pre-selected); rules resolve silently (seats on the day, tagging only with Papic, no sides for a birthday).
- Every builder prompt links this file; a PR that adds a pill row, a confirm dialog, a second place for the same setting or a question we already know the answer to is a regression.

## 7 · Two looks, never mixed up
- **Setnayan look** (the host's tools — dashboard, Guest list, Your Team, the Maker's controls, onboarding's frame): warm paper, ink, gold + wine accents, serif for names/headings, sans for controls. Same everywhere. The dashboard may carry the event's cover + one accent colour at the top, nothing more.
- **The event's own theme** (everything a guest sees — the Event Hub, the landing page, RSVP, tickets, prints, guest emails): the couple's theme (background, fonts, colours, logo); tone follows the event type (a wake is quiet).
- **Where they meet:** the Maker's centre is the event's theme, its controls are the Setnayan look; onboarding starts in the Setnayan look and takes on the chosen event type's style once the type is picked.

## 8 · Phone editing: calm, full width, live preview, Apply publishes (owner, 2026-10-04)
Owner, verbatim: *"Remember our rules. 1. prevent to crowded presentation on mobile. 2. always maximize full width for body for easier editing. 3. Realtime effects for seeing what will change but always need to press apply to publish to the actual event hub"*
- **Not crowded on a phone:** one main thing per screen; few controls visible at once; the rest behind ONE dropdown or a sheet; no side-by-side panels at 375 px.
- **The body is full width:** the content being edited (the page, the form, the list) uses the screen's full width; controls go in slim top/bottom bars or a sheet over the page, never a column that narrows the body.
- **Live preview, Apply publishes:** every change shows on the page at once (realtime), and saves to the DRAFT only; guests see nothing until **Apply**. Opening a panel never writes.
- **One open at a time (auto-collapse):** opening a dropdown, ⋯ menu, popover or fold closes any other one open on the screen (owner 2026-10-04, verbatim: *"when a dropdown opens, the other dropdown collapses"* → *"auto collapse"*).
- **A record edits in place (Event Details, 2026-10-04):** a row opens the SAME field the Maker opens for that fact — on a phone in the half sheet over the page (the row scrolled into view above it; drag up for more; Peek; ×), on a desktop in place under the row — drafted, published at Apply. Opening a row is a plain link and writes nothing. A phone record with many rows is folded into a few groups (one line + summary each, one open at a time).
- **Sections inside a panel = ONE segmented control** (the Keynote/Pages pill group, `ISegmented`; selected segment filled in Setnayan wine): max 3 on a phone, 4 on desktop, one or two words each; never used to pick a value (values are dropdowns). Replaces every other tab style (owner 2026-10-04: *"segmented control"*).
- **Every tool lives in the thumb zone — the lower third (owner, 2026-10-05).** Verbatim: *"we only maximize the height on the lower third. and all tools can only reside on the thumb area/ lower third. Icons, toggles, function, editing tools and any features to be displayed on the lower third must be optimized for the lower third."* On a phone, every EDITING tool and feature — sheets, the scene strip, the Page ▾ / Look / Event Details bar, toggles, style pickers, any add or "···" menu — sits in the bottom third of the screen, sized for a thumb (≥ 44 px targets); the area above is the content/preview. **The top nav is NOT moved** (owner, same day: *"i haven't changed the top nav."*): title · undo · preview · Apply ✓ stay on top as shipped; only its leading "<" becomes the exit (door-with-arrow) icon. A sheet rests at the lower third and scrolls inside itself; it never grows up over the preview except by a deliberate drag. No NEW toolbar is added at the top (no Keynote-style icon pill).

## 9 · The templates — one source per kind of control (owner, 2026-10-08)
Owner, verbatim: *"so the idea is this will be a type of selector we will have and the design will be adapted from one source? so it will always be consistent?"* · *"so we want to map them properly. so when we decide to pill selector, it willuse our tempalte or any"*

**The promise:** every control on every screen is ONE of the kinds below, drawn by that kind's one source. Deciding "this is a pill selector" is the whole decision — the shape, the motion, the size and the behaviour come from the template, never from the page. A page that hand-makes its own copy is a regression.

**How a control gets its kind (the map):** every prototype's map card and every builder's PR lists each control on the screen as `control → kind`. If a control fits no kind, stop and ask — do not invent a seventh on a page.

| Kind | Use it when | Never use it for | The one source | State on 2026-10-08 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pill selector** | switching between 2–3 SECTIONS of a screen (4 on desktop); a 2-way value toggle | picking one of 3+ values (that is a Dropdown) | `PillSelector` / `PillThumb` in `apps/web/app/_components/pill-selector.tsx` (+ `pill-thumb.tsx`); `ISegmented`/`ISeg`, `Phases` and the Stages tool group draw it (local commit, not yet on main) | full pill, the thumb SLIDES (transform, ~220 ms, instant under reduced motion). Audit: ~100 hand-made selectors outside the Maker, ~40% rounded rectangles; `.sn-seg` (the June template) had zero users |
| **Dropdown** | one of 3+ values; "where you are" | anything that steps to the next value on tap | `PickMenu` | shipped, ~158 uses. A ▾ ALWAYS opens its choices; one open at a time |
| **Switch** | on / off | a choice between two named things (that is a 2-way Pill selector) | shared source to be confirmed (`role="switch"` in ~34 files) | the knob travels, it does not jump |
| **Page card** | a door to a page (the Studio home) | a style or picture choice | being designed: mark · topic · one-line description · a small ⓘ beside (not inside) the card | `StudioHome` tiles today; prototype pending |
| **Style card** | choosing a look by its picture (background, scene, effect, theme) | text-only choices | `.sn-phone-card` (3:4) | shipped in the Maker; a tap is answered at once |
| **Form row** | one fact to type or pick in a form | — | being defined on Studio › Info: label left, value or control right, ⓘ beside the label | prototype pending; the other Studio pages reuse it |
| **ⓘ explanation** | the longer "what is this" | anything the user must read to proceed | shared source to be confirmed | its own 44 px target; one open at a time |

**The watch:** each kind gets a check that fails when a page draws its own (a source scan, using `lib/strip-comments.ts`), added in the same PR that makes the kind app-wide. "To be confirmed" above means nobody has measured it yet — measure before citing it.

**Owner's look-and-feel rulings on the templates (2026-10-08, from the live gallery `review/templates.html` on the local copy), verbatim:** *"let's add a bit of bounce and a pulse to imitate it has been pressed"* · *"Terracota is our color? and greyed out when off?"* · *"pill selector should have a consistent color"* · *"Dropdown — Chevron should be teracota color?"* · *"desktop shows dropdown / mobile peeks from bottom to pick?"* · *"Improve Page Card so it looks like an app button that feels consistent with our design"* · *"Style Card should be limited to that height and width. Adjust it always to that. no framing"* · *"Selected Card needs to be highlighted with same terracota"* · *"we want the animation to be in between normal and slow motion for its animation speed"* · *"form row with date"*.
- **One colour:** what is ON or PICKED is terracotta (white ink on it); what is off is grey. Every pill selector the same — this replaces "selected segment filled in Setnayan wine" in § 8 and the white/ink thumbs. The dropdown's chevron and its ticked choice are terracotta; a picked Style card wears a terracotta ring and label (not gold).
- **Motion:** a pick lands with a small bounce and pulses once; speed is "in between" — about twice the first build (the pill travels in roughly half a second). One duration token for the family. Nothing moves under reduced motion.
- **Dropdown:** on a phone the choices rise from the bottom as a sheet; on a desktop they open as a list under the button. A date in a Form row opens the same way, holding a calendar.
- **Style card:** one fixed size everywhere, the picture edge to edge, no frame.
- **Page card:** reads and presses like an app button (the mark as an app-icon tile).
- **Switch:** terracotta when on, grey when off — owner, verbatim: *"switch is teracota or greyed out"* (after first asking about green/red and seeing both). The knob travels and lands with the same small bounce.
- **ⓘ explanation:** on a phone it is a popup in the centre of the screen with one "Got it" button; on a desktop a small note beside the ⓘ — owner: *"explanation on what you show is for desktop / there is an appropriate info like a center screen popup?"*.
- **Form row, editing a typed answer:** tapping the answer opens the field across the WHOLE row (the row's name stays above it as a small caption), with a round ✕ (leave it as it was; Esc) and a round terracotta ✓ (keep it; Enter) at the right — owner: *"when we click to edit. it will fill up the whole width with a (X) (v)?"*. Then "Saved" beside the answer; a failure says so with Try again.
- **Stages tool icons (owner's pick, "S5 T4 A5"):** Style = swatch-book · Text = a-large-small · Animate = orbit.
- **Speed, settled:** the family's travel time is **0.7 seconds** — owner tried Fast (0.23 s), Between (0.46 s) and Slow (0.92 s), said *"slow feels better"*, the controller advised that a full second per tap would feel like waiting in daily use and proposed 0.7 s; owner: *"1. 0.7 seconds"*. One duration token (`--sn-pill-dur`).
- **Style card while a file loads (owner: *"when pressed. show a loading screen 0-100 pie to know how long til it uploads"* · *"when we are picking and the option is not yet done loading, nothing can be pressed"*; and his *"yes"* to both of the controller's limits):** a pie fills 0→100 on the card, then a tick — ONLY where progress is really measured (a picture, a film, an upload); a plain save shows its words ("Applying to your Hub…") and never an invented percentage. While one card loads, the other cards in that strip dim and cannot be pressed; tapping the loading card cancels and nothing changes; after 8 seconds it offers Try again / Cancel. Only that strip locks — never the whole screen, the top bar or Exit.
- **Where the templates apply — everywhere but one place (owner, 2026-10-08):** *"we want the whole app to be adaptive to the same feel"* · *"the only part that does not follow our rules is their customized event hub"*. The templates (shape, terracotta/grey, the press, the 0.7 s speed) are worn by every Setnayan-look surface: couple dashboard, supplier dashboard, onboarding and sign-in, public pages, admin. The ONE exemption is the couple's customised Event Hub that guests see (`app/[slug]`): it wears the couple's own theme and palette (§ 7). A new surface gets the feel by default; the exemption is one named selector, not a list of included areas.
- **More template rulings (owner, 2026-10-08, evening):** *"should dropdown have a consistent width?"* → dropdowns in the same list share one width (a few standard sizes: short · wide · full row; never one width for the whole app), the answer truncates with "…", the arrow is pinned right. *"the collapse should also animate how the Row Name returns"* → closing an edited Form row is animated too: the field folds back to the right, the row's name slides back in from the left. *"pill box not rounded edge"* → a single-line field is a full pill (the same shape as the dropdown button); a multi-line text box cannot be a pill and wears the large rounded corner instead. **Calendar** is the eighth kind: one look for three uses — pick one day (terracotta circle), pick a range (the days between fill softly), see what is on (a dot under the day; tap a day for its list) — drawn in `review/templates.html` § 8, awaiting his look.
- **Anything popped up over the page (owner, 2026-10-08):** *"when there is a pop up. the rest of the screen darkens (except for when there is preview) i think you know what I mean. The darkened area will be blurred and nothing behind it will work. pressing on the dark part removes the pop up. the background will not be scrollable when darkened blurred"*. ⇒ a sheet, a centred explanation, a confirm box: the rest of the screen is dark AND blurred, nothing behind can be pressed, the page behind does not scroll, and tapping the dark part closes it (a confirm box closes as "keep", never as the destructive answer). **Exception — a preview stays clear:** where the screen holds a live preview of what is being edited (Studio › Look's sample), the preview is not darkened or blurred, so a pick in the sheet is seen at once; only the rest darkens. Controller's readings, not his words: a desktop list opening under its own button and a desktop note beside its ⓘ are attached to their control and do not darken the page; a toast never darkens (the user keeps working); where blur is unsupported or the phone is in Low Power, the dark alone is used.
- **Four more kinds drawn in the gallery, awaiting his look (not approved):** Action button (main terracotta · second · quiet · delete · icon-only; pill; shows it is working, then Saved), Field (name above, pill box, plain-words problem underneath; ₱ amount, search with clear, long text with large corners), Ticks · chips · badges, Messages (toast · status line · confirm box). Measured the same day (`controller-2026-10-08/CONTROL-INVENTORY.md`): the app has no single source for the action button's look (~670 files), fields (~550), status lines (~290), back controls (133), chips/badges (~130), checkboxes/radios (~130); `.button-primary` paints GOLD on the dashboards, terracotta on public pages and the couple's colour on guest pages, and the token names are inverted (`bg-mulberry` = terracotta, `bg-terracotta-*` = gold) — the main button's colour needs his ruling.
- **Form row — every answer is the same pill (owner, 2026-10-08: *"do you have a better approach for this? to make it consistent to the design?"*, on a typed answer drawn as plain text + the word "Edit"):** drawn in the gallery, awaiting his look — a typed answer is the SAME white pill, the same width as the dropdown and the date in its list, with one small terracotta mark at its right that says what a tap does: a pencil = type · an arrow = choose · a calendar = pick a date. After a save the pencil becomes a tick for a moment (no separate "Saved" word in the row).
- **Schedule row (owner's idea, 2026-10-08: *"i though of a good way to create the schedule maker. tap the time start and time end and name of that schedule"*):** drawn in the gallery § 13, awaiting his look — one row per moment: start pill · end pill · name. Rule 0: Studio › Schedule already draws `time pill · moment · place · For ▾ · ⋯` with the LENGTH hidden behind ⋯ (`studio-day.tsx`, `MomentInspector`); the delta is the END time shown and tappable in the row. As drawn: times open as the dropdown's sheet/list scrolled to the current one; moving the start moves the end with it (length kept); the end list names the length each choice gives; rows sort by start; an overlap is said in one amber line, never blocked; "Add a moment" starts where the last one ended, one hour long, name ready to type. Place and For ▾ are not in the idea — where they go is open.
- **Toast = a notification from the TOP (owner, 2026-10-08):** *"pop up is like a notification. so it should peek from the top"* (and, on the first drawing, *"the peek on the bottom of the screen saying deleted is not centered properly"*). ⇒ a toast peeks down from the top centre of the screen, its words centred, stays a moment and leaves by itself; it never darkens the page. A failure toast is red and carries Try again; the lasting copy of a failure is the status line on the page. To measure before building: where `useToast` (`app/_components/toast/toast-provider.tsx`, ~35 files) shows today.
- **A field IS a Form row (owner, 2026-10-08: *"field follow form row style"*):** there is no second look for typing. Changing something already there = the row's pill with a pencil, opening across the row with ✕ ✓ (an amount keeps its ₱; a long message opens a taller box under its name). Filling in something new = the same rows with the boxes already open, so a person types straight down the list. Search is its own bar, not a row. **Required (owner: *"highlighted text box are required"*):** a box that must be filled is highlighted in terracotta and says "Required" beside its name (colour alone is never the only sign); the highlight leaves once it is filled; the screen's main button wakes only when every required box is done. **Red is kept for something that is WRONG** — said in plain words under the row — never for "not filled yet". Controller's advice given to him: tap-to-open rows suit changing a few facts; a first-time form of several boxes should be already open, or every box costs an extra tap. Drawn in the gallery § 10, awaiting his look.
- **Timeline row + the time ticker (owner, 2026-10-08):** on the schedule idea's first drawing (a list of every quarter-hour) — *"the popup exceeded the screen when it went up on schedule row. center the time. how about a ticker instead so it does not eat too much space"* → a time is picked on a small TICKER: hour · minute (5-minute steps) · AM/PM, the chosen value in a centre band, the row behind changing as it rolls, one line above saying start – end · length, and Done. **No sheet is ever taller than the screen** (a small gap stays at the top; long content scrolls inside the sheet). *"can also be love story form"* → the row is one kind, **Timeline row** = `when · name`: the Schedule wears it as start · end · name; the Love Story as month-and-year (the same ticker) · chapter · a small square for its photo. Chips: *"consistent size? or adaptive?"* → one height, a minimum width, the width follows the word, and a chip never changes size when chosen. All drawn in the gallery (§ 11, § 13), awaiting his look. Rule 0 for the builder: Studio › Schedule (`studio-day.tsx`) and Studio › Love Story already exist — this is a new row for them, not new pages.
- **A Love Story chapter's "when" is as exact as the couple wants (owner, 2026-10-08: *"add optional for the day it can be month and year only or month year and day or year only"*):** the ticker carries a three-way pill — Year · Month · Full date — and shows only the rollers that precision needs; the row reads "2019", "Jun 2019" or "Feb 14, 2021"; chapters sort by what they have (a year-only chapter sorts at the start of its year). For the builder: check how the shipped Love Story stores a chapter's date before adding anything — a precision must be stored without inventing a day (never save "June 1" for "June"). **Icons (owner, on a share arrow he read as upload: *"this is upload photo?"*):** an icon with no word is only for marks everyone reads the same way (⋯ more, ✕ close, ‹ back, ＋ add); any other action carries its word. A photo slot is a small picture square that shows the upload as a 0–100 pie, then the photo.
