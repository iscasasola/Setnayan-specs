Iteration 0010 · What ships

Mood Board V1 — Palettes + Setnayan Guide

The couple defines colors for every role on their guest list and every part of their venue. The Setnayan Guide engine reviews the full set on every change, surfaces warnings and contradicts when palettes won't work together, and suggests alternatives grounded in color theory and wedding photography best practice. Couple can override the Guide per palette or globally.

The 7 rule categories

01 · cohesion

Themed, not rainbow

Hard limit 8 unique colors across the master palette. Soft target 60-30-10 distribution: one dominant color 60%, one secondary 30%, accents in remaining 10%.

02 · contrast

Newlyweds must pop

Newlyweds ≥ 4.5:1 vs venue dominant; parents ≥ 3.5; BM/MoH ≥ 3.0; Teams + Secondary Sponsors ≥ 2.5; guests no minimum. Inversions caught (no role above their tier).

03 · proximity

Background ≠ foreground

No role color may match a venue color within ΔE 10 (CIE76 perceptual distance). Catches "ivory bride against ivory wall" before photos suffer.

04 · temperature

Pick a lane

Warns when a single palette mixes more than 2 warm + 2 cool colors. Strong cohesion comes from a clear temperature direction.

05 · saturation

Loudest at the focal point

Venue dominant saturation > 70 warns. Guest-tier palettes saturated > 80 warns — guests shouldn't be louder than the focal points.

06 · photography

No skin-cast walls

Saturated red, magenta, chartreuse walls cast onto skin tones in photos. Engine warns when venue dominant lands in those wavelengths with high saturation.

07 · cultural

PH wedding traditions

Bride defaults to white/ivory unless explicitly chosen otherwise. Secondary sponsors pre-fill paired coordination (candle/veil/cord/coin).

override · per couple

Disable Setnayan Guide

Default ON. Per-palette override via "Use this color anyway." Per-event toggle to disable globally — ambient indicator shows when the Guide is off.

Acceptance criteria

Every guest-list role gets an editable palette with up to 6 colors
Default venue palettes (Church, Reception, Cocktail) plus custom venue palettes with free-form labels
Color input: hex, native picker, name autocomplete, image extraction
Image extraction returns 5–8 dominant colors from JPEG/PNG/HEIC
20 pre-templates organized in Style / Color-led / Seasonal tabs; picking one seeds all palettes
Setnayan Guide engine implements all 7 rule categories
Warn = yellow inline banner + suggestions; contradict = red modal blocking save
Disable Setnayan Guide toggle (per-event, default ON) silences engine globally
Per-palette override flag set when couple uses "Use this color anyway"
Master palette dedupes by exact hex AND named color; "Replace across all" works
Reference images attachable per palette (1–3 in R2)
