## 5 hardest (+ quick win)
- Vendor Verification (12-document checklist) — diff 5. Quick win: visually separate the 8 'your action' upload slots from the 4 'we handle this' admin-run slots into two groups so 8/12 reads as 'all 8 of your items', and add a one-line upfront 'documents to gather' summary so vendors collect everything before starting the draft.
- Vendor Services / Packages builder — diff 5. Quick win: collapse the per-service config (currently three forms with three save buttons) into one save scope with progressive-disclosure accordions (Basics / Pricing extras / Availability / Bundling) so there is a single 'Save service' action and advanced fields stay hidden until expanded.
- Couple Seating editor — diff 5. Quick win: add a first-run interactive overlay/legend that names the gestures (drag=move, two-finger/handle=rotate, tap=select, pick-then-tap=seat) and make the Save-vs-Publish split explicit ('Save keeps your work; Publish generates the printable signs'); the MiniTour narrates but the gesture grammar still has to be discovered live.
- Admin Taxonomy & vocab management — diff 5. Quick win: add a confirm-with-summary step (the ConfirmForm pattern already used in event-types) to the cascade-effecting actions (remapCanonical, moveTaxonomyNode, deleteTaxonomyNode, setCategoryEventTypes/setFolderEventTypes) stating in plain English what changes app-wide, and co-locate the five vocab tools under one 'Taxonomy & Vocabulary' sidebar sub-group.
- Couple Orders / apply-then-pay checkout — diff 4 (hardest couple money path). Quick win: show the exact amount + reference code to one-tap copy AT the QR step, and after submit keep the buyer in-context with a 'what happens next + when' status timeline instead of bouncing them to a separate order-detail route to learn they must wait for reconciliation.

- Guest landing page — RSVP (diff 2): three big tap targets, progressive selfie reveal, smart defaults, clear 'place reserved' confirmation.
- Couple Dashboard home (diff 2): clean, well-prioritized single-column cockpit; just scan and tap one next action.
- Admin Real Stories curation (diff 2): per-story Featured toggle + a numeric rank field; simplest surface in the console.
- Vendor Reviews (diff 2): mostly passive/read-mostly; view stats and optionally post a public reply.
- Couple Explore + vendor profile (diff 2): search-first with progressively-disclosed filters and a 1-tap inquiry.
- Guest Day-of mode (diff 2): auto-activating, mostly passive live experience with 0-1 taps.

## Biggest quick wins
- Always show token costs on the action button BEFORE commit, not in success/error notices or fine print. This single pattern fix lowers two vendor surfaces at once: 'Accept inquiry' (e.g. 'Accept · 2 tokens (₱200)' or 'Accept · free, 3 of 10 left') and 'Import outside client' on the Calendar (1-token cost on the button). Removes the most error-prone-per-tap vendor friction for near-zero effort.
- Add a confirmation/diff guard to single-click destructive and cascade actions across the admin console — only 7 of ~40 surfaces have one today. Reusing the existing ConfirmForm pattern on taxonomy remap/move/delete/scope, payment Approve/Reject/Refund, pricing edits (echo the live VAT-inclusive price + 'was ₱X'), and social Post-now/Pull lowers four admin surfaces with one reused component.
- Add a real search input to /help. The article corpus already exists in lib/help.ts, so it is a UI-only addition that closes the metadata-vs-reality gap and fixes the single biggest discoverability gap across all surfaces — drops Help from diff 3 cheaply.
- Default the guest Join role to 'Guest' (17 ceremonial roles behind a 'My role is special' expander) and let low-risk name matches skip the 6-digit OTP (or use a magic link). Turns the common 'I'm just a guest' case from ~6 steps into 2 — the highest-impact guest-funnel fix.
- Show a truthful, branch-aware progress/time estimate on the onboarding wizard (the FLOW_TOTAL=17 denominator understates the live 34-screen path) and hard-cap a usable 'core plan' path, deferring love-story + per-leaf refinement passes to optional post-commit prompts. Directly attacks the length/time-to-finish that drives onboarding's difficulty and the pre-account drop-off risk.

## Note
Consolidated from 5 read-only expert-heuristic audits at commit 09ec9601 (admin cluster read at HEAD 3d7e0cf); NOT user-tested, so latency and real-data-volume effects are unverified. 33 surfaces total, sorted hardest-first (4 at diff 5, 7 at diff 4, 12 at diff 3, 6 at diff 2). \n\nWHERE HIGH DIFFICULTY CONFLICTS WITH THE 'SIMPLE, GUIDED' GOAL — the audits surface a clear pattern that the hardest surfaces are exactly where the product promises to hold the user's hand: (1) The Onboarding wizard is the literal embodiment of 'simple, guided' yet its 34-screen FLOW_IDS path (~18-20 min minimum, 30+ full) risks pre-account fatigue/drop-off before the user ever reaches value — and the progress denominator (FLOW_TOTAL=17) under-reports the real length, so the 'few minutes, mostly tapping' framing is not truthful. (2) The Seating editor (diff 5) is a desktop-class spatial canvas with an undiscoverable gesture grammar and a silent lock that demotes you to view-only mid-edit — the antithesis of guided, and its MiniTour narrates without teaching the gestures. (3) Couple money paths (Orders apply-then-pay diff 4) force an unavoidable manual cross-app transfer + screenshot-proof + up-to-1-business-day human reconciliation, which structurally cannot feel 'simple' until automated reconciliation (the V1.5 roadmap) lands. (4) Vendor onboarding has NO first-run wizard at all — Registration (diff 4), Verification (diff 5), and the Services builder (diff 5) all drop a brand-new vendor onto dense, multi-form walls and make them self-discover gating fields and submit thresholds, which contradicts a guided ethos on the side of the market the product most needs to recruit. (5) A cross-cutting trust/clarity gap undercuts 'guided' on two fronts: vendor token costs are hidden until after an irreversible tap, and ~33 of ~40 admin actions fire destructively/cascade on a single click with no confirm or diff. The cheapest highest-leverage fixes (cost-on-button, reuse ConfirmForm, add /help search, default guest role + skip OTP, truthful onboarding progress) are mostly UI-only and reuse patterns that already exist elsewhere in the codebase.
