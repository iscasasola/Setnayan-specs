Getting Started

Setnayan is a Philippines-first life-events platform. The same app serves couples who plan events, vendors who offer services, guests who attend celebrations, and the Setnayan HQ team who run the platform. Everyone enters through setnayan.com — the app routes each person to the right place after sign-in.

Creating an account

  1. Go to setnayan.com and tap Create account.
  2. Enter your full name and email. Your name auto-formats to Philippine-style title case as you type.
  3. Choose a password (minimum 8 characters) or continue with Google or Facebook.
  4. Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link to activate your account.
  5. After verification you land back on the app and the role-router decides where to send you (see below).
Tip Vendors register separately by going to setnayan.com/signup?as=vendor (or clicking "Register as a vendor" on the homepage). This gives you the vendor-specific onboarding flow instead of the couple flow.

Signing in

Visit setnayan.com and tap Sign in. You can sign in with:

  • Email + password — the most common method
  • Magic link — enter your email, receive a one-click sign-in link (no password needed)
  • Google or Facebook OAuth

Once signed in, Setnayan checks your account type and sends you to the right place.

Couples & Customers

Couples use Setnayan to plan events end-to-end — from the first guest invite to the final seating chart and post-wedding gallery. Everything is free unless you choose to add a paid Setnayan service. The dashboard is organized around one event at a time, accessible at setnayan.com/dashboard/[event-id]/.

Creating your first event

  1. After sign-in, tap "Create event" on the welcome screen (or the + button in the event picker).
  2. Swipe through the event type carousel — Wedding, Debut, Birthday, Celebration, Travel, Corporate, Tournament, Christening, Gender Reveal — and tap your event type.
  3. Wedding taps route into a short onboarding flow that asks about your couple, faith tradition, venue style, guest count, and what services you'd love. All other event types jump straight to a name field.
  4. Type your event name. For weddings this auto-fills as "[Name]'s Wedding".
  5. Tap Create. You land on your event Overview.
Wedding onboarding tip The onboarding questions personalize your vendor search and which services appear in your dashboard. Answer honestly — you can change these later in Settings. The more you share, the more tailored your planning experience.

Overview — your event home

The Overview is the first thing you see when you open your event. It shows:

  • Countdown — days until your event date
  • What's next — deadline-aware checklist cards pulled from your planning timeline
  • Active services — any Setnayan digital services you've purchased, with their current status
  • Planning artifacts — quick links to your mood board, vendor contracts, QR codes, and print pack
  • Activity feed — recent events (vendor messages, RSVP arrivals, payment confirmations)

Guests

The Guests surface is your full invite list. You can:

  • Add a guest — enter name, email (optional), phone (optional), and their role (bride-side / groom-side / family / VIP / coordinator / vendor crew / etc.)
  • Track RSVP status — Pending · Attending · Declined · Waitlisted
  • Manage plus-ones — attach a "+1" to any guest; plus-ones get their own RSVP tracking
  • Assign tables — link each guest to a table from the Seat Plan
  • Filter & sort — by role, RSVP status, table, or side
  • Send invitations — generate personal invite links / QR codes per guest from the QR Hub
How guests receive their invite Each guest gets a personal URL (setnayan.com/[your-slug]?invite=[token]) sent by you via your preferred channel (Viber, email, etc.). Tapping that link opens their personal event page and RSVP form — no Setnayan account required.

Vendors

This is your vendor registry — a list of every service provider you've hired for your event.

Finding and hiring a vendor

  1. Tap Find a vendor (or browse via the in-dashboard vendor search).
  2. Choose a category (e.g., Photography, Catering, Florals) and apply filters like city, budget range, and style.
  3. Tap a vendor card to see their full profile, services, and portfolio.
  4. Tap Inquire to start a conversation. This opens a chat thread with that vendor.
  5. Once you agree on terms, the vendor appears in your Vendors list as a booked hire.

Managing a booked vendor

Each booked vendor card shows:

  • Their service category, package chosen, and total price
  • Payment milestones — log each payment with an amount, date, and proof of payment (screenshot upload)
  • Crew meals & transportation — computed from vendor headcount
  • Contracts — attach a signed contract (PDF) stored securely on R2
  • Message — opens the in-app chat thread with that vendor
Important: Setnayan does not hold your money All payments to vendors happen outside Setnayan — via bank transfer, GCash, or whatever the vendor prefers. Setnayan only tracks payment records you log manually. Setnayan never processes vendor payments.

Schedule

The Schedule surface has two parts:

  • Day-of timeline — your event program by the hour (ceremony start, reception, program items, etc.)
  • Upcoming meetings — vendor appointment reminders in chronological order

Tap Export .ics to download a calendar file compatible with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook. Share it with your vendors or coordinator.

Seat Plan

The Seat Plan editor lets you design your reception layout and assign guests to specific seats.

  • Choose from 13 table types: Round 8/10/12 · Long Banquet 6/8/10 · Family Head 12/14/16 · Sweetheart 2 · Serpentine 6/12/18
  • Drag tables around the floor plan canvas to place them
  • Click a table to assign guests to it (search by name or role)
  • Add a stage marker and head-table area
  • When ready, tap Publish — this generates one QR code per table
  • Download the Print Pack (PDF) — printable QR table cards for your event

On mobile, the canvas shows tables only (chairs are too dense at phone resolution). Tap a table to see its guest list as a sheet.

On desktop, each chair is shown with the guest's profile photo or role-coded initials.

Landing Page (Your Event Website)

Every event on Setnayan gets a free public event website at a URL like setnayan.com/[your-slug]. This is the page you share with guests instead of a traditional paper invitation.

The website has up to 4 phases that unlock over time:

  • Save the Date — the first public reveal: couple names, date, venue teaser
  • RSVP — guests can confirm attendance, add dietary preferences, and optionally submit a face photo for auto-tagging at the event
  • Event — full event details, program, venue map, couple story
  • Editorial — post-event: photos, video highlights, and guest tributes living together as a keepsake page

From the Landing Page editor you can:

  • Toggle widgets on/off (countdown, RSVP form, our story, gallery, etc.)
  • Pick a theme (color palette auto-suggested from your Mood Board)
  • Preview the live page before sharing
  • See page-view analytics

Gallery

The Gallery is where all your event media lives after the event. Before the event it shows placeholder explainers for each section. After the event:

  • Papic photos — every candid photo taken by your Papic paparazzi, organized by tagger, table, and guest tag
  • Panood recording — the full livestream recording if you activated Panood
  • Patiktok reels — guest-created short-form video clips
  • Video messages — tributes recorded via the voice/video guestbook
  • AI Highlights — automatically produced highlight videos (if purchased)

There is a 7-day couple-review window after the event before the gallery unlocks for guests. You can extend or shorten this in Gallery settings.

How to pay for Setnayan services

Setnayan uses an apply-then-pay model. There is no automatic card charge anywhere.

  1. Find a service in the Services tab and tap Avail (or the service's order button).
  2. A checkout drawer appears showing the price and Setnayan's BDO or GCash receiving details.
  3. Pay externally via BDO bank transfer or GCash using the reference code shown.
  4. Come back to the drawer and upload your payment screenshot as proof.
  5. The Setnayan team reviews and approves within 24 hours. You'll get an email confirmation when your service activates.
Check your email Setnayan sends you an email at every key step: payment instructions, payment confirmed, service activated. Make sure notifications@setnayan.com is not in your spam folder.

Vendors

Vendors are the event service professionals on Setnayan — photographers, caterers, florists, coordinators, and every other category. Setnayan charges 0% commission. Vendors keep 100% of what they earn. Setnayan's revenue from vendors comes from optional subscription tiers and token packs, not a cut of bookings.

Registering as a vendor

  1. Go to setnayan.com/signup?as=vendor (or click "Register as a vendor" on the homepage).
  2. Enter your name, email, and a password.
  3. Fill in your business profile: company name, category (up to 1 on Free tier), region/city, bio, and upload at least one service photo.
  4. Add at least one service package with a price range, description, and inclusions.
  5. Set your calendar availability — block dates you're unavailable.
  6. Submit for verification. The Setnayan team reviews your profile within 24 hours. You get an email when approved.
  7. Once verified you receive 100 free tokens (a welcome bonus valid until 31 January 2027) and your profile goes live on the marketplace.

Understanding tokens

Tokens are Setnayan's bidding currency for vendors. They let you respond to couple inquiries. Setnayan earns from token packs, not commissions — your booking revenue is 100% yours.

Token packPriceCost per token
4 tokens₱400₱100/token
10 tokens₱1,000₱100/token
25 tokens₱2,500₱100/token
50 tokens₱5,000₱100/token
100 tokens₱10,000₱100/token

When you accept a couple's inquiry, Setnayan burns 1–3 tokens from your balance depending on the wedding's region (Metro Manila = 3 tokens · provincial = 1–2 tokens). You get one unlock per couple-event pair — you're not charged again if the same couple messages you more.

Free starter balance Every verified vendor receives 100 free tokens as a welcome bonus. That's enough to accept up to 100 inquiries for provincial weddings before you need to buy a pack.

Getting verified

A verified vendor gets a verification badge on their profile, earns 100 free tokens, and shows up in couple searches. To get verified:

  1. Go to Business → Verify in your vendor dashboard.
  2. Complete the 12-item verification checklist: valid ID, business registration, sample work, etc.
  3. Submit. The Setnayan team reviews within 24 hours.
  4. You get an email with the result. If declined, the reason is shown and you can resubmit.

Guests

Guests do not need a Setnayan account. Everything a guest does on Setnayan happens through their personal invite link, which the couple sends via Viber, email, or any messaging app.

Accessing Setnayan as a guest

You receive a link that looks like: setnayan.com/[couple-slug]?invite=[your-token]

Tapping this link opens your personal event page. Your token is unique to you — it gives you access to RSVP, see your table, and access your photos from the event. It expires after 60 days past the event date.

Keep your invite link This link is your key to everything on Setnayan for this event. Save it in your notes or bookmark it. If you lose it, ask the couple to resend your personal invite.

RSVP-ing

  1. Tap your invite link and land on the event RSVP page.
  2. Confirm your attendance: Attending or Not attending.
  3. If attending: enter any dietary preferences or special requests (optional).
  4. You can optionally upload a face photo — this helps the Papic system automatically tag you in candid photos taken at the event.
  5. Tap Submit RSVP. The couple is notified of your response.

Day-of experience

On your event day (and 1 hour before it starts), the event page shifts into live mode. You'll see:

  • What's happening — current program item on the event timeline
  • Your table — which table you're seated at and who's with you
  • Live photo wall — real-time feed of Papic candid photos as they're captured
  • Video guestbook — record a short tribute video for the couple
  • Live schedule — the full event program in order
  • Coordinator updates — announcements broadcast by the event coordinator

After the event, your personal photo gallery unlocks (after the couple's 7-day review period). You can download photos you're tagged in and even build a personal highlight reel (1–30 seconds) from templates.

Watching from abroad? If the couple activated Panood (the Setnayan livestream service), there will be a live stream link on the event page. You can watch the wedding in real time from anywhere in the world.

Admin — Setnayan HQ

The admin console is the internal operations dashboard for the Setnayan team. It lives at setnayan.com/admin and has ~53 routes. Access is gated to accounts flagged as team members in the platform settings. Setnayan HQ staff never appear to couples or vendors as individuals — all team-member communication uses Setnayan branding.

Getting access

To add a new team member:

  1. An existing admin goes to Settings → Platform Settings → Admin Roles.
  2. Enter the new team member's email address.
  3. The person signs up (or already has an account) at setnayan.com.
  4. Their account is flagged is_team_member = true by the admin.
  5. On their next sign-in they are routed to /admin instead of the couple dashboard.
Major actions require two-admin approval Certain decisions (large refunds, permanent account bans, platform pricing changes above a threshold) require a second admin to co-approve. The two-admin queue is in Platform Settings.

Key admin workflows

Workflow 1 — Verify a new vendor

  1. Open Queues → Verification (/admin/verify).
  2. Tap the pending vendor to open their submission.
  3. Review the 12-item checklist: valid government ID, DTI/SEC registration, sample portfolio, service description quality.
  4. Tap Approve → vendor badge activates + 100 free tokens are credited + vendor profile goes live.
  5. If issues exist, tap Decline and write a reason. The vendor receives an email with the decline reason and can resubmit.

Workflow 2 — Approve a customer payment

  1. Open Queues → Payments (/admin/payments).
  2. Find the pending payment — the couple name, service ordered, amount, and reference code are shown.
  3. Open your BDO or GCash inbox and check for a matching transaction with the same reference code and amount.
  4. Tap Approve if it matches → order flips to "Paid," service activates, couple gets a confirmation email.
  5. If the screenshot doesn't match (wrong amount, unreadable), tap Reject — needs more proof. The couple can resubmit. If permanently rejected, use Reject permanently.

Workflow 3 — Resolve a dispute

  1. Open Queues → Disputes (/admin/disputes).
  2. Read the dispute claim — the couple's report, the vendor's response (if any), and any evidence attached.
  3. Contact both parties through the Help Inbox if you need more information.
  4. Tap Resolve inline and write your resolution note. The outcome is logged and both parties are notified by email.

Workflow 4 — Grant a comp (free service) to a couple

  1. Go to Directory → Users and find the couple's account.
  2. Open their profile and tap Issue comp grant.
  3. Select the SKU you want to comp and confirm.
  4. The order is created in "paid" state immediately — no payment required from the couple. The service activates right away.

Workflow 5 — Manage the token burn bands

  1. Go to Money → Token Bands (/admin/token-bands).
  2. The table shows each Philippine region and its assigned token burn rate (1, 2, or 3 tokens).
  3. Edit a region's band and save. The new rate takes effect immediately for all new inquiry-accepts in that region.
  4. Communicate any changes to the vendor community via the platform notifications or email.
