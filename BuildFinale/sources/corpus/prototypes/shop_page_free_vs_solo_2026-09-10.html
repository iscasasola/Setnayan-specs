The universal shop page · the same design, drawn on Free (₱0) and on Solo · not a new page

What ₱0 shows, and what Solo adds

Your words on 10 September: “we want a different look for free and solo. We want minimum website function for free. so they would want to go solo.” This drawing is the corrected universal page (P1) rendered twice, side by side — a Free shop and the same shop on Solo. Free keeps everything that makes the page true and trustworthy; Solo buys the shop its voice and its presentation. Couples never see a padlock; the pull to Solo is shown to the shop alone, in its own editor and its own preview (section D).

Read this before the frames — measured on 10 Sep against the running code, the live catalog and the live plans page.
  • Prices are read, not typed. The live plans page prints Free · Verified — ₱0 forever and Solo — ₱1,000 / 28 days; the production catalog row for Solo is ₱1,000.00. Pro (₱2,500 / 28 days) and Enterprise (₱10,000 / 28 days) are not drawn here, and nothing moves between paid plans — those prices are locked.
  • ₱0 means both Free and Verified. In the code they share one website gate (both sit at rank 0; Solo is where personalising starts), so they get one and the same minimal look. A shop is only public after its papers are checked, so every ₱0 page you see here is a verified shop.
  • Most of the Free-to-Solo difference already ships. About, the accent, which service leads, and choosing what shows are Solo-and-up today. Two caps also ship: two service cards per category on Free, three on Solo; thirty portfolio photos on Free, fifty on Solo. This drawing adds one presentation proposal beyond that (how their work is laid out), marked as a proposal everywhere it appears.
  • Two things a Free shop is NOT denied, and this drawing does not pretend otherwise: a Free shop can be messaged (your 24 July lock: the inbox is never locked) and a Free shop is findable in the marketplace (the search gate has nothing enforcing it). Solo is not sold on either.
  • The stock photo is your open question. On 4 June you ordered the placeholder photo onto real shops; today it shows on every plan when a shop has no photos. Section E draws both options on both plans and decides nothing. “No zero” and “no empty chart” ride with the same question.
  • The pull to Solo lives with the shop, never with couples. The public page carries no lock and no upgrade line; the “what Solo adds” preview sits in My Shop → Website and in the shop’s own preview of its page.
The one sentence: on Free the page is composed by Setnayan from facts — the card, the receipt, the prices, the door; on Solo the shop takes the pen — its own words, its own colour, its own order. Nothing true is withheld from a couple on either plan.

Identical on Free and Solo — what a couple is allowed to know never changes by plan

  • The card: their mark, their name in the serif, what they do and where, the two fact lines, the tagline.
  • The receipt: what a person at Setnayan checked, dated; the Verified line; “New to Setnayan” on day one.
  • What they offer: chips, service cards with prices live from their catalog, the package, Works with, Details, the map row, the first question ready to send.
  • Their work: every photo, every featured video (videos are on every plan since July), the category’s own noun — set list, menus, the space. Only the layout differs (the proposal above).
  • Their record: reviews (never tiered, never hideable), where they have worked, stories they are credited in, the shops that work with them.
  • Fold 5 and fold 6: add them to your event, and the message box that starts masked. The sticky Inquire that follows you down.
  • The laptop layout: one centred column on both. The footer, the share sheet, the loading and error faces.

Stays Pro-and-up — not moved by this drawing

  • A chosen photo on the card’s back and at the top of the page.
  • A pinned review at the top of the reviews.
  • The two-column laptop layout with the trust rail beside the prices.
  • A custom address for the page.
  • The cinematic top (Enterprise): the photograph is the card.
  • Pro ₱2,500 and Enterprise ₱10,000 per 28 days, read from the catalog — their contents are locked and untouched here.

What this drawing decided on its own — reversible, and yours to strike

  • Free’s work strip. The one presentation line this drawing adds beyond what ships. Every photo still shows; only the composition is Solo’s. If you would rather Free and Solo share the grid, strike that row and nothing else changes.
  • The tagline stays on Free. It is one line, it is already the share description on every plan, and a card with no sentence from the shop at all reads as a listing, not a card. If you want the tagline to be Solo’s too, say so — it is one switch, but it would make the Free share card wordless.
  • The three facts (members, set length, own sound; minimum, crew meal, tasting) sit inside the About fold, as the universal drawing placed them — so they arrive with Solo. They are also written into the service cards on both plans, so nothing true is lost on Free.
  • The editor preview lists a team seat and unlimited weekly customers only where the code enforces them today. Four Solo perks named on the plans page (calls, performance trends, business tools, better search ranking) had no enforcing code found on 10 Sep — they are not drawn until confirmed.

What this needs built, once you approve

  • Nothing new on the public page for the shipped rows — About, accent, lead service and section switches are already gated at Solo; the port in F1/F2 keeps those gates as they are.
  • The work strip on Free (if approved): one layout branch in the work fold. If struck, nothing.
  • The editor preview (if approved): the “Make this page yours” box becomes the preview drawn in section D, reading the Solo price from the catalog, never typed.
  • The stock photo: waits on your ruling in section E. If “card stock”, the placeholder comes off every plan; if “keep it”, nothing changes.
