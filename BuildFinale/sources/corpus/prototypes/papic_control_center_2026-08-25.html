Papic Control Centre — the event's one library

The owner locked Papic's purpose: it is where a celebration's media is collected — from crew cameras, guests, suppliers, and your own uploads. The only two things that live outside it are the made films: the save-the-date film and the event film. So this screen's subject is no longer camera settings — it is the collection: what's in it, who it came from, what's still coming. Two questions the old screen asked are gone on his ruling (photo quality, and "where your photos go"), and uploading becomes a front door, not an afterthought.

2026-08-25 · Detail + Sheet + Wizard + Gallery archetypes (approved 2026-08-04) · phone first

What changed, and why the screen is calmer now
01 · ONE LIBRARY

Everything lands in the same place

Crew cameras, guest phones, your photographer's finished work, the old photos on your laptop — one library holds the lot. Only the two made films sit outside it: the save-the-date film and the event film. Those are made for your day; the library holds what was captured of it.

02 · SOURCES, NOT ROOMS

Four ways in, each with its own state

The old three-tab split is replaced by the thing itself: crew cameras · guests · your uploads · suppliers. Each line says what it has contributed and what it is waiting on. That one list is the whole mental model.

03 · ONE GLANCE, ONE NEXT STEP

The page always knows where you stand

Four facts on top — what's in the library, who it came from, what's still coming, credits left — and one card that names the next step: first your camera dates, then handing out QRs, then watching the day, then the Recap.

04 · FEWER QUESTIONS

Two questions deleted, one promise made

Photo quality — gone; the right default is already chosen. "Where your photos go" — gone; we keep them, full stop, and Google Drive becomes an offer: connect it and a copy of everything arrives in your own Drive as it lands.

State 1 of 3 · the state in the owner's screenshot · today every live event is in this state

Before the day — the library is empty, and honest about it

Today this moment lands on a look picker and megapixels. Here it lands on the truth: the library is empty, one thing is needed before cameras can shoot — the dates — and there is already something you can do: start collecting older memories right now. No tabs to choose between; the sources list is the map.

State 2 of 3

On the day — the library is filling, live

Same page, same order — the strip now reads like a cockpit, each source reports live, and the credits pot has its meter with the top-up ladder beside it. The next step knows the moment: "4 QRs are still in your pocket."

State 3 of 3 · the page they come back to for years

After — the library is the headline, with its makers credited

The morning after, the strip becomes the label on the box and the library takes the stage — obsidian, per the approved Gallery archetype. The sources become the credits: who filled it. The one thing still coming is the photographer's finished work — the supplier door stays visible until it arrives.

The port contract

Nothing is lost — every control, accounted for

Losing a control in a redesign is the failure this project pays for most. Here is every piece of today's page and where it lives now. The only deletions are the two the owner ruled, and the machinery behind both survives. The three tabs are replaced by this one page — that replacement is itemised, not silent.
