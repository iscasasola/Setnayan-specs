The one real jump is Free → Solo. On Free a shop cannot write a single sentence about itself — a couple reads prices and nothing in the shop’s own voice. Solo is where the page stops being a listing and starts being theirs. Pro and Enterprise buy presentation; Solo buys a voice.
SetnaProd is already on Solo — its own dashboard says so. So it can write an About and pick an accent colour today; it simply hasn’t, which is why its live page shows neither. Nothing is locked for it. That is a content gap, not a plan gap.
