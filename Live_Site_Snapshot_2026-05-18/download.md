## Four steps, done.

1.  1

    ### Download

    Click Download for Mac above. Your browser saves it to your
    Downloads folder.

2.  2

    ### Open the .dmg

    Double-click the downloaded file. A window appears with the Setnayan
    icon and an Applications shortcut.

3.  3

    ### Drag to Applications

    Drag the Setnayan icon onto the Applications shortcut. That installs
    it.

4.  4

    ### Eject + open

    Right-click the disk icon on your desktop → Eject. Then open
    Applications → double-click Setnayan.

### First-launch warning is normal

The first time you open Setnayan, macOS may say “Setnayan cannot be
opened because the developer cannot be verified”. That’s because the app
is signed by us but not yet notarized by Apple.

To open anyway: in Finder, find Setnayan in Applications, right-click
(or Control-click) the icon → choose Open → click Open in the dialog.
You only need to do this the first time.

### System requirements

- Apple
  Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, or M4 chip)
- macOS
  11 (Big Sur) or newer
- Internet
  connection (the app loads setnayan.com inside a native window)

Not sure if your Mac is Apple Silicon? Click the Apple menu → About This
Mac. If the chip starts with M1/M2/M3/M4, you’re good. If it says “Intel
Core,” please use the web app at [setnayan.com](https://setnayan.com).
