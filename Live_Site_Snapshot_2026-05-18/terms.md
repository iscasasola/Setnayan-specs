# Setnayan terms of service

Effective 2026-05-13 · Starter draft

## Starter draft

This is a starter draft pending legal review. Functional product rules
are accurate; legal phrasing will be refined before any public launch.

## Who we are

Setnayan is a Philippines-first life-events platform operated from
Quezon City. V1 focuses on weddings.

## Your account

- You must be at least 18 to create an account.
- You’re responsible for keeping your password safe.
- One account per person. Couples share an event via event_members.
- We may suspend or close accounts that violate these terms or
  applicable law. Soft-deleted accounts are retained for 30 days before
  permanent removal.

## Payments + receipts

- All Setnayan services are quoted before payment. You only pay what
  you’ve agreed on with our team.
- We issue an app **transaction receipt** for every paid order,
  downloadable from the order detail page. This is for your records and
  is *not* a BIR Official Receipt — the corresponding BIR OR (where
  applicable) is issued by Setnayan separately, offline.
- Pricing is in PHP. Quoted amounts are **pre-VAT base**; 12% VAT is
  added on top per PH BIR rules unless otherwise noted.
- Refunds: contact us via the [help center](/help) within 7 days of
  payment for refund eligibility.

## Content you create

- You own the content you put into Setnayan — guest lists, event
  details, photos, palettes, messages.
- You grant Setnayan a limited license to display + process your content
  for the purpose of providing the service to you and your event’s
  members.
- We don’t sell or share your event data with third parties for
  marketing.

## Vendor terms

- Vendor profiles are published only when you toggle them visible.
- You must accurately represent your business, services, and prices.
- Setnayan masks couples’ identities in chat threads until the couple
  chooses to share. Don’t solicit personal info couples haven’t
  explicitly provided.

## Service availability

Setnayan is provided “as is”. We aim for high availability but don’t
guarantee uptime. We’ll communicate scheduled maintenance via the in-app
notification system.

## Liability

To the extent permitted by Philippine law, Setnayan’s liability for any
claim is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the
claim. We aren’t liable for your relationships with third-party vendors
you book outside the platform.

## Changes

We may update these terms. Material changes will be announced via in-app
notification at least 14 days before they take effect.

## Contact

Questions? Reach us via the [help center](/help).
