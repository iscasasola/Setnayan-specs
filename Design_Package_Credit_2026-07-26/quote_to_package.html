Why this is nearly free to build
The hard half already shipsloadPackageLinesForQuote already turns a package into a quote, carrying every pricing field — basis, per-head rate, minimum pax, hour base, crew meal, transport. This is that function run backwards.
Nothing new to learnThe vendor doesn't meet the builder on a blank page. They meet it already full, with their own words and their own numbers in it.
It solves the empty-database problemThere are zero packages in production today. Every other feature here — credit, choices, requests — has nothing to be tested against. This is what puts real packages in.
The guesses are safe onesEverything is proposed, not decided. The vendor sees each guess with its reason and changes anything before publishing.
What the conversion actually does. It reads the vendor's own vendor_proposals.line_items and writes vendor_package_items, keeping the pricing fields intact and proposing a type for each line from what it already knows.
