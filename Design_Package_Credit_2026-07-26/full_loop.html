This is today's card

Everything here already shipsfrom ₱X or Inquire, the pricing-basis line (₱1,200 / guest · min 100), the best discount badge, free inclusions with their worth, and not-included caveats. Nothing new was invented for this step.
Two actions, not fourAdd to shortlist opens the customization — there's no separate Customize button, because shortlisting something you haven't priced for your own event is half a decision. Lock this is the shortcut for couples who don't want to compare.
Messaging comes after the shortlist, not beforeThe couple shortlists, customizes, then sends. The vendor's first message already carries the spec, the date and the guest count — so nobody opens with "hi, are you available?" and nobody answers "depends, how many pax?"
What each one writesAdd to shortlist → an event_vendors row at considering, plus the configuration.
Send → opens the thread with that configuration attached.
Lock this → straight to contracted, inside Build.
Vendors without a package still workNo package means the shortlist just holds the card at its listed rates. Nothing looks broken, and the vendor can add a package later.
Hide-prices vendorsIf the vendor hid prices, the anchor reads Inquire and every peso figure disappears — including inclusion worths. Customization then has to show figures, which is the open decision.

The loop closes

This proposal can seed the next packageSend it, and it joins the vendor's quote list — where “turn a quote into a package” picks it up. Every lap makes the next one easier.
One adjustment field, not a rebuildThe vendor nudges the total or prices a request. They don't re-enter the configuration — the couple already did that work.
Both sides hold the same listAccepted, priced, or declined with a reason. Nothing evaporates into chat.
