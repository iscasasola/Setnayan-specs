# Papic — brief for redesigning the promotion page AND the control centre

**For:** a session with no access to our code or database.
**Two surfaces:** the public promotion page (§§ 1–9) and the couple's control centre (§ 11).
**Everything below was read out of the running product and the live price list on 29 Aug 2026.**
**Trust this document over anything you infer, and over any earlier Papic draft.**

> **Why this brief exists.** Two previous drafts of this page were written without seeing the
> product. Both were beautifully made and both promised things we cannot do — one printed an
> invented "0.4 second" face-match time, one invented an expiry date we don't have, and one led
> with a headline feature that isn't built. **The single most useful thing you can do is design
> only from § 2 and § 3.** If a claim is not in § 2, we cannot make it.

---

## 1. What Papic actually is

Guests photograph a celebration on their own phones. They scan a code, the camera opens in the
browser — no app, no account — and every photo sorts itself back to the people in it. The couple
receives all of it.

It is **not a standalone photo app.** It lives inside Setnayan, a Philippines-first life-events
platform: the same celebration already has its guest list, RSVP, seating, schedule and public
page. Papic is one of the services on that page. **That is the most important fact about it
commercially** — see § 6.

**Who reads this page:** an engaged couple, Filipino, English-reading, planning over 6–18 months,
**far more often on a phone than a laptop.**

**Where it lives:** `setnayan.com/papic`. It shares one page shape with seven sibling product
pages — see § 7.

## 2. ✅ WHAT IS TRUE AND MAY BE CLAIMED

Every line here is verified in the running product.

### The money

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Free to start** | **50 shots on every celebration.** No card, no trial clock. |
| **The unit is a "shot"** | 1 photo = **1 shot**. A video clip costs **by its length**: up to 2s = 2 · 3s = 3 · up to 6s = 5 · up to 10s = 8. Ten seconds is the maximum clip. |
| **Top-ups** | **16 amounts, ₱50 to ₱11,200** — ₱50 buys 100 shots; ₱11,200 buys 50,000. Every amount is repeatable and **stacks** on what the celebration already holds. Credited in seconds, mid-party. |
| **Cameras are free and unlimited** | You never pay per camera. |
| **The live wall is free** | Every celebration already has it. There is nothing to buy. |
| **Set shots aside for one camera** | The couple can reserve shots for a trusted friend's camera, and take back whatever is unused. |

⚠ **NEVER TYPE A PRICE INTO THE PAGE.** Every figure is read live from our price list, so an
admin change moves the page by itself. Write copy that works with the numbers slotted in.

### Privacy — our strongest material

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Every face is found** | The guest's own phone reads the face; **our own server** does the matching. No outside face service is involved. Opt-in — a guest who never adds a selfie never has a face key. |
| **Any face can vanish** | A guest can ask not to be shown. **We blur the picture itself** — a real blurred copy, not a filter — and if the blurring fails for any reason **the photo does not go on the wall at all.** |
| **Face data is really deleted** | On request, from the same browser tab, with no account. **And automatically, three months after the celebration ends.** |
| **Nothing unscreened is ever shown** | Every photo is screened for objectionable content before it can appear. It is a permission list — unscreened never reaches the wall — and it **cannot be switched off.** |

### The promises

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Every photo reaches the couple** | Tagged or not, and whether or not the person in it asked to be hidden. This is the one promise with no exception. |
| **Nothing is ever deleted** | Only the file size steps down. |
| **Shoot early** | Cameras can open **up to six months before** the day. |
| **Full size for three months** | Originals stay at full resolution for **three months after the celebration**, then the file is compressed. |
| **Kept for life** | The compressed gallery is **free, for good.** No renewal, no expiry email, no subscription. |

### The experience

- **No app, no account.** A code and a browser.
- **Shooting works with no signal** — photos are held on the phone and send when it returns.
- **Each guest gets their own gallery**, and can make a short vertical souvenir reel set to music.
- **A place card tags one guest; a table sign tags the whole table.** Hold it in frame.
- **Photo challenges** with a written library of prompts.
- **Try it with a friend** — a live two-phone demo, one minute, nothing saved, no sign-up.
- **It lives on the couple's own celebration page**, beside the guest list, RSVP and seating — not a separate site that expires.

## 3. ⛔ WHAT MAY NOT BE CLAIMED

**Each of these is either untrue, unbuilt, or checkable against a rival's homepage in fifteen
seconds.**

| Do not say | Why |
|---|---|
| **Any speed or latency number** — "0.4 seconds", "1.4s", "the only published latency figure" | Nothing in the product measures this. An invented number becomes a number a competitor tests. |
| **"Hand every guest a camera that holds twelve shots"** — or any per-guest limit | **Not built yet.** It is being built now; the page may claim it only after it ships. |
| **Chapters / the whole year / engagement-to-wedding / a reel cut from the year** | **Not built.** Nothing links two celebrations. |
| **"The live service closes after six months"** | Nothing closes. The six months is how long you may SHOOT for, not how long we keep anything. **Never turn our shooting window into an expiry date** — being the one without an expiry is our best line. |
| **Prices in dollars, or any second currency** | Pesos only. |
| **A separate address like `papic.setnayan.com`** | No such thing, deliberately. It contradicts our own argument that Papic is not a separate site. |
| **"We invented shot limits"** | A rival (Lense) already does per-guest limits. **What is ours is limits *paired with a live wall* — nobody pairs those.** Claim the narrow thing. |
| **Photo challenges as a headline innovation** | Table stakes — two rivals ship them. |
| **AI highlight reels as an innovation** | Four rivals ship them. |
| **"Unlimited uploads"** | Never. The shots ladder is the entire price of the product. |
| **Any claim about a competitor's retention or pricing** | Only with a source and a date we can stand behind. |
| **"Papic Pool" or "Papic One"** | Retired product names. There is one product: **Papic**. |
| **Ninongs/ninangs getting bigger allowances** | The roles are modelled but **nothing acts on them yet.** |

## 5. WHAT THE PAGE MUST DO

In order of importance:

1. **Make somebody want it.** It currently explains and never seduces.
2. **Say it starts free.** This is the single most converting fact and it is buried 2,196 px down.
3. **Show the product.** Any photograph or screen at all would be an improvement on none.
4. **Answer "is this just a shared album?"** — the real objection.
5. **Get out of the way.** Shorter than today, not longer.

## 6. THE COMPETITIVE POSITION

**The market:** local rivals sell a QR album plus a live wall for **₱499 – ₱999**. Global ones
sell a fuller platform for **$29 – $99**, usually with face search as a paid extra, and keep
things for 12–14 months.

**Table stakes in 2026 — do not sell these as special:** a QR that opens a browser camera, a live
wall, photo games, face search, highlight reels, no-app-needed. Everyone credible has them.

**What is genuinely ours:**

1. **Free at the door.** Every rival's cheapest entry is ₱499. Ours is nothing, and the wall is
   free too. **Nobody in this market has a free floor.**
2. **Consent.** Nothing in the field lets a guest decline facial recognition. We do, we blur the
   photo itself, and we really delete the data. Frame this as **built for Philippine privacy
   law**, not as a perk for famous guests.
3. **Screening before display.** The strongest local rival can only take a photo *off* the wall
   after everyone has seen it.
4. **Kept for life** against a rival's 30 days.
5. **It is part of a wedding planning platform**, not a photo app. A rival would have to build an
   entire planning product to answer that.

⚠ **On the shot limit, the honest frame is a gift, not a ration.** "Every guest gets 15 shots on
the couple" reads differently from "limited to 15 photos". And the reason the rest is free is that
we control how much gets uploaded — anyone promising unlimited uploads must either raise their
price or drop the AI.

## 7. DESIGN CONSTRAINTS — these are locked

**Colours** (exact):
- Page background **white `#FFFFFF`**
- Body ink **`#2C2A29`**
- The one action colour **`#C24E25`**
- Links **`#3B4E67`**
- Gold **`#A9834B`** — **small labels and icons only, NEVER body text.**
  ⚠ Gold has almost no contrast headroom: the text gold `#8A6B39` **fails** on any tinted card.
  If a gold element sits on anything other than plain white, check it or don't use it.

**Type:** one family throughout — **Hanken Grotesk** — with a monospace for small labels. On our
marketing pages the "serif" resolves to the same sans **by design**. Do not introduce a display
serif.

**Layout:**
- **Mobile first.** It is read on a phone far more than a laptop.
- Wide things (tables, code) scroll inside their own box; the page never scrolls sideways.
- Works in **both light and dark**.

🔒 **THE PAGE SHAPE IS SHARED WITH SEVEN OTHER PRODUCT PAGES.** Hero, three steps, a
"versus" comparison, an FAQ and a closing panel come from one shared template. **Changing the
shape changes all eight pages.** Design within it unless you are deliberately proposing a change
to all of them — and say so if you are.

⛔ **NO EXPLAINING LINE UNDER THE HEADLINE.** The owner removed the small eyebrow and the
sub-paragraph from every page in the product on 2026-08-19: *"we do not need these. it just eats
up space and we want it to be simpler to understand on each page without too much side comments."*
Headline, then the buttons. The product gets explained one screen lower.

**Existing copy worth keeping** — the current page's writing is good even where the structure
isn't. The headline **"Every guest goes home with their own photos."** and the comparison rows
(*"You scroll to find yourself" → "Your photos find you"*) are strong. Reuse rather than rewrite
unless you can beat them.

## 11. THE SECOND SURFACE — the couple's Papic control centre

A different page, a different person's state of mind, same product. This is where the couple runs
Papic: `/dashboard/{their celebration}/studio/papic`.

### 11.1 ⚠ READ THIS FIRST — an older brief about this page is now MISLEADING

There is a redesign brief dated **2026-08-10** that describes this page as *"20 cards, one scroll,
1,785 lines"* with *"nothing grouped, nothing findable."* **That complaint has largely been
acted on.** The page has since been reorganised into named groups. **Do not design from the old
complaint — design from the structure below.** There is also a drawn prototype
(`prototypes/papic_control_center_2026-08-25.html`) which is a presentation pass over that
reorganised page; ask for it if you want the visual language.

### 11.2 What is on it now, in order

1. The gallery **opens the page** — a preview, with four key facts above it
2. **"Do this first"** — set the capture window (only shows while it is unset)
3. **"QRs still in your pocket"** — appears only when printed camera codes are unclaimed
4. **"Four ways into your library"** — crew cameras · a camera for every guest · your own uploads ·
   suppliers *(the supplier row is deliberately inert)*
5. **The credits cluster** — how many shots are left · buy more · hand shots to one camera and
   take them back · what guests chipped in
6. **Made from your library** — recap page · keepsake magazine · life flash
7. **Your photos, your say** — supplier media controls · moderation · the shared gallery
8. **"Set once, change any time"** — quiet rows that open a sheet: the window · the look · face
   matching · whether guests may shoot · whether uploads are open
9. **Google Drive** as an offer, not a destination
10. **Extras** — challenges · approving supplier challenges · the live wall
11. Setup & help, then paid offers **last**

### 11.3 The three moods it has to serve

This is the real design problem, and it has not been solved:

| Mood | When | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Setting up** | weeks or months before | long, considered, one-time choices |
| **Running the day** | at the venue, one-handed, in a hurry, maybe bad signal | hand out codes, watch shots arrive, top up fast |
| **Looking back** | after, and for years | browse, download, make keepsakes |

**All three are interleaved today.** A couple at the venue with 90 seconds is scrolling past
setup choices they made months ago. **If you change one thing about this page, make it answer
"which of the three am I in right now."**

### 11.4 Its own locked patterns — work inside these

- **A choice made once becomes one quiet row that opens a sheet.** Not a card, not an inline
  control. *"A row is a different DOOR to the same control, never a second copy of it."*
- **A control that governs nothing must not render at all.** A celebration where guests cannot
  shoot grows no guest-shot row.
- **Numbers are never typed into the page** — capacity and prices are derived, same rule as § 2.
- **Every save must confirm itself on screen.** Nine settings once saved silently into the void;
  there is now a check that fails the build if a new control cannot report its own outcome.
- **Paid offers go last.** The page leads with what the couple already has.

### 11.5 What is arriving in it (design around these)

1. **Shots per guest** — a new row in "Set once, change any time", opening a sheet with: a number
   for everyone else, **naming specific guests off the guest list and giving each an amount**
   (this is the real new screen — nothing like it exists yet), and a live line reading *"120
   guests · 8 named · everyone else gets 14 each · 32 spare."*
2. **"Open the rest to everyone"** — a button for the night, when guests who never showed up are
   holding shares nobody can spend.
3. **Timed challenges** — a challenge with a countdown, and its live answer count.

### 11.6 The honest open problems

- **The three moods, above.** Nothing on the page knows what time it is.
- **The day-of experience is untested on a real phone at a real venue.**
- The page is long, and it is long because the product genuinely does a lot — the fix is
  probably *ordering by mood*, not deletion.

## 10. QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING BACK

If any of these matter to your design, ask rather than assume:

1. Should the shared eight-page template change, or should Papic stay inside it?
2. Is a photograph at the top worth changing all eight sibling pages for?
3. How much of the privacy story belongs on a *wedding* page versus a page aimed at corporate
   buyers?
4. Does the page need a price at all, or just "free to start" plus a link?
5. **For the control centre:** should it reorder itself by mood — setting up / running the day /
   looking back — and if so, does it decide that from the date, or does the couple choose?
