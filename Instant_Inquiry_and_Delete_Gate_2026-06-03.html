What this prototype locks in One-tap confirm seeding (Vendor_Match §7b.1): the dashboard Services list is the couple's onboarding picks; best-fit vendors are pre-filled and sent with a single tap — near-instant, but consent-gated by that tap (not auto-blasted).
  • Delete-gate (Service_Schedule §T1.6): a pending inquiry is non-deletable. The × soft-explains with a countdown until the gate clears.
  • Clears on accept — the vendor accepts (token paid + names revealed + chat) → removal is free thereafter. Or clears on 7-day stale → the delete-or-nudge prompt.
  • Nudge fires the vendor's "you've been nudged · accept or decline" popup and resets the 7-day clock (default cap 1 nudge, then delete-only — §6 flag f).
  • Hybrid-anonymity: the vendor sees a masked "A couple in Manila" until they accept; the couple's real name reveals only on the token-paid accept.
  • Un-inquired rows (e.g. Pick one) carry no gate; locked vendors show no × at all.
