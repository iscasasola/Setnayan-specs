## 1. The change in one paragraph

Setnayan is repositioning from a **wedding planner** ("improve your wedding") to **the home where your life's moments are kept for life** ("set, and kept"). The product still does deep event planning + a vendor marketplace, but the *reason it exists a year later* — and the engine of retention and growth — is the **memory home**: Papic face-tagged per-guest galleries, auto-reels, save-the-date films, and editorial pages, kept forever at cheap display resolution, resurfaced on anniversaries. **Launch stays weddings-only in one metro** (Metro Manila + CALABARZON), monetizing **vendors, never couples**, and growing through the **guest→host loop**. Multi-event breadth stays in the architecture and the brand vision but is **demand-gated, not a launch scope**. **The name Setnayan is kept** — it's a strong, PH-native, event-agnostic asset whose "set" origin bridges *handled* → *kept*.

---

## 2. The strategic pivot — story & decision

**Trigger.** While redesigning the account/user page, we realized the user page isn't a settings screen — it's **the memory home, and it carries the retention of the whole app.** That reframed everything.

**Diagnosis (why this matters).** Events are **one-and-done** — a couple plans one wedding, then leaves. No events app has "won" on the consumer side; the survivors (The Knot ~75% **vendor** revenue; Zola on **registry** commission) sidestepped one-and-done by monetizing the vendor or a transaction the couple makes anyway, and treated couple tools as a free funnel. Charging couples a subscription is the **settled loser pattern** (Riley & Grey stayed tiny; Eventbrite was forced to kill organizer fees, eating ~$20M). So "no one has won" really means **no one has won by charging the consumer.**

**The fix (three moats).** (1) **Memory home** — irreplaceable, un-deletable system-of-record. (2) **Vendor marketplace** — revenue decoupled from couple frequency. (3) **Guest→host loop** — the only ~₱0-CAC growth engine in a Facebook-saturated market.

**Decision.** Reframe before adding one more SKU: *assume the couple never comes back, be profitable on one wedding's margin, keep CAC near ₱0, never sell couples a recurring subscription.* Lead the product and marketing with the **per-guest face-tagged gallery** (the one 10× surface free tools can't do), and make **guest→host conversion the company north-star metric.**

The work is the **brand story** that evolves "it's all set" → "set, and kept for life" — a tagline/voice job, **not a rename.** (Keeps the existing `SETNAYAN` full-spelling brand lock.)

## 4. The strategy — 7 pillars (summary; full detail in the North-Star doc)

1. **Launch scope** — weddings-only, **Metro Manila + CALABARZON**, memory brand from day one. "Build broad, launch narrow." Liquidity is the only launch metric; premature multi-vertical scaling is the #1 startup killer; Zola was weddings-only ~10 years. Multi-event stays in architecture, **zero in GTM** until weddings is won.
2. **Trends to ride** — QR guest galleries (now the wedding standard — Papic *is* the category that won), nostalgia/memory-app boom, 9:16 vertical video, mass-market AI music/video, the digital disposable-camera revival, world-leading PH livestreaming + the OFW triangle, AI-in-planning (with the caveat: **don't depend on AI-search discovery**).
3. **Competitor reframe** — **closest rival = Eventchy** (PH-first AI event marketplace, launched Dec 2025); **no longer** The Knot/Zola/Joy. Face-rec photo apps are the closest *category*. **Align with** generic memory apps (Google Photos/Timehop). Proof point: The Knot's standalone capture app **Veri/The Guest shut down Dec 2023** → standalone capture has no defensibility; weld it to vendor revenue + the loop.
4. **Marketing & growth** — the product *is* the ad; watermarked shareable artifacts; FB/Messenger share-out; PH TikTok/IG creators; SEO/editorial; referral on paid surfaces only; anniversary re-engagement; paid ads last. **Guest→host loop** is the engine; **% of guests who become hosts** is the north-star metric. BE the events platform, SHARE OUT to social — never replace it.
5. **Vendor marketplace** — concierge-build **100–150** verified vendors (8–10 spend-heavy categories, MM+CALABARZON) to a **≥5-per-category** liquidity floor before opening couples; monetize the vendor side (Pro ₱6k / Enterprise ₱10k per 28d + ₱100 tokens + Setnayan Pay 3%; 0% commission). Phased to ~1,000–1,500 vendors + 150–250 paying subs by +12 months.
6. **Market** — PH marriages **declining ~8–10%/yr** (449k→372k) — the data that *validates* the life-events hedge. Wedding TAM ~₱130B; broader life-events ₱250–400B+ growing ~8% CAGR; app-charged SAM ~₱5–15B/yr; SOM ~₱20–150M/yr in 12–24 mo. OFW remittance pool **$38.34B/yr** = send-money + watch-live + keep-the-memory triangle.
7. **Retention + storage moats** — see §5–§6.

## 5. Evidence base (key findings + sources)

**Retention (one-and-done economics).** The Knot ~75% vendor-marketplace revenue (>$300M of >$400M) [theknot/wikipedia]; Zola ~$397M on registry commission, 100% free to couples; Joy $64.9M (2023), free to couples; couple-subscription stayed niche (Riley & Grey $39/mo); Eventbrite killed organizer fees in 2025 (~$20M hit) [SEC]; Lasting/Maroo pivoted to B2B/health when consumer wedding theses failed.

**Memory-home & storage.** Google Photos "Storage Saver" downsizes to 16MP/1080p, stated "indistinguishable to the naked eye" [support.google.com]; iCloud "Optimize Storage" keeps display-res on device, original in cloud, default-on [apple]; **Pic-Time** keeps web-res hot **forever**, moves originals to cold after 12 months [pic-time]; 7-day download windows are the universal norm [Pixieset/ShootProof]; R2 storage $0.010–0.015/GB-mo, **free egress** [Cloudflare]. **Cost proof:** full-res forever ≈ $1.43M/yr @1M couples (compounds) vs display forever ≈ **~₱7/couple/yr** (flat) — **~11×** cheaper.

**Trends (2026).** QR adoption 89%, 87% of weddings offer an interactive gallery; disposable-cam revival ($345–600/100-guest wedding); BeReal/Lapse/Timehop nostalgia boom; 9:16 vertical = 81% of short-form, highest ROI; Suno 2M subs / 44% of new Deezer uploads AI; PH livestreaming #1 globally (41% main reason to log on); **FB auto-deletes livestreams after 30 days**; AI-in-planning ~36% of couples but **84% of vendors have zero AI citation share**.

**PH market.** DataReportal Digital 2026 PH: 98M internet users, ~7.7 hrs/day, median age 26.1, FB 94.9% / Messenger 90.6% / ~23.5h-mo; PSA marriages 449k(2022)→~372k(2024); BSP OFW remittances $38.34B (8.3% of GDP); Bridestory PH 15k+ vendors.

**Competitors.** Eventchy launched Dec 2025 (Xugbo Technologies, Ayala Malls Circuit Makati) — AI matchmaker "Eve", Dream Boards, verified booking, RSVP, cashless pay; **no post-event memory layer**. Veri/The Guest (The Knot) ended uploads Dec 2023. Partiful +2M users 2025, adding albums. Joy $60M Series B "for all life events".

## 6. Product & build implications (what the product becomes)

**A. The memory-home user page (the retention engine).**
- The account/user page = the **Account hub** ("Library" being renamed → **"Account"**, with a **user-icon** glyph), holding: **Photos & Videos · Saved Vendors · Editorials** (cross-event tabs) + **Profile · Settings** (link to the existing settings page). **Switcher footer reduces to Sign out** (Profile/Settings move into the hub). *(Built in branch `claude/library-cross-event`; not yet shipped — held pending this strategy.)*
- Evolve the Photos tab from "album cards that link out" → an **in-place memory environment**: inline reels, **"on this day"**, anniversary re-surfacing, a year/timeline view. Frame as *"the home where your memories live,"* not "see your stuff."
- Switcher also carries a **Library/Account row** so the hub is reachable on **mobile** (the switcher is the mobile account nav; the sidebar is desktop-only). *(Already built in the branch.)*

**B. Storage lifecycle (what makes "forever" affordable — GREENFIELD).**
- **Lifecycle:** hot originals (~90 days) → **deliver originals to the user** (Google-Drive copy is already built + wired; + a 7-day download link) → **auto-downgrade** to display-res (~16MP photo / ~1080p clip) + rendered outputs, kept **forever** on R2 IA; originals age to cold/expire (the couple has them). Warn T-30/T-7/T-1; **never silent; never gate access.**
- **Build work (new):** a **display-derivative pipeline** (photo + thumb + 1080p clip re-encode — reuse the STD-film ffmpeg.wasm path); **serve derivatives, not originals** (today the gallery serves 250 full-res tiles — a real cost+speed bug); the **auto-downgrade sweep** on paid media (extend the existing sampler-expiry pattern); **meter clips harder than photos**.
- **Spec reconciliation:** replace the stale "90-day hot / 5-year cold" + "don't auto-delete within 5 years" with one honest policy: **"we never lose a memory — display kept forever + originals handed to the couple."**
- **Optional one-time "Keep My Originals" keepsake** (admin-catalog priced) for the few who want Setnayan-side redundancy — **never a paywall on memories.**

**C. Guest→host loop surfaces (the growth engine — to build/wire).**
- Give guests **lightweight persistent accounts** (today they're ephemeral sessions) so they stay addressable as future hosts.
- End the three highest-intent guest moments (RSVP confirmation, Papic "here are YOUR photos" delivery, day-of) with a soft *"start your own debut/birthday / save your photos forever"* CTA.
- **Share-out** as a first-class one-tap action on every artifact (reel / STD film / editorial `/slug`), watermarked, deep-link back; **every guest touchpoint a no-install web link**.
- Instrument **guest→host conversion** as the #1 metric.

**D. Vendor concierge-seeding (the supply playbook).**
- Hand-recruit + **build vendor profiles for them** from their FB/IG portfolios; source from PH supplier FB groups + Kasal.com + bridal fairs; keep it free; the Papic gallery + editorial showcase is the supply magnet.

**E. Website repositioning (HOLD until the memory home is real).**
- Front door shifts from "Plan your perfect wedding" → **"Your wedding is one day. Keep it forever."** Lead with Papic + the planning depth underneath. **Sequencing: build the memory home first, then pivot the public messaging** — don't promise what isn't shipped.
