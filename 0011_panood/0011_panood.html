Iteration 0011 · What ships

Panood V1.5 — WebApp track

This iteration ships V1.5 of Panood with a four-tier apparatus-priced lineup and YouTube as the sole viewer-delivery surface. The original 8-tier draft included crew-bundled "Setnayan Team" tiers — those are cut from V1 because they violate the apparatus pricing rule (Setnayan prices the software tool, not the people running it). The original Cloudflare Stream Player landing-page embed is replaced by a YouTube IFrame Player embed — Setnayan's compositor RTMP-relays to YouTube on Setnayan's master channel, the landing page embeds the YouTube player. Setnayan's per-event delivery cost is ₱0 regardless of audience size. The existing "Custom Overlay + Custom Standby" add-on is renamed and rescoped to the Custom Monogram Pack at ₱2,000 (event-wide across Panood, Papic, and every future Setnayan media output).

Custom Monogram Pack — 4 designs × 2 orientations

Couple picks one design when they buy the pack. Their monogram (uploaded PNG with transparency, or auto-generated from their names) replaces the Setnayan logo across every output. Panood auto-uses the landscape variant; Papic auto-uses the portrait variant.

Pro Camera Bridge — DSLR camera feeds

Pro Camera Bridge is a multi-purchase add-on (₱1,500 per DSLR seat) that turns a phone-only Panood camera slot into a phone+DSLR pair. The phone reads the DSLR's clean live-view feed via the vendor's WiFi SDK and republishes it through WebRTC to Cloudflare Stream Live exactly as if it were the phone's own internal camera. The compositor, broadcaster grid, monogram overlay, broadcast style pack, and YouTube delivery all stay unchanged — both phone-only and phone+DSLR slots arrive at the SFU as standard WebRTC streams. Slot rule: a phone+DSLR pair counts as 1 camera against the Panood camera limit. The bridge sells quality (1080p clean, manual aperture/ISO, real low-light), not slot count.

Pipeline when paired

  • DSLR  → SDK / WiFi  → phone
  • Phone (0012 native app, LS camera-operator mode)
  • Re-encodes for WebRTC publish
  • → Cloudflare Stream Live SFU (ingest)
  • → ffmpeg compositor (monogram + broadcaster picks active)
  • → RTMP relay → YouTube → viewers

Operator app surface

  • Phone-only operators continue using the WebApp client (browser, no install)
  • DSLR-bridging operators use the 0012 Papic native app in LS camera-operator mode (one binary, two modes)
  • No standalone Panood native app — vendor SDKs are native-only and the 0012 binary already exists

Slot binding

  • Spend binds to a specific panood_camera_id or papic_seat_id
  • Travels with the slot through battery handoff and device swap
  • Does not transfer across slots in V1 (transfer admin action lands V1.1)

Disconnect handling

  • DSLR WiFi drop → phone-internal camera resumes within 3 s
  • Broadcast does not interrupt; broadcaster grid shows "Camera disconnected" on the affected tile
  • Auto-retry every 5 s; tile flips back to DSLR-source on reconnect
  • Frames during the disconnected window stamp paired_camera_brand=null for post-event analysis

Supported brands at launch: Canon (EOS Camera Connect SDK — R-series, R5/R6/R6 II, R7/R8/R10/R50, RP), Nikon (SnapBridge SDK / MTP-WiFi — Z fc, Z 5 II, Z 6 III, Z 7 II, Z 8, Z 9), Sony (Camera Remote SDK — α7-series, α1, α6700, α7C II, FX3, ZV-E1, ZV-E10 II), Fujifilm (Camera Remote SDK — X-T5, X-T4, X-S20, X-H2/X-H2S, GFX 100 II, X100VI). Vendor SDKs sit behind a single CameraBridge abstraction shared with 0012 Papic — same pairing flow, same fallback rules, same metadata stamping.
