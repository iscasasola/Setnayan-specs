## First — the question: "8 groups, so 8 sessions?"

**No. Roughly 30 working sessions, not 8.**

The eight groups were an answer to a different question — *which items share plumbing* — not
*how the work divides up*. Three things make 8 ≠ 8:

1. **The groups only covered the items that clump.** About 35 of the ~110 remaining items.
   The rest are singles that share nothing with anything.
2. **The groups are wildly different sizes.** One is a redeploy that takes an hour. One is
   112 screens plus three redesigns and will take a fortnight of sessions on its own.
3. **A session is not a unit of work, it is a unit of attention.** Recent history: three
   sessions produced ~40 merged changes. A "large" item is often a session by itself.

Honest shape below: **12 work blocks**, sized in sessions, plus owner errands that are not
sessions at all and outside blockers that are nobody's session.

## The three standing gates

1. **Never auto-merge a fanned-out build.** Ten parallel builds with auto-merge armed shipped
   44 defects.
2. **Nothing that charges money gets switched on without the owner saying so** — the booking
   fee has two switches and has never charged anyone.
3. **Find it before you build it.** Six items on this page were already built and two more were
   half-built with the database side shipped and nothing calling it. Every block above starts
   by opening what exists.
