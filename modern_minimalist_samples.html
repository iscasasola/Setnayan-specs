Modern Minimalist

Five candidate templates for the Modern Minimalist aesthetic category. Editorial restraint, generous whitespace, ink-on-cream. Reference brands: Aesop · Aman · Soho House. Filipino-luxe-modern voice.

Storyboard frames below are visual approximations of the template's aesthetic, not actual rendered output. Each template targets the same 4 narrative beats (Opening · Prep · Climax · Outro) but expresses them through its own visual language.
