# Vendor Types — Philippine Wedding Market

A working catalog of the vendor categories Setnayan expects to coordinate. This file is descriptive (what the vendor does, what they typically charge, what they need from the couple) and pairs with `sync-matrix.md` (which is prescriptive — what data flows where).

When a task involves a vendor type, check both files. If the vendor type isn't here, propose adding it before generating any artifact.

## Photography

The most-demanded vendor in the PH wedding market. Couples often book photo before they book anything else except the venue.

- **Typical package:** ceremony coverage (4–6 hours) + reception coverage (4–6 hours) + raw + edited gallery + 1 album. ₱60K–₱250K mid-market, ₱300K+ premium.
- **Add-ons:** prenup shoot, save-the-date video, drone, second shooter, large prints
- **Deliverable timeline:** 2–6 weeks for edited gallery, 4–12 weeks for album
- **Deposit norms:** 30–50% reservation, balance on or before wedding day
- **Setnayan notes:** photo and video almost always coordinate movement on the day; their packets must reference each other

## Videography

Often a separate vendor from photography in PH (different from Western markets where they're more often combined).

- **Typical package:** ceremony + reception + Same-Day-Edit (SDE). ₱70K–₱300K mid-market, ₱400K+ premium
- **Same-Day-Edit (SDE):** a 5–10 minute highlight reel screened during the reception. Effectively a PH wedding standard.
- **Deliverable timeline:** SDE same day, full film 2–6 months
- **Setnayan notes:** SDE has an explicit screening time during the reception that the run-of-show must accommodate. Typically late in the reception, after dinner, before the dance program.

## Catering

Usually the largest budget line. Either in-house at the venue or external.

- **Typical package:** appetizers + main meal (buffet or plated) + dessert + cake. ₱1,200–₱2,500/head mid-market, ₱3,000+/head premium.
- **Format:** buffet (most common), plated, family-style, or hybrid
- **Vendor allowance:** PH norm is to feed all on-site vendors. Caterer needs an explicit vendor count.
- **Headcount lock:** typically 7–10 days before the event
- **Deposit norms:** 50% reservation, balance 7 days before event. Some collect 100% in advance.
- **Setnayan notes:** must receive aggregated dietary breakdown, never individual guest names with restrictions (privacy). Salu-salo / VIP table treatment is a PH-specific upsell.

## Florist / Stylist

Often combined with overall styling (table setup, draping, lighting accents).

- **Typical package:** ceremony arrangements (aisle, altar, arch), reception centerpieces, bouquets and boutonnieres for the entourage. ₱80K–₱500K mid-market, ₱600K+ premium.
- **Floral count from entourage:**
  - 1 bridal bouquet
  - N corsages for Ninangs (Principal Sponsors, female)
  - N boutonnieres for Ninongs (Principal Sponsors, male)
  - N corsages for mothers
  - N boutonnieres for fathers
  - Smaller corsages/boutonnieres for Secondary Sponsors
  - N bridesmaids' bouquets
  - N groomsmen's boutonnieres
  - 1 flower girl basket / petals
- **Deposit norms:** 50% reservation, balance 1–2 weeks before event
- **Setnayan notes:** stem count is computed from entourage size + theme — when entourage changes, the florist always gets a sync trigger.

## Hair & Makeup Artist (HMUA)

Often a small studio with a lead artist + 1–3 assistants. Sometimes the bride books a separate prenup HMUA and a separate wedding-day HMUA.

- **Typical package:** bride trial + wedding-day HMUA for bride, mothers, Maid of Honor, Bridesmaids, Flower Girl. ₱25K–₱120K mid-market, ₱150K+ premium.
- **Touch-ups:** included or per-touchup add-on
- **Time per head:** 60–90 min for bride, 30–45 min for entourage
- **Deposit norms:** 50% reservation, balance on day
- **Setnayan notes:** call time at prep location is critical and chained to ceremony time minus prep duration minus travel. HMUA team must arrive before photographer for prep shots.

## Wedding Coordinator (Day-Of)

Either professional or family-member-as-volunteer. Setnayan's coordinator dashboard exists for both.

- **Professional package:** day-of coordination, run-of-show drafting, vendor coordination, on-day team of 2–4 coordinators. ₱40K–₱200K mid-market, ₱250K+ premium.
- **Full-service planner:** monthly engagement starting 6–12 months out. ₱150K–₱600K+ for the engagement.
- **Setnayan notes:** if the couple has no professional coordinator, the Setnayan coordinator dashboard is used by a family member (best man, maid of honor, mother). The dashboard must work for both audiences.

## Band / DJ / Live Musicians

- **DJ:** ₱30K–₱100K. Often includes lights and basic sound.
- **Acoustic duo / trio:** ₱20K–₱80K. Common for cocktail hour or ceremony.
- **Full band (4–8 piece):** ₱80K–₱350K. Common for receptions.
- **Setnayan notes:** music coordination with the host is critical. Setnayan's run-of-show export includes cue points the band/DJ reads during the reception.

## Host / Emcee

- **Typical fee:** ₱25K–₱100K. Premium (TV/radio personality) ₱150K+.
- **Includes:** script drafting, on-day hosting, coordination with band/DJ
- **Setnayan notes:** host packet is the most text-heavy of all vendor packets — they need pronunciation, bios, acknowledgments, the full reception flow.

## Officiant / Priest

- **Catholic priest:** parish stipend (suggested donation ₱5K–₱30K depending on parish; not formally a "fee")
- **Civil:** judge fee or registrar fee (much smaller, often under ₱5K)
- **Setnayan notes:** the priest doesn't get a packet in the same way other vendors do — they get a checklist of parish requirements (Pre-Cana, banns, CENOMAR, certificates) that the couple must satisfy by certain dates.

## Choir / Church musicians

- **Parish choir:** included in parish stipend or small additional donation
- **Hired choir / soloist:** ₱15K–₱60K
- **Setnayan notes:** song list is the main coordination point. Choir gets the ceremony processional order and the requested songs.

## Officiant + ceremony stationery

- **Invitation suite (printed):** ₱15K–₱100K depending on quantity and finish
- **Souvenir / giveaway suppliers:** ₱30/head for small, ₱100–₱500/head for premium personalized items, including QR-coded keepsakes (Setnayan's NFC/QR add-ons fit here)
- **Setnayan notes:** Setnayan's QR souvenir add-on integrates with the souvenir vendor — they need final guest count and the couple's branding.

## Transportation

- **Bridal car (decorated):** ₱8K–₱30K for a few-hour rental
- **Guest shuttle (for destination weddings):** ₱20K–₱100K depending on fleet
- **Setnayan notes:** transportation pickup times are chained to call time at prep, ceremony, and reception. Address book is sensitive.

## Photo booth / 360-booth

- **Typical fee:** ₱20K–₱80K
- **Includes:** booth + props + 4-hour reception window + prints
- **Setnayan notes:** integrates with the reception venue's layout and the couple's branding.

## Live painter / Live calligrapher

- **Live painter:** ₱25K–₱80K
- **Live calligrapher (place cards, vows scroll):** ₱15K–₱60K
- **Setnayan notes:** painter needs a setup spot with good lighting; calligrapher needs the guest list 7+ days in advance.

## Venue

- **Hotel ballroom:** ₱150K–₱800K for a 200–400 guest wedding (often with required catering minimums)
- **Resort / beach:** ₱200K–₱1.5M for 100–250 guests, often package-priced
- **Garden / outdoor:** ₱150K–₱500K, requires rain plan
- **Setnayan notes:** venue is the longest-tenured vendor relationship — booked first, often inflexible on date and time. Most other vendors' constraints derive from the venue's access window.
