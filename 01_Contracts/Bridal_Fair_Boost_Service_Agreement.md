## 1. Parties & definitions

This Agreement is between **Setnayan** (the "Platform") and the **Fair Organizer** (the "Organizer") named in the signature block, for the promotion of the wedding / bridal fair (the "Fair") identified below.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fair** | The specific bridal / wedding fair event being boosted, with a fixed start date and venue. |
| **Boost cycle** | The promotion window for one Fair — from listing go-live through the Fair's start date. One signature = one Fair = one cycle. |
| **Featured slot** | A homepage featured-fair card on `www.setnayan.com` (max 3 active platform-wide per §11.3.1) + a dedicated `/fairs/[fair-slug]` landing page. |
| **Email blast** | A pre-Fair promotional email to subscribed Setnayan couples + vendors. |
| **Funnel discount code** | A Setnayan-issued, Organizer-redeemed code that routes Setnayan users into the Fair (vendor booth discount / couple registration perk). |
| **Launch gate** | The platform-readiness threshold (§7) that must be met before Boost opens for signups. |

## 2. Eligibility & the launch gate

### 2.1 Launch gate (Setnayan-side readiness)

Boost does not open for signups until Setnayan reaches **both** (AND gate):

- **≥ 500 verified vendors** with public `/v/[slug]` profiles, **and**
- **≥ 10,000 active couple accounts** (active = signed in within the last 90 days ⚠ **OWNER-VALIDATE** definition).

**Admin manual override** (0023 § 3.16): Setnayan may open Boost to a strategic launch partner before the gate is met. Any pre-gate deal is logged in the admin audit trail with reason, and the Organizer is told in writing that audience numbers are still ramping (see §6 transparency clause).

### 2.2 Fair eligibility (Organizer-side)

To qualify, the Fair and Organizer must meet ⚠ **OWNER-VALIDATE** minimum criteria — starting recommendation:

- Legitimate, identifiable organizer (registered business or established event brand);
- Minimum fair scale (e.g., 20+ vendor booths and/or 500+ expected couple attendance);
- Defined geographic coverage and a fixed, published Fair date and venue.

Setnayan reserves the right to decline or remove any Fair that misrepresents its scale, date, or legitimacy.

## 3. The two models (Organizer picks one per cycle)

Both models are offered simultaneously. The Organizer selects the model that fits their budget and value-exchange preference at signup.

### 3.1 Model A — Barter (no cash exchanged)

**Setnayan provides:**

- Homepage featured-fair slot on `www.setnayan.com` (subject to the 3-slot platform cap);
- Dedicated `/fairs/[fair-slug]` landing page with dual-CTA (couples register / vendors book a booth);
- One pre-Fair email blast to subscribed Setnayan couples + vendors;
- SEO surface + "Powered by Setnayan" referral traffic during the promo cycle;
- On-site Setnayan team presence (a vendor + couple onboarding / redemption station).

**Organizer provides (in-kind):**

- **Major-sponsor billing** for Setnayan on all Fair marketing collateral (printed banners, programs, social collaterals, press releases) — at minimum, logo placement equivalent to a Major Sponsor tier;
- **One free booth** at the Fair (single 3×3m or equivalent) for Setnayan to demo, onboard, and run the discount-code redemption desk;
- **Stage announcements** — 1–3 minutes during opening + one additional mid-event spot (Setnayan supplies the script);
- Honoring the **funnel discount codes** in §4.

No money flows in either direction under Model A. The discount is the Fair's customer-acquisition expense; Setnayan is paid in funnel attribution + brand exposure + free booth + sponsor billing.

### 3.2 Model B — Cash (paid sponsorship)

A clean transactional alternative for Organizers who prefer not to trade booth space, stage time, or sponsor billing. Two SKUs (owner-locked 2026-06-03; ID home: 0042 §6.5):

| SKU | What Setnayan provides | Price (per Fair cycle) |
|---|---|---|
| `boost_featured_plus_email` | Homepage featured slot + `/fairs/[slug]` page + dedicated email blast | **₱9,999** |
| `boost_featured_only` | Homepage featured slot + `/fairs/[slug]` page + sitemap inclusion (no email blast) | **₱2,999** |

Funnel discount codes (§4) are **optional** under Model B — the Organizer may run them to deepen the funnel but is not required to.

**Pre-leverage transparency:** until Setnayan has audience leverage (~10K active couples + ~500 verified vendors), Model B buys early-mover access to a growing surface, not proven reach. This is disclosed at signup (§6). Prices follow the charm-pricing ladder; round-number quotes are not used.

### 3.3 Payment & reconciliation (Model B only)

Model B follows Setnayan's standard apply-then-pay rail (0034): the Organizer receives a unique reference code + BDO / GCash instructions, pays externally, and Setnayan reconciles within the 24-hour SLA before the Featured slot activates. Reference codes expire 7 days after issue if unpaid.

## 4. Funnel discount codes

| Audience | Benefit | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Vendors who register for the Fair via Setnayan | **10–15% off the standard booth fee** ⚠ **OWNER-VALIDATE** (single % vs. tiered) | Setnayan-issued unique code, redeemed at the Organizer's vendor-booking checkout |
| Couples who register for the Fair via Setnayan | **One of: free welcome kit / fast-track entry / first-100-arrivals giveaway** ⚠ **OWNER-VALIDATE** (Organizer's choice; min. one) | Setnayan-issued QR / badge presented at Fair entrance |

**Mechanics (binding where codes are used):**

- Codes are **Setnayan-issued, Organizer-redeemed**. Setnayan generates a unique alphanumeric code per Fair (e.g., `SETNAYAN-WEX2026-V15`).
- The code is shown on `/fairs/[fair-slug]` only to authenticated Setnayan users who clicked the Fair's "Apply for a booth" / "Register to attend" CTA.
- Redemption happens in the **Organizer's own** booth-booking / registration checkout; the Organizer applies the discount and reports redemptions back to Setnayan (webhook or end-of-event reconciliation report).
- Codes **expire at the Fair's start date**. One code per user per Fair; non-transferable.
- For **Model A**, honoring the codes is mandatory and the discount is the Organizer's expense. For **Model B**, codes are optional.

## 5. Term, cancellation & exclusivity

- **Term:** one Boost cycle = one Fair, ending at that Fair's start date. Not a standing subscription; the Organizer re-signs per Fair.
- **Cancellation by Organizer:**
  - *Model A* — either party may withdraw up to **14 days before the Fair**; after that, in-kind commitments (collateral already printed, slot already promoted) stand.
  - *Model B* — refundable in full if cancelled **before the Featured slot goes live**; **50% refundable** ⚠ **OWNER-VALIDATE** once live but more than 14 days pre-Fair; **non-refundable** within 14 days of the Fair (production + email already committed).
- **Cancellation by Setnayan:** Setnayan may pull a Boost (with pro-rata Model B refund) if the Fair is cancelled, misrepresented, or the Organizer breaches §6 / §8.
- **Exclusivity:** ⚠ **OWNER-VALIDATE** — V1 starting position: Setnayan does **not** guarantee category or geographic exclusivity and may boost more than one Fair, including in the same city/month, subject to the 3-slot homepage cap. Any agreed exclusivity window must be written into the signature block.
- **Model A volume cap:** ⚠ **OWNER-VALIDATE** — Setnayan may cap the number of free Model A deals per quarter (on-site staffing has real cost); beyond the cap, only Model B is offered.

## 6. Representations & transparency

- Setnayan represents that Featured slots, the `/fairs/[slug]` page, and the email blast will be delivered as described for the cycle paid/bartered for, subject to the 3-slot cap and the launch-gate state disclosed at signup.
- Where Boost is opened pre-gate (admin override, §2.1), Setnayan discloses current audience numbers in writing **before** signature, so the Organizer's decision is informed.
- The Organizer represents that the Fair is real, the stated date/venue/scale are accurate, and the Organizer has authority to grant the in-kind benefits (Model A) it commits to.

---

## 7. Intellectual property & use of marks

- Each party grants the other a **limited, non-exclusive, cycle-scoped license** to use its name, logo, and marks solely to promote the Fair (Setnayan on the Organizer's collateral; the Fair's marks on Setnayan's surfaces).
- Neither party acquires ownership of the other's marks. All goodwill inures to the mark owner.
- The license terminates at the end of the Boost cycle. Setnayan may retain the `/fairs/[slug]` page in an archived/past-event state with the Fair's marks for historical and SEO continuity unless the Organizer objects in writing.
- Photos, copy, and creative supplied by either party may be used by the other only for promoting that Fair, not for unrelated marketing, without separate written consent.

---

## 8. Data, privacy & compliance

- Funnel attribution data (Setnayan-attributed vendor bookings + couple registrations) flows to Setnayan for negotiation leverage and is handled per the **Setnayan Privacy & Security Policy** (RA 10173 compliant).
- The Organizer must handle any Setnayan-referred user data it receives (booth applicants, couple registrants) in compliance with RA 10173, and must not resell or repurpose it.
- Neither party sells the other's referred personal data to third parties.

## 9. Signing mechanics — manual in V1

Mirrors the Vendor Agreement § 12.1 path:

1. At signup, Setnayan emails the Organizer a PDF of this Agreement (model + Fair details + any negotiated terms filled into the signature block), signed by Setnayan Team.
2. Organizer prints, signs, scans/photographs, and returns the signed PDF to **partnerships@setnayan.com**.
3. Setnayan stores the signed PDF in R2 bucket `setnayan-fair-contracts` under key `fairs/{fair_id}/signed_boost_agreement_{date}.pdf`.
4. For Model B, the Featured slot activates only after both the signed PDF is filed **and** payment is reconciled (§3.3).
5. The Setnayan-signed copy sent in step 1 is the binding document; counter-signature is optional in V1.

**V1.5:** migrate to in-app e-signature (same provider path as the Vendor Agreement) — non-breaking.
