## 1. The verdict in three sentences

**Ship the free watermarked tier — but the overlay is the last piece of work, not the first: `panoodCameraCapForSku` returns 0 for any non-tier code (`apps/web/lib/panood-camera-seats.ts:87-89`) and `panoodCameraClaimUrl` has zero production callers (`:167-170`, test-only), so "connect everything and test it first" is unreachable today at any price, for free AND paid customers alike.** **Three of the owner's four numbers should change: the 30-minutes-per-account meter must be deleted outright (its key is free — signup auto-confirms email through the admin API with no rate limit at `apps/web/app/signup/actions.ts:225-233` and Turnstile is inert until two separate switches are set), "24 hours from unlock" becomes 36 hours from the first *paid* press-live, and there must be no hours-before limit at all; the whole-screen overlay is RIGHT and every seat that tried to soften it produced a mark that measurably cannot be seen.** **The single most expensive thing in this packet is not the paywall: `panood-rtc:{eventId}` has no authorization whatsoever (`apps/web/lib/panood-webrtc.ts:261-265`, zero `realtime.messages` policies in any migration) and the transport is one-publisher-one-viewer, so any stranger with an event id can answer a camera's offer and black out the couple's own monitor mid-ceremony — fix that before a single real-event test, paywall or no paywall.**

### 2.2 The overlay treatment — the LIVE STUDIO LOCK

The council's two proposed treatments both failed on physics, and the surviving design is a synthesis of their corrected halves.

- **Brand's floating glyph is dead.** Computed WCAG contrast for its exact spec (`rgba(23,22,15,.30)` scrim, mark at opacity .26, corners at .18): **1.07:1 against a blown-out white-dress frame, 1.17:1 mid-grey, 1.63:1 in a dim church**, against a 3.00:1 floor for a non-text graphic to be perceivable. That is a brand stamp nobody can see, over video at 70% of original luminance — i.e. still perfectly watchable. It is the one outcome owner message (3) forbids.
- **Red Team's `saturate(0.25) contrast(0.8)` is dead.** OBS ships a Color Correction filter with a Saturation slider; the target population is *by definition* OBS users, and a linear saturation multiply is invertible through a GUI in ten seconds. It also costs a per-frame GPU shader pass on the one laptop already running N WebRTC decodes plus the OBS encode, and it destroys cross-camera white-balance matching — one of the first things anyone checks on a multiview, and therefore part of the proof the free tier exists to deliver.

**What ships:**

1. **Geometric degradation, not colour degradation.** The `<video>` is scaled to ~62% and letterboxed inside a marked frame. Geometry is not invertible by any OBS colour filter, costs one static transform (no per-frame work), and preserves framing, focus, exposure and colour judgement completely. This is Red Team's insight with its defeatable half removed.
2. **A knocked-out, high-contrast mark, not a tinted `<img>`.** `mask-image: url(/brand/setnayan-mark.svg)` + `background: var(--sn-gold-100)` (#F3ECDF), opacity ≥ 0.78, at 52vmin centred, over a scrim of `rgba(23,22,15,.55)`. This clears 3.00:1 in every lighting condition. Brand's "the glyph cannot be tinted or knocked out" constraint is **false** — a CSS mask is literally a knockout, needs no inlining, and cannot collide ids; and the SVG's `clipPath` is a verified no-op (it clips to exactly the viewBox).
3. **Four anti-crop corner marks** at 9vmin, same knockout, opacity .55, inset 4vmin — cropping into a quadrant still yields marked video. This applies to the **tiles too**; Brand's "inspection-class tiles get only a corner bug" is the croppable configuration it condemned two paragraphs earlier, and it silently overrode the owner's explicit "ALL SCREENS." Overruled.
4. **Type stack:** `SETNAYAN` in `var(--sn-font)` 800 uppercase tracking .04em in #F3ECDF; below it a mono pill reading **`NOT FOR BROADCAST`**. Not "PREVIEW" — in this codebase "preview" already means *unbuilt placeholder* (`control-room.tsx:608` "preview — live video arrives with the streaming rollout"; `device-frame.tsx:60-68` on a device mockup). Brand cited both files as precedent without reading what they say. Not "UNPAID" either — under our allow-unpaid-press ruling that string is composited into the couple's own YouTube in front of 300 guests.
5. **Static. No animation. No `backdrop-filter`.** Brand is right on both and the reasoning survives: motion is the visual signature of nag-ware and costs a repaint per frame on the wedding-day machine.
6. **`pointer-events: none`, mandatory** — the SplitProgram divider (`control-room.tsx:660-689`) has pointer-capture and keyboard handlers a full-screen layer would swallow.
7. **Renders UNDER the status layer.** The on-air chip and per-tile status chips (`control-room.tsx:766-774`) stay fully legible. *Mark the picture, never the instruments* — "camera 3 · connected" reading green while the picture is locked is the entire trust mechanism. This is Brand's single best surviving contribution.
8. **Source the mark from `DEFAULT_BRAND_MARK_SVG` (`lib/brand-constants.ts:13`), never `useBrandMark()`.** An admin can swap the brand mark at runtime (`brand-provider.tsx:20-40`); a pale or wrong-aspect uploaded PNG would silently void the paywall.
9. **No price on the lock.** Not because the catalog is unresolved (it is resolved: ₱1,500/₱2,500) but because a price baked into a layer OBS composites into a live broadcast cannot be hot-fixed in front of 400 guests.

### 2.3 What payment unlocks

Nothing, until press-live. **There is no third state.** Paid-and-not-yet-pressed is fully locked, exactly as owner message (5) requires. One instant does both things:

> **press LIVE while entitled → overlay clears on every surface → 36-hour window opens.**

Payment additionally buys: the Desktop 8-camera cap (vs 3), and TURN relay headroom beyond the free allowance. Every *feature* is already in the free tier.

### 2.4 Time windows

| Thing | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Free-tier access | **No limit. Ever.** No hours-before, no minutes, no expiry. |
| Paid window | **36 hours from the first paid press-live**, stamped once, never movable. |
| What the window governs | The right to press live **again** after a stop, and new camera-seat claims. |
| What the window NEVER governs | **The overlay on a running program.** Expiry is evaluated only at fresh page load / fresh pop-out open, and the predicate carries `AND is_live = false`. There is no poll. A session that outruns 36h keeps its clean feed to the end. |
| Second press inside the window | **Free.** Same window. This removes most of the harm from an accidental press and is cheaper than the admin reversal path. |
| Multi-day event | Two event-days = two purchases (2 × ₱2,500 = ₱5,000 **MODELLED**). Sell it as "a second day," never as a renewal. A pre-nup shoot is almost never broadcast, so press-live activation already handles it for free. |

**Why 36h and not 24h, and why not Unit Economics' event-date floor.** 24h fits the PH wedding shape (~18h: 5–6am prep, church mid-morning, reception to midnight) with ~6h slack — but that slack is consumed by exactly one common event: pressing live during the 6am prep-cam check instead of at the ceremony. Hours 25–36 cost us **₱0** and buy immunity from a crash-reload at hour 25. Unit Economics' `GREATEST(first_live_at + 36h, event_date + 1 day)` floor is **rejected**: `events.event_date` is customer-writable (RLS `couple_can_update_event`, `20260512000000_setnayan_base.sql:254-255`; a shipped customer action already writes it at `save-the-date/actions.ts:246`) and covered by no guard trigger. One PATCH setting `event_date` to 2029 buys a clean 8-camera feed for years. That seat applied exactly this rigour to `orders.expires_at` and then anchored its own window on an unguarded customer-writable column.

## 4. The activation trigger

**LOCKED: pressing LIVE.** Not re-debated. Below is how it is made correct.

### 4.1 How first-press is recorded

New nullable column **`panood_control_state.first_live_at timestamptz`**, stamped by a **`SECURITY DEFINER` BEFORE UPDATE trigger** — not by app code, because the couple can PATCH that table directly (`panood_control_state_couple_full`, `20270228010000:139-160`, no `FOR` clause = FOR ALL, `TO authenticated`).

The trigger does three things:

1. **Clamp write-once:** `NEW.first_live_at := COALESCE(OLD.first_live_at, NEW.first_live_at)`. A re-press, a `setLive(false)` then `setLive(true)`, or a direct PATCH attempting to null it are all no-ops. The window cannot be moved or restarted.
2. **Stamp only on a live transition AND only when entitled:** if `OLD.is_live IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE AND NEW.is_live = TRUE AND OLD.first_live_at IS NULL`, then `NEW.first_live_at := now()` **only if** an `EXISTS` check finds an order for this `event_id` with `status IN ('paid','fulfilled')` and `service_key IN ('PANOOD_SYSTEM','PANOOD_SYSTEM_MOBILE')`. This is what kills Engineering's fatal: **an unpaid press stamps nothing**, so approval-time can never clear the overlay without a press, and there is no third state.
3. **Server clock only.** `now()` from Postgres. This also resolves the "two clocks, no shared server `now()`" gotcha — the whole comparison lives on the DB clock.

**Do NOT use `orders.expires_at`.** `guard_orders_protected_columns()` (`20270226279630:38-56`) enumerates 13 protected columns and `expires_at` is not among them, while `orders` is RLS-writable `FOR ALL` by the row owner — a lease riding it is self-extendable in one PATCH. **Add `expires_at` to that guard list in the same migration regardless,** and add a matching guard on `panood_control_state.first_live_at`.

**Clean-feed predicate:** `entitled AND first_live_at IS NOT NULL AND (is_live = TRUE OR first_live_at + interval '36 hours' > now())`. Evaluated at fresh server render only — never on a poll, never against a live session.

### 4.2 The confirmation step

A sheet, not a yes/no, and it must name the calendar:

> **Going live starts your paid 36 hours.**
> It is **5:42 AM, Saturday 12 September**. The SETNAYAN lock clears now.
> `[Start broadcast]` `[Not yet]`

If today is not `events.event_date`, add a second red line: **"Your event is 23 days away. Are you sure?"** That single date check eliminates nearly the whole accidental-burn class, because the accidental case *is* the rehearsal case and we already know the date. (The date is customer-writable, so it is a UX hint only — never an enforcement input.)

### 4.3 Reversal of an accidental start

**Admin-only, from the payments/orders console, by nulling `first_live_at`, logged to the order ledger, no time limit, single-admin authority.** Risk's "within 60 minutes" is unachievable — the same seat cites a 24-hour manual reconciliation SLA on the money path, and the operating model is one human approving exceptions. There is no self-serve undo; a self-serve reset turns 36h into "36h whenever I want." At 63 events it is not farmable. **A second press inside an already-open window is free**, which handles the common accident without any admin at all.

### 4.5 The OBS ordering trap

OBS window-captures the pop-out and streams whatever it shows, so a couple who starts OBS before pressing live pushes a locked feed to their own YouTube. Three mitigations, all inside the `fixed inset-0 z-[9999]` layer so they are what OBS captures:

**While locked**, a bottom instruction band, Space Mono, tracking .2em, over a `rgba(23,22,15,.72)→transparent` gradient:

> `SETNAYAN — NOT FOR BROADCAST`
> `Don't start OBS yet. Press LIVE in the control room. This screen clears, then start OBS.`

**On clear**, a 10-second green confirmation state so the operator *sees* the transition rather than guessing:

> `CLEARED · your feed is clean — start OBS now`

**The clear must be instant and server-confirmed.** The `setLive` server action returns the freshly resolved lock and the control room republishes `ProgramFrame` synchronously on that response. There is no poll, so the clear cannot arrive a minute late. And it must **never** fire on the optimistic local flip at `control-room.tsx:296-303` (`setLiveLocal(next) // optimistic`, rollback at `:301`) — clearing on that flash shows a clean feed to a capturing OBS for the duration of a failed or unpaid press. This was The Filipino Couple's finding and no other seat caught it.

**Rename the buttons in the same PR.** There are **five** "Go live" labels across **three** pages, and the money trigger is only two of them: `control-room.tsx:494` and `:998` (the `is_live` toggle — the paid trigger); `setup/go-live-card.tsx:122` and `:237` (creates the YouTube broadcast; free, host-only, deliberately not SKU-gated per `setup/actions.ts:117-122`); and `panood/page.tsx:219` **"Go live — free"** (the add-on landing hero, documented at `:211-212` as "never a paywall"). Rename the setup control to **"Create your YouTube broadcast"** — which is what it does — and leave the hero CTA's "free" wording intact but distinct. "Go live" must mean exactly one thing.

## 5. The offline problem

**The premise is false and must be corrected in the product copy and in this council's own framing: the Desktop tier does not run offline, has no local signalling, and today cannot.**

- WebRTC signalling rides Supabase Realtime (`lib/panood-webrtc.ts:3-6`, `:55-57`). No internet, no camera pairing, no stream.
- The service worker deliberately does not intercept app-shell navigations — dashboard, login, auth, marketing (`apps/web/public/sw.js:13-14`, `:377-396`), after the Safari "Service Worker context closed" crash. Only the guest `/[slug]` navigation is served offline. A control-room reload with no network is unrecoverable **regardless of any paywall**.

Note for the record: the Unrepeatable-Day Risk seat's cited evidence for existing "in-house / offline stream" marketing copy is **fabricated** — no such copy exists in `setup/page.tsx` or anywhere in the panood tree. There is nothing to delete. But the conclusion stands on its own: **do not add any offline promise, and if one is ever written, it is a wedding-day liability independent of the paywall.**

**Ruling on fail-open vs fail-closed:**

1. **At the gate, fail CLOSED.** Unknown entitlement = overlay ON. This is a money gate and it inverts the codebase's fail-open default deliberately.
2. **Once resolved clean, hold it for the session.** After `entitled AND first_live_at IS NOT NULL` resolves true at page load, the control room and pop-out hold that decision for the life of the surface. There is no re-check, no poll, no heartbeat. A network blip at minute 40 of the reception therefore **cannot** re-lock — because nothing asks again. This is the mechanism that makes "never re-lock a live program" true rather than aspirational.
3. **A fresh load with no network shows a loud recovery state, not a silent lock.** `Re-checking your unlock — Retry` over the black no-signal card, never the gold lock painted silently over a paying couple's feed. If the network is down, nothing works anyway; say so honestly instead of implying a billing problem.
4. **Every `eventSkuActive` consumer in this feature must be wrapped.** `eventSkuActive` throws on any DB error that is not `42P01`/`42703` (`lib/entitlements.ts:246-251`), and only ~14 of 73 call sites catch. An uncaught throw at a new call site produces a 500, not a lock — fine as a paywall, catastrophic as a wedding-day experience. The shared helper catches and returns `locked: true`.

## 6. Answer the owner's question — "how many hours before the event?"

**NONE. There should be no hours-before limit on the free tier at all. Unlimited access from the moment the event exists.** Unanimous across all seven seats and the chair. Four reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The overlay is already the paywall.** A locked feed is worthless as a broadcast. An hours-before window is a second paywall stacked on the first, buying nothing.
2. **It would enforce nothing.** Any hours-before rule is a read-time comparison against `events.event_date`, which the couple can edit themselves (RLS `couple_can_update_event`; a shipped customer action already writes it). It is a wall the honest couple hits and the dishonest one edits away.
3. **The rig test genuinely happens weeks out.** At home 1–4 weeks before, at the venue ocular 2–4 weeks before, during ingress the day before. Every one of those is outside a 24/48/72h window. **MODELLED**: pairing the first camera takes 8–12 minutes (a cousin hunting the claim link in a 200-message Viber thread, then granting camera permission on a phone he has never used this way), 3–5 minutes per camera after; three cameras is 14–22 minutes, eight is 29–47.
4. **The free tier is our own QA.** The controller transport has never been tested against two physical devices — `panood-program-bridge.test.ts` is 14 tests against fakes and there is no WebRTC or two-device integration test anywhere in the panood tree. Every free watermarked test a couple runs is free coverage on the highest-risk untested system we ship. Restricting the free tier restricts our own test data.

**There IS one real hours-before number, and it is a PAYMENT deadline, not an access limit.** Apply-then-pay is reconciled by hand with a 24-hour SLA — an order only becomes an entitlement when an admin flips status (`app/admin/payments/actions.ts:320-327`). The buy surface must say, in plain language:

> **Buy at least 2 days before your event.** We verify payments by hand, usually within 24 hours.

This is not cosmetic. A couple who GCash-pays at 6am on the wedding day and is approved 24 hours later broadcasts their entire wedding locked, having paid ₱2,500. That is the **largest single failure in this design and it is structural, not adversarial**. It is also the strongest argument for Owner decision #8: Live Studio should not become buyable until there is an instant unlock path.
