Maria & Juan Are Married

After seven years, two families became one under the Sto. Domingo lights — and, remarkably, not a single phone got in the way of the moment.
By the Setnayan Desk · Photographs by Liwanag Photography
Maria & Juan, moments after the first kiss — captured by the Papic paparazzi.

It began, as the best stories do, on a rainy afternoon in Baguio. On Saturday it ended — or rather, truly began — before 250 of the people who love them most, in a ceremony that ran, against all odds, exactly on time.

"We just wanted everyone in the room, not behind a screen. Somehow, it worked."

Guests arrived to a garden palette of alabaster and champagne. The processional moved on cue; the coordinator's quiet broadcasts kept the evening gliding from cocktails to the last dance without a single anxious text.

Behind the seamlessness was a plan months in the making — and a small army of vendors, most of them matched, booked, and coordinated in one place. The couple, for their part, say they mostly remember the food, the speeches, and the way the room sang.
