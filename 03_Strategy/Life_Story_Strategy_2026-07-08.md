# Life Story — Strategy

> **"Your life, through the people who lived it with you."**
> A research-grounded strategy for Setnayan's emotional centerpiece.
> Dated 2026-07-08 · a newer sibling to `People_Graph_and_Lifelong_Identity_2026-07-04.md`.
> Status: **Reframe OWNER-LOCKED 2026-07-08** — *"make it while they're alive, not for when they die."* The rest is synthesis/proposal. (see §2)

---

## 0. The thesis, in one breath

The origin idea — *"what you see when your life flashes before you as you die"* — is a powerful **muse**, but it is the wrong thing to **ship or say**. The research is unambiguous on this: the near-death science is our weakest leg, the death framing is ethically fraught and repels users, and the parts that are real (memory, perspective-taking, celebration) don't need death at all.

So we keep the *magic* and drop the *mortality*:

> **Life Story is the memorial of your celebrations — a living life-review you experience while you are alive, assembled from the footage of everyone who was there, and pointed forward, not at the end.**

Every strategic choice below flows from what the evidence actually supports.

---

## 1. The evidence spine — three tiers of confidence

We researched the phenomenon rather than assume it. The findings sort into three tiers, and the product must be built on the firm one and merely *inspired* by the shaky one.

### Tier 1 — FIRM. Build the product on this. (Memory science + wellbeing)
- **The reminiscence bump is one of the most robust findings in autobiographical memory:** people recall disproportionately more memories from ~ages 10–30, driven by **identity- and meaning-formation** (Munawar, Kuhn & Haque, *PLOS ONE* 2018, systematic review of 68 studies). Weddings, debuts, christenings, coming-of-age — **land exactly in this window.**
- **A "golden 20s" positivity peak:** the remembered emotional tone of life events grows more positive with age-at-event, **peaking in the 20s** (Wank, Wirth & Habermas, *Memory & Cognition* 2023). This gives our significance engine a *defensible positive-salience prior* for milestone celebrations.
- **Structured life-review reliably supports wellbeing** — ego-integrity (g≈0.64), purpose-in-life (g≈0.48), life satisfaction, death-preparation — across ~128 controlled studies (Pinquart & Forstmeier, *Aging & Mental Health* 2012). **Direction is robust across decades; magnitude is heterogeneous.**
- **Structure is a measured moderator, not taste:** formal *life-review therapy* beats casual reminiscence; **individual** framing beats group (SMD 1.29 vs ~0); **bounded** (~≤8 "chapters") beats open-ended (Zhong et al., *Behavioral Sciences* 2023).
- **The legacy artifact for family is the single most consistently valued outcome.** The most rigorous end-of-life legacy RCT (Chochinov's Dignity Therapy, *Lancet Oncology* 2011, n=441) **failed to move objective distress** but won on *"helpful to family," "changed how my family saw me,"* dignity, and quality of life. → **The exportable "film" for loved ones is not a nice-to-have; it is the emotional payload.**

### Tier 2 — REAL BUT SELF-REPORTED. Use as *experience*, never as *proof*. (NDE phenomenology)
- **"Through the eyes of the people who were there" is real in the literature** — ~1/3 of life-review experiencers re-live moments from another person's perspective and feel the impact they had on others (Greyson 2021; Tassell-Matamua 2024). **This is the single strongest anchor for our USP** — but it is *reported phenomenology*, not verified perception.
- **Non-linear / by-significance is ONE reported ordering, not the universal law** (chronological, anterograde, and panoramic all occur; Katz et al. 2017). → Offer significance-order as the *default mode*, keep chronological available; don't claim non-linear is "correct."
- **Timeless / panoramic is a robustly recurring theme** (Hashemi et al., *Frontiers in Psychology* 2023, 54 studies / 465 experiencers). → A fullscreen, self-composing montage is phenomenologically faithful.

### Tier 3 — WEAK. Origin story only. NEVER an evidence claim. (Dying-brain neuroscience)
- The famous studies (Vicente 2022 single 87-yr-old; Borjigin 2013 nine rats; Xu 2023 four patients, only two showed the surge, none survived) are **n=1–9, heavily confounded** (hemorrhage, seizures, high-dose anticonvulsants), **correlational**, and a **2024 peer-reviewed critique argues the gamma may be artifact ("fake gamma")** with little direct link to consciousness (Shaw, *F1000Research* 2024). The "life review" reading is **explicit authorial speculation** in every primary source.
- **Marketing rule, non-negotiable:** *"some scientists speculate,"* **never** *"science shows."*

### REFUTED — do not use, ever.
- The popular *"you see and feel your family's grief at your death"* claim was **refuted 0–3** in adversarial verification. The supported feature is perspective-taking **within recalled celebrations** and awareness of **positive impact on others** — the joy you brought, not the grief you'll leave.

**Verdict:** the science that holds up (memory, wellbeing) points at *celebration and curation*; the science that doesn't (dying brain) is the only part that pointed at *death*. Following the evidence **is** the strategy.

## 2. The reframe — OWNER-LOCKED (2026-07-08)

> **Owner's words:** *"We do not want it to sound like it is for when they die — we make it while they are alive."*

**From** *"the flash of your life before you die"* → **to** *"the life review you get to have **while you're still here** — the living memorial of your celebrations, seen through everyone who was there."*

We keep the full power of the life-review *experience* (the panoramic rush of your most significant moments) and strip the mortality entirely. The trigger is **living** — a new milestone, an anniversary, a quiet evening, a story sent home — **never death.** No countdowns, no deathbed, no "someday when you're gone."

Three independent reasons the death framing had to go:
1. **Science** — it was the only part resting on the weak Tier-3 evidence.
2. **Ethics** — death-triggered "grief tech" is the most-criticized category in the literature: monetizing grief, disrupting healthy mourning, "deadbots" and unwanted "digital hauntings" (Hollanek & Nowaczyk-Basińska, Cambridge 2024). A death trigger both invites those harms and repels users.
3. **Culture** — Filipinos already hold *remembrance and joy in one frame* (Undas). We meet them there, in life, not at a deathbed.

**Locked.** Everything downstream assumes the alive-framing.

## 3. The moat — sharpened by the market research

The obvious pitch ("we aggregate everyone's footage") is **already commoditized**: per-event guest-photo apps (Wedbox, Kululu, WeddingPhotoSwap, JoinMyMoment…) all QR-collect many guests' media — but they stop at **one event**. Apple/Google "Memories" auto-montage beautifully — but **only from your own device's library**. StoryFile/HereAfter capture **one interview subject**; Storyworth/Remento capture **one author's words**.

**Everyone is single-source, OR single-event, OR single-author.** The unclaimed intersection is the whole game:

> **multi-source × multi-event × one-person × auto-rendered.**
> Many people's footage, of *many* celebrations, pivoted around *one* life, rendered into a cinematic review. **No incumbent found does this.**

And the defensible layer is **not the montage** (Apple/Google prove that's solved). It is:
- an **identity graph that follows a person across events** (the `person_story_items` spine already designed);
- **cross-event consent/rights plumbing** (who filmed whom, who may use it — heavy under RA 10173, and therefore a barrier that *protects* us once built);
- the **habit of guests and vendors depositing footage into a person's archive by default.**

That is a **data-network moat that compounds** — every event thickens every attendee's story. Setnayan already **sits at the point of capture** (Papic, vendors, Panood), so footage originates *inside* the system instead of being imported after the fact. **The fastest clone path** (a guest-photo app, or Google adding "shared-event → person timeline" stitching) is blocked not by our algorithm but by **owning the PH events + vendor supply side.** Defend there.

## 4. The product, shaped by the evidence

One engine (the **Moment Graph** — weighted, cacheable, tunable), three renderings, each mapped to a finding:

| Rendering | What it is | Grounded in |
|---|---|---|
| **The scroll reel** | The everyday, explorable feed of your moments, significance-ordered (chronological toggle available). | Reminiscence bump + "bounded ≤8 chapters" structure; non-linear-as-one-mode. |
| **The flash** | The cinematic auto-play: opens on your most-recurring face, moments surface by weight, **turns once to someone else's camera** ("this is how Bea saw that day"), holds quiet on the ✦, **ends on the present pointing forward** — never death. | NDE phenomenology (perspective-taking, panoramic, timeless) as *experience*; forward-ending from the ethics + culture guardrails. |
| **The film** (Phase 2) | The exportable, big-screen legacy artifact for family; shareable to diaspora relatives. | Chochinov: the **family-legacy artifact is the most-valued outcome.** This is the monetizable payoff. |

**Significance engine** (isolated, unit-tested, tunable) weights: recurrence of key people (Tier-1), in-memoriam presence (held longest — opt-in only), people-present count, event-type weight (kasal/binyag/debut rank high — reminiscence-bump-aligned), multi-camera coverage, user pins. **Curated highlights, never exhaustive completeness** — because the positivity effect *reverses for whole-life narratives* (Martin et al. 2023); give the user editorial control over what's included.

## 5. Positioning & go-to-market

**Voice.** Experiential, never clinical: *reflect · celebrate · preserve · share · leave a legacy.* Absolutely no medical claims ("reduces depression"), no death imagery, no countdowns, no "keep them alive." Honest wellbeing posture: *"grounded in decades of research on life review and reminiscence"* — true; **not** *"clinically proven."*

**Culturally native — this is a Philippines wedge, not a US one.**
- **Kapwa** (shared self): memory is experienced *together* → the multi-perspective model is culturally intuitive, not novel.
- **Life narrated through celebrations** (binyag → debut → kasal → fiesta → reunion) → the archive maps onto how Filipino life is already structured.
- **Undas**: joyful, communal remembrance → our exact emotional register; a positioning gift no US grief-tech has.
- **~2.2M OFWs**: shared media is the emotional bridge across separation. **The life-story sent home to relatives abroad is a distribution wedge with real emotional pull** — arguably the strongest single GTM lever.

**Market framing — corrected.**
- **Do NOT lead with the brief's US$356.7B wedding-videography figure** — it's internally inconsistent (implies a ~$1T wedding market vs Grand View's own ~$420B) and won't survive diligence. Anchor on the **~US$25B independent wedding-photography** range as a conservative floor, plus PH marriage counts.
- **PH marriages are declining** — 414,213 (2023) → 371,825 (2024, −10.2%; PSA). **This is a strategic mandate, not a footnote:** frame the product as a **lifelong life-events archive**, not a wedding feature. Weddings are the *entry point*; the archive is the *product*.

## 6. Ethics & emotional safety — as architecture, not a policy page

The grief-tech literature gives a concrete checklist; we bake it into the data model:
1. **Author *about* a person; never impersonate them.** No autonomous AI "voice," no chatbot of anyone living or dead. Minimal behavioral agency. *(This is the single line between us and the deadbot harms.)*
2. **Consent is mutual, explicit, revocable — and defaults to the living.** V1 centers self- and family-authored stories of living people, where consent is unambiguous. (The existing **counsel gate on cross-event participant media is correct** — keep it.)
3. **In-memoriam is opt-in only — never a surprise.** No auto-resurfacing, no "on this day" grief nudge, no ads in a lost person's context. The ✦ is a feather, never a banner; a gentle, skippable content note precedes any memorial view.
4. **Adults-first; dignified retirement.** Minors are a later, guardian-held, counsel-gated phase. Users can pause/close their story.
5. **Monetize the celebration, never the grief.** The literature's sharpest critique is charging the bereaved; keep every grief-adjacent surface upsell-free.

## 7. Build sequence (aligned to the existing plan)

- **Phase 1 — ships live now (own-events).** Flash + scroll reel + significance engine, **celebration-framed**, assembled from the user's *own* events' multi-camera footage. The perspective-shift lives **within your own event** (many cameras at one wedding), so the signature beat works with no counsel dependency.
- **Phase 1.5 — the true moat (counsel-gated).** Cross-event multi-homing via `person_story_items` — a story from events you only *attended*. Unlocks after PH counsel; the schema, flag, and consent plumbing already exist and are deliberately off.
- **Phase 2 — the legacy artifact.** The exportable film (Remotion/FFmpeg + Setnayan-owned music) for big-screen projection at events and **diaspora sharing**. This is the premium payoff the wellbeing evidence points at.
- **The moat deepens** as the deposit-by-default capture habit spreads across the vendor + guest supply side.

## 8. Honest risks & unknowns

- **Neuroscience can't be leaned on** — kept as muse, never marketing.
- **PH marriage decline** — mitigated by the lifelong-archive framing (the reason to broaden beyond weddings).
- **Clone risk** — guest-photo apps or Google could stitch shared-event→person; defended by supply-side ownership + the consent graph, not by the montage.
- **No PH debut/christening market data found** — a sizing gap to fill, not a zero.
- **Wellbeing claims must stay experiential** — the effects are real in direction but heterogeneous in magnitude and concentrated in clinical populations; a general-audience medical claim is unsupportable.
- **Perspective-taking is self-report** — market the *experience*, never a metaphysical or proven-mechanism claim.

---

## 9. What "winning" looks like (the bets to instrument)

- **Capture density per event** — average distinct cameras (seats/guests/vendors) depositing per celebration. *(Feeds the moat + the perspective beat.)*
- **Person-graph growth** — people recurring across a user's events over time.
- **Flash completion + felt response** — do people finish it; do they replay it.
- **Film attach rate** — the premium legacy payoff.
- **Diaspora share rate** — stories sent to relatives abroad (the OFW wedge).
