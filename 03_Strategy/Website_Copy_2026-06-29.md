## Planuhan — Planner
**"Your spreadsheet doesn't know your guest list, your budget, or your day."**

A spreadsheet holds numbers. It doesn't know who RSVP'd, who's seated where, or which vendor is free on your date. Planuhan does, because every piece talks to every other piece.

It's a free, connected suite:

- **Guest list** — guests self-register with a personal QR, you assign an assistant to help manage them, and the system flags anyone who shows up uninvited.
- **Seat plan** — rule-based seating that lays itself out, live in 2D today, with a 3D walk-to-seat view rolling out so a guest can see the path to their chair before they arrive.
- **Mood board** — set your palette and direction once; it auto-copies into every service that needs it, so nothing gets re-explained.
- **Budget planner** — integrated with payments and your schedule, so what you owe and when you owe it sit in the same place as the plan itself.
- **Date picker** — pick a date by seeing which vendors are actually free, instead of guessing and backtracking.
- **Checklist** — what's done, what's next, what's overdue.
- **Printable PDF** — the whole plan, on paper, for the people at the venue who don't want an app.
- **Scheduler** — the run-of-show for the day itself.

This is the reason to open the app on a Tuesday night.

**"You've already opened twelve tabs and decided nothing."**

- **Adaptive checklist** — a plan that reshapes itself around your event, your date, and what you've already done.

Surian unlocks with a paid tier — a fraction of what a coordinator costs, doing the work of a planning team that would cost many times the price. See the full breakdown at [/pricing](/pricing).
