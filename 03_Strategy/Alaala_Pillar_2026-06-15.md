# Alaala — the Living-Memories Pillar (canonical definition)

> Owner-locked 2026-06-15. **Alaala** (a·la·A·la) — Tagalog for *memory · remembrance · keepsake*. The named, customer-facing pillar that gathers ~25 Setnayan features into one promise: **your wedding, kept alive.** This doc is the source of truth for the name, voice, the role-arc, the guardrail, and where Alaala embeds across the app. Pairs with [[Living_Memories_Manifesto_2026-06-14]] (the manifesto = the *why*; Alaala = the *name*).

## The name

- **Alaala** = the couple's living memory of their wedding — the collective noun over all the memory features.
- **Gloss once per surface for non-Tagalog guests:** *"Alaala — the memory you keep."* (The brand is Filipino-first and every memory feature is already Tagalog: Papic · Panood · Kwento · Pakanta · Pakulay · Pabati · Patiktok. Alaala is the umbrella they roll up into.)
- **Usage:** features keep their own names; they *become part of your Alaala.* "Your Alaala is being made." NOT a new SKU — a pillar/concept.
- Headline line still **"A new way to remember"**; tagline **"Memories that move"**; close *"Set na 'yan."*

## The thesis (present tense — locked 2026-06-15)

Not a glimpse of the future — **we are the future, and it's happening now.** Memory-keeping evolved: **paper albums → digital albums → Alaala (living memories).** A wedding was never meant to be frozen; the technology can finally hold what it actually was, and Setnayan delivers it *today.*

## The guardrail (load-bearing)

**The essence of the wedding will never be ruined.** Alaala *enhances* the day and never intrudes — the tech recedes so the couple and guests are **more present, not less** (presence over production). This is the sharpest wedge: most wedding-tech ADDS distraction (livestreams, photo walls, phones up); Alaala disappears. Already encoded by existing locks — Papic 5-Seats "stop filming, live it," non-disableable NSFW filter, face-blur opt-outs, couple 7-day review window, untagged-still-delivered, "let photographers take the grandest moments." Every Alaala surface should *show* this promise, not bury it.

## The role-arc (how the ~25 features compose)

Alaala is the arc of the day. Full feature list + build states in [[project_setnayan_living_memories_pillar_map]].

1. **The opening** — Save-the-Date · landing-page hero
2. **Capture the moment** — Papic (5 Seats / 3 Seats / Guest) · Camera Bridge · Patiktok · Custom QR
3. **Bring in the people who can't be there** — Live Studio · Papic Guest · day-of live mode
4. **Your guests' words & stories** — Kwento · Video Guestbook · Pabati
5. **Produce the keepsake** — SDE · Thank-You Video · AI Highlights · Personal Reels · Auto-Recap · Kwento Magazine
6. **The look & the sound** — Animated Monogram · Mood Board (Pakulay) · Pakanta + Song Bank · LED Background
7. **The living destination** — Editorial (front-page story) · Event Website · Gallery · Live Photo Wall · Seat-plan "where everyone sat"

## Execution — 3 lanes (owner: "all three, sequence; check in per lane")

- **Lane 1 — Narrative spine** *(now, copy/framing):* name Alaala in the live marketing (`/our-story` + homepage teaser) · plant the promise in **onboarding** · reframe the couple dashboard **Services into one "Alaala" pillar** (the role-arc, not a flat grid) · surface the **guardrail as a visible promise** · the **photographer-ally** line for vendors.
- **Lane 2 — In-app Alaala hub:** a dedicated couple surface with the save-the-date → day-of → editorial **through-line** the couple watches fill.
- **Lane 3 — Finish the keystones (the uncopyable layer):** **Kwento → Live Photo Wall → produced-output (SDE / Thank-You / Highlights)** — spec-only today; the parts competitors structurally can't copy.
