## Why we win vs a generic "30+ benefits" competitor
A generic directory's "30+" is mostly the same listing-and-leads commodity restated. Setnayan's edge is the spine no competitor bundles: **true 0% commission with direct off-platform GCash/BDO payouts (Setnayan never sits in your payment chain) · RA 8792 e-signed contracts as dispute-proof record · one cross-event identity that grows from weddings into every life event · a first-party Productions + crew ecosystem you can resell.** We're at parity on table stakes (microsite, calendar, reviews, basic analytics) but genuinely differentiated on **money freedom (0% commission, you keep 100%, paid directly), anti-fraud integrity, and the first-party ecosystem** that turns the platform from a lead list into the vendor's actual operating system. _(Note: Setnayan is NOT a tax/withholding agent for vendors — couples pay you directly, so no Form 2307, no Setnayan-issued vendor ORs.)_

## 1 · Discovery & Lead Quality
- **● 0% Commission, Forever** — Keep every peso of every booking; Setnayan never takes a cut, on or off platform.
- **● Faith & Region Matchmaking** — Surfaced to couples by the rites you serve (Catholic, Muslim, Tsinoy, Mixed) and the provinces your crew travels to — a tea-ceremony or Cebu-only specialist reaches the right couples instead of competing nationwide.
- **● Date-Open Priority** — Couples filter for who's actually free on their date; keeping your calendar current pushes you to the top.
- **● Lead Capture & Couple Matchmaking** — Every couple who finds you is a captured lead; the deterministic matchmaker pairs you to couples whose budget, style, and date you fit.
- **◐ Shortlist Radar** — Live count of couples who saved you + an alert when a rival enters a date you're bidding on.
- **◐ First-Look Window** — Reply fast + complete your profile to earn a head-start slot in front of new in-region couples.
- **◐ Booked-Out Waitlist** — Fully booked? Couples join your waitlist instead of bouncing — a cancellation becomes a recovered booking.
- **○ Style-Twin Discovery from Real Stories** — Couples who love a Real Story tap through to the vendors who made it; your past work becomes a look-alike funnel.

## 2 · Booking, Contracts & Operations
- **● Automated Bookings** — Inquiries flow into a real pipeline with quotes, packages, and milestones attached — no copy-paste from chat.
- **● Ultimate Team Calendar** — One shared calendar across services + crew: every held date, call time, deadline.
- **● Headcount That Quotes Itself** — Catering/styling quotes auto-update from the couple's live guest list + your crew covers ("180 guests + 22 crew = 202 covers").
- **● One-Tap RA 8792 Contracts** — Pull a PH-vendor contract from the clause library, drop in the package + milestones, both e-sign in-app — legally valid, no printing.
- **◐ Deposit Reservation, Lock-Free** — A recorded deposit holds the slot; money settles to your GCash/bank (we never touch it), the date is locked.
- **◐ Double-Booking Guard** — A held slot blocks a second booking, scoped per-service + per-crew-role — you physically can't oversell.
- **◐ Change-Order Trail** — Mid-plan add-ons become logged, both-acknowledged change orders with their own amount + deadline.
- **○ Day-Of Run-of-Show & Handover** — A shared minute-by-minute timeline the couple/coordinator/crew see live; mark the gallery delivered against the booking.

- **● Stand-Up-for-Yourself Dispute Mediation** — A neutral team reviews the on-platform chat/contract/milestones before anything touches your rating.
- **● Contract-on-Record Protection** — Every quote, e-signed contract, and milestone is timestamped on-platform — a clean RA 8792 paper trail.
- **● Reverse-Image Theft Watch** — Your shots are tied to the events you covered; reposts of your portfolio get flagged.
- **◐ Receipt-Backed Reviews** — Every rating carries a "booked through Setnayan" mark; each finished event is a permanent dated line on your profile.
