What's different on mobile

  • • 5-tab spine in the thumb zone — Home · Inbox · Bookings · ₱ · More.
  • • Home = signal cards, not a profile page. Tap to act.
  • • Token chip pinned in the header; the burn cost shows on the Answer button.
  • • “More” is a 3-section accordion (Storefront / Reach / Account) + search — never a 15-item dump.
  • • Locks are visible (🔒 Ent) with a tap-through to upgrade.
  • • Same group definitions as desktop — one source of truth.

Tip: Inbox → tap a lead → Answer · ◎-3 to watch the token chip tick down.
