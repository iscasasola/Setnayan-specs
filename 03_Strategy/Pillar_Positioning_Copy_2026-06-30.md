## Thesis — Never lose a milestone

Never lose a milestone memory with Setnayan. While other apps are single-use
utilities, Setnayan is built for the long haul. It is a single, continuous space
where all your life events live on. From initial planning to live execution and
lifelong preservation, it is the only event tool you will ever need.

---

## Ala Ala · Your Lifelong Collection of Memories

A photo album is just a record. Ala Ala is the memory itself.

This is the heart of the Setnayan experience. While traditional event planning
ends when the day is over, your memories shouldn't have to. Ala Ala is your
permanent memory hub, a single, continuous space where every event you hold and
every event you attend lives on. It is not a temporary utility you close after
the guests leave. It is a secure, personal archive that grows richer year after
year. By transforming fleeting logistics into a lifelong timeline, Ala Ala
ensures you never lose a milestone memory.

---

## Likha · Your Creative Studio

Your event has a look. Give it a voice.

Likha means to create, and this is your free creative studio built to handle the
digital services that enhance the overall look and feel of your celebration.
Gone are the days of using multiple disconnected tools to manage your design;
Likha brings your entire visual identity into one continuous workspace. It is an
evolving toolkit packed with premium digital features — from launching adaptive
websites, designing animated monograms, and capturing candid moments with photo
apps, to managing live feeds and streaming the experience directly to guests who
cannot make the trip. Every tool is meticulously designed to elevate the sensory
experience of your milestone, giving your event a distinct and unforgettable
presence from day one.

---

## Plano · The Ultimate Planner

Your spreadsheet doesn't know your day.

Gone are the days when you needed multiple documents to handle your planning and
scattered websites just to manage your logistics. Plano brings everything into
one single platform where all your details instantly intertwine, automatically
connecting your creative concepts and guest lists straight to your vendor
services. You no longer have to plot details manually one by one. When you adjust
a single item, your seating chart, budget tracker, checklist, and day-of
schedule all synchronize to keep the entire ecosystem accurate. It replaces
fragmented, broken workflows with a simpler, smoother, and completely reliable
dashboard, giving you a genuine reason to open the app on a Tuesday night.

---

## Suri · Setnayan AI

You are the host, not the coordinator.

Suri means to analyze closely. It is your quiet planning brain, an intelligent
layer built to rescue you from the exhausting mental load of event coordination.
Traditional tools leave you to drown in options, but Suri instantly checks every
vendor against your budget, date, and venue proximity to narrow the field down to
a few optimized choices. Working silently in the background, it reshapes your
checklist, manages your budget, and acts as a logistical safety net to watch for
hidden risks before they happen. It replaces the anxiety of guessing with the
absolute confidence of a full coordination team. Unlocks with a paid tier.

---

## Tiangge · The Marketplace

Supporting cast, there when you are ready, invisible when you are not.

Tiangge means the marketplace, and it is built entirely on your terms. We have
curated the finest vendors in the market who visually align with your specific
demands. Instead of forcing you to browse endless listings, we gather and match
them precisely to what you need, showing only the qualified options that fit your
highest expectations. Because every milestone requires a deep level of
personalization, we start by finding your perfect creative matches, letting you
immediately start a direct conversation to customize your exact demands.
Inquiring and communicating with them is entirely free, ensuring you maintain a
private, direct relationship with your vendors from day one.
