# Living Memories — Manifesto Film (script + storyboard)

> Owner-requested 2026-06-14 as one of four launch surfaces for the [[Living_Memories_Manifesto_2026-06-14]] idea. A ~55-second narrated brand film: **paper album → digital album → living memory.** Companion to the motion program ([[project_setnayan_motion_ad]]). Renders in **16:9** (site/YouTube) + **9:16** (Reels/TikTok/Stories).

## Intent

Make a viewer *feel* the manifesto in under a minute: a still wedding photo literally **comes alive** (the "Harry Potter newspaper" move), proving the thesis that a wedding was never meant to be frozen. Warm, Filipino, cinematic — slow open, uplifting assembly, quiet logo resolve.

## Production rules (locked)

- **Real SETNAYAN logo composited in post — never AI-generated** (per [[project_setnayan_motion_ad]] / [[project_setnayan_hero_video_chaos_to_order]]).
- **Owned AI music only** (Sunday-Morning / Bridgerton feel; soft piano build → warm swell at the assembly). No major-label.
- Sells **feeling, not features** — no product UI, no SKU names on screen ([[feedback_setnayan_public_surface_hygiene]]).
- "Still-comes-alive" shots = image-to-video (Higgsfield/Kling); keep people warm and real, avoid uncanny faces (hold candid moments short, ~1.5–2.5s each).

## Shot list for generation (per "alive" beat)

1. Old hands / yellowed paper album page-turn (warm, shallow DOF).
2. Phone in hand, digital gallery grid of wedding stills (cool light).
3. Static wedding still → micro-motion start (the "wake-up").
4. Lola, 60s, laughing warmly in a banquet row.
5. Friend in barong/gown wiping a tear at vows.
6. Cousin abroad on a couch at night, watching a phone, smiling.
7. Table of guests laughing at a joke.
8. Composite/transition: frames converging into a newspaper-style page (post/After Effects, not generated).
9. Couple watching a tablet; printed keepsake with QR sliding out.
10. Logo plate (composited).
