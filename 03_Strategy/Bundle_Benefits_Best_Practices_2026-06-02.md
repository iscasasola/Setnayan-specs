# Bundle Benefits — How to Make Them Convincing
## Deep-research synthesis · 2026-06-02

**Why this doc exists.** Owner reviewed the live onboarding `/proto` "Your Plan" bundle 2026-06-02 and said: *"the information about the features on the bundle does not look convincing. please do a deep search online on how we can accomplish the best way to show the benefits of the bundles."*

The current bundle ships benefit-led item rows (per the same-day 2026-06-02 lock — *"how would someone new know what these are?"*) + a savings hero (*"show total savings with plan"*). The COPY is already structurally right (research below confirms benefit-led is correct). The gap is **visual evidence, narrative grouping, and social proof** — what makes a bundle feel SHOWN, not just told.

---

## A · The 12 evidence-grounded findings

Each finding cites the strongest source from the parallel research lanes.

### 1. Benefit-led copy is necessary but not sufficient
The best-in-class consumer bundle ([Apple One](https://www.apple.com/apple-one/)) uses **service name as heading + 1–2 sentence benefit description** — NOT just the feature, NOT just an emotional line, but a concrete outcome. *"Stream or download more than 100 million songs and 30,000 playlists"* (Apple Music) reads as outcome + scale + specificity. **Our prototype already does this** — the copy is right. The gap is what surrounds it.

### 2. Visual hierarchy beats verbal content; users scan
*"Most users don't read content word-for-word online; they scan"* — [Interaction Design Foundation 2026](https://ixdf.org/literature/topics/visual-hierarchy). *"People perceive images faster than words"* — [SiteGround Academy](https://www.siteground.com/academy/visual-hierarchy/). On a long bundle of 11 items, a wall of text — even great text — disappears into "noise" because the eye gives every row the same weight.

### 3. Imagery > text for outcome-led benefits
*"Original illustration, prominent hero banners, and engaging photos play a big role in building visual hierarchy and make the copy content more digestible"* — IxDF. For weddings — a visually-driven product — every benefit benefits massively from a tiny image: a candid wedding photo for Papic · an album-art shot for Pakanta · a still of a highlight reel for SDE · a printed QR card on a table for Custom QR. **The single biggest unlock.**

### 4. Group benefits by job-to-be-done — turn a list into a story
*"Discover what jobs your prospects will hire your product/service to do"* — [Copyhackers / JTBD](https://copyhackers.com/2014/11/jobs-to-be-done-copywriting/). A flat list of 11 items reads as inventory. The same 11 items grouped into 3 JTBD arcs reads as a story:
- **Plan it →** Setnayan AI + Advanced Website
- **Capture & celebrate it →** Papic for guests + Same-Day Edit + Guest Stories + Pabati + Papic 5 seats
- **Remember & share it →** Animated Monogram + Thank-You Video + Pakanta + Custom QR

### 5. Loss aversion + anchoring need to come BEFORE the offer
*"Customers make emotional decisions about price, not logical ones... loss aversion is powerful—people fear losing more than they enjoy gaining"* — [vezadigital](https://www.vezadigital.com/post/best-saas-pricing-page-examples). **Our prototype already does this** with the "₱300,000 elsewhere" anchor + "★ YOU SAVE ₱278,001" hero (same-day lock). ✓

### 6. Social proof is the biggest under-used conversion lever
*"Video testimonials increase conversion rates by 80%... real-time social proof implementations can boost conversions by 98%"* — [Trustmary](https://trustmary.com/social-proof/social-proof-software-solutions-to-increase-conversion-rates/). Featuring numbers like *"Trusted by 10,000+ customers worldwide"* is a powerful conversion lever. **Our bundle has ZERO social proof inline.** This is the second-biggest unlock.

### 7. Place social proof at the moment of decision
*"Place customer testimonials right alongside conversion forms... companies like Yousign smartly position a customer testimonial alongside their signup form to provide an extra push at critical moments... high-intent pages are where visitors are closest to making a decision, so trust signals have the biggest impact there"* — [Trustmary](https://trustmary.com/social-proof/social-proof-software-solutions-to-increase-conversion-rates/). On the bundle card itself — not at the page top — is where the trust signal counts.

### 8. Emotional + functional benefits stack
*"Each job will likely consist of a functional aspect and an emotional one"* — [Coursera JTBD](https://www.coursera.org/articles/jobs-to-be-done). The strongest benefit lines do both. **Audit our 16:** Pakanta *"Yours, forever"* = emotional ✓ · Papic Guest *"land in your gallery, live"* = functional + immediacy ✓ · Custom QR *"opens their table, schedule, and photos with one tap"* = pure functional, **needs an emotional anchor**: *"no awkward 'which table am I at?' moment for any guest."*

### 9. ONE memorable signature outcome
Best bundle pages carry one stat that sticks: Apple One *"Save up to 31%"* · Disney+ *"4 services for the price of 1"* · Spotify Family *"6 accounts, one bill"*. For Setnayan a candidate: *"12 wedding services in one — for less than one venue."* or *"Save ₱278K and 200 hours."* **We have the savings hero but not the one-line outcome above it.**

### 10. De-risk the decision
Apple One closes with *"Any subscriptions you don't already have are free."* — addresses the objection that they might already have some. For weddings: *"Swap any item you don't use for another Setnayan service — no questions asked"* or *"30-day money-back if your wedding gets postponed."* **Our prototype has no de-risk line.**

### 11. The Goldilocks effect — label the recommended tier
*"Three-tier pricing converts at 1.4× the rate of two-tier and 1.8× the rate of four+ tiers... position the middle tier as recommended"* — [PipelineRoad](https://pipelineroad.com/agency/blog/saas-pricing-page-best-practices). Our budget-matched bundle shows ONE tier (matched to the couple's chosen budget) so no recommendation is needed at the surface level — but adding a *"Most picked for 150-guest weddings"* or *"You picked Classic — the most popular setup"* label would carry the same psychological lift.

### 12. Bundle discount range = 15–30%
*"An appealing discount for a SaaS bundle usually falls between 15% and 30% off the total price"* — [getmonetizely](https://www.getmonetizely.com/articles/saas-bundle-pricing-tests-unlocking-growth-while-avoiding-common-pitfalls). **Our 30% off is at the top of the credible range** — keep it.

## C · The 7 biggest unlocks to apply

Ranked by expected impact × effort to ship.

### 🥇 #1. Visual evidence per benefit — small photo per row
**Impact:** Massive. The single biggest gap. Per Finding #3.
**What it looks like:** Each `.bli-rich` row gains a 56×56 rounded thumbnail on the left — a real wedding photo showing the actual outcome (a candid Papic shot · a still from an SDE highlight reel · an album-art-style Pakanta cover · a printed QR table card · a guest watching Panood from abroad on a tablet · etc.). Same wedding-photo aesthetic as the pax + budget + feel screens already shipped.
**Generation:** ~16 Recraft images at ~520² · Setnayan watermark inherits from the existing CSS overlay rule.
**Effort:** ~1 hr · CSS + Recraft batch + image preload.

### 🥈 #2. Group items by job-to-be-done story arc
**Impact:** Very high. Turns a flat list into a story. Per Finding #4.
**What it looks like:** Three mono eyebrows (`Plan it · Capture & celebrate · Remember & share`) split the 11 items into 3 thematically-grouped sections. Each section gets a small italic-serif intro line in the brand voice.
**Effort:** ~30 min · new `BUNDLE_GROUPS` map + render logic in `renderMatchedBundle`.

### 🥉 #3. Inline social proof badge on the bundle name
**Impact:** High. Per Finding #6 + #7.
**What it looks like:** A small gold pill above or beside the bundle name: *"★ Most picked for ~150-guest weddings"* or *"★ 1 in 3 couples at your guest count adds this"*. For pilot — until real data — use a credible-but-conservative figure ("Often picked at your guest count" + a small "★" mark) and wire to PostHog post-launch.
**Effort:** ~20 min · CSS pill + one line in render logic.

### #4. Audit benefit lines to add an emotional anchor where missing
**Impact:** Medium-high. Per Finding #8.
**What it looks like:** Re-write 4–5 benefit lines that are purely functional today. E.g. Custom QR: from *"opens their table, schedule, and photos with one tap"* → *"opens their table, schedule, and photos with one tap — no awkward 'which table am I at?' moment for any guest."* · Papic 5 seats: from *"Five dedicated phone cameras for friends or family roaming the venue"* → *"Five trusted friends, five cameras roaming the venue — candid angles a single photographer couldn't catch."*
**Effort:** ~15 min · edit `BUNDLE_BENEFIT` map.

### #5. One-line signature outcome above the benefit list
**Impact:** Medium. Per Finding #9.
**What it looks like:** Above the 11 rows, a single italic-serif line: *"Twelve wedding services in one — for less than your reception venue."* or *"Plan it, capture it, share it — done."* (band-specific copy per the BUNDLE_TAGLINE pattern we already have.)
**Effort:** ~10 min · already have `BUNDLE_TAGLINE` map; just elevate its visual treatment.

### #6. De-risk line below the price
**Impact:** Medium. Per Finding #10.
**What it looks like:** Small calm line below the Add CTA, above the free whisper: *"Postponed wedding? We hold your bundle until your new date."* or *"Swap any item you don't use for another Setnayan service — no questions asked."*
**Effort:** ~10 min · CSS + one inserted div.

### #7. Per-item value reveal (interactive expansion)
**Impact:** Medium-low. Nice-to-have. Per Finding #5 reinforcing.
**What it looks like:** Each row carries a tiny grey amount on the right edge — *"₱35,000 elsewhere"* per row. Couples scanning see the savings is real per-line, not just in aggregate. Or — a tap-to-expand pattern reveals it on demand.
**Effort:** ~20 min · render the per-item `SVC.out` next to each row.

---

## D · Recommended priority order

If we apply ALL 7 the bundle becomes overwhelming. Recommended ship sequence:

1. **#2 group by JTBD story arc** — costs least, organizes everything. Foundational.
2. **#1 add a thumbnail per row** — the biggest visual unlock, makes the whole card feel premium.
3. **#3 social proof badge** — small, high-leverage trust signal.
4. **#4 emotional-anchor pass on copy** — polish over the now-grouped + visually-anchored set.
5. (Hold #5, #6, #7 until pilot data informs which converts best.)

The combined treatment of #1 + #2 + #3 + #4 turns the bundle from *"a list of products with prices"* into *"a styled story of YOUR wedding, with photo evidence + social proof — for ₱278K less."*
