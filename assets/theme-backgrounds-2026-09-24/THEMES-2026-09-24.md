## How the numbers were made — re-measure, do not trust

- **Palette**: `ffmpeg -i <slug>-loop.mp4 -vf fps=2,scale=108:192 -f rawvideo -pix_fmt rgb24` → k-means (k=7,
  all frames) for the dominant clusters; hue-filtered means on the 540×960 poster for small accents (wisteria,
  lanterns, neon signs, fern). Scripts: `measure.py`, `sat.py`, `hues.py`, `contrast.py` in the session scratchpad;
  results copied here.
- **Contrast**: WCAG 2.x ratio of the ink against every sampled pixel of the **text zone** (rows 29 %–83 % of the
  frame, all frames) after compositing the scrim/panel in sRGB (as CSS does). The figure reported is the
  **5th percentile** (the worst 5 % of pixels — chandelier sparkles, a sun-lit window — are ignored, and the
  single-pixel minimum is listed where it dips). Targets: body ≥ 4.5, muted ≥ 4.5, heading ≥ 3.0, button label ≥ 4.5.
  Backdrop blur is used on panels but was **not** counted (it only helps).
- **Two fails fixed** before shipping: Luxe muted text 3.88 → 4.70 (lighter parchment `#e6cdb9`); Vintage muted
  4.27 → 5.49 (darker `#5a4128`). Modern muted 4.22 → 5.37 (`#5c574f`, panel 0.86).
- Fonts are from the 112-face library in `build-sessions/prototypes/monogram-typefaces-2026-09-23.html` only.

## Shared vocabulary

- **Motion tokens**: `--ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)` · `--ease-soft cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)` · `--ease-snap
  cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`. Content presets — **Still** 0.7 s fade, stagger 0.05 s · **Calm** 1.2 s fade + 10 px
  rise, stagger 0.14 s · **Editorial** 0.75 s wipe (clip-path) + 22 px rise, stagger 0.09 s, snap ease ·
  **Cinematic** 1.9 s rise 28 px + blur 8 px → 0, stagger 0.24 s; monogram scales/rotates in. Order: eyebrow →
  monogram → names → date → venue → rule → RSVP (last). `prefers-reduced-motion` → 0.4 s fades, no transforms.
- **Transitions** (per scene, couple-editable): **Scroll** = ordinary · **Scrub** = pinned, scroll-driven,
  reversible (`animation-timeline: view()`, entry 15–75 % in, exit 25–85 % out) · **Auto** = two scenes stacked,
  hand-off on a clock, speed Slow 1.4× / Normal 1× / Fast 0.6× (one hand-off `--F` = 1.2 s). Mechanics are the
  verified ones in `scenes-three-modes-2026-09-24.html`.
- **Reveal templates** (`RevealTemplateId`): four-flap · two-flap-vertical · two-flap-horizontal · church-doors ·
  veil-sheer. Five new signature reveals are marked **new**. Every reveal is **Pro**; Classic's free default is
  **No reveal**. Couples may pair any reveal with any theme.
- **Radius**: only on pressables (buttons, chips) and the frame; panels/sheets take the theme's own radius token.
- **Print**: media themes print the loop's **first frame** (`<slug>-poster.jpg`, t = 0; paper cannot move).
  Pieces: event pass **CR80 3.375 × 2.125 in** (business card 3.5 × 2 is 4 % wider) · invitation **3 cards, 5 × 7 in**
  · welcome/QR poster **A3 297 × 420 mm** · event card **3 : 4**. Safe area 5 mm, bleed 3 mm.

## Data the build reads for the invitation set (and what is missing)

Card 1 · Invitation: monogram (`mono.svgpaths`, 4 paths, viewBox ~1024) · couple names · **ceremony time from the
ceremony block, not the first schedule item** (hub shows 1:30 PM; the owner's print says 2:30 PM) · ceremony venue
+ address · reception venue + address · cocktails/reception times. Card 2 · Entourage: guest roles grouped —
Principal Sponsors (pairs) · Best Man/Woman · Maids of Honor · Secondary Sponsors (Veil · Candle · Cord) ·
Groomsmen · Bridesmaids · Ring/Coin/Bible Bearer · Flower Girls. Card 3 · Finer Details: `dress_code_config` by role
group + palette swatches; RSVP contact; E-Gifts (own GCash/Maya/bank) → QR + masked account line.
**Missing fields today (flag):** parents' names with a **†** marker; the religious opening line ("With thanksgiving
to God and with the blessing of our parents,"). Placeholders on the board: `[RSVP contact] +63 9XX XXX XXXX`,
`[Bank] · Account name · Account number: •••• ••••` — never real numbers.

### Notes for the build

- The event card (3 : 4) on the board is the **hub hero's own markup** at card scale; keep that coupling in code
  (one component, two scales) so the two can never drift.
- The AUTO run's story scene must fit one screen (it hands off on a clock and cannot scroll) — the board uses a
  compact two-column media block there.
- Regency's video is **not** Wedgwood blue as the prompt asked; it is warm parquet + cream with wisteria as a
  detail. The palette follows the video, per the owner. If the owner wants blue/lilac dominance, the video must be
  regenerated — do not fake it in the palette.
- `luxe-loop.mp4` and `ballroom-velvet-chandeliers-loop.mp4` are the same file (same MD5). Keep one.
- Modern's "fresh green" accent (`#3a4a1c`) is the video's fern lifted for legibility; the measured fern itself is
  `#242c0b`. Flagged, not hidden.
