Setnayan · Deliverable 6 · From inquiry to locked

Ask → talk → proposal → lock — one thread, start to finish

The whole deal lives in one chat thread. Vendors don't just type — they send structured things through the ➕ menu: a proposal, an appointment, a contract amendment, a payment request. Each arrives as a card the couple can act on, and each acted-on card moves the booking forward: considering → in conversation → proposal sent → locked. Money settles off-platform (GCash, bank) and is recorded here — never held.

Rules this shell enforces
Terracotta acts, gold highlights. The ➕ and "Start an event" are the only terracotta in the chrome. The active slot is marked by a gold underline — never a filled button.
Five slots, locked. No sub-menus open out of the bar. The fifth slot appears only for people who run something; the other four never move.
Honest badges. A badge is a loaded count. Failed load → hollow dot, not "0", not nothing.
Cream on cream. Page and chrome share #FDFBF7; the bar and rail separate by a 1px #EBE5D9 border, no second background.
44×44 minimum on every tappable thing in the chrome — the slot targets are full-column, larger than their icons.
