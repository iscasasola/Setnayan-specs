Iteration 0007 · What ships

Budget & Expenses

The couple's payment ledger for every vendor at their wedding. Each vendor has three line items — Package, Crew Meal, Transportation — each with its own deadline and payment log. Vendors share their GCash, Maya, or bank QR; the couple scans to pay. Payment proofs upload to R2; calendar reminders export to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook with one tap.

The five core mechanics

01 · per vendor

Three line items

Package, Crew Meal, Transportation. Each has its own contract amount and deadline (couple-input in V1).

02 · per line item

Multiple payment records

Couples often pay in down + mid + final. Each payment captures amount, date, method, reference, and a proof screenshot.

03 · per line item

Vendor's payment QR

Vendors upload their GCash / Maya / bank QR in 0006. Tap [QR] to scan with your banking app and pay directly.

04 · top of panel

Live rollup cards

Total amount, total paid, remaining balance, next payment due — refreshes the moment a payment is recorded.

05 · one-tap export

Native calendar (.ics)

Per-line "Add to calendar" + bulk export with duplicate avoidance. Default reminders fire 24h and 1h before each due date. Per-line "On calendar" toggle skips already-tracked entries from bulk export.

06 · auto from wallet

Setnayan platform costs

Papic seats, templates, Custom Monogram Pack, Pro widgets, Panood — auto-populated from 0003 wallet, read-only.

Acceptance criteria

Every booked vendor in 0006 auto-creates a budget row with three line items
Couple inputs amount and deadline per line item independently
Multiple payment records can be recorded against any single line item
Payment validation: amount can't exceed remaining balance
Proof screenshots upload to R2 and display inline on the payment row
Vendor's QR (when uploaded in 0006) renders next to each line item
Rollup cards update in real-time on every payment record
.ics file imports successfully into Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook
Stable VEVENT UIDs — re-importing the same .ics updates the existing event, doesn't duplicate
Per-line tracked_in_calendar toggle; bulk export skips lines already flagged as tracked
Default reminders fire at 24h and 1h before deadline
Setnayan platform costs auto-populate from 0003 wallet spend records
Miscellaneous expenses can be added free-form
Audit log captures all payment record edits
