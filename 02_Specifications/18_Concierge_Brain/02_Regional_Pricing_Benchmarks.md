## NCR (Metro Manila) — venue + catering pricing

**Tags:** ncr, manila, venue, catering, pricing, regional, hotel, garden
**Applies to:** NCR couples (Metro Manila)
**Cross-ref:** 08_Budget_Allocation_Reference.md, 0016 § 4
**Source:** Public hotel rate cards · Setnayan internal vendor data (preliminary)
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

NCR venue + catering pricing varies widely by venue type and tier:

- **Mid-tier hotel ballroom** (Marriott · Holiday Inn · Manila Hotel banquet halls) — ₱3,000-5,000/head all-in (venue + catering bundled). 100-200 guests typical.
- **Premium hotel ballroom** (Peninsula · Shangri-La · Fairmont · Marco Polo) — ₱5,000-8,000/head all-in. Tier 3 territory.
- **Iconic / luxury hotel** (Sofitel · Raffles · Aman) — ₱8,000-15,000+/head. Tier 4-5.
- **Garden venue with catering** (Ten The Day · The Glass Garden · Hortaleza · 8 Spades) — ₱2,500-6,000/head depending on tier; venue rental + outside caterer model
- **Restaurant venue** (smaller intimate weddings) — ₱2,000-4,000/head; Tier 1-2

Saturday peak dates (especially in Ber months + April-May) command 15-30% premium vs Sunday or weekday weddings.

### Common follow-ups
- "Can we have a Manila wedding for under ₱500K?"
- "What's the difference between Marriott and Sofitel pricing?"
- "Are garden venues cheaper than hotels?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't quote specific venue names as recommendations — that's marketplace territory.
- Don't promise specific pricing — venues update rates seasonally.

## Tagaytay — destination garden estate pricing

**Tags:** tagaytay, destination, garden-estate, venue, pricing, climate
**Applies to:** Tagaytay-bound couples (or NCR couples considering Tagaytay)
**Cross-ref:** 03_Seasonal_Weather_Reference.md (Tagaytay micro-climate)
**Source:** Public venue rate cards · Setnayan internal vendor data
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Tagaytay is the most popular NCR-adjacent destination for garden-estate weddings (1-2 hour drive from Manila, cooler climate, scenic views). Pricing:

- **Mid-tier garden venue** (Tagaytay Highlands · Pink Sisters · Domus) — ₱2,500-4,500/head; includes basic venue + outside catering allowed
- **Premium garden estate** (Hillcreek Gardens · The Glass Garden · Antonio's) — ₱4,000-7,000/head; venue + catering bundled or pick-your-own
- **Luxury destination resort** (Twin Lakes · Discovery Country Suites) — ₱6,000-12,000/head; full-service with accommodations included for guest blocks

Add **transport cost** to your budget — most Tagaytay weddings need guest shuttles from NCR (₱30K-80K per shuttle for ~50 guests round-trip). Vendor transport fees often apply (caterers, florists, photographers charge ₱2K-10K transport).

Tagaytay's climate runs 5-8°C cooler than Manila, which is wonderful for outdoor receptions year-round — but rain risk peaks June-November. Lock indoor backup options.

### Common follow-ups
- "Is Tagaytay worth the transport cost?"
- "What's the rain backup in Tagaytay?"
- "How far in advance do garden estates book up?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific transport costs — they vary.

## Cebu — Visayas wedding pricing

**Tags:** cebu, visayas, venue, catering, pricing, regional, beach, hotel
**Applies to:** Cebu couples (or destination-Cebu couples)
**Cross-ref:** 03_Seasonal_Weather_Reference.md (Cebu's drier climate)
**Source:** Public venue rate cards · Setnayan internal vendor data
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Cebu pricing is generally 20-30% lower than NCR for equivalent tier, with strong beach + hotel + garden venue options:

- **Mid-tier Cebu hotel** (Bayfront · Quest · Cebu City Marriott) — ₱2,500-4,500/head all-in
- **Premium Cebu hotel** (Crimson Resort Mactan · Shangri-La Mactan) — ₱4,500-7,000/head
- **Beach-resort venue** (Bluewater Maribago · Plantation Bay) — ₱5,000-9,000/head; destination-wedding pricing includes resort access
- **Heritage venue** (Casino Español Cebu · Cebu Provincial Capitol grounds) — ₱2,000-4,000/head; more intimate

Cebu's distinct climate (drier than Manila for much of the year) makes year-round outdoor weddings more feasible. NCR couples often choose Cebu specifically for the beach + better weather.

Cebu's vendor pool for photo/video is smaller than NCR — peak-Saturday photographers book out 18+ months in advance. Plan accordingly.

### Common follow-ups
- "Is it cheaper to have my wedding in Cebu vs Manila?"
- "Do Cebu vendors travel to NCR?"
- "What's a typical Mactan beach wedding cost?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific vendor availability — Cebu's smaller market means tighter availability.

## Peak season vs off-peak — pricing dynamics

**Tags:** peak-season, off-peak, ber-months, summer, pricing-pressure, weekday-weddings
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 03_Seasonal_Weather_Reference.md, 04_Planning_Timelines.md
**Source:** Setnayan internal data · industry observation
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

PH wedding industry has two peak seasons:

- **Ber months (September-December)** — heaviest peak. Weather is dry, Filipino diaspora is home for the holidays, calendar season culminates with Christmas/New Year. Saturday rates in Ber months can be 20-30% above off-peak. Best venues book 14-18 months out.
- **Summer (April-May)** — secondary peak. Weather is dry but hot; outdoor receptions need climate management. School graduations free up family availability.

**Off-peak windows:**
- **June-August** — rainy season risk drops outdoor venue demand; pricing softens 10-20%
- **January-March** — post-Christmas lull; pricing softens 5-15%
- **Weekday weddings (Tuesday-Thursday)** — significant negotiation room across all seasons; 15-30% off Saturday rates

**Sunday weddings** — slightly cheaper than Saturday (5-10% typical), but still anchored to weekend pricing.

If you have flexibility, an **off-peak weekday wedding** can save ₱50K-200K on a ₱1M+ budget. The trade-off is guest attendance — weekday events sometimes have lower turnout.

### Common follow-ups
- "How much can we save with a weekday wedding?"
- "What's the cheapest month to get married?"
- "Will my guests attend a Tuesday wedding?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific percentages — they're rough industry averages.
- Don't recommend off-peak if the couple has stated guest-experience as priority.

## Photo / video tier pricing

**Tags:** photography, videography, pricing, tier, packages, regional
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 07_Vendor_Decision_Logic.md
**Source:** Public PH photo/video studio rate cards · Setnayan internal data
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Filipino wedding photography + videography pricing tiers (NCR; provincial markets typically 20-30% lower):

**Photography only:**
- **Entry tier** (1-2 person team, 6-8 hours coverage) — ₱30K-60K
- **Mid tier** (lead + assistant, full-day coverage, edited gallery + 1 album) — ₱60K-120K
- **Premium tier** (lead + multiple assistants, prenup included, multiple albums, RAW delivery) — ₱120K-250K
- **Luxury tier** (international-level photographer, multi-day coverage, custom album, video tie-in) — ₱250K-600K+

**Photography + Videography bundled:**
- **Entry tier** (single team handling both) — ₱60K-100K
- **Mid tier** (separate photo + video teams, Same-Day Edit, ceremony + reception highlights) — ₱120K-200K
- **Premium tier** (full coverage, drone, multi-camera reception, ceremony cinematic + feature film) — ₱200K-400K
- **Luxury tier** (international-level cinematography, multi-event coverage including prenup + day-of) — ₱400K-1M+

Peak-date premiums (Ber months Saturdays) add 15-25%.

### Common follow-ups
- "Is ₱100K enough for a good photographer?"
- "Why is videography so much more than photography?"
- "Do I need both photo and video?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific photographer names match these tiers — vendors price independently.
