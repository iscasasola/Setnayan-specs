## What to book FIRST in order

**Tags:** book-order, priority, dependency, decision, locked-sequence
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 1 Locked Sequence, 0016 § 0b wizard architecture
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

The order matters because each upstream decision constrains the next. Book in this sequence for the smoothest planning:

1. **Ceremony venue** (church or civil registry) — locks your date most rigidly
2. **Reception venue** — needs to be ≤8km from ceremony for guest comfort
3. **Caterer** — drives per-head budget math for everything else
4. **Photographer + videographer** — peak-date Saturdays book 12+ months out in PH
5. **Wedding coordinator** — orchestrates everything downstream; book early to maximize value
6. **Florist or stylist** — proposals depend on your mood board
7. **Band, DJ, or live music** — entertainment programming
8. **HMUA** — locks your day-of beauty timeline
9. **Bridal car** — fleet inventory is limited
10. **Bridal gown + groom suit** — custom designs need 3-6 month lead

After these are locked, operational decisions (invitations, seating, photobooth, dance choreographer, lights/sound details, attire fittings, cake design, save-the-dates) flow naturally.

### Common follow-ups
- "What if I find a photographer before a venue?"
- "Can we skip the coordinator?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't insist on rigid ordering — couples may already have specific vendors committed.

## The 8km proximity rule

**Tags:** proximity, 8km, ceremony-venue, reception-venue, travel-time
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 2
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Setnayan's default vendor search filters to ≤8km from your ceremony venue. The reasoning is practical:

- Manila weekend traffic: 8km ≈ 30-45 minutes drive
- Guest comfort: long transitions kill event momentum
- Couple efficiency: outfit changes need short transitions
- Photography: golden-hour timing depends on quick venue moves

You can override with the "Show beyond 8km" toggle — far venue cards display a `+Nkm · ~Nmin travel` badge so the trade-off is visible. The rule adapts for Tagaytay or destination ceremonies — the radius pulls in same-region venues.

### Common follow-ups
- "Can I have my ceremony and reception in different cities?"
- "What if my venue is in a small town with few caterers?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't enforce 8km rigidly for destination weddings — same-region is more practical.

## How to compare two photographer options

**Tags:** photography, decision, comparison, criteria, portfolio
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0006 vendor profile structure
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

When comparing photographers, evaluate these dimensions:

- **Hours of coverage** — typical PH packages are 8-12 hours; some include prep + reception, others only ceremony + reception
- **Deliverables** — total edited photos, RAW file delivery (yes/no), highlight album, Same-Day Edit slideshow
- **Style fit** — review their portfolio for similar weddings (Catholic Manila vs Tagaytay garden vs Cebu beach all look different); match their aesthetic to your mood board
- **Crew size** — solo, lead + assistant, or full team; bigger crew = more coverage angles
- **Prenup package included** — bundled vs separate
- **Album / printed product** — most PH packages include 1-2 printed albums

Don't pick on price alone — peak-date Saturdays warrant the strongest portfolio you can afford.

### Common follow-ups
- "How many photos should I expect?"
- "Do I need a videographer separately?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't name specific photographers — that's marketplace territory.

## How to compare two caterer options

**Tags:** catering, decision, comparison, per-head, tasting
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0006 vendor profile structure, 0007 budget § crew meals
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Catering comparison dimensions:

- **Per-head pricing** — most PH caterers quote per-pax (per-plate or per-buffet-cover)
- **What's bundled** — tableware, servers, table linens, chair covers, corkage policy, ice
- **Buffet vs plated** — buffet allows more variety, plated is more formal
- **Tasting policy** — never sign without a tasting; some caterers charge for tasting (₱500-1,500 typically credited toward final bill)
- **Crew-meal inclusion** — most caterers add crew meals at lower per-pax (₱250-500/crew); your other vendors (photo team, HMUA, coordinator, lights/sound) expect to eat
- **Menu flexibility** — Filipino caterers usually offer a "Filipino favorites" station (lechon, kare-kare, sinigang) + international option

Always confirm the **plus-one cushion** policy — Filipino weddings often have 5-10% walk-in guests beyond the RSVP'd headcount. Some caterers absorb this; others charge per overage.

### Common follow-ups
- "What's a fair per-head rate?"
- "Do I have to feed the photographer's assistant?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't recommend skipping the tasting — caterer who refuses is a red flag.

## When to hire a coordinator (on-the-day vs partial vs full)

**Tags:** coordinator, decision, criteria, tiers, otd, partial, full-planning
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 0c Pro Weekly bundle, 0016 § 0d coordinator delegation
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Three coordinator tiers in PH practice:

- **On-the-day (OTD)** — coordinator joins 4-6 weeks before the wedding, runs the actual day-of. ₱15K-30K typical. Best for couples who self-planned but want the day-of pressure handled.
- **Partial planning** — coordinator joins 4-6 months before. Helps with vendor selection, contracts, timeline. ₱40K-80K typical. Best for couples who want help but have time.
- **Full planning** — coordinator joins from engagement. Handles everything end-to-end. ₱80K-200K+ typical. Best for couples who are short on time / decision fatigue.

**Setnayan Concierge handles much of the partial-planning load** (vendor recommendations, timeline nudges, payment tracking, auto-share-packs). You still need a human OTD coordinator for the wedding day itself — Concierge doesn't replace that.

**If you book your coordinator through Setnayan and they're on Pro Weekly**, Concierge unlocks for free as a perk of their subscription. You also get coordinator delegation — your coordinator can act on action items in your dashboard, and you see attributed updates ("Anna confirmed your Caterer Y deposit").

### Common follow-ups
- "Can Setnayan Concierge replace a coordinator?"
- "Do I need an OTD if I have Concierge?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't say Concierge replaces a human coordinator — they're complementary.

## How to decide between rental and purchase (gown)

**Tags:** gown, decision, rental, purchase, custom
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0006 vendor categories
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Three paths for the bridal gown:

- **Rental** — ₱15K-50K depending on designer. Best for couples who want a designer look without the cost or storage commitment.
- **Off-the-rack purchase** — ₱30K-150K. Faster than custom; less control over fit.
- **Custom-made** — ₱80K-500K+. Longest lead time (3-6 months minimum). Maximum personalization.

Custom is the **hardest deadline** in your timeline — most Filipino-couture designers (the well-known names couples chase) book out 6-12 months in advance for peak seasons. If you want custom, lock the designer at 6-8 months out at minimum.

### Common follow-ups
- "Is rental tacky?"
- "How much does a custom gown actually cost?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't recommend specific designers.

## Vendor verification tiers — what they mean

**Tags:** verified, coming-soon, marketplace, tier, badge
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0006 § Verification
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Setnayan vendors fall into two tiers:

- **Verified** — passed the 12-document verification flow (DTI, BIR 2303, Mayor's Permit, gov ID, bank verification, portfolio review, client references, AMLC sanctions screen, live selfie + ID liveness). Verified badge visible on profile. Can receive Setnayan Pay (the 5% in-app payment rail). Eligible for Pro Weekly, Boosted Ads, Sponsored Boost.
- **Coming Soon** — registered but not yet verified. Marketplace profile + listing only. 3-stage Setnayan-managed payout (20%/60%/20%) if booked.

**For couples:** Verified is the safer choice — it's been through KYC + AMLC screening. Coming Soon vendors are still real businesses, but Setnayan hasn't completed the trust verification yet. Couples can still book Coming Soon vendors with appropriate caution (signed contract, clear deliverables).

### Common follow-ups

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't disparage Coming Soon vendors — they're still real businesses, just earlier in their Setnayan onboarding.

## "No Convenience Fee" badge — what it means

**Tags:** no-convenience-fee, badge, vendor, opt-in, pricing, setnayan-pay
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0006 § convenience-fee absorption, 0034 § 6.8
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Setnayan Pay charges a 5.0% convenience fee on top of vendor price by default — visible at checkout. Some vendors opt to **absorb that fee themselves** rather than have it added to the customer bill. Those vendors get the "No Convenience Fee" badge on their profile + search results + detail page.

The badge means: the price you see on their profile is the all-in price. No extra 5% at checkout.

Vendor revenue from Setnayan Pay is the same either way — the 5% always goes to Setnayan. What changes is whether the customer sees it as a line item.

### Common follow-ups
- "Why would a vendor absorb the fee?"
- "Should I prefer vendors with this badge?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't recommend prioritizing this badge — it's a vendor's pricing strategy, not a quality signal.

## Vendor payment milestone patterns

**Tags:** payment, milestone, reservation, downpayment, balance
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0006 § payment milestones, 0007 budget
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Typical PH wedding-vendor payment ladders:

- **Reservation + Downpayment + Balance** (most common) — ₱10K-50K reservation fee to lock the date · 30-50% downpayment 3-6 months out · balance day-of or post-wedding
- **4-partial + Balance** — for high-value bookings (venue + catering); spreads payment over 4 milestones leading up to the wedding
- **Lump Sum** — usually only for low-cost vendors (cake, save-the-date video); paid in full at booking

Setnayan's Budget tool tracks these milestones automatically and reminds you 7 days before each is due. If you're on Concierge, the wizard surfaces overdue payments in your Next Actions feed.

### Caveats / what NOT to say

## Vendor red flags (couples should walk away)

**Tags:** red-flags, vendor, walk-away, due-diligence, scam-prevention
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0019 vendor chat, 0023 disputes
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Walk-away signals (any one is enough):

- **No written contract** — verbal agreements lead to disputes
- **No portfolio examples** for the specific service you're asking about
- **Full payment upfront** demand — legitimate vendors never require 100% before delivery
- **No force-majeure clause** — what happens if a typhoon hits your venue? Real contracts address this
- **Vague deliverables** — "great photos" isn't a deliverable; "X edited shots within Y weeks" is
- **Refusing tasting / consultation** — major-vendor red flag (catering, photo, video, HMUA)
- **Pressure tactics** — "this rate is only valid today" is a high-pressure-sales tell
- **No business permit / DTI / BIR** — they may not be a registered business

If you encounter these, walk away. Setnayan's verified vendors have all passed KYC, but couples can still book off-platform — apply the same discipline.

### Common follow-ups
- "What if a vendor I love has one of these flags?"
- "How do I check if a vendor is legit?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't name specific vendors as having red flags — talk about behavior patterns only.

## Crew-meal expectations by vendor type

**Tags:** crew-meal, vendor, headcount, catering, crew-size
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0006 § crew meals + crew size, 0007 budget
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Vendors that expect crew meals at the venue:

- **Photo team** — typically 2-4 crew (lead + assistants)
- **Video team** — typically 2-4 crew (lead + drone op + 2nd camera)
- **HMUA bride + entourage** — typically 1-3 crew (artist + assistants)
- **Coordinator team** — typically 1-3 crew (lead + on-the-day team)
- **Lights & sound** — typically 1-2 crew (technician + assistant)
- **Live band / DJ** — typically 2-6 crew (full band has more)
- **Florist setup crew** — usually leaves after setup; sometimes 1-2 stay

Per-meal pricing from your caterer typically runs ₱250-500/crew (much lower than guest per-pax). Setnayan's Budget tool auto-aggregates expected crew meals from your booked vendors' `crew_size` declarations.

**Couples often undershoot.** A typical PH wedding has 15-25 crew across all vendors. Plan accordingly.

### Common follow-ups
- "Why do I have to feed the photographer's assistant?"
- "Can I just skip crew meals?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't recommend skipping crew meals — it's industry-standard and creates bad-faith conflict.

## When to lock vendors vs when to negotiate

**Tags:** negotiation, lock, timing, vendor, deposit, peak-season
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 1 Locked Sequence
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Negotiation leverage in PH wedding industry:

- **Peak-date Saturdays (Ber months, Apr-May)** — book fast, less negotiation room. Vendors have demand; couples have less leverage. Lock the date with reservation fee, negotiate on terms (delivery scope, crew size) rather than price.
- **Off-peak Tuesday-Thursday weddings** — significant negotiation room. Many vendors offer 10-20% off-peak discounts.
- **December engagement weddings** (planning during the holiday rush) — vendor availability is tightest; book fast.
- **Last-minute (< 6 months)** — couples lose most leverage; book what's available.

**General rule:** lock at 80% of your wishlist price target; budget reserves the remaining 20% for the negotiation surprises. Never lock without a written contract.

### Common follow-ups
- "Can I negotiate with a Verified vendor?"
- "What's a reasonable discount to ask for?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific discount percentages — they vary by vendor and market.

## Vendor disputes — what to do

**Tags:** dispute, vendor, escalation, force-majeure, help
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0019 vendor chat, 0023 disputes, 0029 help center
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Setnayan's dispute escalation path:

1. **Direct chat with vendor** — most issues resolve at this level (0019 chat)
2. **Help-center ticket** — if chat doesn't resolve, file a ticket (0029); admin mediates
3. **Force-majeure flow** — for typhoon/disaster cancellations; admin coordinates replacement vendor or refund per the force-majeure clause in your contract
4. **Refund processing** — admin-actioned via 0023 disputes queue if applicable

Setnayan acts as mediator when both parties paid through Setnayan Pay. For off-platform bookings, Setnayan can advise but enforcement is between the parties.
