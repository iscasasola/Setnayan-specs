## Marriage license application — the 120-day hard window

**Tags:** marriage-license, lcr, requirements, paperwork, civil, 120-day-validity
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 04_Planning_Timelines.md (marriage license timing)
**Source:** PSA · LCR procedures
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Your marriage license is valid for **120 days from the date of issue**. Apply too early and it expires before your wedding; apply too late and you risk the LCR's 10-day publication period bumping into your timeline. **Optimal application window: 4 months before your wedding.**

Required documents (PSA-issued unless noted):
- Birth certificates for both parties
- CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage Record)
- Valid government-issued ID for both parties
- Community Tax Certificate (cedula)
- Parental consent if either party is 18-21; parental advice if 22-25 (notarized)
- 4 1x1 photos each
- Marriage application form (filled at the LCR)
- Filing fee (varies by LGU, typically ₱100-500)

Apply at the LCR of either party's city or municipality of residence (your choice). After application, the LCR posts a 10-day public notice. After the notice period, the license is issued and the 120-day clock starts.

### Caveats / what NOT to say

- Don't promise specific LCR fees — they vary by city.

## CENOMAR — Certificate of No Marriage Record

**Tags:** cenomar, psa, single, civil-status, marriage-license-requirement
**Applies to:** all couples needing a marriage license
**Cross-ref:** marriage license chunk above
**Source:** PSA application procedures
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

CENOMAR confirms that you have no existing marriage record in the PSA database — required for both parties when applying for a marriage license. Request it from PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority):

- **In-person at PSA Census Serbilis Centers** — typical 1-3 business days processing
- **Online via PSA Serbilis** — typical 5-10 business days delivery
- **Through authorized partners (e.g., Robinsons Business Centers, eCensus)** — varies

**Cost:** ₱430-500 per copy as of 2026 (subject to PSA fee schedules).

**Validity:** No formal expiry, but most LCRs require the CENOMAR be issued **within the last 6 months** of marriage license application.

Request **multiple copies** at once — you may also need PSA-issued birth certificates (₱155 each) and baptismal/confirmation certificates (for Catholic weddings). Bundle the requests to save trips.

### Common follow-ups
- "How long does CENOMAR processing take?"
- "Can we apply online?"
- "What if I'm previously married?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific processing times — they vary.

## Catholic parish requirements (Pre-Cana + church docs)

**Tags:** catholic, parish, requirements, pre-cana, baptismal-certificate, confirmation, banns
**Applies to:** Catholic couples
**Cross-ref:** 04_Planning_Timelines.md (Pre-Cana timing)
**Source:** CBCP marriage handbook · diocesan procedures
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Catholic parishes typically require all of these before a church wedding:

- **Pre-Cana seminar** — 1-2 day marriage preparation course; parish needs **60-90 day notice** to schedule you in
- **Marriage Encounter Weekend** — alternative to Pre-Cana in some dioceses; longer format
- **Family Life or Couple-for-Christ seminars** — some parishes require additional seminars beyond Pre-Cana
- **Canonical interview** with the parish priest — typically 30-60 minutes; assesses readiness for marriage
- **Baptismal certificate** (PSA-issued, within 6 months) — both parties
- **Confirmation certificate** (within 6 months) — both parties
- **CENOMAR** — both parties (also needed for civil marriage license)
- **Banns of marriage** — in some dioceses, your marriage banns must be publicly posted at both parties' home parishes for 3 consecutive Sundays before the wedding
- **Sponsor list with names, baptismal records** — confirmed before invitations design

Parish requirements vary significantly diocese-to-diocese. Confirm the full list with your specific parish at 4-5 months out.

### Common follow-ups
- "Do we have to do Pre-Cana?"
- "What if we live in a different parish than where we'll get married?"
- "Can we get married outdoors as Catholics?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise the same requirements across all dioceses — they vary.
- Don't recommend skipping Pre-Cana — Catholic couples typically can't get married without it.

## Prenuptial agreement basics

**Tags:** prenup, prenuptial-agreement, marriage-settlement, conjugal-property
**Applies to:** all couples considering prenups
**Cross-ref:** Philippine Civil Code Articles 74-82
**Source:** Philippine Civil Code (Marriage Settlement provisions)
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

In the Philippines, the default marriage property regime is **Absolute Community of Property** — all property acquired during marriage is jointly owned. A **prenuptial agreement** (called a Marriage Settlement) lets couples opt into a different regime:

- **Conjugal Partnership of Gains** — each party retains pre-marriage property; gains during marriage are shared
- **Complete Separation of Property** — each party retains all property separately
- **Custom regime** — couples can structure their own (within Civil Code limits)

Marriage Settlements must be:
- Executed before the wedding (cannot be created post-marriage)
- Notarized
- Registered with the Local Civil Registry where the marriage license was issued

Most Filipino couples don't sign prenups (cultural norm + most couples don't have significant pre-marriage assets). High-net-worth couples, second marriages, or couples with substantial business assets often do.

Consult a family law attorney if you're considering a prenup — generic templates don't cover individual circumstances.

### Common follow-ups
- "Do we need a prenup?"
- "How much does a prenup cost?"
- "Can we add a prenup after we're married?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't recommend specific attorneys — that's a personal choice.
- Don't promise specific prenup terms work for all couples — consult a lawyer.

## RA 10173 — Data Privacy Act and guest information

**Tags:** privacy, ra-10173, data-privacy-act, guest-data, gdpr-equivalent
**Applies to:** all couples managing guest lists
**Cross-ref:** 0001 guest list, 0002 QR invitations
**Source:** Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Under Philippine RA 10173, you (the couple) become a "personal information controller" when you collect guests' contact info, dietary restrictions, and other personal data. Key obligations:

- **Purpose limitation** — only collect what you need for the wedding (contact for RSVP, dietary for catering, etc.); don't use for unrelated purposes
- **Storage discipline** — don't share guest data with third parties beyond vendors who need it (caterer needs dietary, photographer doesn't)
- **Deletion** — after the wedding, retain data only as long as needed for follow-up (thank-you cards, vendor reviews)
