## The 5 working-budget tiers

**Tags:** budget, tiers, per-head, working-budget, simple, charming, grand, distinguished, luxurious
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 4
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Setnayan organizes weddings into 5 per-head budget tiers. Pick the tier that matches the *feel* you want; the wizard computes your working total from tier × guest count.

| Tier | Name | Per-head spend | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Simple and Intimate** | ₱1,500–3,000 | Restaurant or modest hotel · immediate family + closest friends · minimal extras |
| 2 | **Charming and Personal** | ₱3,000–5,000 | Mid-tier hotel or curated garden · wedding party + extended family · thoughtful styling |
| 3 | **Grand and Beautiful** | ₱5,000–8,000 | Premium hotel or full-service garden estate · full design layer · SDE + cocktail extras |
| 4 | **Distinguished and Refined** | ₱8,000–13,000 | Top-tier hotel or destination estate · stylist-driven design · premium photo/video · couture-adjacent gown |
| 5 | **Luxurious and Beyond** | ₱13,000+ | Iconic hotel grand ballroom or premium destination · couture gown · full-event stylist team · designer everything |

Most Filipino couples land in Tier 2-3. Tier 1 fits intimate destination or restaurant weddings (50-80 guests). Tier 4-5 is destination-luxury territory.

### Common follow-ups
- "How do I know which tier we should pick?"
- "What if our budget falls between tiers?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't tell couples they "need" a higher tier — the tiers describe outcomes, not aspirations.

## Estimating your total budget from guest count + tier

**Tags:** budget, estimate, guest-count, total, working-budget
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 4 working-budget table
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Working budget formula:

```
working_budget = guest_count × tier_per_head_rate
```

Example tables:

| Guests | Tier 1 (₱1.5-3K/head) | Tier 2 (₱3-5K) | Tier 3 (₱5-8K) | Tier 4 (₱8-13K) | Tier 5 (₱13K+) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | ₱75K – ₱150K | ₱150K – ₱250K | ₱250K – ₱400K | ₱400K – ₱650K | ₱650K+ |
| 100 | ₱150K – ₱300K | ₱300K – ₱500K | ₱500K – ₱800K | ₱800K – ₱1.3M | ₱1.3M+ |
| 150 | ₱225K – ₱450K | ₱450K – ₱750K | ₱750K – ₱1.2M | ₱1.2M – ₱1.95M | ₱1.95M+ |
| 200 | ₱300K – ₱600K | ₱600K – ₱1M | ₱1M – ₱1.6M | ₱1.6M – ₱2.6M | ₱2.6M+ |
| 250 | ₱375K – ₱750K | ₱750K – ₱1.25M | ₱1.25M – ₱2M | ₱2M – ₱3.25M | ₱3.25M+ |
| 300 | ₱450K – ₱900K | ₱900K – ₱1.5M | ₱1.5M – ₱2.4M | ₱2.4M – ₱3.9M | ₱3.9M+ |

What's INCLUDED in the working budget: most wedding-day vendor categories (venue · catering · photo/video · florals · stylist · HMUA · coordinator · invitations · entertainment · attire · cake · transport).

What's EXTRA (budget separately): engagement ring · wedding rings · prenup shoot (some couples bundle, some don't) · honeymoon · post-wedding celebrations · vendor team gifts · guest-block hotels for destination weddings.

### Common follow-ups
- "How accurate is this formula?"
- "What if our actual spend is way over?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific accuracy — the working budget is a planning estimate, not a contract.

## What's not in the working budget

**Tags:** budget, not-included, excluded, extras, rings, honeymoon
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 4
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

The working budget covers wedding-day operations. Budget separately for:

- **Engagement ring** — varies wildly (₱20K-1M+)
- **Wedding rings (couple's set)** — ₱20K-150K typical
- **Prenup shoot** — ₱30K-100K if separate from photographer; usually bundled
- **Honeymoon** — ₱50K (domestic) to ₱500K+ (international)
- **Pre-wedding celebrations** — bridal shower, despedida de soltera, bachelor/ette party
- **Post-wedding celebrations** — thank-you dinner for sponsors, vendor team gifts
- **Vendor team gifts / tips** — ₱500-2,000 per crew member for direct day-of crew
- **Guest-block hotels** for destination weddings — varies
- **Marriage license fees + PSA documents** — ₱500-2,000 total
- **Wedding registry / gift fulfillment** — couple-led, not vendor cost

Setnayan's Budget tool lets you add these as separate line items so you don't get blindsided.

### Common follow-ups
- "Should we save up for the honeymoon separately?"
- "What's a reasonable tip for the photographer?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't quote specific tip amounts — they're personal and vary.

## Tier 1 — Simple & Intimate (₱1,500-3,000/head) allocation

**Tags:** tier-1, simple, intimate, allocation, low-budget
**Applies to:** Tier 1 couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 5 Tier 1
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Tier 1 weddings are intimate (typically 50-80 guests) at restaurants or modest hotels. Allocation pattern (using 50 guests × ₱2,500/head = ₱125K working budget as example):

| Category | % | ₱ allocation |
|---|---|---|
| Reception venue + food | 40-50% | ₱50K – ₱62K |
| Photography (4-6h, basic) | 12-15% | ₱15K – ₱19K |
| Florals + simple lights | 7-9% | ₱9K – ₱11K |
| Attire (off-the-rack) | 8-10% | ₱10K – ₱12K |
| Coordination (OTD only) | 6-8% | ₱7K – ₱10K |
| HMUA (bride only or shared) | 3-5% | ₱4K – ₱6K |
| Bridal car | 2-3% | ₱2K – ₱4K |
| Music / DJ | 2-3% | ₱2K – ₱4K |
| Invitations | 1-2% | ₱1K – ₱2K |
| Cake | 1-2% | ₱1K – ₱2K |
| Buffer | 6-8% | ₱8K – ₱10K |

**What to cut first if over budget:** prenup, dance choreographer, photobooth, fancy invitations.

### Common follow-ups
- "Can I do a wedding for less than ₱100K?"
- "What if we want florals on Tier 1?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't dismiss Tier 1 weddings — many are beautiful and intentional.

**Applies to:** Tier 4 couples
**Cross-ref:** 0016 § 5 Tier 4

## When to upgrade or downgrade your tier

**Tags:** tier, upgrade, downgrade, decision, budget-realism, honesty
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0007 budget tool
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Signals that you should **downgrade your tier**:

- Your initial vendor quotes consistently come in 30%+ above your per-head allocation
- You're sacrificing on multiple categories (cutting flowers, downgrading photo, switching to plated) just to fit
- Your savings + parent contributions can't reach the working budget for your chosen tier × guest count
- You're feeling stressed about the financial reach

Signals that you should **upgrade your tier**:

- You have headroom and your initial quotes leave 20%+ unallocated
- You want a specific vendor (designer gown, top-tier photographer) that doesn't fit your current tier
- Your guest count is smaller than typical for your tier (50 guests at Tier 3 means each head gets more spend)

The Concierge wizard surfaces gentle prompts when your booked-vendor pattern doesn't match your declared tier — *"Your current vendor mix suggests Tier 3 spending, but you selected Tier 2. Want to update?"*

### Common follow-ups
- "How do I tell my partner we should downgrade?"
- "What if our parents are contributing — does that change the tier?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't shame couples for either direction — financial realities are personal.

## Where couples typically overspend

**Tags:** overspend, budget, common-mistakes
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0007 budget alerts
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Common overspend categories:

- **Florals** — initial estimates often hit 1.5-2× actual final spend. Designers add installation pieces, larger centerpieces, more ceremony arrangements. Get itemized quotes; specify maximum per category.
- **HMUA entourage expansion** — couples start with bride + 3 entourage HMUA, end with bride + 8 entourage; per-artist costs add up
- **Videography upgrades** — Same-Day-Edit add-on creep, drone, additional cameras
- **Guest count creep post-RSVP** — plus-ones not initially counted, late RSVPs
- **Cocktail booth proliferation** — couples add 2-3 booths thinking it adds delight; budget impact is significant
- **Save-the-date + Invitation upgrades** — Monogram Hero + Live Schedule add to the base
- **Dress alterations** — bridal gown alterations can be ₱5K-30K depending on complexity

Setnayan's Budget tool alerts you at 80% and 100% of each category's allocation so you catch creep early.

### Common follow-ups
- "How do I keep florals on budget?"
- "What's a fair cocktail booth budget?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't recommend rigid cost-cutting — couples regret pinching on memories.

## Where couples typically underspend (and shouldn't)

**Tags:** underspend, budget, mistakes
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 0007 budget
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Categories where couples save money but regret it:

- **Coordination** — DIY coordinating your own wedding day means YOU are running logistics instead of being present. Hire OTD at minimum.
- **Catering buffer for plus-ones** — Filipino weddings always have 5-10% walk-ins; under-quoting headcount means running out of food
- **Guest transport in destination weddings** — if you're not in NCR, guests need shuttles from major arrival points
- **Vendor contingency for force-majeure** — typhoons cancel weddings. Read your contracts; budget for an emergency relocation fee just in case
- **Photo / video coverage hours** — under-shooting means missing key moments. Pay for adequate hours.
- **Crew meals** — see vendor decision logic chunk; under-counting causes vendor friction

### Common follow-ups
- "Do I really need an OTD coordinator?"
- "How much do force-majeure relocations cost?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't insist on specific upgrades — fit the couple's actual constraints.

## 5.0% Setnayan Pay fee — where it shows up in budget

**Tags:** setnayan-pay, fee, budget, 5-percent, no-convenience-fee
**Applies to:** couples paying vendors through Setnayan Pay
**Cross-ref:** 0034 § 6, 0006 § convenience-fee absorption
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

When you pay a Setnayan-verified vendor via Setnayan Pay (in-app GCash/BDO/credit card rail), Setnayan adds a 5.0% convenience fee on top of the vendor's listed price.

Example: vendor quotes ₱100,000 for photography → Setnayan Pay cart shows ₱100,000 + ₱5,000 convenience fee = ₱105,000 total.

**Exception: "No Convenience Fee" badge vendors** absorb the fee themselves. Their listed price is the all-in price; no extra 5% at checkout.

Factor the 5% into your working budget if you plan to pay most vendors through Setnayan Pay. Setnayan's Budget tool can auto-add the 5% to vendor line items when they're paid through SP.

- "Why is there a 5% fee?"
- "Should I pay outside Setnayan to avoid the fee?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't recommend off-platform payment to avoid the fee — couples lose Setnayan's dispute mediation + Concierge auto-unlock via Pro Weekly bundle.
