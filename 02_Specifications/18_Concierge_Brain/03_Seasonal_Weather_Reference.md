## Philippine dry vs wet season (Luzon)

**Tags:** weather, season, dry, wet, rainy, luzon, pagasa
**Applies to:** Luzon couples (Manila / Tagaytay / Batangas / etc.)
**Cross-ref:** 02_Regional_Pricing_Benchmarks.md
**Source:** PAGASA climate classification
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

PAGASA classifies the Philippine climate into four types based on rainfall distribution. For most of Luzon (including NCR and Tagaytay), the relevant pattern is **Type I — pronounced dry season (November-April) and pronounced wet season (May-October)**.

For wedding planning:
- **November-April (dry)** — best weather for outdoor receptions; clear skies, manageable heat, golden-hour photography is reliable
- **May (transitional)** — variable; afternoon thunderstorms possible
- **June-October (wet)** — afternoon rain showers common, occasional all-day rain, typhoon risk elevated. Outdoor weddings need solid indoor backup or tented venues with sidewalls.

January-February typically have the most stable weather in Luzon — clear skies, cooler temperatures, low rain probability. December is dry but warmer; March-April climb into summer heat.

**Air quality:** December-February has the lowest PM2.5 in NCR (winter monsoon brings cleaner air); April-May has the highest (pre-monsoon haze + dust). If outdoor photography matters, January-February gives the cleanest backdrops.

### Common follow-ups
- "When's the best month for an outdoor wedding?"
- "Can we have a June wedding outdoors?"
- "What's the difference between Luzon and Visayas weather?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise rain-free weather — even dry season has occasional showers.
- Don't recommend skipping indoor backup for outdoor wet-season weddings.

## Cebu, Visayas, Mindanao climate differences

**Tags:** weather, regional, cebu, visayas, mindanao, davao, climate
**Applies to:** Visayas + Mindanao couples
**Cross-ref:** 02_Regional_Pricing_Benchmarks.md
**Source:** PAGASA regional classifications
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Different PH regions have distinct climate patterns:

- **Cebu (Type III climate)** — drier than Manila for much of the year; less pronounced wet/dry contrast. Beach + garden weddings are feasible nearly year-round; September-November is the wettest period but rarely catastrophic. **This is one reason couples choose Cebu for destination weddings.**
- **Davao + most of Mindanao (Type IV climate)** — relatively uniform rainfall throughout the year, lower typhoon risk than Luzon. Less seasonal weather pressure on weddings.
- **Boracay (Type III)** — strong wind season November-March (Amihan), calmer June-September (Habagat). Beach weddings should plan around wind direction.

**Typhoon risk by region:**
- **Northern Luzon (Aurora, Cagayan, Isabela)** — highest typhoon frequency; June-November peak
- **Eastern Visayas** — moderate-high frequency; September-December peak
- **Mindanao** — lowest typhoon risk; rare landfalls

If you're flexible on location, Mindanao + Cebu both offer significantly lower weather disruption risk than Luzon.

- "Is Davao a good wedding destination?"

## Typhoon season risk + force-majeure planning

**Tags:** typhoon, bagyo, risk, weather, force-majeure, contingency
**Applies to:** all couples (especially Luzon June-November weddings)
**Cross-ref:** 0019 vendor chat § force-majeure flow
**Source:** PAGASA historical typhoon data
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

PH typhoon season peaks **June-November**, with September-October being statistically the most active months. Notable landfall regions are Northern Luzon (Aurora, Cagayan), Bicol, Eastern Visayas (Samar, Leyte). NCR + Tagaytay sometimes feel typhoon effects (heavy rain, wind) without direct landfall.

**Contingency planning for at-risk weddings:**
- **Indoor backup venue** — confirm your venue has a covered alternative if storm closes outdoor areas
- **Tented venue with sidewalls** — better than open tents; can withstand moderate wind/rain
- **Vendor force-majeure clauses** — read every contract. Real vendors include force-majeure language (typhoon, government lockdown, family emergency); contracts without it are a red flag
- **Setnayan force-majeure flow** — if a typhoon strikes within 14 days of your wedding, Setnayan's 0019 force-majeure escalation triggers admin assistance with vendor coordination + possible date relocation

**Insurance:** wedding insurance is rare in PH but available for high-budget weddings. Discuss with a broker if your budget is > ₱2M.

### Common follow-ups
- "What if a typhoon hits the week of our wedding?"
- "Should we get wedding insurance?"
- "Will vendors refund if we have to postpone?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't promise specific refund outcomes — they depend on individual vendor contracts.
- Don't dismiss typhoon risk for couples planning June-November weddings.

## Best outdoor months per region

**Tags:** outdoor, weather, best-months, dry-season, photography
**Applies to:** couples planning outdoor or semi-outdoor receptions
**Cross-ref:** 02_Regional_Pricing_Benchmarks.md
**Source:** PAGASA · industry observation
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

If outdoor weather is critical:

- **NCR + Tagaytay** — **January-March** is optimal (dry + cooler + lowest rain risk + clean air). December is dry but warmer. April-May is dry but hottest (35°C+ afternoon heat in NCR).
- **Cebu / Mactan / Bohol** — **March-May** is driest. October-December has occasional storms.
- **Davao / Mindanao** — relatively flexible year-round. **December-February** has the steadiest weather.
- **Boracay / Palawan** — **December-April** (Amihan calmer waters, sunny but coolish). Avoid August-September (Habagat rough waters).
- **Baguio / mountain regions** — **November-February** is the cool dry season; December nights drop to 12-14°C. Cooler weather and pine-tree photography backdrops attract destination weddings.

**Combined factors:** if your top priority is golden-hour outdoor photography on a manageable budget, **January-February in NCR/Tagaytay** is the sweet spot — dry weather + lowest peak pricing (post-Ber months lull) + clean air.

- "Should we get married in Baguio for the weather?"

## Peak-pricing vs weather tradeoff

**Tags:** pricing-tradeoff, peak-season, ber-months, weather, decision
**Applies to:** all couples
**Cross-ref:** 02_Regional_Pricing_Benchmarks.md, 04_Planning_Timelines.md
**Source:** Setnayan internal data
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

The peak months (Ber + April-May) have the **best weather** for outdoor weddings in Luzon — but they also have the **highest pricing** (Saturday rates +20-30% vs off-peak) and the **tightest vendor availability** (best vendors fully booked 12-18+ months out).

The trade-off:
- **Peak season, peak weather, peak prices** — December-February: clear skies + ₱3K-5K/head premium + 14-18mo vendor lead time
- **Off-peak season, decent weather, much lower prices** — June-August: rain risk + 10-20% lower prices + 8-12mo vendor lead time
- **Shoulder season, mixed weather, mild pricing** — March-May or September-November: variable weather + standard pricing + 10-12mo lead time

**Setnayan's view:** if you have flexibility, **January-March or November in NCR** gives 80% of the weather benefit at ~50% of the peak-pricing premium. Best couples-on-budget combination.

### Common follow-ups
- "Should we pay the peak premium for December?"
- "Is the price difference really worth it?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't override the couple's date preference — they may have personal reasons.

## Holy Week, Lent, and All Souls' Day considerations

**Tags:** holy-week, lent, all-souls, religious-calendar, scheduling
**Applies to:** Catholic + Christian couples
**Cross-ref:** 01_Filipino_Cultural_Reference.md
**Source:** CBCP liturgical calendar
**Last verified:** 2026-05-18 · Setnayan team

Catholic Filipino tradition affects certain calendar windows:

- **Holy Week (Maundy Thursday-Easter Sunday, varies by year)** — Catholic weddings are **traditionally not held** during this week (Tridiuum requires the Church's full liturgical attention). Some parishes will officiate; many will not. Confirm with your parish.
- **Lent (Ash Wednesday to Holy Saturday, ~40 days before Easter)** — Catholic weddings are permitted but the liturgy is more reserved; some couples postpone celebratory elements. Many couples avoid Lent for cultural reasons.
- **All Souls' Day (November 1-2)** — extended-family obligations (cemetery visits) make this weekend less attended. Many couples avoid the first weekend of November.
- **Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve** — weddings are scheduled but compete with family obligations; lower attendance is common.
- **Independence Day (June 12), Bonifacio Day (Nov 30), Rizal Day (Dec 30)** — long-weekend dates favored for destination weddings but premium-priced.

### Common follow-ups
- "Can we get married during Lent?"
- "Is Holy Week a good wedding week?"

### Caveats / what NOT to say
- Don't dismiss religious calendar concerns — they matter to Catholic Filipino families.
