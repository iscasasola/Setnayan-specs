# Template Catalog V1 — Locked Picks

> **Source of truth** for which templates ship in Setnayan V1. Each entry locks the template's aesthetic system (palette, typography, motion, music, storyline pacing) and SKU compatibility. Engineering reads this when building Remotion components.

**Status:** under active curation — picks locked as the owner approves each candidate batch.
**Pattern locked 2026-05-12:** ~10 aesthetic categories total. Free tier holds 3–5 flagship templates per category; Pro tier holds variations + bolder/niche options. Quality > breadth. All templates flagship-grade per the no-filler rule.
**Cost reality:** Per-render cost to Setnayan is ~₱2–5 for templated renders (FFmpeg + Lottie + R2). Free-vs-pro is NOT a cost-driven split — it's a design-effort and upgrade-signal split. Pro templates require more complex motion graphics, deeper Lottie work, niche music pairings, or exclusive feel-categories. Free templates are the catalog's universally-loved backbone.
**SKU compatibility legend:** STD = Save-the-Date · PR = Personal Reel · AIVH = AI Video Highlight 60s · AIEH = AI Edited Highlight 3-min · SDE = Same-Day Edit · LED = LED Background loop. *Patiktok is a booth/station SKU at ₱2,499/booth (iteration 0017), NOT a template-pick-driven render — it uses auto-generated branding from the couple's mood board palette + monogram, not the 30 aesthetic templates here.*

## Heritage Filipiniana — locked 2026-05-12

### `HF-01 · Capiz Garden` · Free

**Aesthetic.** Traditional Filipino warmth meets editorial restraint. Maria Clara silhouettes seen through capiz-shell window patterns. The kind of wedding video your *lola* would frame and hang in the sala.

**Palette.** Cream `#FAF5EE` · capiz pearl `#F0EAD8` · malachite green `#5A7560` · aged gold `#B8945A` · deep ink `#1A2346`

**Typography.** Cormorant Garamond italic display + Manrope body. Subtle Baybayin script as decoration on opening title.

**Motion.** Slow 12% zoom on stills (Ken Burns), capiz-pattern overlay drifts left at 4% opacity, name reveal types letter-by-letter, scene transitions cross-dissolve through capiz-pattern wipes.

**Music pairing.** Acoustic kundiman · strings only · no vocals · Suno-owned catalog track.

**LUT.** Warm filmic — lifted shadows, soft highlights, +5 saturation, slight green-magenta cast for the cream-and-malachite palette.

2. **8–22s · Prep.** Bride in saya, groom in barong, parents — soft golden-hour light, Ken Burns zoom.
3. **22–38s · Climax.** Vows, ring exchange, kiss. Music swells on the kiss.
4. **38–55s · Celebration.** First dance, parent moments, candid laughter, family circle shots.
5. **55–60s · Outro.** Sparkler exit, monogram fades back in, sign-off text: "Salamat sa pag-ibig ninyo."

**SKU compatibility.** STD ✓ · PR ✓ · AIVH ✓ · AIEH ✓ · SDE ✓ · LED ✓ (adapt capiz-pattern as ambient loop)

**Best for.** Traditional Filipino-Catholic weddings · capiz/wood/heritage venues · family-centric celebrations.

## How this catalog gets consumed

When engineering builds the Remotion video-render pipeline, each entry above becomes:

1. A **Remotion JSX component** at `src/templates/{category}/{template_id}.tsx` parameterized by `(event_id, photo_set, music_track_id, output_format)`.
2. A **`.cube` LUT file** at `assets/luts/{template_id}.cube` for the color grade.
3. A **Lottie JSON manifest** at `assets/lottie/{template_id}/` for motion-graphics overlays.
4. A **music track reference** to the owned Suno catalog (per `14_Music_Catalogue_Cowork_Playbook.md`).
5. A **template manifest entry** at `template_library/{category}/{template_id}.json` describing the storyline pacing per event-type × SKU format.

A single template entry powers all compatible SKUs from one definition — Capiz Garden's render of a Save-the-Date and Capiz Garden's render of an SDE both pull from the same manifest, just with different durations and music-edit points. This is what keeps the catalog manageable as new categories ship.
