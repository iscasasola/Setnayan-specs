## 3. The fused verdict — our best strategy

**Name it plainly: the anchor graph is the moat; the Membership is the annuity; vendors are the engine.** Every verified finding slots into the model we already chose — the research changes *emphasis and sequencing*, not direction:

1. **Ship the free reminder loop FIRST (PR-A + PR-F).** F0+F1 say anchor-date triggers are the best-documented re-engagement mechanism in CRM, and we hold the rare position of needing zero prediction. The Year view + first-anniversary card is simultaneously: retention surface, Membership home, and (per F5) the renewal engine. It is the highest-leverage build on the board.
2. **Reshape billing to value-led annual renewal — never bet on silent auto-renew (F5).** Annual prepaid + a renewal reminder that lands ATTACHED to a milestone moment ("your Membership covers Amara's 7th in March") — the renewal ask arrives exactly when the value is visible. The GCash one-tap-unlink reality makes "re-earn yearly" a design constraint, not a nice-to-have.
3. **Expand by demand-pull, not by roadmap (F3 — Zola's lesson).** We enabled 14 types; do NOT deep-invest per type on speculation. Instrument: anchor adds, go-signal taps, type repurposing, dependent creation. When couples pull (as Zola's did with 1,800 repurposed registries), the next investment picks itself. This gives the owner's don't-over-invest lock its operating mechanism.
4. **Keep matching + reminders free forever (F6 + Rule 1).** Eventchy made free AI matching table stakes. The paid line = what only we have: the lifecycle graph, Kasangga protection/concierge, Alaala memory, vendor depth.
5. **Never starve the vendor engine for the Membership bet (F7).** Vendor-side is the PROVEN revenue model at leader scale; the couple Membership is our novel experiment. Protect vendor trust economics (hold-and-release, verification) as the load-bearing revenue; let the Membership prove itself through instrumented conversion (PR-A already logs suggested-event → Membership).
6. **The Knot's arc (F2) is our long map** — wedding wedge → adjacent life stages, organic-first. Our anchor model does organically (one graph, derived milestones) what The Knot needed brands + acquisitions to do. Debut = our "Bump" (second hero, already locked).

**Sequencing consequence:** PR-A → PR-F rise to the top of the whole build queue (ahead of most previously-queued work); the annual-billing build follows them; PR-D waits on counsel as planned. The open research question (post-event churn anti-patterns) re-runs later.
