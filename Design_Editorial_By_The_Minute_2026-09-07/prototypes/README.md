# Prototypes

Open either file in a browser. They are the design, not illustrations of it — every control works.

## `story.html` — the public story
Live links: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0f47b375-7c77-4719-9df8-b2c4b07d4e34

Walk it in this order:
1. **Scroll slowly** — the light moves from morning to night, and the dial's needle follows.
2. **Tap any bar on the dial**, including a quiet one between moments.
3. **The occasion** (cover) — Wedding → Wake. Watch what disappears.
4. **Viewing as** — *a stranger* + *on the day, 3:10 PM*: the guests' layer goes.
5. **Colours from the mood board** — Jack & Jill's, Sofia's: the same story, their palette.
6. **⌕ Find** in the dial — try `7:12`, then `Bing`.
7. **Host view** — the approval switch under every entry.
8. The **back cover**, after the colophon.

## `story-maker.html` — the host's desk
Live link: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/25b99803-038b-416f-a53c-182be00e84bb

1. **The desk** — accept, edit and reject across all three sources; watch the counters.
2. **Theme → Make my own** — tap a swatch for the mood board's own picker.
3. **Cover** — pick one; three previews change together.
4. **What's next** — opens with nothing chosen, and the back cover quiet.

## What is placeholder
Photos and clips are colour plates (the real files are on R2 and neither page can reach them).
The extra voices, the letters, the seating names and the per-table counts are written for the
Maria & Juan sample. Everything else is the shipped fixture, verbatim.

The colour names in the maker's picker are a 60-entry stand-in for `lib/color-names` +
`lib/color-search`.
