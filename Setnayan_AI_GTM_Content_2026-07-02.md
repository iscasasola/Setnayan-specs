## 1. Positioning

**Primary angle: "Not a chatbot — a monitoring engine," warmed by relief.**

Setnayan AI is a *different category* from every other "wedding AI." They are generative **chatbots you go to** (ask a question, get an answer, one-time, they forget). Setnayan AI is a **monitoring engine that comes to you** — it watches your shortlisted and booked vendors and the live marketplace, and taps you only when something needs you. Like **Hopper for flights** or **Zillow for homes**, finally built for a wedding.

Why this leads: it's the only angle that (a) names a category Setnayan owns and (b) a cold visitor decodes in ~3 seconds. It's warmed by the **relief** promise (the real thing couples buy — the 2 a.m. "did I forget something?" mental load lifting), and justified deep-scroll by the **impossibility math** (matching it by hand takes a team you'd never hire).

**The model, stated everywhere the paywall appears:** every planning tool stays **free**; Setnayan AI is the **paid brain on top** — never a tollgate on the basics.

## 3. Narrative arc (5 beats — category-first, warmed by relief, math held for the skeptic)

1. **Hook — the category flip.** "It doesn't chat. It watches." Name the difference before any feature; anchor with Hopper/Zillow.
2. **Relief — why you want that.** Translate "watches" into the felt benefit: the 2 a.m. dread goes away because the list runs in the background, not through your head. *"Plan less, feel engaged more."*
3. **What it watches — shipped-only proof.** Three concrete jobs, live today: **does the legwork** (finds/ranks vendors, one-tap outreach, chases quiet ones, lines up quotes), **stands guard** (deposit due, price hike on an unlocked vendor, double-booking, quiet/unverified vendor, and — weddings only — the license/Pre-Cana/PSA clock), **reassures with evidence** ("great pick — 47 reviews, 4.8 stars"). No Inference/Trend anywhere.
4. **Restraint — trust by design.** Quiet most weeks, loud exactly when it counts: one calm weekly digest, an interrupt only when it truly can't wait, every reason shown plainly, no fake countdowns.
5. **Justify + model — math as reassurance, not the lead.** Doing this by hand is a huge ongoing job; matching it live would take a small team you'd never hire (*illustrative PH estimate*). Land the model: planning is free, this is the paid brain — ₱499 first 28 days, then ₱799/28 days, all your events, 0% vendor commission. CTA.

## 5. Honesty guardrails (CRITICAL — every writer/designer obeys these)

1. **NEVER present Inference ("I noticed you keep viewing garden venues…") or Trend ("68% of couples like you…") as live.** Dormant pending DPO sign-off. Only on /setnayan-ai, only in a future-tense "coming" block. **All cohort / "couples like you" / "fair-price benchmark" language is BANNED from live-feature copy until sign-off.**
2. **Live story is SHIPPED-ONLY:** Secretary (legwork) · Guard (risk/deadline watching) · Commend (evidence reassurance) · vendor matchmaking. No "learns your taste" line anywhere.
3. **Competitor claims stay DEFENSIBLE** — "we looked and couldn't find another…", "appears alone." NEVER an absolute "the only one" / "no one else has this." Do not tighten a hedged line into a superlative.
4. **Cost/labor anchors** (~₱50–100k/mo team, ~12-hour pass, ~150–300 hrs) are **ILLUSTRATIVE PH ESTIMATES — labeled every time.** /setnayan-ai only, never a hero or ad. If unsure, soften to "more than most couples could ever hire."
5. **The price (₱499 first → ₱799/28d) MUST be verified against the live catalog at publish** — pricing has reset repeatedly (public surfaces have shown ₱3,999). No price line without confirming the current figure.
6. **NEVER name the underlying model/tech.** It's "Setnayan AI" only — not an LLM, not a rule engine, no vendor name. Sell benefits, not implementation.
7. **"24/7 / never sleeps"** describes the background **monitoring** only (real). Do NOT stretch it into instant push for every event — today's cadence is a weekly digest + time-critical interrupts (real-time push is V1.1). Never imply a human is watching, and never imply it acts (sends inquiries, decides) without the couple initiating/approving.
8. **Hopper/Zillow analogy:** Setnayan monitors a **curated marketplace, not the open web.** Keep "your actual vendors, not the whole internet"; never imply we track prices everywhere.
9. **NO fake urgency / countdowns / scarcity / FOMO** on any surface. Restraint is a feature — copy it faithfully. If a number or alert isn't real, it isn't shown.
10. **Voice:** warm, premium, plain "smartest-friend"; sentence case; no eyebrow-kicker clichés. Keep the free-floor line wherever the paywall is mentioned; keep "0% vendor commission" wherever recommendations are claimed.
