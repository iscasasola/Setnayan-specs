## 0 · THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION

Setnayan (SET-na-yan, from *"Set na 'yan"* — "that's all set") is a Philippines-first life-events
platform: one place for a Filipino household to **plan a celebration, capture it, and keep it for
life**. It is built and operated entirely in the Philippines for Filipino celebrations, not a
foreign platform with localisation added. Seventeen kinds of celebration are live. One application,
three doorways: **hosts** plan and run the event, **suppliers** run a business profile and get
booked, and the **Setnayan team** runs operations behind it. The organising idea is that a Filipino
celebration is never one family's — it belongs to the whole samahan — so the capture and memory
layer is built for everybody who showed up, not for one couple and one hired camera.

### Two promises worth stating exactly

**No photograph is ever deleted.** At six months from the first capture — and never sooner than
three months after the celebration *ends* — the full-resolution original is replaced by its
compressed copy. That copy is kept **free, for life**. Only the resolution changes. Google Drive is
the only route by which a couple keeps originals.

**0% commission, always.** The couple pays the supplier directly, off-platform. Setnayan never
touches that money.

## 3 · PAPIC — the flagship

Every guest's phone becomes part of a coordinated photo and video crew. Photos auto-tag to the
guests in them, land in one shared gallery, and every guest goes home with their own copy.

### The pricing model is the unusual part

**You buy shots, not cameras.** Credits go into one shared pool for the whole celebration. Cameras
are free and unlimited. The host can set credits aside for one trusted person's camera — nobody
else can spend those — and **when they run out, that camera keeps shooting from the shared pool**.
Dedicated credits are a floor, never a ceiling.

A photograph costs **one** credit. A clip costs **two to eight** depending on length (1–2s = 2,
3s = 3, 4–6s = 5, 7–10s = 8). Ten seconds is the hard cap.

## 4 · PLANNING — what a host actually gets

**Two timelines, both free.** A planning checklist that counts back from the date and changes shape
per celebration type, and a run-of-show for the day itself with an emcee script, a preparation
agenda and a travel itinerary.

**Seating that lays itself out.** A real solver: it ranks guests by role and priority, keeps groups
together, respects table shapes and where the stage is, and re-settles the plan when something
changes. Thirteen table types.

**Choosing a date you do not have yet.** A find-a-date screen, and a date-selection screen that
checks **which suppliers are actually free** on each candidate date — so the date is picked against
what the market can serve. Where BaZi date data is captured, the app deliberately **refuses to
compute a verdict**; it hands the details to a real date specialist and derives only a harmless
zodiac label.

**Coordinator access, granular.** A coordinator can edit the guest list, seating, schedule,
suppliers and invitations; can only *view* the mood board; **never** sees the budget; and sees
photos only if the couple switches it on.

**Multiple hosts, distinct from the celebrant.** An event can have several hosts; the person being
celebrated is a separate idea and can be one person, a couple, or several.

## 5 · THE DAY, AND AFTER

**On the day** the Event Hub becomes the live page: schedule, seats, what is happening now, the
live wall, the livestream, arrival greetings, face enrolment, and the supplier's own desk in place.

**A supplier's desk lives inside the Event Hub**, and it has four states across the year: a call
sheet before the day (date, countdown, venue, running order, headcount, the organiser's private
lines marked as private), the day itself, a look-back for the week after, and one quiet line long
after — because a supplier's past work is their portfolio.

**Clearance** closes the day: one action stops the livestream, freezes the photo wall into the
recap, closes check-in, and flips the whole menu from day-of to after.

**Afterwards** come the recap, the editorial story, the thank-you film, the gallery kept for life,
and — with consent, 30 days on — a published Real Story that credits the suppliers who made it.

### The three things no local rival offers a shop

1. **A page that fills itself.** Each service card compiles its own record — celebrations booked,
   which of its own options customers actually chose, how many events it has photographs from. The
   shop types none of it. Where a celebration becomes a published story, the shop is credited and
   the credit links back. **Credit is free on every tier.**
   ⚠ A new shop shows nothing: a line needs at least three bookings across three different
   customers, deliberately, so one customer is never identifiable.
2. **Portfolio theft monitoring.** We fingerprint the shop's images and detect reposts elsewhere,
   robust to re-encoding and light cropping. Admins see every flag; a shop sees a scoped view of
   thefts of *its own* work.
3. **Search visibility, automatic.** Its own clean web address at the root of the site, a canonical
   link, a social card, business structured data with breadcrumbs, a self-maintaining sitemap, and
   a live-generated index for AI crawlers.

## 7 · HOW MONEY WORKS

Customers pay in pesos by **GCash or BDO**, upload proof, and a person checks it against the account
— target within 24 hours. A receipt is issued. There is no wallet and no token currency; the
vendor-side token currency was retired entirely in August 2026.

**Proven:** four bills paid end to end on both rails, with proof, hand-matching and receipts.

### The booking fee — and why it is not a commission

**5% of the first ₱100,000, then 1% above, a ₱50 floor, no cap** — charged to the **shop**, for the
introduction and the in-app sync. It applies **only to customers Setnayan found for them**. A
customer the shop brought itself, or a returning one, is free forever, and a verified shop's first
five sourced bookings are free.

The couple still pays the shop directly, off-platform. That is why 0% commission and a booking fee
are both true at once. 🔴 **Never call it a commission, anywhere — product, copy, logs or admin.**
It is currently switched off; nobody has been charged.

### Three places money moves and Setnayan does not touch it

1. **Supplier bookings** — paid directly, off-platform.
2. **Pabuya**, the digital money dance — the couple connects their **own** GCash, Maya, bank or
   PayPal handle and QR; guests send straight to them. We host the display, nothing else.
3. **Day-of crew gigs** — the host posts a gig, a nearby shop's crew accepts, the host pays direct.

Because Setnayan never sits in the booking-money path, it withholds no tax on supplier income and
has **no Form 2307 obligation** toward suppliers (RR 16-2023). The old vendor tax-documents surface
was retired in May 2026 for exactly that reason. Suppliers handle their own as income recipient.

## 8 · WHICH CELEBRATIONS

**Seventeen kinds, live**, each with its own onboarding questions, vocabulary, checklist and tone:
wedding · debut · christening · birthday · graduation · anniversary · gender reveal · reunion ·
wake · corporate · gala night · tournament · travel · date · hangout · celebration · simple event.

New kinds can be created, renamed, switched on and retired from the admin console — no code change.

### Filipino by construction, not by translation

- **Twenty role tiers** — ninong, ninang, principal and secondary sponsors, cord, veil, coin,
  candle, bearers, entourage.
- **Seven ceremony types** — Catholic, Civil, INC, Christian, Muslim, Cultural, Mixed — each a
  first-class planning branch.
- **A Chinese (Tsinoy) wedding is a tradition layer** over a church or civil rite, not a competing
  faith — which is how those families actually experience it, and which most software gets wrong by
  forcing a choice.
- **A wake carries a solemn register**: no countdown, no marketing upsells, an RSVP that reads
  "Will be there / Unable to come", a gift page that says "a gift of sympathy", its own short
  checklist and helper roles. Every celebratory type stays byte-identical.
- **200+ supplier sub-categories** including entries international platforms structurally lack:
  lechonero, Filipiniana attire, barong, Pre-Cana and CFO seminars, pamamanhikan, sponsor
  coordinators, kulintang, Maranao okir motifs.

## 9 · PEOPLE, GROUPS AND MEMORY

**People.** Each guest is tied to a person record — the thing that would let them join future
celebrations without retyping anything. First-degree family is stored (seven relations) and
extended kin is **derived**: lolo, lola, tito, tita, pinsan, pamangkin, apo, including the Filipino
courtesy rule that a parent's close friend is also a tita, with blood and courtesy kept
distinguishable.
⚠ **The derivation reaches no screen yet** — see § 12.

**Records for others.** A person, a pet, **a business**, an item you own, or "other". A business is
asked for a founding date rather than a birthday.

**Samahan.** A group with its own chat, 24-hour stories, a group photo, and the ability to own a
celebration. Anyone may rename it; a group closes only when the last member leaves.

**Life-Flash.** A scrolling reel of your celebrations, pointable at your whole life, one year, one
month or a single event.

## 10 · TRUST, SAFETY AND PRIVACY

**Both directions are covered.** Disputes · fraud detection with cluster sweeps and enforcement ·
an integrity watch · user reports · chat flags · concierge-abuse · force majeure · review-fraud
screening · a plausibility scanner · supplier fake-inquiry protection · and a deposit path where a
supplier can say "I was never paid" without erasing the couple's own record.

🔑 **Judgement queues deliberately have no one-click button.** Where being wrong costs most, the
console shows a sentence instead of a fast action.

**Safety on media.** An NSFW screen that cannot be switched off, running *before* anything is
displayed. A guest can blur themselves. Geolocation is stripped on outbound shares.

**RA 10173.** The DPO is the proprietor. Guest consent at RSVP; face matching is per-event scoped
and never reused across celebrations; data export, erasure and face-data revocation all ship.
**Data residency: nothing is hosted in the Philippines** — the database is in Singapore and object
storage is Cloudflare R2 in Asia-Pacific.
⚠ **The compliance pack still says the photos sit in the Philippines. It is wrong and needs
correcting.**

## 12 · WHAT IS **NOT** PART OF THE OFFER

> Listed so nobody advertises them by accident. **None of these may appear in public copy until it
> ships.**

**Finished but cannot run**
- **Live Studio streaming.** The machinery is complete; the YouTube connection was disconnected on
  2026-07-26 and never restored. Do not sell it until one real test stream has run.

**Written but reaches no screen**
- **The family tree drawing.** The derivation in § 9 is finished, tested, and imported by nothing
  but its own test.
- **A shop cannot set its own venue kind.** The field exists and two public surfaces read it; only
  an admin can write it.
- **Who took each photograph is never recorded**, so per-person folders group an empty set.

**No code at all**
- A public feed where people, shops and celebrations post.
- A page and timeline for a business — and therefore none for a child either.
- A business as the subject of an event (only five personal types can name a subject).
- Opening a shop does not create its business record.
- An avatar maker — everyone in the 3D room is a generic figure.
- Affiliate recommendation pages.

**True but not yet demonstrable**
- **Every Real Story on the site is a labelled sample.** The pipe is built and connected to the
  home page; nothing real has been published, and the first was always meant to be December 2026.

## 13 · WHERE THE BUSINESS STANDS

**Pre-launch.** Roughly forty distinct capabilities are code-complete and payment works end to end,
but almost nothing has met a real customer. A handful of test celebrations, two supplier shops, and
**no real supplier booking has ever run through the platform**. Public launch is targeted for
December 2026.

The honest claim today is *"nobody has built this"*, not *"people are switching to us"*.

🔑 **The single most valuable measurement in this document:** the supplier journey — find, enquire,
negotiate, lock, pay — is fully built and has **never once run**. That is where launch risk lives,
and running it is the first item of the completion plan.
