# Event Hub theme backgrounds — image prompts (2026-09-24)

Owner: *"we do not want just color changes or font changes on themes. we want the overall look."*
Themes: **Classic · Rustic · Modern · Enchanted · Luxe · Vintage · Fairytale**.

Each theme gets **three backgrounds** that the editor offers:

1. **Main** — the continuous canvas behind every scene (quiet, tileable-feeling texture).
2. **Frame** — a scene background with ornament at the edges and an **empty centre** for words and photos.
3. **Atmosphere** — a full-bleed mood image for hero and "big moment" scenes.

Generate each at **two sizes**: 1080×1920 (phone, 9:16) and 2560×1440 (desktop, 16:9).

## Rules for every prompt (append to each)

> Background plate for a wedding and celebration website, no text, no letters, no words, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no faces, no hands. Large calm negative space in the centre third so text and photos can sit on top. Soft, even lighting, gentle low contrast, nothing busy behind the middle. Print-quality detail, natural materials, elegant and timeless, suitable for a Filipino celebration. Colours must stay legible under dark or light text.

**Negative prompt (shared):** text, typography, letters, watermark, signature, logo, frame border cut off, people, faces, hands, clutter in the centre, harsh shadows, oversaturated, neon, cartoon, 3D render look, plastic, stock-photo feel, lens flare.

## Classic
*Timeless, restrained, white-tie. Cream paper, fine gold rules, symmetry.*

- **Main:** Warm ivory cotton paper texture with a barely visible laid-paper grain, soft even light, flat and seamless, faint warm shadow at the edges.
- **Frame:** Ivory paper with a thin double gold foil border inset from the edge, delicate engraved corner flourishes, perfectly symmetrical, wide empty centre.
- **Atmosphere:** Soft-focus white peonies and garden roses on an ivory linen table in morning window light, shallow depth of field, creamy highlights, the centre gently blurred and open.

## Rustic
*Earthy, handmade, outdoors. Wood, linen, dried grasses, Filipino abaca and rattan.*

- **Main:** Natural raw linen and abaca fibre texture in oat and sand tones, soft daylight, subtle woven grain, seamless and calm.
- **Frame:** Kraft paper background with loose dried pampas, eucalyptus sprigs and twine tucked at the top-left and bottom-right corners, rattan weave peeking at one edge, open centre.
- **Atmosphere:** Golden-hour light across a long wooden farm table under an acacia tree, blurred fairy lights and dried flowers, warm amber haze, soft bokeh, calm centre.

## Modern
*Clean, architectural, gallery-like. Negative space, one strong line, matte surfaces.*

- **Main:** Smooth matte plaster wall in warm greige with a very soft vertical light gradient, seamless, minimal, no texture noise.
- **Frame:** Off-white matte background with a single thin charcoal line forming an off-centre rectangle and one soft architectural shadow falling diagonally, generous empty space.
- **Atmosphere:** Minimalist concrete and glass interior with one arching window, soft diffused daylight, a single sculptural white orchid stem out of focus at the edge, vast calm negative space.

## Enchanted
*Mystical garden at dusk. Moss, ferns, fireflies, moonlight, deep greens and silver.*

- **Main:** Deep forest-green velvet texture with a faint silver shimmer like scattered starlight, soft vignette, seamless and quiet in the centre.
- **Frame:** Dark emerald background with trailing ferns, moss and tiny white wildflowers growing in from the corners, faint firefly glows at the edges, open misty centre.
- **Atmosphere:** An enchanted forest clearing at blue hour, mist between tall trees, soft moonbeams, glowing fireflies, a path of petals leading into soft blur, dreamy and calm.

## Luxe
*Opulent evening. Black-tie, marble, deep jewel tones, polished gold, candlelight.*

- **Main:** Polished dark marble in deep charcoal with fine gold veining, soft reflective sheen, seamless, calm without sharp highlights in the centre.
- **Frame:** Midnight navy silk background with an ornate gold filigree border and art-deco corner fans, rich but restrained, wide open centre.
- **Atmosphere:** A candlelit ballroom with crystal chandeliers softly out of focus, warm gold bokeh, deep burgundy and black shadows, velvet drapery at the edges, luminous calm centre.

## Vintage
*Heirloom, nostalgic, 1920s–1960s. Aged paper, lace, pressed flowers, sepia warmth.*

- **Main:** Aged cream letter paper with subtle foxing, soft tea-stained edges and a faint lace pattern watermark, warm sepia light, seamless.
- **Frame:** Antique lace doily edges and pressed dried roses with a faded ribbon along the top and bottom, soft sepia tones, empty aged-paper centre.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm film-photograph look of an old Spanish-colonial Filipino church interior with capiz windows, dust motes in slanted sunlight, soft grain, gentle vignette, calm centre.

## Fairytale
*Storybook romance. Soft pastels, castles in clouds, blossoms, sparkle.*

- **Main:** Soft blush-to-lilac watercolour wash with gentle cloud-like blooms and a faint pearly shimmer, seamless, airy and light.
- **Frame:** Pastel watercolour background with climbing cherry blossoms and tiny sparkles framing the sides like an open storybook page, soft ribbon at the top, open centre.
- **Atmosphere:** A dreamy pastel sky at sunrise with a distant castle silhouette in soft clouds, drifting petals, gentle sparkles, soft glow, the centre open and luminous.
