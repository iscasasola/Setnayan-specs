# Setnayan — North-Star Strategy (2026-06-23)

> Consolidated from a multi-session strategy arc: switcher/account redesign → the realization that the **user page = the memory home = the retention engine** → retention research → storage-lifecycle economics → the "memories, not wedding-planning" positioning pivot → this full go-to-market analysis. This is the reference everything we build and market should trace back to.

---

## Bottom line

**Launch weddings-only in Metro Manila + CALABARZON, branded as the memory home from day one.** The multi-event vision is the right north star but a *liability* as a launch scope — keep it in the architecture and the pitch, ship **zero** of it in go-to-market until weddings is won. Seed vendor supply by hand to a liquidity floor (≥5 vendors per core category) before opening demand, **monetize the vendor side (never couples)**, and let the **~₱0-CAC guest→host loop — powered by Papic depth no competitor matches — be the growth engine.** The head-on rival is **Eventchy**, not The Knot; beat it on the **post-event memory layer it doesn't own.** Be profitable on one wedding's margin, win one metro completely, earn breadth on proof — not a calendar.

## Vision & Mission

- **Vision:** *To be the home where every Filipino's life moments are kept — for life.*
- **Mission:** *To take care of everything around your celebration — the plan, the people, the photos, the memories — so the day is set, and stays with you for life.* ("Set na 'yan.")

## 1. Launch approach — "build broad, launch narrow"

**Decision: weddings-only, one metro, memory-home brand.** The evidence is one-sided for a two-sided marketplace with real vendor supply + per-event margin:

- **Liquidity is the only launch metric that matters.** Spread supply across many event types and the couple hits an empty shelf and never returns. Premature multi-vertical scaling is the #1 startup killer (~74% of high-growth failures, Startup Genome).
- **Every analog launched narrowest-first:** Zola weddings-only ~10 years before adding baby (and only after 500+ inbound requests + 1,800 users already hacking it); Facebook one campus; Airbnb one city (door-to-door, reshot listings); Uber/DoorDash one city/vertical.
- **Keep multi-event in the architecture + brand, express it as ONE event type in GTM.** The wedge is a precise entry into a large market, not a small vision.

**Honest caveat that actually *validates* the pivot:** PH marriages are **declining ~8–10%/yr** (449k in 2022 → ~414k → ~372k in 2024). That's the strongest data-backed argument *for* the life-events brand — but expansion is a **demand-gated hedge you unlock later**, not a launch scope.

**In scope at launch:** one wedding, end-to-end and deep — Papic face-tagged galleries + 5s clips + auto 9:16 reels + STD films + editorial showcase; full planning (guest list, RSVP, seating, budget, mood board); a hand-seeded vendor marketplace in the 8–10 spend-heavy categories; apply-then-pay GCash/BDO; the anniversary memory loop; 100% no-install browser/QR guest flow.

**Out (built in architecture, hidden in GTM):** debut/christening/birthday/corporate/reunion; a second metro; couple subscriptions (never); registry revenue (weak in cash-gift PH).

## 2. Competitor analysis — the field has changed

We **no longer mainly compete with US wedding incumbents** (The Knot, Zola, Joy) — they're US-centric, registry-revenue-driven (weak in PH), and have no candid-capture moat or PH rails.

**Closest competitor now: Eventchy** (Xugbo Technologies, launched Dec 2025, Ayala Malls Circuit Makati) — the only player overlapping us on **both** axes: PH-first **and** multi-life-event marketplace. Near feature-for-feature on the planning+marketplace spine (AI matchmaker "Eve" ≈ Setnayan AI, Dream Boards ≈ mood board, verified booking, digital invites/RSVP, cashless pay). **Its gap: no post-event memory layer** — no candid capture, no face-tagged per-guest galleries, no auto-reels, no forever memory home, no guest→host loop. **That gap is our sharpest wedge.** (Monitor it continuously; if it bolts on capture, the moat narrows.)

| Competitor | Category | Setnayan's angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Eventchy** | Head-on (PH AI event marketplace) | Same vendor marketplace **+ the memory moat + the ₱0-CAC guest→host loop they lack.** Lead with what every guest *keeps*. |
| Face-rec photo apps (Kwikpic, FotoOwl, Memzo, Honcho…) | Closest category — auto-face-delivery is table stakes | Face-tagging is the **entry hook bundled into deep planning + vendor revenue + a forever home.** Out-context them, don't out-feature them. |
| QR-album apps (POV, WedShoots, Wedibox, GuestCam) | Commodity shared album | **Per-guest tagged delivery + untagged-still-delivered guarantee** — the QR gallery that actually gives each guest *their* photos. |
| **Veri / The Guest** (The Knot — **DEAD**, ended Dec 2023) | Proof point | Standalone capture has **no defensibility** — even inside a giant. Justifies welding the photo layer to vendor revenue + the loop. |
| Google Photos / Apple / Lapse / Timehop | **Align WITH** | Don't replace the camera roll — own the **event** memory (face-tagged, multi-guest, vendor-produced), borrow "on this day," **share out** to them. |
| Partiful (+2M users; adding albums) | Adjacent threat with a clock | We go **deep** where it goes **light** — but it's drifting toward memories; our breadth has a clock. |
| Joy (withjoy.com, $60M for "all life events") | Strategic-shape | Same wedding→all-life-events thesis — **proves the pivot is fundable.** PH-native + vendor-revenue + Papic moat is the defense. |
| Kasal.com, Bridestory PH (15k+ vendors) | Supply-side flank | Convert the same supplier supply into a **transactional app with memory retention**, not a lead-gen directory. |

**Positioning statement:** *For Filipino families marking life's biggest moments, SETNAYAN is the home where every celebration is planned, captured, and kept forever — the depth Facebook and spreadsheets can't reach — so no moment, and no one in it, is ever lost.*

## 3. Trends to maximize (2026)

1. **QR guest-photo galleries are now the wedding standard** (89% QR adoption; 87% of weddings offer an interactive gallery; hashtags are dead). **Papic isn't riding this — it's the category that won.** Lead all marketing with the one thing no one else does: *every guest gets THEIR photos.*
2. **Nostalgia / memory-app boom is structural** (BeReal, Lapse, Timehop 20M+ daily, 1SE). Steal the proven engine (24-hr resurfacing, anniversary "on this day") but anchor it to a *real wedding* so it carries emotional payload they lack — at ~₱7/couple/yr to keep.
3. **Short-form vertical 9:16 dominates** (81% of short-form is vertical; PH = ~40h46m/mo on TikTok). Auto-reels already ship in the winning format — **render, then share OUT** to TikTok/Reels; don't build a feed.
4. **AI music/video crossed into mass-market** (Suno 2M subs; 44% of new Deezer uploads AI). Owned-catalogue + template renders are now *normal*, ~₱0 marginal cost; the no-major-label lock is now a cost + legal advantage.
5. **Digital disposable-camera revival** (a 100-guest wedding burns $345–600 on throwaways). Papic = the film feel, but every guest actually *gets* their photos + face-tagging physical cams never can.
6. **PH livestreaming world-leading** (41% cite livestreams as a main reason to log on — highest globally). Validates Panood; tie it to the **$38B OFW remittance pool** — *send money + watch live + keep the gallery*, a triangle FB/Sheets can't assemble. (Bonus: **Facebook now auto-deletes livestreams after 30 days** — a direct opening for "kept forever.")
7. **AI-in-planning adoption ~36% of couples** — justifies the paid Setnayan AI tier, but **do NOT depend on AI-search discovery** (84% of vendors have zero AI citation share). The guest→host loop is the only safe ₱0-CAC engine. *(Most important caveat.)*
8. **Gen Z weddings: smaller, experience-first, guest-as-participant** (43% target <$20k). Validates per-event SKUs + guest-participation monetization over couple subscriptions.

## 4. Marketing & growth strategy

**Core message:** *Every guest gets THEIR photos — and your whole day stays with you forever. (Para hindi makalimutan.)* Lead with the memory the guest keeps, not the vendor we book.

**Channels, in order:**
1. **Involuntary product-led loop** — the product IS the ad (Partiful/Calendly model; no incentive).
2. **Watermarked shareable artifacts** — the reel / STD film / editorial `/slug` page IS the ad ("Made with Setnayan").
3. **Facebook + Messenger share-out** (PH reality: FB 94.9%, Messenger 90.6%, ~23.5h/mo).
4. **TikTok + IG Reels** via PH nano/micro wedding creators (₱1–20k/post, ~3× engagement).
5. **SEO + editorial evergreen moat** — long-tail PH geo intent ("affordable wedding photographer Cavite"), schema-marked public showcase pages.
6. **Two-sided referral on PAID surfaces only** (Dropbox model — host give-get, vendor-refers-vendor).
7. **Anniversary "on this day" re-engagement** — retention + a second re-share pulse per wedding.
8. **Paid ads — last resort only.**

**The growth engine (guest→host loop):** every wedding touches ~150 guests; every guest touches a branded **no-install** browser/QR surface (RSVP, face-tagged gallery, their personal 9:16 reel); each seeds FOMO + a "plan your own / save your photos forever" CTA. Those ~150 guests are future hosts at ~₱0 CAC. **It only fires if the first wedding experience is excellent** — which is why depth on weddings is the growth prerequisite. Keep it incentive-free; never force an app install (kills 70–85% of participation). **Make "% of an event's guests who later create their own event" the company north-star metric.**

**Social coexistence:** BE the events platform, SHARE OUT to social — never replace FB/IG. Default primary action on every artifact = one-tap Share to Messenger/FB with a rich OG card.

## 5. Vendor marketplace — timeline & goals

**Where we are now:** founder-only / single-published-vendor by design (Maria & Jose sample event live with `is_sample`/`is_demo`). Rails are built + locked: verification FREE + 100 free tokens, Pro ₱6,000/28d, Enterprise ₱10,000/28d, flat ₱100/token, 0% commission, Setnayan Pay 3%. **No real third-party supply seeded yet — this is the supply-density build phase.**

**Pre-launch goals:** hand-recruit + **concierge-build** 100–150 verified vendor profiles (build their profile *for* them from their FB/IG portfolio so the barrier is zero and the directory looks full day one), covering only the **8–10 spend-heavy categories** (photo/video, venue, catering, coordinator, HMUA, host/emcee, mobile bar, lights/sounds) in **MM + CALABARZON**. **Liquidity floor: ≥5 active verified vendors per core category** before opening the couple side. Source from PH supplier FB groups (WAESP, Best Wedding Suppliers PH) + Kasal.com + bridal fairs. Keep it FREE; the Papic auto-gallery + editorial showcase is the **supply magnet** (vendors want their work showcased); monetize only after they feel real couple intents.

## 6. Market analysis

- **TAM:** ~372k PH weddings/yr × a conservative blended ~₱350k/wedding ≈ **~₱130B/yr** in wedding spend (most is venue/F&B we don't capture). Add debut, christening, kids' birthdays, corporate/MICE → the broader PH life-events economy is plausibly **₱250–400B+/yr, growing ~8.1% CAGR even as marriages fall** — that divergence is *the* reason the life-events repositioning is correct.
- **SAM** (app-charged slice — vendor placement/transaction + per-event SKUs): anchored on the vendor economy (Bridestory's 15k+ vendors × Pro ₱6–10k/28d ≈ a ₱1–7B/yr vendor-SaaS ceiling on weddings alone) + per-event media SKUs (₱2–28k) → **~₱5–15B/yr**, expanding as event types unlock.
- **SOM** (12–24 mo, single-digit-thousands of events + a few hundred paying vendors): **~₱20–150M/yr.** Works because each wedding is profitable on its own margin (**R2-only marginal cost ~₱7/couple/yr** after the storage downgrade; renders/face/egress ~₱0).
- **Segments:** MM + CALABARZON couples (~26% of national marriages); Gen Z experience-first couples (won't subscribe, will pay per memorable experience); **the vendor supply side (load-bearing revenue).**
- **Diaspora:** the **$38.34B/yr OFW remittance pool** (8.3% of GDP) funds events back home AND is the audience for Panood + forever-galleries across timezones — the *send-money + watch-live + keep-the-memory* triangle is a genuinely under-exploited wedge.

## 7. Retention moats (from prior analysis)

1. **The memory home** — galleries kept forever at cheap **display-res** (originals delivered to the couple's Drive, then auto-downgraded), with anniversary "on this day" re-engagement. The un-deletable system-of-record.
2. **The vendor marketplace** — revenue **decoupled from couple frequency** (a vendor is bought once, pays across hundreds of couples). 0% commission + per-intent tokens = the structural anti-Knot.
3. **The guest→host loop** — the only organic, ~₱0-CAC growth engine in a one-and-done, Facebook-saturated market.

Reframe of the whole app: planning tools are what get people *in*; **the memory home is the only thing that keeps them.** Judge the app on guest→host conversion + anniversary re-opens, **never** on daily-use metrics.

## 8. Storage lifecycle (what makes "forever" affordable)

**Don't be the vault — be the delivery + the gallery.** Full-res-forever is an unbounded liability (~$1.43M/yr at 1M couples); the display tier kept forever is a **flat ~₱7/couple/yr** (~11× cheaper, free egress on R2).

1. **Hot originals** (~90 days) — pristine, downloadable.
2. **Deliver originals to the user** — Google-Drive copy (already built) + a 7-day download link. *Their* originals live with *them*. (Connecting Drive is also the conversion event that makes the gallery permanent.)
3. **Auto-downgrade forever** — keep only display-res (~16MP photo / 1080p clip) + rendered reels/films/editorials, warned (T-30/T-7/T-1), never silent. Originals age to cold or expire (the couple has them).

Downgrading **does not** weaken the moat — nostalgia runs on display-res (Google ships 16MP downsize as "indistinguishable"; reels are already 1080×1920). Full-res-forever would *threaten* the moat (the runaway bill forces a future paywall). **Never gate access** (the Snapchat/Flickr third rail). Optional one-time "Keep My Originals" keepsake for the few who want Setnayan-side redundancy — never a wall on memories.

**Build work this implies (greenfield today):** a display-derivative pipeline; serve derivatives not originals (the gallery currently serves 250 full-res tiles — a real cost+speed bug); the auto-downgrade sweep on paid media; meter clips harder than photos. Reconcile the stale "5-year / hot-90" spec to one honest policy: *"we never lose a memory — display kept forever + originals handed to the couple."*

## Key risks

- **Eventchy (or Partiful) bolts on candid capture** → extend Papic depth (face-tagging, reels, forever galleries) faster than they can copy.
- **PH marriage decline shrinks the core** → unlock the 2nd event type (debut) sooner once weddings is liquid (demand-gated).
- **Going broad too early** re-creates cold-start + burns runway → hold one metro ~12 months.
- **AI-search discovery is closing** (84% vendors zero citation share) → never depend on it; the guest→host loop is the safe engine.
