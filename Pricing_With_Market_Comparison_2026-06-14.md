**Read this carefully:**
- **All prices PROVISIONAL** (owner's standing rule — one holistic pass after all features ship). Nothing re-priced here.
- **"Market typical"** = the realistic price a Filipino couple/vendor pays elsewhere for the equivalent. The **low** is usually a DIY/template that doesn't fully match; the **high** is premium/bespoke.
- **Media SKUs compare software to labor.** Panood, Patiktok, SDE, Pabati, Guest Stories, Camera Bridge market prices bundle physical gear + on-site crew. Ours is software-only — savings are real but not strictly like-for-like.
- USD converted at ~₱58/US$1 where used.

## G. Headline takeaways

1. **We undercut the market on almost everything** — the media SKUs (SDE, Patiktok, Panood, Pabati, Guest Stories) by 5–20× because we replace gear+crew with software.
2. **Three spots where we're at/above market — worth an eye in the holistic pass:**
   - **Editorial Website ₱7,999** > typical ₱6,999 (only below truly-bespoke ₱15k).
   - **RSVP Pro ₱4,499** ≈ market ₱4,500 (at par — no headroom).
   - **Pro Vendor ₱6,000/mo** sits at the premium end of PH vendor directories (typical ₱2,500/mo) — justified only if the bundled website + AI + unlimited inquiries are seen as worth it.
3. **0% commission is the single biggest vendor lever** — every competitor takes ₱1,200–₱11,600 per booking; we return all of it.
4. **Setnayan AI's market story is "cheaper than a coordinator," not "cheaper than other AI"** (other AI planners are free) — position on *value displaced*, not feature-price.
