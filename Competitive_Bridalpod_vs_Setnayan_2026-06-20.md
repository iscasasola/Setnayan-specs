## Where Setnayan is clearly ahead
AI matchmaking · in-app media stack (Papic/Panood/…) · editorial/Alaala + Kwento · mood board · bespoke monogram · seat-plan **editor** (vs lookup) · explicit 0%-commission disclosed model · premium Save-the-Date tier.

## Parity (both free)
Core planning suite · couple website + RSVP + guest mgmt · couple↔vendor messaging · verified-vendor marketplace concept.

## Where Bridalpod is ahead / simpler (honest)
1. **End-to-end booking works *today*** (Setnayan's was partly unbuilt — Phase 1 just shipped the proposal→shortlist→lock bridge, closing this).
2. **Public vendor pricing on listings.**
3. **Live, populated marketplace + Search by Map** (Setnayan founder-only pre-launch, no map search).
4. **Virtual Wedding Expo / Online Fair** (recurring nationwide channel since 2020) — Setnayan has no equivalent.
5. **Contradiction-free couple offering** (Setnayan has RSVP SKU collision + unsynced free/paid copy + unconfirmed prices).
6. Simpler vendor onboarding promise (Setnayan vendor surface deeper but with known gaps).

## Monetization contrast
- **Bridalpod:** couples pay nothing; vendors free; **no disclosed revenue model** (presumably vendor/fair-side, opaque).
- **Setnayan:** two disclosed lanes — couple 4-tier paywall (Free · AI ₱3,999 · Essentials ₱12,999 · Complete ₱27,999) + 19 SKUs; vendor 28-day subs (₱6k/₱10k) + token bidding economy, **0% commission**, money off-platform.

## ⭐ Strategic read — this DEFINES the free-vs-AI line
- **The "bare minimum free" floor is dictated by Bridalpod, not chosen.** Everything in the **Parity** rows (plan · website · RSVP · guests · browse · inquire/message) is **free on Bridalpod** → Setnayan must keep them free or couples defect to the free competitor. This is *why* the free-to-plan / free-website / free-RSVP / free-seat locks exist — they're competitive table stakes for the two-sided cold-start, not arbitrary generosity. **Do not paywall them.**
- **Setnayan AI = pure differentiation Bridalpod cannot counter.** AI matching, media stack, editorial, mood board, monogram, premium STD — Bridalpod *doesn't build any of it.* This is the entire paid surface, and it's safe to charge for because there's no free competitor for it.
- So "bare minimum free, everything else AI" resolves to: **free = match Bridalpod's free (table stakes); paid = everything Bridalpod doesn't even have.** That maximizes the paid tier without losing the adoption race.
- **The one urgent competitive gap = the booking flow** (their only real lead). Phase 1 (just shipped) + Phase 2 (payments) directly close it.
- **Cleanup the comparison surfaced:** RSVP SKU collision, unsynced free/paid website copy, unconfirmed prices → fold into the holistic pricing pass (Bridalpod's simplicity is a real advantage here).
