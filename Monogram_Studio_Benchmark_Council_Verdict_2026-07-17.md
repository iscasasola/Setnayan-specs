# Monogram_Studio_Benchmark_Council_Verdict_2026-07-17

> **Owner ask (verbatim):** "use the council to check what we have and what is available in the internet. and improve our idea to create the best version in the internet available. 3D doesn't feel 3D enough. Also want the play button to be at the bottom of reveal box settings … We want it to be simple to use but very premium when created."
>
> **Council:** 3 web scouts (competitor makers · premium reveal motion · simple-but-premium tool UX, all with live WebSearch/WebFetch) + 2 councilors (product · motion) + synthesis judge. Baseline = the studio as shipped 2026-07-17 (verdict PRs #3344–#3353 + refinements #3357/#3359/#3360/#3361).
>
> **Already shipped from this brief (PR #3361):** Play button → bottom of the reveal box · first-pass physical 3D (tilted axis, depth zoom, dynamic shadow, canvas light sweep). §3's Medallion Turn is the full prescription that supersedes the first pass when built.

**Scope:** The Monogram Studio benchmark — studio editor, reveal runtime, and save/deliverable flow, product + presentation only. Baseline: the studio as shipped 2026-07-17 behind its flag (paper.js + opentype.js vector engine, 8 OFL faces, per-crossing boolean weave, 15 parametric frames, 7 reveals, free SVG/PNG export, ₱999 Animated Monogram upsell). Inputs: three scouts (makers, motion, premium-ux) and two councilors (product, motion), merged here with conflicts resolved explicitly. Pricing is untouched: the studio stays free, the reveal SKU stays ₱999. Owner brief honored throughout: (a) best monogram maker on the internet, (b) 3D must feel 3D, (c) Play button to the bottom of the reveal settings box (in flight — recorded in §1), (d) simple to use, very premium when created.

**The organizing ruling:** The field audit shows we already hold the only two assets money can't quickly copy — a true per-crossing boolean weave on arbitrary font outlines, which no mainstream tool computes (Icons8 fakes interlock with size/opacity overlap — [icons8.com](https://icons8.com/make/interlocking-monogram-generator-free); Suncatcher pre-draws it as a font — [suncatcherstudio.com](https://suncatcherstudio.com/monogram-maker/)), and platform propagation of one mark across QR codes, live website, film, and PDFs, which no maker or wedding platform does (Zola/Minted apply marks to their own print catalogs — [zola.com](https://www.zola.com/wedding-planning/invitations/shop/monogram), [minted.com](https://www.minted.com/monogram)). The gap is therefore not capability — it is **staging**. Both councilors converge on the same three-act cure, and this verdict adopts it as the spine: **curation at the entry** (named styles, rings of disclosure), **one physics system in the middle** (a shared choreography module with the Medallion Turn as its proof), **one choreographed payoff at the end** (the save-time "your monogram, everywhere" sequence). Premium is restraint plus material response, not more tools ([developer.apple.com — Procreate](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=e409h6ja), [hapticmedia.com — Baume](https://hapticmedia.com/3d-configurator-watch-baume/)).

## 1 · Entry & creation flow — "simple to use"

- **KEEP — presets-first entry with the couple's real initials**, including the computed pre-woven interlock. No competitor shows *your* letters already woven on screen one (scout gap #7; Minted only approximates it — [minted.com](https://www.minted.com/monogram)). The studio never opens on the blank tool.
- **CHANGE — 6 presets → 12 named, art-directed Styles** ("Alon", "Sampaguita", "Balangay"…), the Minted/Zola named-style pattern: font pairing, optical spacing, weave gaps, frame stack, and palette **pre-solved** per style. No kerning slider ships, ever — pro consensus is that no algorithm replaces a trained eye at logo level, so spacing is pre-solved inside curated presets instead of exposed ([southype.com](https://www.southype.com/Commerce/6554-2/)).
- **CHANGE — restructure the editor into three rings of progressive disclosure.** Ring 1 (default surface): style picker + palette mood + reveal picker — a couple finishes in ~90 seconds. Ring 2 (a quiet "Refine" affordance): frames, per-letter tilt/flip, drag/rotate. Ring 3 (tap a crossing / long-press): boolean weave controls, nibs, mirror drawing, stamps. Staged disclosure for first-run, progressive disclosure inside the editor — never wizard-ify the editor, never accordion the onboarding ([uxdatabase.io](https://www.uxdatabase.io/newsletter-issue/04-staged-vs-progressive-disclosure), [uxpin.com](https://www.uxpin.com/studio/blog/what-is-progressive-disclosure/)). All current power survives; only its altitude changes.
- **CHANGE (in flight — recorded per owner instruction):** the **Play button moves to the bottom of the reveal settings box**, below reveal choice and tempo — settings read top-down, the action verb sits last, matching the dock grammar elsewhere in the app. Already being shipped; logged here so this verdict is the complete registry.
- **ADD — exactly one branded micro-interaction:** the weave-flip gets a custom haptic tick (mobile) + a 120ms gold glint at the crossing. Everything else stays silent — delight only at genuine milestones reads premium; confetti-on-everything reads cheap ([charisol](https://medium.com/charisol-pulse/better-ux-through-microinteractions-the-details-that-define-delight-d8fd4b8b22a6), [saropa.com](https://saropa.com/articles/2025-guide-to-haptics-enhancing-mobile-ux-with-tactile-feedback/)).

## 2 · Premium-output guarantee — make ugly impossible

- **ADD — 8 named palette moods in Ring 1** (ink+gold, wine+cream, forest+brass…), contrast pre-balanced across letters/frames/accents. Hex input is exiled to Ring 3. Couples pick a mood, not a hex — the Tiffany five-styles lesson: zero freedom where freedom produces bad results ([tiffany.com](https://www.tiffany.com/client-services/personalization.html)).
- **ADD — "can't-leave-ugly" engine invariants:** weave gap auto-scales to stroke weight; frame auto-fit maintains optical margins measured on ink bounds, not em box; letter overlap clamps so counters never fully occlude; accent count caps per enclosure. Deterministic rules, zero curation labor, zero LLM — consistent with the platform's deterministic-AI lock.
- **ADD — auto-derivatives on every save:** a simplified variant (letters only, one color) and a **gobo/foil variant** (single-color, high-contrast, minimum stroke width enforced). Bliss & Bone charges US$40–120 for a mark *system* ([blissandbone.com](https://blissandbone.com/wedding-monogram)); ours is a boolean pass on paths we already own. No maker exports a gobo-ready file today (scout gap #10 — [shipour.wedding](https://shipour.wedding/rentals/uplighting/gobos/wedding-monogram-lighting/)).
- **KEEP — free forever, permanently re-editable, SVG included** — and say so on the landing page. The field gates vector at US$65 (Looka — [costbench.com](https://costbench.com/software/ai-design-tools/looka/)) and charges to re-download after edits (Wix — [websiteplanet.com](https://www.websiteplanet.com/blog/wix-logo-maker-pricing/)).

## 3 · The 3D prescription — the Medallion Turn (owner brief: "3D must FEEL 3D")

The current rotateY reads flat because it is a zero-thickness plane under no light with no shadow, at constant angular velocity. **CHANGE — rebuild "3D Turn" as the Medallion Turn**, CSS/WAAPI on inline SVG, no WebGL. This is the exact chosen prescription (both councilors agreed on substance; the one numeric conflict — product's 700px vs motion's 750px perspective — resolves to **750px**, motion's 2.5×-mark-width rule, with 700 inside the sanctioned 600–900 band anyway; sources: [polypane.app](https://polypane.app/css-3d-transform-examples/), [sleekinteractive.com](https://sleekinteractive.com/blog/post/simulating-lighting-with-css-perspective/), [joshwcomeau.com](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/animation/linear-timing-function/), [3dtransforms.desandro.com](https://3dtransforms.desandro.com/perspective)):

1. **Perspective on the parent** (one vanishing point, never `transform: perspective()` per child): `perspective: 750px` for a ~300px hero mark, 600px on mobile; `perspective-origin: 50% 35%` — the raised origin implies an elevated camera and asymmetric foreshortening.
2. **Compound axis:** `rotateX(8deg) rotateY(var(--turn))` — never pure Y; the constant tilt makes top and bottom edges foreshorten differently every frame.
3. **Angle-driven light (the single biggest upgrade):** parallel WAAPI track on `filter: brightness()` — 1.0 → 0.72 near 80° → a 1.06 "catch" flash at landing → 1.0. Backface, when shown: `brightness(.85) saturate(.9)`.
4. **Specular sweep clipped to the letterforms** (SVG mask = the mark's paths): narrow 115° gradient (`rgba(255,246,220,.85)` core), `mix-blend-mode: screen`, translating across the mark *during* the turn, brightest in the final 350ms.
5. **Thickness:** 4 stacked copies of the mark at `translateZ(-1px…-4px)` filled deep bronze (#6b4a1f) inside `preserve-3d` — the medallion rim at oblique angles. The difference between a sticker turning and a medallion turning.
6. **Breathing contact shadow:** an ellipse (or rotateX(90°) flattened copy, `brightness(.1)` + blur) keyframed against the turn — 0°: `scaleX(.55), opacity .35, blur 6px`; 90°: `scaleX(1), opacity .15, blur 14px`. The shadow reacting to the rotation is what anchors the object to a floor.
7. **Spring easing:** CSS `linear()` sampled at **48 points** (stiffness 170, damping 20, mass 1; generate via linear-easing-generator), landing with a ~3° overshoot and settle. Fallback: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Kill every linear/uniform rotation.
8. **Intra-mark parallax:** frames at `translateZ(-8px)`, letters at 0, accent sprigs/sparkles at `+6px`, all inside one preserve-3d turn — the strongest "truly 3D" cue available without WebGL. Our layer model already separates these groups; this is nearly free.
9. **Beat structure (Classic, 1900ms):** 300ms hold at −78° → 1100ms spring turn with the cosine brightness ramp → specular traverse at 55–74% → stillness, one 4px sparkle ping on the heaviest stroke 200ms after rest. Ceremonial = +300ms hold, 1.4× durations, dim echo sweep at +1.2s. Quick = 1100ms total, no echo. Stillness before and after is part of the effect ([artivoxa.com — Celine](https://www.artivoxa.com/celine-beauty-cgi-parisian-minimalism-in-motion-design-using-houdini/)).

- **CHANGE — kill the in-studio 2D fake.** The studio previews reveals by **portaling the identical live-site markup/WAAPI over the canvas** (we already portal the Gold/Molten React components). One implementation, zero studio-vs-live drift — the preview becomes a promise, the Baume "connected to the engraving machine" trust move ([apviz.io](https://apviz.io/blog/engraving-customization/)). The paper.js trapezoid approximation survives **only** as the reduced-motion/very-low-end fallback: perspective-correct quad from the same 750px focal length, cosine brightness on the fill, trailing rim sliver of width `sin(angle)×4px`, breathing ellipse — four cheap draws.
- **CHANGE — Gold Turn merges into the Medallion Turn.** Conflict resolved: the product councilor kept both turn reveals; the motion councilor's cut is adopted — the rebuilt turn's face *is* metallic (specular + brightness), and two turn doors is a menu smell.

## 4 · The reveal menu & house choreography

- **Menu ruling — seven doors stay seven:** Handwriting · Bloom · Petal Fall · Molten Gold · **Medallion Turn** (absorbs 3D Turn + Gold Turn) · **Selyo** (Foil Stamp, new) · **Tinta** (Ink Bleed, new). **Trace is CUT** — strictly dominated by per-stroke Handwriting; it survives only as a Quick-tempo Handwriting alias for existing links. (Conflict resolved: product's specular ruling listed Trace as surviving; motion's dominance argument wins.)
- **ADD — one shared choreography module, applied to all seven:** 250–400ms entry hold and ≥600ms settle on every reveal; every landing on the 48-point `linear()` spring; the **specular sweep as a shared pass** — Handwriting gets it after the last stroke, Bloom at full open, Petal Fall 300ms after the final piece, Molten Gold gains the sparkle ping + echo sweep. Tempo presets scale durations, never delete holds. This one module is the highest premium-per-line-of-code item in the entire benchmark.
- **ADD — Selyo (Foil Stamp), the new flagship genre:** static paper-texture ground (feTurbulence at 4%); 250ms hold; the mark **stamps** — scale 1.05→0.985→1.0 over 380ms on `cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` — synchronized with a two-pass bevel fading in (light #fff8e7 offset −1.2,−1.2; dark #3a2a10 offset +1.2,+1.2); contact shadow blooms .4→.18 over 500ms; +600ms, one slow 900ms foil sweep. Matches the tactile-stationery luxury language couples already buy (foil/emboss physical upsell — [shutterfly.com](https://www.shutterfly.com/t/wedding-invitations/?theme=monograms)); nobody on the web does a live version of your own mark.
- **ADD — Tinta (Ink Bleed):** gooey mask of 12–20 blurred circles seeded along letter skeletons via `getPointAtLength`, randomized 40–120ms staggers over 1400ms, blur+contrast-threshold merge; edges bleed ~6% past the final form, then dry — blur 6px→0 over the final 700ms, ink darkening #2a2320→final color, ending pixel-crisp on the true vector. feTurbulence-displaced variant desktop-only. Pairs with the script faces and the fountain-pen layer.
- **CHANGE — Handwriting upgraded to per-stroke pacing:** segmented masks, durations proportional to path length, linear mid-stroke with eased entry/exit, 80–140ms pen-lift micro-pauses, the existing golden nib rotating along the path tangent ([motiontricks.com](https://www.motiontricks.com/svg-calligraphy-handwriting-animation/)).
- **CHANGE — Petal Fall physics:** per-piece randomized delay (0–350ms), spring settles, per-piece 4px micro-shadows that merge at landing, last piece deliberately +250ms late — the "final click."
- **ADD (P3) — Gold Particle Assembly** in the existing Molten Gold WebGL context (one point-sprite buffer targeting `getPointAtLength` samples). **Replacement-only rule:** it enters the menu by replacing the weakest performer, never as an eighth door.

## 9 · Rejected proposals

- **Gold Turn as a separate door** — merged into Medallion Turn; two turn reveals is a menu smell.
- **Trace as its own reveal** — strictly dominated by per-stroke Handwriting; alias only.
- **Kerning/letter-spacing sliders** — spacing is pre-solved in Styles; no algorithm replaces a trained eye ([southype](https://www.southype.com/Commerce/6554-2/)).
- **Hex-first color UI** — moods own Ring 1; hex is a Ring 3 concession.
- **Rive/Lottie for reveals** — pre-authored formats, wrong tool for user-generated runtime paths ([dev.to](https://dev.to/uianimation/rive-vs-lottie-which-animation-tool-should-you-use-in-2025-p4m)).
- **Default three.js PBR extruded gold turn** — the CSS Medallion gets ~80% of perceived quality at ~5% of the cost; PBR remains a P3 experiment at most.
- **Expanding the menu past seven reveals** — restraint is the premium signal ([developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=e409h6ja)); Particle enters only by replacement.
- **AI prompt-a-monogram mode** — raster-only, mangles letterforms and dates ([lightxeditor.com](https://www.lightxeditor.com/ai-design/ai-wedding-monogram-generator/), [dreamina](https://dreamina.capcut.com/resource/wedding-monogram-generator)); also against the platform's deterministic-AI lock.
- **⚠ OWNER-OVERRIDDEN same day (app PR #3367):** "upload your own png/svg/eps file and we will decypher it and create elements of each item and help them animate it" — shipped: dependency-free PNG tracer (one evenodd path per connected piece = per-piece animation), SVG via the studio sanitizer, EPS declined with convert-first guidance; writes the dormant `monogram_uploaded_svg` + reveal pick; dashboard + public doors. Original deferral kept for lineage: **Image/artwork uploads this cycle** — deferred, not denied: no scouted evidence uploads drive premium; curation-not-freedom is the winning pattern (Tiffany, Minted rails — [tiffany.com](https://www.tiffany.com/client-services/personalization.html), [minted.com](https://www.minted.com/lp/customization)). Revisit after P2.
- **Delight animations on every step** — one branded weave-glint moment only; milestone-rationed delight is what reads expensive ([charisol](https://medium.com/charisol-pulse/better-ux-through-microinteractions-the-details-that-define-delight-d8fd4b8b22a6)).
