## 1. What Setnayan is now

**Setnayan is the operating system for a Filipino family's celebratory life** — a faith-aware *person graph* where every milestone across a lifetime and across generations (birth → binyag/aqiqah → 7th → first communion → confirmation → debut → wedding → anniversary → the couple's own children's milestones → their godchildren's) is anticipated, planned, captured, and shared, with vendors + membership + gifts as the economic engine.

It is no longer a wedding app, or even an events app. Three layers are fused into one product:
1. **A people graph** — you, your union, your dependents, your godchildren (a *social/relationship* layer, not just a planner).
2. **A faith-aware event engine** — the milestones that recur forever, and *the right rite per religion*.
3. **A relationship economy** — the vendor marketplace, e-gifts flowing *between people*, the memory archive (Alaala).

One line: **"from the wedding, the whole family life."**

## 2. The positioning discipline (this is the load-bearing part)

The redefinition is powerful but its risk is **breadth-dilution**. Locked discipline (consistent with the wedding-first + secondary-types-ride-the-same-rails locks):

- **Wedding is the wedge, not the whole.** Enter as the best PH wedding platform; the family life-OS is reached by RETENTION, not by scattering the brand across 14 event types on day one.
- **The person-graph is an LTV/retention engine, not a new front door.** It is how a one-time wedding becomes a 30-year relationship — exactly what the annual Membership monetizes.
- **Culture is the moat, so lean into it, don't sand it off.** Ninong/ninang, binyag/aqiqah/kumpil, pabuya, the grand debut — the specificity is the defensibility.

## 4. Closest real analogs — and the (confirmed) whitespace

- **The Knot Worldwide** — the events+vendors+lifestage analog, but US/generic, brand-fragmented (separate apps, not one graph), faith-blind, not PH.
- **FamilyAlbum / Tinybeans** — the family-graph+memory analog at real scale (30M / 1M+), but child-photo-focused with **no events, vendors, faith, or gifts** (verified — their expansion is into prints and family-safety/telehealth, never a marketplace).
- **Eventchy** — the direct PH events-marketplace competitor, but *flat*: AI matching only, no lifecycle graph, no faith, no gifts, no memory.
- **GCash** — the gifting-rail incumbent, but a custodial wallet feature, not a family-events graph.

**Whitespace — verified: no competitor anywhere combines all five layers** (person/relationship graph + lifecycle events + marketplace + memory + P2P gifts) in one product, and **no player is building a faith-aware (Catholic/Muslim/INC) family-events product** with the ninong/ninang graph + binyag/kumpil/aqiqah + pabuya. The integrated, faith-culture-specific bundle is the genuine whitespace — defensible *because* it is culturally specific: a global incumbent cannot casually localize it and, on the evidence, isn't trying.

## 5. Strategic implications

- **Monetization is unchanged and reinforced:** couples = demand engine (cheap AI + emotional à-la-carte + annual Membership); vendors = the recurring revenue (subs + per-lead tokens, 0% commission). The person-graph feeds the Membership (the all-events annuity). E-gifts (Pabuya) add a relationship flow that is **QR-DISPLAY ONLY** — Setnayan shows the recipient's own receiving QR, the giver pays peer-to-peer, money never touches the platform (definitively not an EMI; the BSP OPS question is open — see the regulatory correction below). Not a revenue line; a retention/relationship surface.
- **Build order stays wedding-first + demand-pull** (per the lifecycle research: instrument anchor-repurposing, invest where families pull — Zola's lesson).
- **The counsel gate is now strategic, not just legal:** the graph's most valuable edges (children's milestones, godparent networks, e-gifts) are exactly the sensitive-data / third-party-notify / money pieces. Clearing them through DPO/counsel is on the critical path to the differentiated product, not a side task.
- **⚠ Regulatory correction (verified — supersedes an earlier overclaim):** the QR-display e-gift model is definitively **not an EMI** (Setnayan never holds/issues e-money) AND is the *strongest possible facts* for staying out of money-service territory. BUT it is **NOT an automatic licensing shield.** Under **BSP Circular 1049 (RA 11127, National Payment Systems Act)**, an "Operator of a Payment System (OPS)" includes any person that "maintains the platform that **enables** payments or fund transfers, regardless of whether the source and destination accounts are maintained with the same or different institutions" — so a platform facilitating P2P transfers *can* fall under OPS registration even when money moves between two outside accounts and Setnayan never touches it. Whether *pure static-QR display* (arguably content display, with GCash's rail doing the actual transfer) escapes OPS is a **counsel question, not a settled fact.** Do not assume "no BSP registration needed." → route to counsel before launch.
- **Most urgent competitive move:** Eventchy is live in the same market with free AI matching. It validates that matching is table stakes and that speed matters — but it is *flat* (no graph, faith, memory, or gifts). The response is not to out-match on AI (a race to parity) but to **get to the differentiated depth** (the faith person-graph + lifecycle + memory + gifts) that Eventchy structurally lacks.

## 6. Promise vs. delivery — the honesty audit (owner 2026-07-12: "make sure our promise is what we can deliver")

The vision ("the OS for a Filipino family's celebratory life") is a north star. What we can HONESTLY promise TODAY is narrower — and saying so protects the brand.

### What we can deliver NOW (built + shippable)
- **Wedding planning, done well** — the vendor marketplace (0% commission), the checklist, seat-plan/3D, guest list, day-of, QR.
- **The anchor lifecycle for the couple's OWN data** — create an anniversary (real or memorable-date) → it appears on the home + Year view → the annual reminder fires (built this session: PRs #3175/#3176/#3178/#3180). Wedding → its own anniversary, automatically.
- **Memory** — Papic capture + the Alaala gallery + renders.
- **Free deterministic matching + emotional à-la-carte SKUs** at PH prices.

### What we CANNOT yet deliver (design-stage / gated / infra-dependent) — do not promise these as live
1. **The multi-generational family graph** (children's milestones, godparents, faith rites for kids, gender-reveal due dates, the per-person life-stage picker) is **designed, not built, and COUNSEL-GATED** — it stores minors' + sensitive data, so it needs DPO/counsel clearance first. Not available today.
2. **The seamless annual Membership** ("one AI across all your family's events, auto-renewing") depends on a **recurring-billing gateway that does not exist** — today it's manual re-payment. The single highest-leverage unbuilt dependency.
3. **The AI is deterministic + free (Rule 1), not a conversational planner** — authored rules + templates + date math, not an LLM. Benefit: free, private, instant, no per-use cost. Limitation: it routes and reminds; it does not "chat" or reason open-endedly.
4. **E-gifts are QR-display only** — and even that has an **open BSP OPS question** (§5). Cannot promise "send gifts through Setnayan" until counsel clears it.
5. **We store events, NOT documents/identity** — by design not a system-of-record for IDs, certificates, or a full family record. A system-of-record for *celebrations*, not legal/identity data.
6. **Optional data = optional value** — reminders/personalization are only as good as the anchors given; with nothing entered, it degrades gracefully to blank-create. Religion + civil status are **reference-only, never required.**

### How it's beneficial for daily life — honestly framed
Setnayan is **occasional-use, not a daily-open app** (families open it around events, not every day) — and promising "daily engagement" would be dishonest. The true daily benefit is **ambient, not engagement**: it *works for you* in the background so —
- **you never miss what matters *in your own household*** — anniversaries, your + your partner's milestone birthdays, your children's/elders' milestones, and the right lead time to plan arrive on their own (the "when do we start planning X?" mental load is offloaded). *(Scope honesty, owner-decided 2026-07-12 "keep it to just you": birthday reminders cover the user's OWN graph — self · partner · <18/>50 dependents — NOT arbitrary 18–50 friends/relatives. We do not claim "never miss ANY birthday.")*
- **when a moment comes, everything's ready** — one tap turns a reminder into a planned event with vendors, checklist, and the date already known;
- **your family's important days live in one place** — the Year view is a *derived, always-current* calendar of what matters, not a to-do list you maintain;
- **memories are captured + kept**, and **the people you love connect to the moments** (gifts, godparents — once built + cleared).

**The through-line:** Setnayan's daily value is that it *quietly remembers and prepares*, so the family's celebrations are never missed and never stressful — the value shows up at the moments that matter *precisely because the app did the remembering in between.* That is a promise we can keep today for a couple's own events, and grow into for the whole family graph as the gated + unbuilt pieces land.
