## 1 · Summary for the owner

We rebuild the Event Hub Maker to the approved phone prototype in **six PRs, one after the other**, and nothing reaches couples until you switch it on: every PR hides behind one switch (`NEXT_PUBLIC_MAKER_STAGES_STUDIO_ENABLED`, off in production) that is also **on for your own internal accounts**, so you can check each step on the live site with the test wedding (maria-and-jose) while every real couple keeps today's Maker, byte for byte. PR 1 is the frame — the top nav (✕ · Stages | Studio · ↺ · ✓ Apply), the half-screen lower panel with 44 px rows, the one bottom-sheet style, and the Studio home of eleven tiles that open the editors we already have (Seat plan opens the shipped seat plan unchanged). PR 2 is Stages — the stage ▾ picker with each stage's guest pages, the parts strip, and Style · Text · Animate for every part, with plain words typed on the page and saved to Info. PR 3 is the parts list — ＋ above/below, the Post Event scenes, drag to reorder, remove/delete, and the Reveal as the first part of three stages. PR 4 redraws the Studio editors to the prototype — Info (one form, with Your Event Hub and "Which version guests see · All of them"), Look's four tabs, Schedule, Love Story, E-Gifts, RSVP with "How guests answer", Prints as the eleventh tile. PR 5 is Mood Board & Dress Code (lanes, inspiration by slot, palette from photos, ✨ Auto, the colour picker) and the three Logo additions. PR 6 is what guests see — the four per-guest parts on Invitation › Me, the Reply card, the full-screen camera with its three looks, and the passcode after Welcome (the only migration). Each PR ends with a check card: a link, at most three steps, and a phone screenshot; you reply "ok" before the next PR starts. The seat plan editor is not touched.
