The model: the reception venue is the one anchor. distance(venue → vendor) ≤ vendor's service radius decides who shows.
· ① Choose venue: browse reception venues across the couple's 2 onboarding areas (Tagaytay · Quezon City), compare, pick one.
· ② Venue set: every other category is sorted by distance to that venue; vendors who can't reach it drop off. Switch the chosen venue and the whole vendor list re-filters — pick a Tagaytay venue vs a QC venue in phase ① and watch who appears.
· Ceremony in another city? V1 keeps the reception as the only matching anchor; the ceremony is just an address on the schedule + website.
