## Six NEW defects created by the latest fixes

**1 · The photo wall now goes DARK on ordinary free weddings — worse than before.**
Fixing the wall that switched itself on for everyone swapped in the wrong question. The gate
asks whether the couple has *crew cameras* — but free guests shoot on the free 50-shot pool, and
those cameras only exist if the couple happened to open the Papic screen. So on a free wedding
whose couple never opened it: **guests take real photos, the photos land in the wall, and the
wall is refused on every phone.** Before the fix those photos reached phones.

**2 · The paid "mark this moment" button now appears and then refuses.**
Teaching the control room about the by-hand on-air switch made the button visible to a
hand-streaming host — the handler behind it was never taught the same fact and still insists
they are off air. **That button is the exact control the change was made for.**

**3 · The never-interrupt protection reaches only one screen.**
The control room honours the on-air moment; the guest page and the projector do not. A paid host
streaming by hand whose day lapses mid-ceremony sees *"nothing is interrupted"* while guests drop
to one camera. **The file's own comment warns against exactly this disagreement.**

**4 · Two of the couple's own screens still announce the wall on a wedding with no Papic.**
The guest-facing *"warming up"* promise was removed; the couple-facing twin was not.

**5 · The real defect that was retracted** — see the top of this file.

**6 · A guard was re-pointed and cannot fail.** The test protecting the new printed camera cards
looks for a name *anywhere in the file* — and the file mentions it in two comments. The old
behaviour can be restored wholesale and the test stays green. 🔑 **It was reported as
mutation-tested because the occurrence count changed. A count changing is not a test failing.**
